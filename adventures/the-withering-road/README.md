# The Withering Road — Adventure 01

**Edition:** 2.1 (approved narrative draft)  
**Scope:** Five-session Veilbound adventure for a party beginning at Level 1.  
**Spoilers:** The GM manuscript and quick reference reveal the mystery. Only share individual player handouts as they are discovered.

## Source manuscripts

- [GM manuscript](gm-manuscript.md) — adventure overview, Dunmere introduction, three middle chapters in any order, Ember Watch finale, and appendices.
- [GM quick reference](gm-quick-reference.md) — clue network, chapter-order handoffs, evidence tracking, and milestones.
- [Player handouts](player-handouts.md) — **17 individually labeled, player-facing props**: P-1/P-2 introduction, M-1–M-3 market testimony and petition, D-1–D-5 manifest/receiving/transfer/vault/archive records, C-1–C-4 dispatch/route/salvage/optional courier note, and F-1–F-3 release and public follow-up forms. **Only distribute the individual handout that the GM reveals; do not share the entire file during play.**

## Run order and advancement

1. Session 1: **A Village in Need**, Level 1.
2. Sessions 2–4: **The Market's Stories**, **The Sealed Depot**, and **The Old Road Cavern** in any order. The first meaningful group breakthrough is a possible GM-declared milestone from Level 1 to 2, normally after Session 2.
3. Session 5: **Ember Watch and the Reckoning**, intended Level 3 after a second, separately GM-declared milestone for assembling a workable relief expedition, normally after Session 4.

Completing all three middle chapters is not a prerequisite for reaching Ember Watch. The Market earns trust and support, the Depot produces records and equipment, and the Cavern establishes route truth and safety. Earlier chapters can improve later options without locking them.

## Status and rules boundaries

This is an **adventure narrative draft**, not a complete or independently balanced encounter release. Lesser cave spiders and B06-EN-04 (Veil-touched Dire Spider) still require executable stat blocks, encounter tests, and measured battle maps. The Hunter and Ordinary Soldier fixtures are not certified as level-appropriate for this adventure. The dire spider's fang/web/Vigor profile is an **approved provisional adventure design**, not a change to canonical creature rules. No automatic level awards, rest or resource refill are implied.

The Markdown files are the editable source. Print-ready PDF exports were generated separately; this source directory does not claim to contain compiled PDFs.

### Player prop consistency

The numbered props in `player-handouts.md` are the printable documents referenced by the matching identifiers in `gm-manuscript.md`. D-1 and D-2 intentionally present separate outbound/receiving counts for player comparison; no GM solution is printed beside them. C-1 and F-1 provide the essential dispatch and editable release receipt. Talla's optional C-4 route note may be found at the abandoned refuge, but **Mira's actual Life/Restoration-marked ring remains with Talla at Ember Watch**, unless play changes its custody. The cavern's older courier strongbox is unrelated to Talla.
