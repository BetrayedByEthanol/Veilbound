# THE WITHERING ROAD — GM QUICK REFERENCE
## Five sessions · Three middle branches in any order · Spoilers

### The six truths to keep straight

1. A storm and a detour to **roofless Old Kiln Rest** spoiled grayleaf; **not** magic or theft.
2. **Renn Calder** authorized the diversion and concealed the known shortfall out of fear.
3. **Lot 17** outbound: `01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06`; returned to depot: `01, 02, 04, 06`; left at relay: `03, 05`.
4. A **separate intact dry reserve** is held by **Naeris at Ember Watch**; custody requires sensible accountability, not completed-chapter flags.
5. **Talla Fen** is alive at Ember Watch and carries Mira's inactive signature-bearing ring; the ring is not a cure.
6. Cavern spiders and the territorial **Hunter** are uninvolved wildlife; Heartweb is an **optional** dangerous boss and salvage, not the location of the essential case.

### Six possible orders: what the next chapter gains

| Order | Carry into second branch | Carry into third branch |
|---|---|---|
| Market → Depot → Cavern | Edda's testimony can support supervised depot access. | Depot records identify missing crates and authorization. |
| Market → Cavern → Depot | Jorin's route account can guide the cave approach. | Dispatch directs the record comparison. |
| Depot → Market → Cavern | Signed paperwork sharpens witness questioning. | Witness and receipt details guide cargo recovery. |
| Depot → Cavern → Market | Numbered receipts focus the physical search. | Independent physical evidence strengthens the public correction. |
| Cavern → Market → Depot | Salvaged dispatch gives witnesses specific facts. | Attested testimony supports archive verification. |
| Cavern → Depot → Market | Case points to matching depot source records. | Double-sourced evidence supports a durable public account. |

Each handoff depends on **actual earned evidence, assistance and preserved originals**; later locations never require that asset to function. The Market builds **people and legitimacy**, the Depot builds **material proof and cargo support**, and the Cavern builds **field proof and road safety**. Each confirms the Ember Watch lead independently. All three improve the **quality of the solution**, not the party's permission to travel.

### Hard rules and production boundaries

- No universal Persuasion, Stealth or Investigation Training. Relevant Ability + **actually applicable narrow Learned Discipline** if consequentially uncertain.
- **Depot example targets:** main Lockwork 12; force lock 15; roof Climbing 12; lift Engineering 15; herbal samples 12; optional archive Engineering 15 / maintenance Lockwork 18 / force panel 18.
- Core evidence is visible when properly inspected. Check failures change time, quality, social response or risk, not whether the story exists.
- **Naeris's eight dry parcels:** 2 already allocated locally; 6 unassigned; Mira's immediate emergency need ≈4, narrative units only.
- Hunter: source-backed B06-EN-03, **unrated**, do not force Level 1 combat.
- Hale: source-backed Ordinary Soldier B06-EN-02, **unrated** for this party.
- Lesser cave spiders and Dire Spider: **not executable yet**; require stat blocks and encounter testing before real rules-complete fights.
- Dire Spider **approved design only:** fangs 6+d8 Piercing Pen2, d20+6, TX-MY DC10 on actual puncture; Vigor64, HP16, Armor3, regen5/real own turn; two web shots range3 squares; wall/ceiling climb; prey isolation; **no burn or offensive magic**. Web escape/action and climbing MP are still unfinished.
- Two explicit GM-declared milestone **windows**: meaningful first branch breakthrough (L1→L2), actionable relief expedition (L2→L3, intended before Session 5). Not automatic after a session or fight.
- No automatic rest, XP/CR, free spell, forced confession or fixed ending.

### Contingencies: what if they…

| Player action | Respect and respond |
|---|---|
| Go to Ember Watch immediately | Allow limited genuine relief, testimony and practical release negotiation; finale still needs transport/accounting |
| Accuse Renn in Session 1 | Let him answer, ask for supporting proof; don't hide the other locations |
| Break into depot violently | Track damage and credibility, allow surrender and retreat; use real authorized combat profile only |
| Ignore the vault | Ordinary vault holds essential reserve voucher; no secret-room dependency |
| Avoid entire cavern fight | Case is accessible via refuge and ledge; use exact route and observation consequences |
| Leave spider alive | Story remains completable; optional chamber and silk unclaimed |
| Bring only one clue | Naeris can cross-check need, Talla, Edda or another record; no mandatory evidence score |
| Save no medicine | Still conclude in-world: explain costs, remaining people and future options |
