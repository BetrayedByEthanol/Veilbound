# THE WITHERING ROAD — PLAYER HANDOUTS
## Adventure 01 · Edition 2.1 · Print one record at a time

**For the GM preparing props:** Each heading has the same identifier used in the GM manuscript. Print or copy **only the individual handout that the characters actually find, inspect, receive, or help complete**. Do not hand players this entire collection: later records contain discoveries they may not yet have earned. Dates, witness names, signatures, and release quantities are intentionally left blank where they depend on play. The documents are mundane records, not enchanted evidence.

---

## Handout P-1 — Dunmere's Public Notice

**NOTICE TO ALL WHO HANDLE GRAYLEAF**

The latest grayleaf shipment, **Lot 17**, is being inspected. Residents should not distribute unverified bundles. Those who have suffered from spoiled stock should speak to Healer Mira Fen.

The municipal depot holds incoming stores for inspection. Movement of medical supplies beyond the road gate requires a name, destination, and reason. The ledger keeper will consider lawful requests to inspect the public shipment record.

— Dunmere Stores Office

## Handout D-5 — Protected Archive: Older Emergency Procedure

**FORMER STOREHOUSE PROCEDURE — EMERGENCY DISTRIBUTION**

1. Record the household or settlement requesting assistance.
2. Keep the original store's custody ledger intact.
3. Identify a recipient and a witness for each handover.
4. State how vulnerable freight will remain dry and accounted for in transit.
5. Record what was delivered, what remained, and any later correction.

**Protected duplicate notation:** *Ember Watch dry reserve is a separate holding, not a recovered Lot 17 crate.*

**Covered-route memorandum:** *The maintained watch road can accommodate ordinary freight when its crossings are sound. A smaller footpath approaches the old road; verify it before committing a loaded wagon.*

**Available cargo fittings noted in old plans:** canvas cover; cord and straps; wooden support pins.

*This is historical guidance, not a standing order compelling any steward to release goods.*
