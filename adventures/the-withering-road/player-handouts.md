# THE WITHERING ROAD — PLAYER HANDOUTS

*Print each handout separately. The GM chooses when each becomes available. This file contains no GM mystery resolution beyond what players might legitimately discover.*

## Handout 1 — Dunmere's Public Notice

**NOTICE TO ALL WHO HANDLE GRAYLEAF**

The latest grayleaf shipment is being inspected. Residents should not distribute unverified bundles. Those who have suffered from spoiled stock should speak to Healer Mira Fen.

Known shipments may be checked at the eastern supply cache. Movement of medical supplies beyond the road gate requires a name, destination and reason. The ledger keeper will consider lawful requests to inspect the public shipment record.

— Dunmere Stores Office

## Handout 3 — The Courier's Note

> Mira,
>
> We reached the eastern relay after the rain came in hard. I sent the second dispatch toward Ember Watch, where the reserve should still be sealed and dry. I left the old ring with the cache pouch so there would be something familiar to show if I could not come back by the lower road. Please tell anyone looking for me that I went uphill.
>
> — Talla

*This note is inside the cache pouch near the ring. It is handwriting, not an enchanted message.*

## Handout 4 — Ember Watch Reserve Receipt

**EMERGENCY STORES — EMBER WATCH**

Received: sealed grayleaf reserve, separate from Lot 17. Vault seals intact at time of entry.

Release requires a named recipient and a documented plan for safe onward transport and distribution. A certified record, recognized courier or persuasive evidence of urgent public need may support the request. Allocation and escort remain the shrine keeper's responsibility.

— Ember Watch, inventory desk
