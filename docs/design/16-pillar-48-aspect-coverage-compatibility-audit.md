3. **Current status:** [Gifted](../rules/08-archetypes/gifted.md) explicitly says the matrix remains to be **migrated to structured content**; the repository currently has schemas, **no populated `content/aspects/` directory**, and **no executable catalogue covering these 48 Aspect techniques**. The [October review follow-ups](october-2026-review-followups.md) deem 48 names and boundary rulings migration candidates, **not** automatic promotion of old spells or effect numbers.
4. **Mechanics constraints:** [technique tiers](../rules/07-veil-magic/technique-tiers-and-expression.md), [Archetypes](../rules/08-archetypes/archetype-identities.md), [manifestation](../rules/07-veil-magic/manifestation-resolution.md), [Direct Veil](../rules/07-veil-magic/resonance-and-intrusion.md), [HP/Vigor/wounds](../rules/06-vigor-wounds-death/dying-and-wounds.md). **Veil Inspection L1 / Blink L3 are separate automatically acquired Universal procedures**, not one of the 48 Pillar techniques.
5. **Caution:** exact Aspect names and compatibility ratings have source support; **spell effects, available subdomains, and role tags below do not automatically follow from a name**. Example: "Fortune–Luck → DF/CT" is an **authoring hypothesis**, *not* permission for a defensive reroll or probability edit. Where the archive provides representative examples (e.g. Fire Conflagration, Life Restoration, Earth Fortification, Gifted conduits), use them only as direction, not as complete spells.

## Coverage method — qualitative, not balance certification

Proposed authoring tags describe **a likely way to test an Aspect**, not a mandatory feature or an already executable effect:

| Tag | Test function | Example question to answer during technique authoring |
|---|---|---|
| **AT** | Harm / direct attack | Does it generate a legal direct HP or Vigor damage packet, and by what delivery? |
| **DF** | Defense / prevention | Does it create a Guard-compatible, ward, protection or mitigation effect? |
| **HE** | Healing / recovery | Can it restore HP, Repair Progress, Vigor or prevent deterioration *within existing rules*? |
| **CT** | Control / denial | Does it alter movement, circumstances, access, concentration or willingness? |
| **MV** | Mobility / traversal | Can it aid positioning, escape, travel or environmental navigation? |
| **KN** | Investigation / information | Does it provide bounded sensory clues, recall, diagnosis, identification or insight? |
| **SO** | Social / support / deception | Does it protect alliances, communicate, persuade by normal means, or mislead perception? |
| **CR** | Craft / creation / environment | Does it shape materials, equipment, terrain, reagents or ecology? |

**Eight tags are dimensions for audit coverage, not a claim that every Pillar must have all eight, nor that the existence of a tag implies a level-1 spell.** A legal Pillar/Aspect identity may have multiple compatible techniques, none, or more narrow boundaries once authored. The risks in the Pillar table are authoring priorities, not contradictions already found in canonical rules.

## Archived PR #59 48-Aspect identity and category hypotheses — SUPERSEDED BY CANON

### Current canonical taxonomy (PR #59 assignments below are historical)

The **current** [Veil and Pillars taxonomy](../world/veil-and-pillars.md#three-aspects-per-pillar) and [canonical 48-Aspect reference](../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md#reading-the-categories) supersede PR #59's earlier self-oriented wording. The following table states **current canon**, not the historical working definitions:

| Canonical Aspect category | Current canonical meaning | Not automatically equivalent to |
|---|---|---|
| **External** | Expressing a Pillar outward through phenomena, forces, interactions or influence on the world. | Offense, **[Projection]** eligibility, a specific range, or High Focus. |
| **Internal** | Operating within **any valid subject's** physical, bodily, mental or metaphysical condition, including the caster, an ally, an enemy or a valid object/structure. | Self-only enhancement, defense only, the **Inward** technique tag, or Low Focus. High-Focus projected techniques can target another subject's interior. |
| **Transform** | Changing properties, state, composition, form, configuration or nature; the change may be temporary or lasting. | Utility only, automatic crafting, or unrestricted alteration of creatures or matter. |

**Historical note:** In PR #59, the Internal wording emphasized *the self* and the category assignments below were E?/I?/T? hypotheses. Both are superseded by the later B05 decisions. The archived wording and guesses must **not** be interpreted as alternative canonical definitions.

**These are lore/metaphysical domain categories, not mechanical combat-role categories.** The separate [technique-expression metadata](../rules/07-veil-magic/technique-tiers-and-expression.md#universal-technique-expression-metadata) assigns **[Projection], Inward, [Embodiment], Consecration, Devotional Rite, Conduit Form compatibility and Cadence** to authored *individual techniques*, not automatically to entire Aspect types. Likewise the [Archetype identity rules](../rules/08-archetypes/archetype-identities.md#archetype-comparison) expressly say offense, defense, control, support and utility arise from build and technique choices rather than being fixed Archetype combat roles.

**Historical evidence limit (as of PR #59):** canonical files at the time confirmed **16 Pillars, three category definitions and 48 named Aspects**, but did not assign categories per name. The compatibility matrix contains *names and S/P/— ratings*, not complete category/boundary prose. **This limitation was subsequently resolved by explicit author decisions**, not by discovering the original source mapping; see the [canonical 48-Aspect reference](../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md). The table below is retained as **historical comparison material**, not the current classification.

**Interpretation rule for the next table:** **E? / I? / T? = proposed External / Internal / Transform mapping, unverified**; the question mark is intentional, **not** a compatibility rating. Each Pillar is given a **candidate one-of-each arrangement for evaluation**, not a proof of its original naming-order mapping. **All 48 domain and boundary clauses are explicitly editorial hypotheses** informed by the names and scattered historical examples. A boundary clause flags what a mechanic **must not be assumed to include automatically**; it is **not** a final ban on a carefully authored, justified effect. No attack, defense, healing or skill bonus is created here.

### Five concrete boundary regression questions for technique authoring

1. **Healing vs prevention:** What specifically separates **Life–Restoration**, **Life–Preservation**, **Kinship–Sacrifice** and **Alchemy–Elixirs**? Which, if any, directly adds **HP**, **Repair Progress**, prevents **Dying**, or changes a condition, and with what VP/action/cadence costs? The names alone confer none of those.
2. **Mental effects vs appearances:** Does a **Mind–Psionics** or **Secrets–Hallucination** technique act *inside* a protected mind (therefore [Intrusion](../rules/07-veil-magic/resonance-and-intrusion.md)) or create an external stimulus/phenomenon (therefore the appropriate Manifestation/environmental pathway)? Naming an effect illusion does not bypass internal resistance.
3. **Defense ownership:** Does **Earth–Fortification**, **Order–Stability** or **Kinship–Guardian** alter Armor, Guard, the incoming attack, a battlefield obstacle, or an actual [Protective Intervene](../rules/05-combat/protective-intervene-v0.md) resource? A defensive lore label grants **none** of those for free.
4. **Change vs creation:** Does **Fire–Forge**, **Alchemy–Transmutation**, **Arts–Creation** or **Earth–Bounty** work pre-existing materials, convert compositions, produce culturally meaningful objects, or improve growth? Avoid granting unlimited goods, magic items, wealth or crafting skips through vocabulary.
5. **Freedom vs movement:** Does **Liberty–Autonomy**, **Liberty–Defiance**, **Liberty–Exploration**, **Skywinds–Swiftness** or **Lightning–Reflexes** protect choice, resist coercion, cross terrain, change ordinary MP costs or affect reactions? Do not grant [Blink](../rules/07-veil-magic/blink-v0.md), unlimited Turn AP, or free teleportation by analogy.

## Constraint-satisfying candidate sets — both use exactly eight distinct Pillars

### A. Historical Gifted stress-test set — VERIFIED LEGAL, NOT NECESSARILY BEST MVP CONTENT

This set appears in the historical Gifted v27 three-Focus stress test (emotions remapped to the same data's explicit order). It satisfies **all eight S links**:

| Emotion | Aspect | Matrix |
|---|---|---|
| Joy | Liberty–Exploration | **S** |
| Sadness | Life–Restoration | **S** |
| Anger | Fire–Conflagration | **S** |
| Fear | Lightning–Reflexes | **S** |
| Surprise | Mind–Insight | **S** |
| Disgust | Alchemy–Concoctions | **S** |
| Trust | Earth–Fortification | **S** |
| Anticipation | Strength–Training | **S** |

**Pillars:** Fire, Lightning, Earth, Life, Liberty, Mind, Alchemy, Strength. The 48×8 matrix permits **202 distinct Strong-only emotion-to-Pillar perfect matchings** within these eight Pillars (ignoring which of a Pillar's three Aspects is chosen). The particular eight links above are one legal matching.

**Advantages:** historically tested conceptually against High/Medium/Low Gifted; intense martial/evasion coverage, physical amplification, damage, defense, recovery, movement, perception, reagents and self-improvement. **Costs:** narrower dedicated teamwork/kinship and concealment/illusion families, high overlap of Lightning/Strength with other offense/defense/mobility functions. The old stress test validated **chassis differentiation**, not real spell balance or a populated spell catalogue.

### B. Role-diverse eight-Pillar hypothesis — VERIFIED LEGAL, PROPOSED FOR MVP EVALUATION

Retain Fire, Life, Earth, Liberty, Mind, Alchemy, and **replace Lightning + Strength with Kinship + Secrets** to gain explicit group/relationship and deception/shadow domains. The following independent assignment gives **all eight S links**:

| Emotion | Aspect | Matrix |
|---|---|---|
| Joy | Liberty–Exploration | **S** |
| Sadness | Life–Restoration | **S** |
| Anger | Fire–Conflagration | **S** |
| Fear | Earth–Fortification | **S** |
| Surprise | Secrets–Hallucination | **S** |
| Disgust | Alchemy–Concoctions | **S** |
| Trust | Kinship–Guardian | **S** |
| Anticipation | Mind–Insight | **S** |

**Pillars:** Fire, Life, Earth, Liberty, Mind, Alchemy, Kinship, Secrets. The matrix permits **188 distinct Strong-only emotion-to-Pillar perfect matchings** within these eight Pillars (same counting convention). **This proves feasibility, not balance or an optimal unique answer**; arbitrary weights on magic roles would bias a numerical optimization.

**Why this set is the preferred design candidate, not a lock:**
- It includes explicit domains for **harm/Manifestation (Fire), healing (Life), protection/terrain (Earth), traversal/agency (Liberty), mental information/Intrusion (Mind), preparation/material change (Alchemy), cooperative safeguarding (Kinship) and concealment/illusion (Secrets)**.
- It provides **eight different Pillars to a Gifted** without treating every emotion as necessarily combat-aggressive, and can supply distinctive one-Pillar Priest identities as well as contrasting one-Aspect Anointed signatures.
- It tests **different adjudication paths**: physical damage, wound repair, defensive actions, movement, mental Intrusion, item/craft boundaries and ordinary social/perception interaction. This is more valuable for the first MVP than a second speed/burst-focused Pillar *if* these hypotheses survive actual technique authoring.
- It sacrifices **Lightning's overt kinetic/electrical action** and **Strength's direct martial enhancement**, which should remain first-class expansion candidates, not be declared redundant or removed from the world.

### Selection sensitivity

1. **For front-line action / reactive defense priority:** use the historical eight-Pillar set; Lightning + Strength have clearer mechanical synergy with weapons, reactive defense and Focus differentiation.
2. **For mixed social / exploration / intrigue MVP:** investigate the role-diverse set first; Kinship + Secrets deserve meaningful mechanics rather than being decorative labels.
3. **For Order/Death/Water/Skywinds/Fortune/Arts representation:** a **ninth or tenth Pillar** may be justified by actual campaign needs or missing content boundaries, but the matrix **does not force it**. Adding Pillars creates more three-Aspect priest content and Signature work, so weigh the *number of playable techniques*, not only setting diversity.
4. **Plausible (P) connections may be used** in any future character build with their creation interpretation; requiring Strong-only is an optional quality filter for this audit's examples, **not a new Gifted rule**.

## MVP technique production and Archetype compatibility gates

A set of legal Pillars is **necessary but not sufficient** for a usable level-1–3 catalogue.

| Archetype / Focus | Must be tested before declaring an eight-Pillar MVP playable |
|---|---|
| **Priest / High–Medium–Low** | Each selectable Priest Pillar needs **three usable Aspect identities with at least one substantive legal early technique where appropriate**; author starting Prepared Prayer counts, Active Prayers, Consecration and/or Devotional Rite compatibility. **One-Pillar identity cannot rely on another Pillar's catalogue**. |
| **Scholar / High–Medium–Low** | Need independently learned, selected cross-Pillar techniques; actual known/prepared counts; High synthesis, Medium inward rerouting and Low prepared Embodiment choices. Do not charge automatically unlocked Universal Inspection/Blink against Pillar repertoire. |
| **Gifted / High–Medium–Low** | All **eight actual chosen emotion-linked Aspects** require a **usable early technique path**. High needs authored `[Projection]` qualifying expressions, Medium needs compatible **Conduit Forms/Grade I–III/cadence**, Low needs **Embodied Expressions** for chosen Anchors. Check tempo-safe Riders/Pulses and emotional accessibility. The compatibility matrix alone cannot supply these effects. |
| **Anointed / High–Medium–Low** | For selected bonded Aspect examples, author **baseline 0-VP Signature Expression**, sensible Birth Imprint, bounded Authority and Destiny compatibility, and Focus-appropriate expression. Full Aspect catalogue access does not replace the missing Signature records. |
| **Shared** | For each early technique: state **tier 1/2 or otherwise eligible level**, action/Turn AP category, VP state/cost, targeting and range, tags, delivery (Intrusion, Imposition, Manifestation), defense, hit/HP-or-Vigor packet, duration/maintenance/cadence, environmental constraints and failure. Existing magic/durability rules take precedence. |

**Minimum content is not necessarily 24 spell names.** A full eight-Pillar / 24-Aspect roster is a reasonable *content-development target* if every chosen Priest Pillar is to be unrestricted; however, the real acceptance test is that *every permitted MVP character build* has a meaningful authored technique/Signature/feature loop at levels 1–3, that it can afford and use. It may take multiple techniques and expression variants per Aspect; raw counts are not sufficient.

## Practical prototype recommendation and lock gates

**Do not lock the eight Pillars yet.** The data supports a defensible **candidate B (Fire, Life, Earth, Liberty, Mind, Alchemy, Kinship, Secrets)** and a meaningful alternative **candidate A (historical Fire, Lightning, Earth, Life, Liberty, Mind, Alchemy, Strength)**. Choose based on pilot-adventure identity after short content prototypes.

**Prototype first:** **Fire + Mind** as deliberately different mechanical domains: Fire tests external Manifestation/physical harm/area/Forge boundaries, while Mind tests protected internal targets, Intrusion, cognitive information and non-damage outputs. **Add Life immediately in the first content expansion** to test healing and wound repair before claiming a combat MVP. This order is a **proposal**, not a rule.

**Selection may be locked only after:**
1. Validated canonical-compatibility migration, with **16×3×8 row/column integrity** and legal distinct-Pillar example assignments.
2. At least one **fully specified level-1/2** technique or authored expression for each planned early-play Aspect, plus multiple test cases where role overlap would otherwise be ambiguous.
3. For chosen Priest Pillars, viable one-Pillar sample loadouts; for Gifted, three-Focus eight-link samples with **real** techniques/Conduits/Embodiments; for Scholar, worthwhile modular selection; for Anointed, playable Signature/Authority example(s).
4. Native interaction/balance review of Intrusion, Manifestation, Defense/Intervene, HP/Vigor recovery, movement/tempo, and resource affordability.
5. A pilot adventure scope confirming the **mandatory** magical domains and identifying any missing Pillar that materially improves coverage.

### Open design risks / negative findings

- **No authored complete 48-Aspect technique catalogue** exists in current source. Therefore **none** of the role tags certify actual MVP mechanical coverage.
- Archived 48×8 matrix is marked locked in the audit and conceptually retained in current Gifted rules, but **has not yet been migrated to canonical machine-readable records**. This audit does **not** promote it by itself.
- **Three Aspects per Pillar and all 48 named identities/categories/boundaries are now canonical** in the [world reference](../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md), established by subsequent author decision rather than assumed row order. The E?/I?/T? rows above are **superseded historical candidates**. Their speculative exclusions must not override the newer author-approved domains.
- Word overlap does **not** justify importing D&D spells. The domains and boundaries for **Death/Mastery**, **Water/Medium**, **Fortune/Manipulation/Luck** and **Arts/Creation** are now documented canonically; **their concrete technique effects, interactions, costs and access** still require authoring.
- **Eight is the mathematical minimum**, not automatically the best number for campaign scope; **nine or ten** is a content decision, not a compatibility requirement.
- **Gifted S and P are equal mechanically after creation.** Do not introduce extra VP, a disadvantage, level delay or per-cast approval for P links.

## Audit decision summary

**CURRENT SOURCE HIERARCHY:** all 16 Pillars and 48 exact Aspect names plus their **author-approved category/identity/boundary definitions** are canonical in the [world reference](../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md). The PR #59 table above is **historical/provisional**, not current canon. Eight Gifted emotions, the exact unchanged compatibility table, two **verified** eight-Pillar Strong-only legal assignments and a method for evaluating authored mechanics remain valid design input.

**PROVISIONAL RECOMMENDATION:** compare the historical eight against the role-diverse eight, begin Fire + Mind prototypes with Life close behind, and choose based on playable content and pilot scope. **No Pillar or Aspect has been locked into or excluded from the MVP by this audit.**

**NEXT WORK:** select prototype domains and author level-1/2 techniques and three-Focus expression tests using the **canonical Aspect boundaries**. The lore classification milestone is complete, but B05 MVP technique content is not. The existing [Roadmap and MVP Readiness](veilbound-roadmap-and-mvp-readiness.md) B04/B05 blockers remain open until the technique, creation and encounter content exists.
