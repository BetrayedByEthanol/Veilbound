# B04/B05 — Anointed L3 Guided Regeneration Under Native G5 Pressure v0

> **Bounded L3 High/Medium/Low Restoration encounter witness; not certified combat readiness.** This extension of merged [#172 native defensive Soldier/Sentry encounters](b04-b05-anointed-three-focus-native-g5-defensive-witness-v0.md), [#173 natural recovery comparisons](b04-b05-anointed-natural-regeneration-g5-attrition-v0.md) and [#171 source-paid Guided Authority](b04-b05-anointed-l3-guided-regeneration-native-authority-v0.md) tests whether a genuinely paid **Guided Regeneration Primary** can give its **existing extra +1 HP** Vigor-free after one genuine G5 injury and the recipient's G4 Step. It creates no new Step, talent, Signature, attack profile or Authority grade.

## Exact policy scope

The [native G5 Guided pressure runner](../../scripts/b05_anointed_native_g5_guided_pressure_v0.py) resolves **24 deterministic policies = 3 Restoration Anointed Focuses (AH/AM/AL) × 2 opponents (authentic Soldier and prepared Firebound Sentry) × 2 modes (Fixed/Maintained) × 2 Authority choices (ordinary/one-Destiny Lesser)**. Each witness is **L3** only. The enemy's single Round-1 G5 paid melee action/hit and genuine G4 source-backed physical/Fire/Vigor damage are unchanged from #172; an ordinary natural `RegenerationChoice(hp=1)` is chosen in Round 2, and Round-1 optional HP/Repair allocation is explicitly zero. All actors complete **two actual G0 rounds**.

The authored policy names are **`ordinary_guided`** (pay the Guided host, make the normal Round-2 1-HP/1-Vigor allocation, **do not** pay Destiny or claim a +1 special HP increment) and **`lesser_guided`** (same paid host and identical normal 1-HP/1-Vigor allocation, then spend **exactly 1 Destiny** for the already-active Guided extra **+1 HP with 0 additional recipient Vigor**). The source-authorized extra increment is **not** retroactive regeneration, a second Step, Vigor generation, wound Repair Progress, free Guided activation, extra throughput beyond the established +1 or a waiver of the natural 1-Vigor cost.

At **High** Focus, the G0-authenticated **20-AP Projection / 2 VP** Guided host is established in Round 1 **before** the enemy strikes; its patient receives authentic injury before Round-1/2 Steps. At **Medium** Focus, Round-1 genuine **24-AP General Investment** selects the willing living patient as its only Vessel, then the enemy hits and the patient takes its normal Round-1 Step. Medium uses its next genuine Round-2 own turn to spend **24 AP and 2 VP** for the active Guided Primary before the invested recipient's Round-2 Step. At **Low** Focus, the G5 enemy strikes Low before the real Low Round-1 Step. Low casts the **30-AP / 2 VP Self [Embodiment]** Guided Primary later on that same own turn (not during the already-ended Step), and its extra +1 can only apply at the real **next-round own Step**.

Both **Fixed** and **Maintained** are paid on real G0 action and VP receipts. Fixed spends **2 Spent VP** and has a bounded existing G3 `round+10` expiration; Maintained commits **2 Committed VP** to a live G3 host. Neither changes the ordinary 1-point recipient-Vigor debit, grants free Focus AP, or creates a duplicate Destiny pool. **High/Medium Single Target** uses the already-correct [Guided technique-local weighted range](../rules/07-veil-magic/guided-regeneration-v0.md), visibility and clear path, plus Medium's currently invested living Vessel. **Low Self** requires actual Embodiment.

The delivery policy is **explicitly unopposed**. High/Medium call `cast(...,visible=True,unopposed=True)`, and the beneficiary/Medium Investment is consenting; Low is Self. This is *not* a contested beneficial **+8 Intrusion Barrier** adjudication, changing consent, hostile reaction/interception, blocked HP healing or a dynamic sight challenge. Each authentic defender explicitly selects `take_hit` for the one G5 attack. Natural G4 HP/Repair decisions are explicit policies, not presumed optimal play.

## Actual evidence and conservation

The [ten focused tests](../../tests/test_b05_anointed_native_g5_guided_pressure_v0.py) verify all **24** source cases, one G0-paid Soldier/Sentry G5 hit with real G4 damage, exactly **two recipient Regeneration Steps**, ordinary 1-HP/1-Vigor R2 allocation, real Focus-specific G0 Projection and G3 host/AP/VP receipts, Medium's earlier paid one-Vessel Investment, and Focus-correct host timing across rounds. They compare identical same-damage/mode baseline and Lesser cases: Lesser restores **exactly +1 HP** with **no extra recipient Vigor** and spends **1 of 2 finite Destiny**; the normal 1-HP allocation still costs **1 recipient Vigor**, and the ordinary no-waiver case leaves Destiny at 2. The separate **`g4_anointed_guided_waiver_applied`** event must follow the actual Round-2 `regeneration_step` and refer to the authentic G3 paid host, not an external source flag. Tests also reject forged reports even with recomputed digest, wrong source level/inputs, and unearned Returning Step pulses.

Every report includes source character/enemy SHA256, actual G0–G5 ledger, host action/payment indices, exact G4 injury/HP/Vigor resources, Destiny/VP remaining and full deterministic `attest_policy()` replay. **This remains a bounded fixed-policy witness, not certified role effectiveness**, a calibrated win rate, variable tactics, L3 High/Medium/Low combat proficiency, representative GM participation, or campaign play.
