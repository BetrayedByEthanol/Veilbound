# B04/B05 — Anointed L3 Guided Regeneration Native Authority v0

> **Bounded High/Medium/Low Life/Restoration L3 G0/G3/G4 source delivery.** This requires a genuinely paid Guided Regeneration Primary host and spends one Destiny only on its authored **additional +1 HP**, after the recipient's own real natural Regeneration Step. It does not create a new technique, Step, combat encounter, independent Focus certificate or higher-grade Authority.

## Paid, exact host

[Native source adapter](../../scripts/b05_anointed_native_guided_authority_v0.py) requires the exact L3 AH/AM/AL source SHA, existing matching native Signature adapter from [#168](b04-b05-high-anointed-restoration-native-returning-step-v0.md), [#169](b04-b05-medium-anointed-restoration-native-investment-step-v0.md), or [#170](b04-b05-low-anointed-restoration-native-embodied-step-v0.md), and a real G1 map, G3 spell engine and G4 injury state. It **shares** that native actor's finite two-Destiny source referee. No second Destiny pool and no G0 `Actor.destiny` field is invented.

At the caster's genuine ready own turn, `cast(recipient,mode=...)` pays **20/24/30 AP** for High/Medium/Low via the native G0 `projection` action and **2 VP** through an actual G0 `vp_transition`. In **Fixed** mode, the 2 VP are Spent, and the G3 host expires on its native existing `round+10` six-second round clock; in **Maintained** mode, 2 VP remain Committed to the G3 host until its legal release/end. Both create precisely one live **Guided Regeneration Primary** G3 instance with authentic action, VP, host-ID and caster SHA provenance; the original G3 Priest Self-Guided route is not replaced. A caster cannot use L3 Authority on a caller-provided `active_paid_guided_instance=True` claim or a stale released/expired host.

For High/Medium the Primary is **Single Target**, visible and on an unobstructed **0–3 square** current G1 magical path; High L2's +1 Signature range does not modify Guided. For Medium, that living target must *also* be the separately invested one-Vessel recipient, and its earlier **24-AP General Investment** remains mandatory. Low uses only **Self [Embodiment]** and must still be manifested. No extra action comes from L2 Channel Through Vessel or Living Instrument. The prototype accepts only visibly **unopposed**, positive-HP living recipients. Actual perception, changing consent, post-commit responses and refused **+8 Intrusion** require separate source evidence and are deliberately unresolved.

## Exactly one native Step increment

`declare_waiver()` selects the currently scheduled next recipient G0 Step; it pays **no Destiny** yet and cannot forge a Step. G4 at the authentic existing recipient **Regeneration Step** checks the actual G3 instance, its original AP/VP payments, recipient, Focus/Vessel/Embodiment and current path, explicit beneficial delivery and live HP-restoration blocks. One competing Guided host, a fully suppressed +1 Guided throughput, or an unresolved simultaneous native Returning Step + Guided Authority on the *same Step* fails closed rather than inventing priorities, stacking or discounts.

G4 validates the normal player-controlled HP/wound Repair allocation *first* against the **ordinary available natural throughput**, reserving **only the already-paid incremental Guided +1** for Authority. For this selected step, normal allocation is limited to base/other authorized throughput, and the natural HP/Repair debit still spends **its own beneficiary Vigor**. If the **living non-Dying** recipient has at least one missing HP **after that natural allocation**, the existing [canonical L3 source referee](../rules/08-archetypes/anointed-signature-authority-l1-3-v0.md) authorizes exactly **one more HP** with **0 beneficiary Vigor** and spends **1 Destiny**. G4 applies that one additional HP **after** its genuine `regeneration_step`, with a separate `g4_anointed_guided_waiver_applied` event containing the original host, action/VP receipt indices, source SHA, natural Step round, before/after HP/Vigor and Destiny state.

This is **not** free wound Repair Progress, cancellation of Dying, an extra Step, more than +1 effective Guided throughput, VP forgiveness, recipient Vigor replenishment, or a waiver of the ordinary player's chosen regeneration points. Even at **0 recipient Vigor**, an eligible paid and unsuppressed Guided slot can restore its one missing HP for one Destiny, while ordinary regeneration spends zero. If the normal choice would already fill Max HP, the requested waiver is invalid and no Destiny is deducted; the Step can be retried with a legal allocation or waiver canceled. Missing/invalid natural allocations, changed recipient eligibility and active HP blocks do not partially pay Destiny or restore HP. Native dead-recipient Step expiry and explicit pending-waiver cancellation are also bounded.
