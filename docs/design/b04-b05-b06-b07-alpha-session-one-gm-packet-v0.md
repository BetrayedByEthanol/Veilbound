## Player-facing opening (read aloud)

> The village's medicinal herbs have spoiled, leaving families worried and patients waiting for usable supplies. The last shipment's route is uncertain. A slick bridge or a ford leads to the supply cache; farther along the road, a Soldier watches the way to a keeper's public records. You can investigate, ask for help, seek another route or turn away. What do you do?

Do **not** reveal a predetermined culprit, imply a ring magically cures the village or announce that the Soldier must fight. Those claims are not established by the source and must arise from actual GM truth and player decisions.

## Scene 1 — Crossing (Supply, L1)

**Ask:** “Do you use the narrow bridge, survey the ford, scout something else or spend a known source technique? Which legal alternative are you declining?”

| Existing *authored* source example | Check, if its exact fictional premise applies | Time paid on real G4.2 exploration clock |
|---|---|---|
| Slick bridge | d20 + Agility + **actually learned Acrobatics**, target **15** | **90 seconds** |
| Ford survey | d20 + Awareness + **actually learned Navigation**, target **12** | **180 seconds** |
| Declared slower footpath after failed direct route | No automatic new check; actual GM-established alternative | **300 additional seconds** only in this authored example |
| Different player-invented route | GM establishes actual lawful Ability/Training/magic and risk before the roll | Actual adjudicated time, never assumed from examples |

A failed check does **not** create an automatic wound, compulsory encounter, arbitrary forced detour or invented magical knowledge. Record what was attempted, actual d20 and source ranks, declared time, true outcome and what other route remains.

## Scene 2 — Herb cache and ring (Supply, L1)

**Ask:** “Are you testing the herbs, seeking visible evidence, checking the ring, collecting a sample or asking another character for help? Which information are you actually seeking?”

| Source-eligible approach | Existing example and effect boundary |
|---|---|
| Intellect + purchased **Medicine** | Target **12**, **240 authored seconds** for a genuine ordinary spoiled-herb diagnosis; does not prove concealed Veil cause or identify the culprit |
| Awareness observation | **90 authored seconds** to search for visible stains; visible stains do not automatically establish cause |
| Eligible actually **held ring** and Universal Veil Inspection | Paid **Full Action + 2 VP**; visible **Life / Restoration** signature only, not ring function, causal connection or mass treatment |
| Scholar/High known source utility | Only actual L1 formula access, original AP/VP/range/hand costs and eligible target; an ordinary retrieved object or Phantom Sound cue cannot compel an NPC or diagnose spoilage |
| Other Focus actions | Require the exact selected character's source access and actual GM premise, never a technique borrowed from another Focus |

On failure or ambiguity, preserve truthful incomplete clues and offer lawful alternatives (sample collection, records, witnesses, detour). Record **what was learned, what remains uncertain and how resources/time changed**. Do not substitute a successful scripted sample for the real player choice.

## Scene 3 — Soldier road gate and public log (Gate, L1)

**Ask:** “The Soldier questions passage, while the neutral keeper controls the public log. Do you petition, present real medical evidence, cite a local precedent, seek another witness, withdraw or choose a different tactic?”

| Source-backed authored social example | Actual eligibility and example result |
|---|---|
| Presence petition to this **neutral keeper** | Target **12**, **60 seconds** in the sample; keeper's actual motives determine whether a successful appeal gives access |
| Intellect plus **actually learned regional/era History** | Target **12**, **120 seconds** with a genuinely established local public-record precedent |
| A medical-proof argument | The example permits **+2 situational** support only following a real successful diagnosis *and* GM-approved neutral NPC premise; no universal Medicine-to-social transfer |
| Failed petition, alternate route or another witness | Real delay, refusal, partial clue or unchosen consequence, as established by GM truth; never guaranteed access |

A refusal is not a compulsory fight. Keep access, information gained, risks, pursuit and follow-on clues distinct. A known Scholar/High Phantom Sound can communicate a permissible short cue, but cannot automatically distract the Soldier, compel a keeper or grant persuasion bonuses.

Native [Alpha L1 tactical choices](b04-b05-b06-alpha-l1-tactical-defensive-response-v0.md) demonstrate pre-roll **Take Hit, Guard and Dodge**, genuine Dodge Pressure/Guard commitments, authored paid Focus actions and an original G1/G5 exit at **specific source geometry**. The five machine policies do **not** supply a general-purpose hostile AI, arbitrary Interception/Opportunity, unprovided natural-20 critical confirmation, every target choice, every GM map or guaranteed victory.

**If a player chooses a legal-but-unimplemented tactical branch:** resolve it independently under canonical rules with a separate real source/GM ledger *or* record **SOURCE_BLOCKED** and halt that branch. Never change the player's declared choice into a scripted result. Record optional combat's actual start/end HP, Vigor, VP, AP, Guard, Dodge Pressure, rolls, wounds/Dying, movement, effect hosts and terminal/unfinished status. A scene-local withdrawal does not certify safety, recovery or an ended pursuit.

If no fight occurs, keep the worksheet's **gate_hostility** stage unobserved. This makes no claim of L1 combat Focus certification; a later genuinely adjudicated L1 scene may be needed for C3.

## Session-end handoff, noncombat consequences and milestone

At both supply and gate boundaries, preserve an actual **before/after state** for each of four PCs, VP Available/Committed/Spent, HP/Vigor, wounds, prepared hosts and equipment, **G4.2 world time and six-hour recovery deadline**, evidence gained, negative/alternative choices, and whether the Soldier/keeper remains a threat. Account separately for ordinary travel and any safely earned downtime; there is no free refill merely because Session 1 ended.

**GM's real Milestone One question:** Did the party genuinely establish a credible supply cause **or actionable route** and communicate its significance to the village? If the GM **actually** decides this is a significant group-story beat, record original declaration, achievement, scene/time and four separately reviewed real L2 destination sheets. It is **not** earned automatically by gate success, a Soldier hit, an Anointed personal Mission beat, elapsed hours or candidate Python output. If no award is declared, the party remains **L1**. Do not run an L2 Hunter with made-up progression.

A real Session 1 handoff consists of actual participant selection and reviewed sheet refs, scene choices and *declined* legal alternatives, GM decisions, resource and time receipts, what the village and gate genuinely learned, any source-blocked actions and whether GM awarded a level. Leave unanswered questions unresolved rather than fabricating a positive outcome.
