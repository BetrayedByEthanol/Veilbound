## Player-facing premise: The Village's Withering Supplies

A settlement's medicinal herb shipments are spoiled, leaving residents afraid and several patients in need of **ordinary help and source-legal treatment**, not an assumed mass healing spell. The supply-cache route is watched by an ordinary Soldier. An overlooked route passes near a territorial Hunter's forest. A Firebound Sentry guards a shrine that may hold credible records or evidence about the dangerous supply source. The characters must establish what actually happened, find a workable remedy and ensure the village can safely obtain future supplies.

**GM-truth sheet (fill before session one):** Decide whether a mundane spoilage explanation is sufficient, whether the observable Life/Restoration pattern on an actually held ring is related or coincidental, who is responsible for the shipment and what non-magical or source-legal repair is possible. The existing [three-scene supply-cache sample](b05-b06-integrated-noncombat-adventure-scenes-consequences-v0.md) **does not** prove a magical cause or identify the ring's function; its default check results are preauthored mock fixtures. Choose a truthful, discoverable causal chain *before* asking for checks. Do **not** make real players' unsuccessful checks secretly succeed, or require a guessed unsupported miracle to complete the quest.

## GM setup and table contract

1. Choose **3, 4 or 5 sessions**. The [session planner](../../scripts/b04_b05_b06_b07_integrated_pilot_adventure_v0.py) groups the **same five chronological story beats** without advancing anyone automatically. Prepare a level-one complete sheet for each participating PC and record initial **HP, Vigor, VP Available/Committed/Spent, wounds, weapon/ammunition, source path/SHA, known/prepared/linked/bonded access, Talent and Training saved/selected ledger**. Low Priest A/B physical G0 proxies are **not complete magical character sheets**.
2. Ask actual players what they want to do and what alternative they consider. Record **legally available actions, any off-turn response window, paid AP/MP/VP, rolls, actual effect and immediate consequence**. Technical skill, social influence, exploratory observation and Universal Veil Inspection are governed by existing [ordinary noncombat](b04-b05-source-legal-noncombat-scene-resolution-v0.md) and [technique](b04-b05-focus-noncombat-technique-integration-v0.md) rules. Nothing gives universal Investigation/Persuasion bonuses or auto-casts another Focus's utility spell.
3. Set scene geometry, arrival time, warning and escape choices. **No fight is compulsory**: Soldier, Sentry and Hunter can produce meaningful dialogue, avoidance, noncombat consequences or genuine combat. For hostile L2/L3 encounters use the [G8/G7H/G7F source-native pack](b06-representative-soldier-sentry-hunter-gm-encounter-packs-v0.md) as a verified **reference**, *not* a replay whose fixed A/B/R/F party snapshots magically replace the real group's HP/VP/positions. Record decisions with a real source-legal resolver and independent session notes.
4. **At session and scene boundaries** distinguish source rule effects from GM fiction. Carry forward **actual wounds, Dying, Vigor, VP, ammunition, ongoing protections, toxin/venom and G4.2 time**, with a source/event reference for each transition. A border exit does **not** settle an active Hunter pursuit; a six-hour recovery must be legitimately earned while actually safe. No arbitrary inter-session free refill.
5. Obtain actual GM **Milestone One** and **Milestone Two** declarations at significant group-story events, if and when genuinely fulfilled. Then record the source-legal L1→L2 and L2→L3 character sheet changes and resource rules; **never** award from script completion, a predetermined session index, a combat win, an Anointed personal Mission or elapsed downtime.

## Five chronological story beats — concrete runnable choices

### A. Spoiled supply: search and diagnosis (**target L1**)

**Entrance:** A report of contaminated herbs, the old bridge/ford, a loose supply sample and an observable mark on a ring. GM establishes which items are **actually held or unattended**. The [merged #188 source fixture](b05-b06-integrated-noncombat-adventure-scenes-consequences-v0.md) supports **Agility/Acrobatics footbridge** versus **Awareness/Navigation ford survey**, **Intellect/Medicine spoilage diagnosis**, and an **actually held** Universal Veil Inspection for visible Pillar/Aspect only. Source-authorized High Priest Woodland Rapport and Scholar utility may be considered where the character genuinely knows/prepares them. Other Focuses do **not** acquire their known spells by narrative convenience.

**Failure that changes the route:** The sample GM fixture can add **300 actual exploration seconds** on failed direct crossing, or leave the spoilage cause uncertain, without auto-damage or auto-guessing. The GM may instead introduce a declared alternative clue with its own real costs. The ring's visible Life/Restoration pattern **never automatically reveals the contamination cause or an exact cure**.

**Required recorded decision:** Did the player choose to spend AP/VP on utility, rely on an untrained/trained check or preserve resources? What changed in the actual world/time? Which legal alternative was declined?

### B. Road gate and missing supply log (**target L1**)

**Entrance:** One actual **B06-EN-02** Ordinary Soldier questions the party; a neutral storekeeper controls the public supply log. A fact-based **regional/era History** argument, **Presence petition** or documented medical diagnosis is an authored candidate; the specific GM fixture in #188 allows a **+2 situational bonus only with an actual successful diagnosis**. This is not a universal Medicine→social conversion.

**Consequences:** Negotiation may grant access, require a new witness or end in denial and delay. Players can seek another route or withdraw. **If the Soldier turns hostile at L1, STOP the G8 replay**: the [G8 Soldier combat fixture](b06-representative-soldier-sentry-hunter-gm-encounter-packs-v0.md) only executes L2/L3 A/B/R/F. The GM must resolve any L1 opposition via *separately verified real L1 source-legal actions*, or defer combat and record this outstanding C3/Resolve coverage. No down-leveled G8 statistics, forced Soldier surrender or unsourced “tutorial battle” is allowed.

**Candidate Milestone One — The Supply Truth:** When the group has **actually established a credible cause or actionable alternative route and communicated its significance to the village**, the GM may explicitly recognize this as a meaningful group-story turning point. If not, it remains **unawarded**. The printed itinerary displays a **possible award window after B**, never a level grant. Each player must have time to demonstrate some genuine L1 combat and noncombat role choice for eventual C3/C4; this may require a separately refereed L1 encounter.

### C. Hunter's forest corridor (**target L2, only after actual Milestone One**)

**Entrance:** Tracks, damaged trees and disturbed prey announce a **1×2** Hunter. Players can avoid its territory, use source-legal scouting, retreat or choose combat. Existing [G7-F/G7-H GM reference](b06-representative-soldier-sentry-hunter-gm-encounter-packs-v0.md) permits **L2 P3/P4 forest ordinary versus real Pounce**, and **L2 P4 source-native win, lethal loss and genuine opposed withdrawal**. It does **not** authorize a universal surprise action, hallucinated extra Bite, blanket armor-piercing Horn or guaranteed avoidance.

**Failure-forward:** Genuine contact may injure, envenomate or cause Dying. A legal border exit is **scene-local**: if a living Hunter might pursue, the GM must decide safety and response to an actual danger before advancing a long exploration clock. A successful avoid route still offers substantive evidence of player decisions and time spent, but must **not** be mislabeled as a native terminal victory.

**Required choices:** Who frontlines? Are Guards, DODGE pressure, reactions, helpful casting, prepared spells and paid withdrawal actually legal? Record native action/roll/wound/VP/venom events, or explicit independent GM/source references if resolved manually.

### D. Shrine passage and the Sentry (**target L2, only after actual Milestone One**)

**Entrance:** Source **B06-EN-01** Firebound Sentry, potentially **cold** or **prepared**. The GM must first decide whether it had the actual opportunity to prepare: cold Reserve costs genuine **30 AP and 3 Committed VP**, while prepared starts with the source's **3 committed**. Existing G8 reference supports **L2 P4** three native terminal policies for each start state in an open 8×7 source lane; it **does not** prove barred doors, extra magic wards, frightened civilians or custom cover geometry. Parley/avoidance is also lawful when supported by GM adjudication.

**Failure-forward:** If they cannot pass, the party may withdraw, seek a separate entrance or use documented supply evidence to request admission. A prepared/remaining Sentry may still be a threat; retreat does not magically release its source commitments or heal its foes.

**Candidate Milestone Two — The Way Forward:** The GM may recognize a genuinely meaningful **group achievement securing a path to resolve the supply crisis**, with real cost and a validated return route. It need not involve killing the Sentry. Until an **explicit new, distinct GM-declared event** is recorded and the L2→3 sheet independently reviewed, the adventurers remain at L2. This is **not** an Anointed Mission Fulfill or automatic Destiny award.

### E. Return and village resolution (**target L3, only after actual Milestone Two**)

**Entrance:** The party returns with whatever medical, documentary and magical knowledge it **actually** obtained. Villagers are affected by the supply crisis; a workable ordinary replacement supply, safe decontamination or valid individually paid restoration can matter. **No technique grants a mass cure by implication**. The GM can use original L3 Soldier/Sentry/Hunter source-native reference encounters **only if the new real scene has legally matching source start state and actors**; otherwise resolve actual play independently and retain the fixed reference as a benchmark.

**Meaningful endings:** Record whether the supply route reopened, patients benefited from specific legal action, an offender/guardian remains a threat, recovery was safely earned, and which alternatives remain. An unresolved outcome can be the true ending; do **not** force a perfect victory or hide failures to improve balance. Anointed Mission remains an ongoing journey; a GM-recognized personal beat is a **separate** decision from either group level milestone.

**Observed level drift:** Re-evaluate Focus identity at L3 with real paid combat/noncombat choices and a weakness/countercase rather than assuming the original structural v0 build establishes fairness.

## Approved session compression: same beats, not different levels

| Sessions | Session allocation and target levels | **Candidate** GM award windows (only if earned and actually declared) |
|---|---|---|
| **3** | S1: **A+B, L1**; S2: **C+D, L2**; S3: **E, L3** | After S1/B propose L2; after S2/D propose L3 |
| **4 (default)** | S1: **A+B, L1**; S2: **C, L2**; S3: **D, L2**; S4: **E, L3** | After S1/B propose L2; after S3/D propose L3 |
| **5** | S1: **A, L1**; S2: **B, L1**; S3: **C, L2**; S4: **D, L2**; S5: **E, L3** | After S2/B propose L2; after S4/D propose L3 |

This is **session planning**, not a universal “level up at the end of session 1” rule. If the GM delays or denies a milestone, **do not run higher-level reference encounters against lower-level source actors**. Reschedule the subsequent segment, choose an actually legal encounter or log a blocked candidate and revised session count. Two distinct *actually* GM-declared milestones remain a hard human-pilot acceptance requirement, including when the story branches away from the example beat.

## Twelve-Focus participation without inventing a 12-PC party

The **same 3–5-session narrative arc** supports multiple player parties, rotating cohorts or follow-up runs so all **12 Priest/Scholar/Gifted/Anointed × High/Medium/Low** pairings are observed across L1–3. The script binds the real independently audited source fingerprints for all 12 and opens exactly **36 pairing × level candidate review slots**. The existing G7/G8 benchmark party **A/B/R/F** is **not** a substitute for a Gifted or Anointed real player or a complete Low Priest magical sheet.

At **each level**, collect: (1) legal full sheet/resources, (2) at least one genuine combat choice and alternative, (3) a genuine noncombat choice and alternative, (4) adverse/weakness countercase, (5) independent reviewer/GM session reference, and (6) level-correct source/action evidence. Pairing-specific requirements are in [C1–C7 source certification protocol](b04-b07-independent-twelve-focus-certification-protocol-pilot-roster-v0.md). The pilot adventure **cannot** self-certify 36 source slots, and a single party with four benchmark roles does not cover the 12-Focus requirement. **L1 combat is a particular outstanding gap** in this integrated fixture set and must be observed/refereed with original level-one source builds.

## Evidence intake: mandatory real handoff, but not external authentication

The [pilot organiser and intake preflight](../../scripts/b04_b05_b06_b07_integrated_pilot_adventure_v0.py) generates a fixed authored schedule and entirely blank actual-play form. Record **session ID/date, GM observation, player declarations/alternatives, each beat's outcome, GM consequences, original G0/G4.2 event references, source initial/final resource states, prior-session handoff and healing/recovery/pursuit decisions**. Across levels, carry the exact original source fingerprint and review actual C1–C7 choices. Any L2/L3 reported session must cite a **prior distinct GM-declared milestone** and sheet review; an undeclared milestone cannot hold an event ID. Duplicate IDs, omitted prior sessions, forged source hashes and invented certification flags are rejected.

A pass of intake `preflight` proves **only schema and syntactic chronology**, not that a person/GM/record exists, a roll was legal, the source resources match, the milestone really happened or an independent reviewer approved the campaign. External review of original play records is still required. It always returns **0 externally authenticated sessions, 0 independently verified milestones, 0/12 full Focuses and 0/6 exits**, even with all fields populated. Never paste fabricated names or invented human logs into the repository as if a pilot occurred.

**GM handoff decisions to make before an actual game:** player party/cohorts and reviewer assignment; actual village cause and whether it has multiple solutions; L1 source-legal combat coverage; map entry and safe withdrawal; Sentry preparedness; Hunter hostility/pursuit/safe downtime; how to respond if meaningful group milestones are **not** completed at the proposed points; and which original source action/technique is used at an actual climax. These are operational/adventure choices, **not open approvals for already-decided Rooted Guard +1, Unbound Step −1 MP, eight-Pillar menu or milestone method**.

## Explicit acceptance and remaining gates

This PR supplies **a complete authored GM story arc, 3/4/5 possible session arrangements, source-anchored choices and a conservative evidence intake protocol**, **not** observed representative real sessions, validated source-legal four-PC encounter chaining, a final calibrated Hunter CR, verified two level gains, accepted source-purchased Low Priest character records or independent review. No Soldier/Sentry/Hunter fixed-policy snapshot is silently copied into the next encounter as the real party's current HP/VP/wounds. A legal G4.2 six-hour recovery requires a **real safe interval**, and a source exit does not prove a living Hunter stopped pursuing.

Still required: actual party choices and GM play, full C1–C7 source review for all 12 pairings/36 level slots, credible L1 combat, genuinely continuous multi-encounter resource/terrain/response evidence (including support/protection and Hunter withdrawal), real GM awards, independent danger/attrition/pacing measurement and explicit approval of the **Create, Act, Resolve, Advance, Challenge, Observe** gates. The current *source-baseline* remains **12/12 structural, 0/12 independently fully-play-certified, 0/6 accepted exits, 3/7 original blocker definitions closed**. B06 G0–G8 unchanged; no G9 or quantitative CR.
