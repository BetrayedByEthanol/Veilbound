## Decision D4 — MVP level gains: MILESTONES

For this **pilot only**, characters begin **L1** and gain **L2**, then **L3**, after **two separately recorded, GM-declared adventure milestones**, not a computed XP threshold. Do not invent that the first/second session, a win, a rest, a personal Anointed Mission event or six-hour Destiny recovery automatically awards a level. The authored adventure must specify the two actual group-story beats and record GM recognition in session evidence; their narrative content and precise pacing are separate implementation tasks.

Each award is a **single level step**; reusing an event, skipping L2, or granting extra Talent, Training, VP, new techniques or a free resource refill beyond current authored progression is not approved. Independently audit the legal L1→L2→L3 sheet for each of the twelve Focus pairings, preserving their distinct resource and Talent schedules. This does **not** define global XP/milestone advancement for other campaigns or L4–20.

**Watch LV-01:** Measure time spent using L1/2/3 mechanics in the actual 3–5-session pilot, player comprehension of earned features, meaningful role drift, too-early/late advancement, and whether two milestone occasions disrupt gameplay pacing. Revisit location of milestones on real evidence; the pilot policy is provisional.
