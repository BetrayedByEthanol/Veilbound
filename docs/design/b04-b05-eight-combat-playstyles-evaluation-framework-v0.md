# B04/B05 — Eight Combat Playstyles: Build Identity and Evaluation Framework v0

> **Status: design and testing protocol, not canonical game rules.** The eight original combat playstyles are role goals, **not classes or Archetype/Focus locks**. This document creates no spells, techniques, combat permissions, new AP/VP costs, enemy abilities, build certificates, or additional MVP gates. [Canonical rules](../rules/), structured content and executable source cases take precedence.

## Purpose and evidence policy

A Veilbound build has a **primary** combat playstyle and may demonstrate a **secondary** one. Assign roles from genuine equipment, techniques, Training, Talents, actions and resources, not from names or theoretical future features. The L1–3 pilot establishes a usable role baseline; it does **not** demand that mature L4–20 identities or power curves be settled. A role can be stronger at some levels or in some encounters if it has meaningful drawbacks and other roles receive real opportunities to excel.

Distinguish **authored intent**, **legal structural build**, **source-native bounded action evidence**, **role scenario tested**, and **independent complete combat-and-noncombat Focus certification**. None implies the next. Fixed authored scenarios cannot establish sampled win rates, player preference or statistically measured general balance.

## Original eight playstyles

| Playstyle | Combat goal and expected strength | Designed vulnerability / tradeoff | Typical Veil Point pattern (hypothesis) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fighter** | Sustained weapon pressure, frontline endurance, reliable contact | Needs access to foes; weaker direct control when contact is denied | Low setup; low burst; moderate–high sustained if magic is used |
| **Defender** | Hold space, protect allies, intercept or deny threats | Lower damage; position, threatened lanes and response availability matter | Low–moderate setup; low burst; low–moderate sustained |
| **Spellblade** | Weapon/magic synergy, switching offense/defense, meaningful strike bursts | Setup, held weapon/channel continuity and competing AP/VP | High initial commitment; low burst VP after setup; low–moderate sustained |
| **Assassin** | Gain an opening and rapidly eliminate or disrupt one priority target | Inconsistent without an opening; risky when exposed or fights drag on | Low preparation where positioning permits; very high episodic burst; low sustained |
| **Archer** | Precision/ranged interference, controlled lanes and priority targeting | Cover/screens, ammunition and dangerous close contact | Moderate setup; low burst; moderate sustained |
| **Combat Caster** | Direct aggressive magical pressure and close/mobile offense | Resource-heavy, vulnerable to interruption and counterpressure | Low setup; low–moderate burst; very high sustained |
| **Battlemage** | Area and terrain control, multiple-target threats and field manipulation | Area restrictions, setup, expensive effects; inefficient if isolated targets | Moderate setup; moderate–high burst; moderate–high sustained |
| **Healer / Support** | Sustain party function through recovery, prevention and enhancement | Limited direct pressure; depends on allied opportunities, line of effect and timing | Moderate setup; low burst; highly variable sustained |

These qualitative patterns are **design expectations, not rules for VP prices**. A strong Fighter may spend little VP; an Archer may be a high-damage specialist with authorized techniques. Do not conflate **Combat Caster** (direct magical pressure) with **Battlemage** (control), **Defender** (threat management) with **Healer / Support** (restoration), or **Spellblade** with a character merely carrying a sword and knowing magic.

## Source-backed build role card (mandatory before a role claim)

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Identity | Original source character/digest, Archetype × Focus, Pillar/Aspect, current level, primary/secondary role |
| Legal build | All nine Abilities, Training/Talents, weapon/hand/gear, known/prepared repertoire, Used Tier, AP/Physical/Projected Tempo, Guard, Max/Available/Spent/Committed VP |
| Positive role test | One **actually usable at this level** paid/source-authorized action that advances the role objective and has a meaningful effect |
| Countercase | One real unfavorable encounter in which the role has a cost, limitation or risky alternative |
| Resource rhythm | Setup and maintained reservations, burst and recurring Spent VP, AP/MP/Reaction conflicts, depletion/actual Recovery and lawful hand transitions |
| Progression | L1, L2, L3 source decisions/affordability and role effects **separately**; L4+ speculation labeled outside strict MVP |
| Evidence boundary | Authored vs structural vs bounded native vs full role-scenario vs independent Focus-and-noncombat acceptance |

A Fighter label does not create an attack profile; Defender does not create a taunt or free Intervene; Battlemage does not create an area technique; and a Low Priest is not granted unauthorized enemy control or external Chosen Rites by this document.

## Eight future role-validation scenarios

Every case uses **genuine** source roster, legal geometry, paid G0/G1/G2/G3/G4/G4.5/G5 as relevant, one authentic natural Step 2 per real eligible turn, AP/VP/MP/Reaction accounting, correct equipment/target authority, truthful terminal limits and real wound/HP/Vigor transitions. Run a **same-source, level, enemy, pressure, tactics and exposure** no-role-action baseline. No invented damage after misses, no post-incapacitation turns, no fabricated threat switching, no assumed immunity, and no falsely safe scene exit. If a required ability has no executable profile, report **BLOCKED** (content/engine gap), not poor role balance.

| ID | Role | Positive source test | Counterpressure and discriminating objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PS-01** | Fighter | Paid multi-turn physical offense, maintaining effective contact and survival | A mobile foe denies reach; account for movement and guard opportunities |
| **PS-02** | Defender | Actual threat prevented, intercepted or delayed to preserve an ally's meaningful action | Flanking/alternate target, lost contact or simultaneous eligible Reactions |
| **PS-03** | Spellblade | Paid wielded-weapon + Veil effect produces real attack/control choice | Setup disruption, stow/drop/disarm channel end or AP/VP conflict |
| **PS-04** | Assassin | Earned opening creates genuine priority-target impact | No opening or prolonged response to exposure |
| **PS-05** | Archer | Legal aim/shot, ammunition, Cover and living-screen response alters target pressure | Restricted lane, adjacent threat and ammunition expenditure |
| **PS-06** | Combat Caster | Several actually payable direct magical pressure actions | Sustained VP depletion, interruption and unsafe close range |
| **PS-07** | Battlemage | An **authored** area/multi-target/terrain effect changes enemy decisions or access | Single target, restricted area, friendlies in effect or unsupported profile |
| **PS-08** | Healer / Support | Actual ally HP/Vigor restoration or prevention at correct time and paid price | Two recipients under pressure, depleted VP, blocked delivery or lost turn |

**Required output:** genuine attempts/hits/misses and effects, realized round count, HP/Vigor/wounds, ally actions saved, prevented enemy opportunities (with actual evidence), AP/MP/VP and Reaction expenditure, finite budget/refunds, per-role objective outcomes, full ledger/source digest, censored/blocked status and exposure-matched baseline deltas. Multi-foe/choice variation and observed tabletop/GM policy should be later evidence, not secretly assumed. Never equate raw healed HP, prevented Vigor, inflicted damage and an enemy action denied as interchangeable points.

## Fairness, level drift and provisional balance tolerances

- **Identity first:** Is the intended primary role **usable at L1, L2 and L3**, with at least one source-legal meaningful choice and a genuine countercase at each? A weak or unaffordable spell profile is first a B05 access/content problem, not necessarily a numerical imbalance.
- **Comparability:** Analyze appropriate common objectives and similar exposure, party/foe context and budget **within each level**. Do not rank Focuses by a universal weighted sum; do not insist that all Focuses match healing, DPS or armor.
- **Tolerance, not rule:** A roughly **15–20%** difference in an explicitly agreed **comparable mixed-objective outcome** can be a practical *investigation band*. A **25–40%** situational specialist lead can be acceptable with credible countertradeoffs; a **persistently greater than 25%** comparable-objective disadvantage deserves review. These are **unvalidated proposals**, not automatic pass/fail thresholds and not applicable to raw damage/healing or fixed no-probability witnesses.
- **No dead level:** L1–3 focuses on baseline playability, not mature peak. Record level-specific spikes, temporary relative weakness, VP growth, tempo changes and feature unlocks; do not average away a nonviable L1 using L3 results. Later L4–20 powers do not certify the MVP.
- **Before tuning:** Diagnose missing mechanics/content, legality, scenario construction, role-context, expected weakness and only then reproducible systematic numerical imbalance. Require source-legal before/after comparisons and a separately approved rule change.

Future-only hypotheses: Medium Priest second Consecration L4/free Reassign L5, Low Priest second Rite L4, High Priest faster Open Invocation L5. These canonical feature directions do not supply usable L1–3 budget or certify projected power.

## Priest Life L1–3 initial roles — hypotheses, not role certificates

| Source build | Proposed playstyle | Verified mechanics and intended strength | Exposed limitation / missing proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High Priest Life H** | **Healer / Support**, secondary defensive support | Prepared 20-AP Basic Heal/Guided/Shelter; L3 two distinct maintained ally effects, paid Full-Action Open Invocation | Party/target dependent, consumes VP; verify changing priorities, unfavorable foe pressure and actual self-defense |
| **Medium Priest Life M** | **Healer / Support**, secondary tactical Defender | 24-AP Consecration/reassignment and L3 paid 2-Spent-VP + Reaction Intercession, actual beneficiary effects | Only one host through L3; opportunity cost of Reaction and alternative initiative/targets needs study |
| **Low Priest Life A (Guided)** | **Fighter**, secondary Defender | Self Chosen Guided, real regeneration, L3 Held Renewal; legal ally Basic Heal when hand/VP permits | Needs paid risky physical engagement to pressure foes; L1 cannot reserve self Rite and pay 2-Spent Heal together |
| **Low Priest Life B (Shelter)** | **Defender**, secondary Fighter | Self Chosen Shelter, finite prevention, L3 Vital Interposition | Mace + shield use both hands; no free-hand external Basic Heal verified; genuine ally-protection/Intervene needs scenario proof |

These role assignments are **proposed evaluation targets** based on the real source builds, not claims of measured Fighter, Defender or combat-caster proficiency. The current [formal Priest Life assessment](b04-b05-formal-priest-life-focus-balance-assessment-v0.md) covers **64 two-round self + 912 extended + 408 multi-recipient = 1,384 primary deterministic cases**, excluding 36 distinct historical High/Medium ally cases from the primary count. It finds no mandatory numerical change, recommends **retain existing AP/VP** for controlled testing, and explicitly sets **global balance certification false**.

Low B's narrow external healing is a role/equipment limitation, not proof of a failed Defender. High's stronger ally healing is intended support specialization, not proof of overall dominance. Medium's Intercession opportunity cost is open. Actual objective outcomes, varied enemy actions, noncombat decisions and role counters remain necessary.

## Adoption and MVP boundary

B04 attaches a role card to each lawful example character; B05 validates affordable actions and missing technique profiles; B06 exercises PS-01–PS-08 as native cases *where mechanics exist*; B07 checks the same role at every L1–3 level and records drift. Full MVP requires independent noncombat roles, twelve complete Archetype × Focus certificates, varied encounter types, a 3–5-session playable adventure and representative player/GM evidence.

**This document changes no B01–B07 blocker status, no six MVP exit-gate checkbox, no Hunter CR, no rule numbers and no historic encounter attestation.**
