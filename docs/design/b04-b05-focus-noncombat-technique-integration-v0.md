## Source and action integrity

The renderer uses the existing [source-backed character adapter](../../scripts/b04_b05_noncombat_scene_resolution_v0.py) and [#184 independent roster](../../scripts/b04_b07_twelve_focus_certification_protocol_v0.py); it preserves original G0 `party_actor` and actual High/Medium Gifted constructors rather than inventing new role IDs. All actors retain their authentic source SHA-256 and fixed L1–3 Abilities, learned disciplines and actual AP/VP. The example's action cost is committed through `Encounter.spend_ap`; VP is moved Available→Spent through `Encounter.spend_vp`, so native conservation and event-ledger validity apply. One Full Action consumes all original available Turn AP, including the L1–3 **30/36/42 AP** allotments. Other Projection actions pay High 20 or Medium 24. Invalid access, insufficiency, tier/eligibility, hand/held item, blocked sight, unacceptable animal state, excessive mass or an attended object fail preflight *before* consuming source AP/VP.

An explicitly modeled **post-commit interrupted** scenario retains AP cost but spends **0 VP** and grants no effect/information. This is a bounded revalidation branch—not a simulation of every native interception, spontaneous response or ongoing G3 maintenance. Source-legal **Medium Priest L3 TAL-M03 Frugal Technique** may lower a once-per-own-turn 2-VP Inspection to **1 VP**; this fixture allows only one action per turn and rejects other actors' invented discounts. Some techniques or targets are too complex to support in this bounded example: no full object-attendance Imposition contest, exceptional-object protection, collision, animal-response AI, sustained effect expiry engine, or ordinary passing of 10 minutes was created.

**Target claims are authored mock GM fixture predicates**, not independently established physical-world truth: an author-provided physically held portable ring, clear initial line, ordinary unopposed animal, or loose unprotected small object. The native G0 ledger confirms that action points and VP paid for a **legal fixture premise**; it does not confirm that a human GM observed the item being acquired, that a live animal remained receptive, or that a real NPC noticed the noise. Effects in this v0 are *instant bounded outcomes / initial properties*, not reusable world-state objects. No real G4.2 expiration, repeated-cast persistence, multi-caster stacking or cross-scene movement continuation is claimed.

## Different purposes and meaningful negative cases

- **Investigation:** all Focuses may spend a full action and 2 VP to identify only patterns on a *held* ring; compared with the mundane [#186 core-check scenes](b04-b05-source-legal-noncombat-scene-resolution-v0.md), this provides **automatic observation of source-visible magic**, while a legitimate interpretation check receives a precisely bounded +2. No held ring, contact loss after commitment or an unobservable pattern yields a different cost/outcome without invented knowledge. Ordinary Intellect/Medicine/History do not secretly reveal suppressed magic.
- **Exploration:** High Priest may prepare Woodland Rapport as Life/Nature utility, separate from routine route surveys and technical checks; a 12 kg fox may perceive modest peaceful cues, but **the animal's actual behavioral choice is reserved for a human GM**. High/Medium Scholars can use eligible Minor Telekinesis to move one loose item across a clear short route, not open an attended locked door by fiat.
- **Social/communication:** High Scholar's nonverbal Phantom Sound is an *external noise*, not a Persuasion bonus or automatically successful stealth distraction. Listening NPC actions and the interpretation of a coded cue remain contextual GM decisions. Anointed's Mission and Destiny effects are not automatic alternatives here; the existing native G4.2 evidence still has distinct external GM event requirements.
- **Rejected source access:** Scholar/Medium L1 has not learned Minor Telekinesis yet; Low Scholar's known repertoire lacks it. Medium/Low Priest, Gifted and Anointed cannot invoke Woodland Rapport just because a Life or Emotion bond exists. High Scholar cannot cast Woodland Rapport. The unimplemented extra Aspects and other unprepared techniques remain watches/conditional work, not new fixed MVP quotas.
