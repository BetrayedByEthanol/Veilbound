## Canonical source and one birth identity

[Character creation](../rules/02-character-creation/character-creation-foundation.md), [Training](../rules/02-character-creation/abilities-and-training.md), [Focus tempo](../rules/05-combat/action-categories-and-physical-tempo.md), [Anointed](../rules/08-archetypes/anointed.md), [canonical L1–3 Signature/Authority](../rules/08-archetypes/anointed-signature-authority-l1-3-v0.md), [Talent schedule](../rules/10-advancement/talents-framework-v0.md), [Talent catalogue](../rules/10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md), [equipment](../rules/11-equipment/economy-and-starter-equipment-v0.md), [Basic Heal](../rules/07-veil-magic/basic-heal-v0.md), and [Guided Regeneration](../rules/07-veil-magic/guided-regeneration-v0.md) provide the mechanical authority. Historical [Restoration Birth/Mission design #160](b04-b05-anointed-restoration-birth-mission-signature-baseline-v0.md) supplies the chosen narrative identity.

**The Returning Steps:** generations of successful rehabilitation practice adopted across settlements culminated in an exceptional collective Volition surge. This Anointed was born during it, not tasked by a deity. Their one bond is **Life / Restoration**; Birth Imprint **The Returning Step**; lifelong Mission **“Make recovery possible where injury or illness has taken that possibility away.”** Starting Mission state **Aligned**, universal Resonance **0**. The Mission never completes; significant events may qualify for capped **+1 Destiny** at GM discretion, and lasting deliberate rejection makes the bond Dormant. Aspect Intuition notices genuinely Restoration-relevant patterns, but supplies no automatic diagnosis or hidden knowledge.

The [Conflagration pilot](../rules/08-archetypes/anointed-signature-authority-l1-3-v0.md#kindled-flame--fire--conflagration) is an **alternative single-bond character**, not a second catalogue, second Signature, or additional Authority for this sheet.

**Destiny is not refilled by levelling:** the two initial points are granted when creating the L1 character; L2/L3 use the actual carried Destiny account, with only canonical six-hour or GM Mission Event recovery.

**Returning Step:** 20 AP, 0 VP, one living positive-HP patient, 0–3 squares L1 or 0–4 L2+, visibility/path and beneficial Intrusion. Up to **2 missing HP** are recovered at **1 recipient Vigor per 1 actual HP**. There is no new Regeneration Step, Dying rescue or wound repair. **Spare the Reserve** costs 1 Destiny on one eligible attempt to waive that recipient Vigor; a resisted legitimate attempt spends Destiny with 0 HP restored. A full-HP patient, blocked path, missing AP, or pre-application invalidity cannot be exploited for a free/charged effect. Two 20-AP projections fit the **42 AP** at L3, but do not gain a third effect.

**L3 Manifest Authority:** one 1-Destiny Lesser enhancement can apply to **one real +1 incremental HP** from an already legitimately active Guided Regeneration **`Single Target` Primary** during a genuine recipient Regeneration Step, with the +1 not fully suppressed and actual missing HP after ordinary allocation. It waives exactly that extra HP's recipient Vigor, not Guided's **2 VP cost**, not the normal Step Vigor, and not a second Step. **Basic Heal cannot carry Lesser Authority**, despite sharing Restoration; it separately restores HP and Vigor at normal VP/AP expense. Other grades, T3, or High L5 Sovereign Projection are not available.

## Bounded checks, actions and negative cases

The executable [sheet](../../scripts/b05_high_anointed_restoration_l1_l3_v0.py) and [regressions](../../tests/test_b05_high_anointed_restoration_l1_l3_v0.py) verify L1–3 point budget, independent Training, gear, one High Talent, AP/VP/Tempo, tier/universal unlocks, lawful Signature range progression, 1:1 Vigor cost, finite Lesser expenditure, resisted/illegal/dormant cases, GM event anti-farming, and L3 incremental Guided eligibility. The deterministic **example** uses a hypothetical damaged patient (5/12 HP, 6 Vigor): 2 HP restored for 2 recipient Vigor and 20 AP; a separate Lesser example restores 2 HP with 0 Vigor and 1 Destiny; this is **not** a native scene outcome. The L3 Guided example requires caller-provided "genuine Step / unsuppressed" flags: **no G4 attestation is produced**.

**Noncombat choices:** investigate a failed rehabilitation process with Medicine/Pillar Lore/History and one bounded Imprint Recall reroll, or project Returning Step when a patient has real missing HP/Vigor; a zero-Vigor patient may instead receive paid Basic Heal to restore Vigor, or a finite-Destiny Signature benefit. The Talent never guarantees hidden information. **Combat choices:** spend 20 AP on modest Signature healing versus 20 AP/2 VP on Basic Heal's HP-and-Vigor utility, invest 20 AP/2 Committed VP on Guided for later genuine Steps, or defend/move/retreat. None is represented as an observed win rate or player choice frequency.
