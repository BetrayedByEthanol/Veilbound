Other authored ordinary linked-Aspect techniques remain available when their matching emotion is Active, but they do not gain Amplification just by possessing `[Projection]`.

**Character concept:** a travelling community performer and medic accustomed to holding emotions in check and throwing bright, contained flames when joyful. The eight links are **fixed at creation** and persist through all level-ups. The two Plausible links are justified at creation: **Joy → Fire/Conflagration** (communal hearth-fire, lanterns and celebratory flame), and **Anger → Mind/Psionics** (forceful will to move a resisting obstacle). Neither receives a penalty, and neither permits cross-Aspect spell use.

| Active emotion | Pillar / Aspect | Compatibility | Authored ordinary linked-Aspect access in the restricted roster |

| Surprise | Secrets / Hallucination | **S** | Phantom Sound; no forced distraction/command |
| Disgust | Liberty / Defiance | **S** | Weaken Restraints; Full Action, only a mundane eligible restraint |
| Trust | Kinship / Guardian | **S** | Protective Ward; separate legal pre-roll **Reaction**, VP, and ally-only target |

One **Active Emotion** establishes one eligible catalogue during its Emotion Window. The sheet deliberately leaves current Active Emotion/Intensity **unset** until the fictional start-of-turn procedure. A Universal Veil procedure does not establish a link, Heighten, Amplify, Surge or consume an emotional slot. Two or more catalogues cannot be activated simultaneously by simply listing eight links.

## Verified choices and hard restrictions

| Situation | Required lawful result / source boundary |
|---|---|
| **Evoked Joy / T1 Throw / Surge** | Spend **20 AP + 3 VP** (2 ordinary + `ceil(2/4)=1` Surge); select **one** authored Potency, Reach or Precision; never a free Heightened Step. Minimum range still **1 square**, ordinary max **3** or **4 with Reach** |
| **Naturally Heightened Joy** | One safe Step *if genuinely supported by the fiction*. Safe Reach + purchased Surge Precision costs **20 AP + 3 VP**, has original `d20+4` plus **1** Precision and max **4 squares**; no duplicate option |
| **L3 T2 Flame + Surge** | **4 ordinary VP + 1 Surge VP**, 20 AP. A T2 cast does not automatically improve Fire HP fixed packet or multiply VC; `d8` scaling follows the authored T2 expression |
| **L3 two standard Projection Actions** | `20 + 20 = 40 ≤ 42 AP`. Two actions do **not** refresh the one Surge allowance, one safe Step or other Window resources |
| **Paid Deepen (Evoked)** | **24 AP** and `d20+4 vs DC12`; failure does not cause Instability. **24+20=44 > 30/36/42** — same-turn normal Throw is illegal at every MVP level |
| **Paid Overload (Heightened)** | **24 AP**, creates risky Step; **24+20=44 > 30/36/42** — no normal same-turn Throw. Overload alone never rolls Instability; second Step attempted with an independently lawful technique is required |
| **Instability / miscast** | `d20+3 vs DC13` for Tier 1/2; severe miss triggers the universal d20 miscast. The isolated PR #151 zero-AP fixture was **not** an authorized Reactive Thrown Flame. This sheet **does not claim a real native L1–3 miscast scene**; no naturally Overwhelming grant or free reaction is invented |
| **Trust Protective Ward** | Actual incoming attack and a separate eligible **Reaction**, **2 VP** and ally-only range/line requirements. Authored ordinary defensive spell; no Gifted Amplification option for this Ward |
| **Fear Stone Skin** | Self-only; **ceil(Max VP/3) = 2/4/6 Committed VP** at L1/L2/L3. May be authored reactive when the trigger is real, but no automatic independent Amplification permission |
| **Sadness Basic Heal** | **20 AP / 2×T Spent VP** with lawful living recipient and restorative Intrusion; not a free spontaneous heal while Joy is Active |
| **Anticipation Spark of Inspiration** | **20 AP / 2 VP**, knowingly willing recipient, **one** qualifying creative/expression undertaking, +2 to first relevant check; does not force belief or award extra actions |
| **Surprise Phantom Sound** | **20 AP / 1 VP** single localized short nonverbal event; no guaranteed distraction/Stealth bonus |
| **Disgust Weaken Restraints** | One paid **Full Action / 2 VP** to weaken an eligible physical restraint; separately paid physical break attempt; no instant escape |
| **Universal Inspection / Blink** | Inspection **Full Action / 2 VP**, requires held item; Blink L3 **20 AP / 10 VP**, requires live view/open path/clear arrival. Neither consumes or refreshes Gifted Amplification |

**Noncombat contribution is source-available, not an observed playtest certificate.** Medicine +2 and an actual kit support ordinary injury diagnosis/first aid; Disguise +2 applies only when genuinely relevant to a disguise; Veil Theory +1 at L2 can interpret appropriate known Veil patterns, and ordinary Inspection may reveal observable held-item signatures. Phantom Sound, Spark of Inspiration, Weaken Restraints and Minor Telekinesis offer independently conditional scene utility. No automatic GM cooperation, audience persuasion, hidden knowledge, or unlimited unattended force is claimed.
