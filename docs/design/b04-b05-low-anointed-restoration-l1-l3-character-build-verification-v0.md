## Low Embodiment and the distinctive self-preservation loop

**L1 Embodied Signature — Returning Step:** while actually embodied, at the beginning of the character's **existing own-turn Regeneration Step**, convert up to **one missing self HP** from **one available self Vigor**, costing 0 AP and 0 VP. Self is the only legal recipient (distance 0). Do not use it on a Dying/dead character, full HP, or before a real Step; it never makes another Regeneration Step, adds wound Repair Progress, grants passive Guard/Dodge, affects an ally, or restores any Vigor. If the character has 0 Vigor, normal baseline restores 0 HP. An actual regular subsequent regeneration allocation (baseline 5, L2–3 +1 Talent = 6) is still subject to player choice, Vigor and suppression.

**Lesser Authority — Spare the Reserve (1 Destiny):** on **the same legal once-per-Step Signature event** (not an extra event), restore **up to 1 HP** without spending self Vigor. At 0 Vigor the one HP is allowed with sufficient Destiny and positive-HP eligible self. Spend Destiny on the lawful attempt, even if an actual independently adjudicated refusal/Intrusion defeats it. No Destiny may be paid for an impossible zero-HP application.

**L2 Living Instrument:** self/wielded physical embodiment remains part of the Low Anointed expression, but the authored **Restoration** v0 **grants no extra Returning Step pulse, no retroactive Step retiming, no free physical attack and no reactive Guard effect**. Suppress/resume the embodied Signature **only at the start of the Anointed's own turn**, with no free activation in the middle of a Step/response. Two attacks fitting the L3 42 AP do **not** produce two healing pulses. This guardrail prevents borrowing the separate Conflagration-on-hit delivery.

**L3 Incarnate Authority via ordinary technique:** one actually paid and active [Guided Regeneration](../rules/07-veil-magic/guided-regeneration-v0.md) **`Self` Primary [Embodiment]**, either legal Fixed or Maintained mode, can spend **1 Destiny** at one genuine existing own Regeneration Step to make **exactly one still-eligible unsuppressed +1 incremental HP** consume **0 recipient Vigor**. It neither pays 2 VP for the technique nor grants a new Step, additional throughput, free wound Repair Progress, Dying relief, non-self treatment or authority on Basic Heal. The ordinary Guided spell at a separate **30-AP Projection Action** remains available even when no Destiny is used. Each enhanced increment requires its own legal receipt and Destiny. At 0 Vigor the one unsuppressed incremental HP may qualify, but not if no HP is missing.
