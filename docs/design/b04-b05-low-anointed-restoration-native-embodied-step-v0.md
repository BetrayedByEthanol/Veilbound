## Authoritative sourced Low Focus

The exact SHA-bound **AL** Life/Restoration [Low L1–3 character](b04-b05-low-anointed-restoration-l1-l3-character-build-verification-v0.md) has HP **12**; Vigor **12/24/36**; Low VP **2/4/6**; Turn AP **30/36/42**; Physical/Projection **20/30 AP**; baseline natural throughput **5/5/5**, increasing to **5/6/6** with L2 `TAL-C02 Enhanced Regeneration`. At L3 the chosen `TAL-C05 Guard Reserve` adds **one Guard capacity** (normal 3), **not** an Opportunity, extra Step, Response or Returning Step pulse. Its mundane Sword and two possible L3 paid Physical Actions never automatically apply a Signature on hit.

The [native Low adapter](../../scripts/b05_low_anointed_native_returning_step_v0.py) is installed on the exact G0 source and genuine G4, maintaining a separate finite **two-Destiny** source state. No extra `Actor.destiny` or false G5 weapon attack profile is created. Starting source Embodiment is manifested. `toggle_embodiment(manifested=...)` can run **only once inside the actual own-turn-start G0 `on_regeneration_ready` callback**, before the true existing Regeneration Step. An external caller cannot truthfully pass `at_own_turn_start=True` after the start window closes, toggle after a suspended step, or activate a mid-response pulse. **G0 grants that transient start-only capability exclusively on the original Step-1 entry**, even if no callback was supplied then. A missing or invalid natural Step-2 allocation leaves the same turn suspended; a retry may still run an ordinary `on_regeneration_ready` policy callback but **never receives fresh Embodiment-toggle authorization**. A new round's genuinely new own-turn entry is required to toggle again. Suppression makes the self Signature unavailable. Resuming on a later genuine own-turn start can permit a normal optional pulse on that same Step.

## One legal self-only Step, real accounting

`declare_pulse(lesser=False)` is **optional**, and may be chosen immediately before the real Low own Step (when G0's Initiative cursor identifies Low as the next actor) or within the actual start callback. It never creates a Regeneration Step, inserts an activation Action, or triggers from a paid Physical attack. G4 builds the source `returning_step` receipt and own-turn ID from its **current, pending, not-yet-resolved** true Step 2; the caller cannot assert `authenticated_regeneration_step=True` to create an artificial source.

Eligibility: source bond active and Embodied, a living self at **positive but missing HP**, legal current HP-restoration blocks and a normal available self Vigor unit (unless enhancing). Base pulse restores **1 missing self HP** and spends **1 self Vigor**, **0 AP, 0 VP**. For **1 Destiny**, Lesser **Spare the Reserve** restores the same one missing self HP with **0 Vigor spent**, including at self Vigor 0; it never restores wound progress, cancels Dying, replenishes Vigor, adds throughput, or permits a second pulse. Destiny is finite and does not automatically refill on level-up.

**Atomicity:** G4 independently previews the source pulse without state mutation, projects the post-pulse HP/Vigor onto the existing ordinary `RegenerationChoice` validation, and **only after the natural HP/Vigor/wound preflight passes** enters a transient G4-only application window. The native Low `_apply_step` validates that window and rechecks the source preview before editing real HP/Vigor and debiting finite Destiny. One `g4_anointed_low_returning_step_applied` ledger event precedes exactly one normal `regeneration_step` event, whose separate HP/Repair Progress debit still costs actual remaining self Vigor. Example: 5 HP/7 Vigor, one base pulse and player-chosen one natural HP restore becomes **7 HP/5 Vigor**, not 7 HP/6 Vigor or two natural Steps.

If normal regeneration asks for excess HP after the pulse or exceeds the remaining Vigor, the pending Signature stays unspent and the suspended true Step may be retried. `cancel_pulse()` can abandon it without resources and permit ordinary regeneration. A character already dead at its actual Step expires the queued pulse with no Destiny/Vigor/AP payment. A failed live HP prohibition is re-read at Step, not trusted from an earlier declaration. There is no independently invented universal out-of-combat Step timer.
