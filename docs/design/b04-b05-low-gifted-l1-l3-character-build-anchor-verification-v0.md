**Important budget countercases:** At L1 T1 Stone Skin commits 1 of 2 VP, leaving 1 Available, which **cannot** fund a separate 2-Spent-VP Basic Heal/Conjure Flame. One Low Projection Action costs **30 AP**. L3's 42 AP supports two ordinary **20-AP Physical Actions**, **not two finite Anchor Pulses**. Neither Break Anchor (24 AP) + Projection (30 AP) nor paid Deepen (24 AP) + Projection (30 AP) fits that turn; Break + paid Deepen (48 AP) does not fit either. L3 *successful Settled Deepen* can be **0 AP** when the pre-existing uninterrupted Anchor and actual check qualify. Knowing Blink at L3 grants no 10 VP, discount or free casting; with Max VP 6, an ordinary undiscounted Blink is unaffordable.
