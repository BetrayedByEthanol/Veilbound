## Investment, Signature and L3 Authority: the defining Medium play loop

At a legal own-turn setup, **Invest exactly one consenting living patient as one Vessel** (self permitted) using a **24 AP General Tempo Action, 0 VP**, with source-resolved patient and delivery eligibility. Switching patient takes a fresh paid setup, releasing is free only under normal timing. No army, area, settlement or party-wide Vessel. At **the invested patient's own genuine Regeneration Step**, with present visibility, unobstructed magical path and range **0–3 squares**, apply **one** Returning Step pulse *before* normal allocation. Baseline converts **1 patient Vigor → up to 1 missing HP**, 0 activation AP/VP, never creates a new Step, wound progress or benefit for a Dying patient. Existing patient refusal triggers standard +8 Intrusion; a legitimately resisted pulse fails without healing. Range is still **3** at L2: Medium Channel Through Vessel creates no High Expanded Projection or remotely free activation.

**Spare the Reserve**, declared for one legal pulse, costs **1 Destiny** even if subsequently resisted, and waives exactly the **1 recipient Vigor** for one positive-HP restoration, including when the patient has 0 Vigor. It has no application on a full-HP patient, a missing/mismatched Vessel, an ineligible or repeated Step, or a lost path. Ordinary regeneration remains separately player-allocated and pays its normal Vigor. The invested Signature cannot fire per physical swing or during an arbitrary noncombat six-second interval.

The Medium Anointed can independently cast **Basic Heal** (24 AP, T1 2 Spent VP, normal healing and Vigor replenishment, actual Intrusion) or **Guided Regeneration** (24 AP, fixed 2 Spent VP or maintained 2 Committed VP), subject to each technique's current target/range and paid activation. At **L3 Deep Channeling**, one lawful `Guided Regeneration Single Target Primary` on the same actual Invested patient may pay 1 Destiny to make **only one otherwise eligible unsuppressed incremental +1 HP** at a genuine Regeneration Step consume **0** patient Vigor. It still requires the ordinary active paid technique and normal Step allocation, and cannot provide free wound repair, Dying rescue, an extra Step, unapproved ranged delivery, or **Authority on Basic Heal**.
