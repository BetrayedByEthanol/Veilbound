# B04/B05 — Medium Anointed Restoration Native Investment and Recipient Step v0

> **Bounded native G0/G1/G4 integration** of the Medium Life/Restoration Signature: source-identified Investment and an actual recipient-own-turn Regeneration Step, **not** native Guided Regeneration Authority, a complete Soldier/Sentry/Hunter encounter or an independently certified Focus. The structural roster remains **12/12**, full Focus certifications **0/12**, six strict MVP exits **0/6**.

## Source and action contract

The [native Medium adapter](../../scripts/b05_medium_anointed_native_returning_step_v0.py) requires the exact source-hashed **AM** High/Medium/Low [G0 actor bridge](b04-b05-anointed-restoration-three-focus-native-g0-actor-source-bridge-v0.md) and the authored [Medium L1–3 character](b04-b05-medium-anointed-restoration-l1-l3-character-build-verification-v0.md) (PR #165), anchored to the [canonical Anointed Signature and Destiny](../rules/08-archetypes/anointed-signature-authority-l1-3-v0.md).

A living consenting recipient, including self, can be Invested as the **one primary Vessel** by an actually ready Medium Anointed through exactly **one paid 24 AP G0 General Action**, **0 VP**. The G0 `action_committed` index, caster source SHA, source-person identity and `b05_medium_anointed_investment_established` receipt are required before any pulse. Switching to another Vessel consumes another full General Action. This v0 supports **explicit willing living recipients only**; hostile investment or consent withdrawn at delivery needs separate adjudication rather than an accepted Boolean. Investment persists until changed/released by an authored action; mere persistence does not create remote range or unlimited casting.

## Exact real Step 2 delivery

After the Investment turn ends, the player may choose and declare one **optional** pulse for the **next actually scheduled turn of that same recipient**. The declaration must be made when the genuine G0 round/initiative cursor identifies the intended recipient as **next** and the Medium caster's turn is finished; it cannot create, advance or simulate a Step. At the true **G4 regeneration boundary**, after G0 has started that recipient's real own turn (`current`, `pending_regeneration`, current round and unchanged Step eligibility), G4 itself calls the adapter's pure source preview. The caller never passes an `authenticated_regeneration_step=True` flag. The source `returning_step` reference receives a receipt and recipient turn ID **derived from real G0 state**.

The pulse resolves **before natural regeneration** and restores up to **1 missing HP** from **1 of the recipient's Vigor**, with **0 activation AP, 0 VP and no extra Step**. Actual recipient HP/Vigor are mutated by G4, under a transient **G4-only source authorization**, only after the **subsequent ordinary `RegenerationChoice` has passed full G4 preflight** using the projected post-pulse HP/Vigor, so there is no partial heal or Destiny debit if an invalid ordinary choice requested extra HP or Vigor. A legal **Spare the Reserve Lesser** converts this single pulse to **1 HP, 0 recipient Vigor, 1 finite Destiny**, including at recipient Vigor 0, without transferring unused Destiny to a different pulse. The normal Step then independently consumes its own recipient Vigor for selected natural HP/Repair Progress; it must respect remaining missing HP, throughput, poison and wound/Dying prohibitions. The G4 ledger records distinct, ordered `g4_anointed_medium_returning_step_applied` and existing `regeneration_step` receipts, with independently conserved HP/Vigor, source digest, investment action index, round, Lesser cost and finite account state.

This is **one pulse for one actual invested recipient Step**. The next step needs its own decision and native cursor; L2 Channel Through Vessel does not grant a second pulse, additional AP or range. No unbounded noncombat Step cadence exists. If the recipient moves out of range, becomes Dying, reaches full HP, loses Vigor, gains a current HP block or refuses after declaration, resolution **fails closed before healing**. If a queued recipient is already **dead** at its true Step, the queue expires with **no** HP, Destiny or AP payment and the ordinary dead Step remains authoritative. The declaration can be canceled without AP/Destiny cost even after a failed/suspended G4 regeneration preflight; normal G0 Step 2 may then resume. A new natural regeneration event cannot be manufactured by calling the adapter, replaying a receipt or providing a fresh `focus_turn_id`.

## Delivery and Intrusion boundary

**Medium range remains 0–3 squares even at L2–3.** The live G1 position, solid-wall ray and corners are independently checked at declaration and again at the true G4 Step; an intervening creature requiring magical path adjudication also prevents this bounded automatic delivery. `visible=True` is an explicit scene declaration, **not a verified perception event**, and never overrides a solid wall. The G4 healing-block filter may be declared statically and checked again through a separately supplied live `restoration_check` callback at the actual Step; live HP restoration prohibitions block the pulse. A prohibition on *external Vigor healing* alone does not prohibit spending the recipient's own Vigor.

This PR supports **only clearly unopposed beneficial Intrusion**. Any current/prior refusal requires a separate successful **+8 Intrusion Barrier** Direct Veil contest, which remains **unresolved** and is not silently granted by a Boolean or automatic recipient Step. Authenticated willing consent and actual eyesight remain scene inputs; the native AP and Step identity are the parts enforced by G0/G4. No response-window reentry, hostile Vessels, G5 attack, wound repair by Signature, Dying healing, added regenerations or automatic out-of-initiative pulse is implemented.
