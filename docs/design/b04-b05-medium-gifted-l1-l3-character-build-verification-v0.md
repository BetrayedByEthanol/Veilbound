## Rules and restricted identity

- Sources: [Gifted](../rules/08-archetypes/gifted.md), [character creation](../rules/02-character-creation/character-creation-foundation.md), [abilities/training](../rules/02-character-creation/abilities-and-training.md), [equipment](../rules/11-equipment/economy-and-starter-equipment-v0.md), [Medium action costs](../rules/05-combat/action-categories-and-physical-tempo.md), [VP](../rules/07-veil-magic/volition-points.md), [talents](../rules/10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md), [Ward Conduit v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/medium-gifted-ward-conduit-v0.md), and the [historical #154 design](b04-b05-medium-gifted-l1-l3-conduit-expression-design-v0.md).
- Identity: travelling community first-aider who sustains a self-anchored **Ward** and draws on Fear to brace against a physical blow or Trust to shield an **orthogonally adjacent other ally's direct Vigor**. Uses the same expressly restricted **six Strong / two Plausible eight-emotion eight-Pillar map** already audited for High Gifted, not a mandatory universal Gifted creation template. All other source-authored linked-Aspect techniques remain ordinarily priced, cannot be freely cast off-emotion, and are **not** converted into Ward outputs.
- Distinct Focus from High Gifted: **no Projected Surge**, High Focus 20-AP casting, Low Anchored Embodiment, Priest Rites, Scholar Composite or other Focus benefits. Ward is one self-anchored Form, not a free Stone Skin, Protective Ward, separate Tier-1 technique, or automatically placed level-up effect.

## Paid action and VP witnesses (isolated, not native damage receipts)

- **L1 Grade II:** establish Fear Ward at 24 AP, reserve **2 VP** from Max 4, leaving **6 AP / Available 2 / Committed 2 / Spent 0**. Fear's first eligible post-mitigation physical HP packet may prevent up to **2** under the new provisional rule; a second eligible same-Window packet gets no additional Pulse. The model accepts supplied packet values only; this is **not** a real G4 result.
- **L1 Grade III countercase:** 3 Committed leaves only 1 Available. On a later Joy Window the Ward is **Dormant**, but an otherwise valid **2-Spent-VP** Tier-1 Thrown Flame still cannot be paid. Grade I would leave 3 Available and permit that single ordinary 2-VP cast on a later 24-AP action, without Surge, extra free Step or Ward activation.
- **L2 Grade I → II Reconduction:** from `Available 7 / Committed 1 / Spent 0`, pay 24 AP + 3 Spent VP + 1 additional Committed VP → `Available 3 / Committed 2 / Spent 3`. At L1 with Grade I, only 3 Available remain, so changing to II needs 4 and is illegal. A **Grade II → I** at L1 also cannot use its future Grade refund to prepay the 3 VP surcharge.
- **L3 two Wards:** two 24-AP establishments cannot fit the same 42-AP turn (`48 > 42`); they may be established on separate turns, each maintaining its own Grade reservation. Two Grade III Wards leave `Available 6 / Committed 6`; they protect separate eligible events, **never double-pulse the same damage event**. Two Grade II Wards leave Available **8**, so the independently priced **10-VP Blink** is not presently affordable; ordinary release and later setup still obey their rules.
- **Release no cheap Reconduction bypass:** voluntary release returns only its own Committed VP and blocks same-Ward re-establishment on that self-anchor for the rest of this Emotion Window. A new Window permits a new paid Ward with a new instance identity; Reconduction cannot target a retired serial. Ordinary true loss of consciousness terminates maintaining it, never grants a free freshly established effect.
- **One real active emotion:** retuning from Fear to Trust changes eligible packet/recipient; it does **not** spend VP, convert an already-spent Pulse into a fresh one, enable simultaneous Holdfast and Shared Reserve, or change active Grade. The six other restricted emotions remain Dormant. No unlimited Rider/VP regeneration.
- **Deepen+cast failure:** `24 + 24 = 48 AP` exceeds 30/36/42; no same-turn paid Deepen plus ordinary projected T1 action at L1–3. Natural Heightening and Emotional Poise are not automatic, and one Ward Pulse per Window is independent of Amplification Steps. Unlike High Gifted, Medium cannot buy Projected Surge.
- **Noncombat identity:** Medicine +2 with actual kit, Disguise +2 when circumstances support disguise, L2 Veil Theory +1 and conditional Inspection/Phantom Sound/Spark/Telekinesis/Weaken Restraints provide exploration/support options but **not** automatic GM outcomes. The Ward's Trust protection requires real adjacency and incoming direct Vigor; it cannot be claimed as a general social benefit.
