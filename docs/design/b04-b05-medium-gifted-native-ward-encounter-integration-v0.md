# B04/B05 — Medium Gifted L1–3 Native Ward Conduit Encounter Integration v0

> **Bounded native G0/G1/G3/G4/G5 evidence; NOT a full Medium Gifted Focus certificate, representative encounter pack, or global finite-prevention priority rule.** This design record integrates the already-merged provisional [Ward Conduit v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/medium-gifted-ward-conduit-v0.md) and [Medium Gifted L1–3 structural build](b04-b05-medium-gifted-l1-l3-character-build-verification-v0.md). It neither creates a new Conduit Form nor extends any B06 G0–G8 gate.

## Source and actual delivery chain

- `scripts/b05_medium_gifted_native_ward_v0.py` uses the source-fingerprinted **Medium Gifted** L1–3 character sheet: exact nine abilities, weapon/armor, Level 1–3 HP/Vigor/VP and Turn AP, and eight fixed emotion links. Its `MG` source identity is distinct from the existing High Gifted `G`, Medium Scholar `S`, Medium Priest `M`, and enemy IDs. A forged archetype, altered digest, mismatched native stats or non-Medium Focus cannot establish or Pulse a Ward.
- G0 **Step 5** establishes each new Emotion Window exactly once after actual turn resources and before actions/movement. Fear → Earth/Fortification, Trust → Kinship/Guardian, other six linked emotions or unset → Dormant. No automatic free active emotions, forced emotional transitions or Universal-to-linked conversion is implied. Window refresh is not triggered by an enemy's turn or a second attack.
- **Own-turn placement** consumes an actual 24-AP General Tempo Action and 1/2/3 **Committed** VP for Grade I/II/III. L1–2 permits one Conduit, L3 two. **Grade Reconduction** consumes native 24 AP, 3 **Spent** VP and a positive/negative commitment delta without borrowing future Grade releases. Free voluntary release is available even with fewer than 24 AP remaining, only at a legal own-turn decision; it returns only this instance's Committed VP and locks out same-Window re-establishment. G0's generic VP-release path cannot refund VP still owned by active G3 effects **or** Ward Conduits.
- Source enemies' **G5 pre-roll assigned defense**, genuine hit/miss, sourced damage dice, criticals and native G4 packet creation precede any Ward Pulse. An ordinary miss yields **no Ward prevention**. The G4 preview used by G5 wound preflight sees the same **post-Armor / post-resistance** packet as G4's actual application. This prevents a false wound threshold, extra Armor/resistance reduction or refund caused by a later unrecognized Ward. A committed Pulse is logged before the G4 applied-damage receipt under the **same original G4 event key**. The G4 record carries the Ward receipt, while `packets[].mitigated`, HP/Vigor actual loss and G5 resolved damage use the residual amounts.
- **Holdfast** protects only the caster's one original direct Physical **HP** packet (source physical weapon mitigation already applied). It is **not** a defense against Fire/Electrical, toxin damage, Vigor damage or a second attack in the same Emotion Window.
- **Shared Reserve** protects only one other genuine allied actor's positive original **Direct Vigor** packet while that ally is at one **orthogonally adjacent single-square G1 position**. It never acts on self, enemies, distant or diagonal allies, wrong resource/type, zero Current Vigor, or multi-square geometry requiring unauthored reference-cell/range rules. Unlike Protective Ward it neither contests the attack roll nor grants a Reaction.
- If two Conduits are established at L3, select the **oldest still-eligible instance**. One original event may consume at most one Conduit Pulse; a separate later event may use another. A grade change or later G5 hit does not restore the prior instance's Pulse; only a real next Emotion Window does. Losing consciousness/death ends the caster's Wards and returns their Committed VP under the original source rule.

## Explicit refusal and non-certification boundaries

- **No invented finite-prevention priority:** if a Ward would overlap with Low Life's finite HP diversion, a live Vital Shelter budget on the same recipient, or paid Medium Priest Intercession on that same G4 Vigor event, the combination remains unresolved. Reject before committing the Ward budget or recipient HP/Vigor. No universal ordering is inferred from this narrow profile.
- The Ward adapter does **not** create arbitrary G4 damage events. Its Pulse commit requires the exact applying native G4 typed packet plus the genuine native G5 melee-roll/source-profile receipt, or the existing synchronous G3→G4 Flame authority. Direct calls using fabricated causes/packets cannot claim a Ward Pulse. G5 still controls attacker contact, AP, defenses, criticals and confirmed delivery.
- Native **one-plane one-cell G1** geometry is deliberately restricted. Multi-square bodies, flying/3D diagonal range, actual wall-intersection beyond the simple adjacent grid, non-melee mixed living screens, multiple overlapping different prevention effects and original monster hazard/venom interactions are **not certified**. There is no extra G5 protective-response mechanism, gifted offensive casting, combat role balance, or noncombat outcome proof in this PR.
- Medium Gifted remains **one of 8/12 structurally verified Focus pairings, 0/12 independently end-to-end certified**, with **B04/B05/B06/B07 still blocking**, 3/7 original blockers closed and 0/6 strict MVP exit gates. B06 remains its established G0–G8 implementation register.
