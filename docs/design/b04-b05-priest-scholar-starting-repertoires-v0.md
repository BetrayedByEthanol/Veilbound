# B04/B05 — Priest and Scholar Starting Repertoires v0 (Level 1–3)

> **PROPOSED numeric/operational MVP baseline for review**, not final balance or a complete character catalogue. This closes starting-count decisions **A01, A02 and A05** from the [Archetype Access and Starting Repertoire Audit](b04-b05-archetype-access-and-starting-repertoire-audit.md) once merged. It **does not** author new techniques, Consecrations, Devotional Rites, Inward Boosts, Embodiments, Signatures, Gifted expressions or the missing Tier-2 catalogue. The [canonical Priest](../rules/08-archetypes/priest.md) and [Scholar](../rules/08-archetypes/scholar.md) Archetype rules govern individual casting; this page records the numerical rationale, tests and outstanding gaps.

## One-page decision table

| Focus | Priest base Prepared Prayers L1 / L2 / L3 | Priest starting Accord | Priest actual capacity with Accord +1 | Scholar individually known Pillar techniques L1 / L2 / L3 | Scholar additional preparation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **High** | **3 / 4 / 4** | **+1** | **4 / 5 / 5** | **5 / 6 / 6** | **1 / 2 / none required** prepared **composite formulas** at L1/L2; at L3 **Formula Fluency** removes pairing preparation |
| **Medium** | **2 / 3 / 3** | **+1** | **3 / 4 / 4** | **4 / 5 / 5** | No general prepared-technique cap; one **active Inward Boost** at L1–3, if authored and affordable |
| **Low** | **1 / 2 / 2** | **+1** | **2 / 3 / 3** | **3 / 4 / 4** | **2 / 3 / 3** prepared known techniques; at most one active eligible Embodiment at L1–3 |

**Priest numbers above are *base* before Accord, relevant Talents and any explicit features.** From one chosen Pillar, the Priest may prepare **only techniques genuinely authored and accessible in one of that Pillar's three Aspects** at the level's normal tier ceiling. Starting capacity is an **upper bound**, not a guarantee that the catalogue contains enough entries. A Pillar with one authored technique fills at most one slot, even if the Priest has capacity 4. No cross-Pillar filling, duplicated prayer to simulate distinct techniques, free unlisted spells or Universal Veil preparation slots.

**Scholar counts refer to individual known Pillar/Aspect techniques from potentially any Pillar.** One known technique with several expressly authored profiles is **one known technique**, not one known slot per profile or per Used Tier. High's prepared composite **formula** is a separate declared pair of **two already-known** technique identities plus an **exact mutually supported profile/roles and compatible casting mode**, not another learned spell or permission to mix unsupported effects. Low's Prepared Techniques are a subset of known, not an additional spell knowledge grant. Medium's Inward cap is an **active channel cap**, not a known/prepared count.

## Priest starting state, daily prayer capacity and recalculation

1. **Starting Pillar Accord:** set **+1 (Stable)** on an ordinary coherent L1 Priest. This is **not** the character's independent universal **Resonance**, which still begins at 0. Starting at another Accord value requires an explicit story/campaign character-creation exception; don't silently award +2/+3 or substitute one for the other.
2. **Effective preparation capacity** at each preparation: `max(1, base_for_level_and_focus + current_Accord + explicit_base_capacity_modifiers)`. Accord ranges **−3 to +3**. This formula gives the already-established **+1/+2/+3 prepared slots** at positive Accord, explicit negative reductions, and a **floor of one**, so severe dissonance impairs breadth rather than automatically erasing all ordinary known-Pillar prayer access. The floor **does not** guarantee the Pillar has any playable spell. For example a Low L1 Priest with base 1, Accord −3 has capacity 1; a High L1 with base 3, Accord +3 has capacity 6. [TAL-P01](../rules/10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md#tal-p01--expanded-liturgy) is an explicit **+1 base capacity** modifier if legitimately selected; never a Pillar Accord change.
3. **Preparation:** a Priest may **replace/fill the entire prepared chosen-Pillar list** after a deliberate, uninterrupted **10-minute prayer/preparation practice**, **at most once per six elapsed in-world hours**. The *ordinary starting list* may be established at L1 character creation without consuming in-game time, and a newly awarded level-up expansion may be prepared on the next valid preparation without requiring the player to wait to claim the capacity itself. **This six-hour gate does not recover VP, reset Accord, forgive Miracle Debt or constitute a Faith Rest**. It does not require a particular prayer building, devotion gesture or deity approving the spell. GM may place ordinary safe-time/interruptions on such preparation; it is not a combat/free Reaction step.
4. **Prepared list validity:** each entry is a distinct actually available **chosen-Pillar** technique at the current level's permitted tier and with any specific access requirement met. Only **currently prepared** entries may be invoked as Prepared Prayers; otherwise High Priest may use separately paid Open Invocation when legally eligible, and Miracle Petition remains separate. Repreparation can replace a technique only from the same Pillar, not change the chosen Pillar. The action does **not** pay a technique's activation VP or conjure its persistent effect; independently authored prepared ammunition/other special modes retain their own costs.
5. **Accord change after preparation:** recalculate permitted capacity immediately. If it decreases below the number prepared, the player designates enough currently prepared entries as **inactive/suspended** to fit the new capacity **at the Accord change**; no opportunistic choice after an attack roll, no free refund of technique VP, and existing legally activated sustained effects follow **their own** maintenance/end rules (the list shrinking does not recast or retroactively invalidate a paid activation). Suspended prayers cannot be newly activated, count against no remaining capacity and may be reselected only on the next normal preparation. An increase creates **empty capacity** until the next valid preparation, except an independently authored spontaneous feature such as High's Open Invocation (which uses neither a new prepared slot nor an Open Prayer instant refill).
6. **Accord's numeric effect:** positive Accord's **technique-specific final-effect bonus** applies only where individually authored; it does **not** confer an unlisted universal +1 attack roll, +1 resistance DC, +1 damage or a new spell. **Miracle Petition requires positive Accord** separately; no automatic Petition spam or Accord advance from six hours of prayer.

**High Priest Open Invocation** at L1–4:

- An **authored normal-tier chosen-Pillar technique not currently Prepared**, with all its own legal targets, knowledge/access prerequisites, casting and timing requirements, may be invoked by **one Full Action** (instead of a shorter Projection Action where relevant) plus **1 additional Spent VP surcharge**, on top of the technique's **entire ordinary Spent and/or Committed VP cost**. At L5 the feature's existing normal-action permission resumes, but the **same +1 surcharge** remains.
- **Available VP must cover both** the complete technique cost and this surcharge at legal declaration and actual effect application; neither a depleted pool nor Frugal Technique converts the surcharge to 0 or lowers Committed VP. Legal discounts to the *ordinary* Spent component do not apply to the **separate Invocation surcharge**. An unprepared 2-Spent-VP spell therefore requires **3 Available VP** at base; an unprepared 4-Committed-VP Tier-1 Reserved Weapon requires **5 Available VP**, if its actual use is otherwise legal.
- Charge ordinary technique VP and the **one surcharge VP on the same successful magical application** (for an integrated physical projectile delivery: only on an **otherwise legal launch**, before the single ordinary attack roll). If declaration was illegal: no AP/VP. If a legally committed Full Action is interrupted or the target/affordability becomes invalid before magical application: **Full Action lost, 0 technique VP and 0 surcharge VP spent**; any separately legally declared Talent use follows its own consumption rule. If the effect validly applies but is later resisted/blocked/misses, the surcharge remains Spent.
- This permission **does not create a physical weapon attack or prepared-ammunition batch**, provide an extra Projection Action/Reaction, bypass technique restrictions, authorize an unprepared self-restriction-breaking effect, evade the one-enchantment-per-projectile rule, or turn Miracle Petition into normal casting. No cross-Pillar invocation, Tier 2 at L1–2, unauthored effect, unlimited Committed VP, or free Invocation during somebody else's turn. Integrated-shot and Full Action interactions must satisfy the **actual paid weapon action** and the entire Open Invocation Full Action without granting more than one shot.
- **Level 3 Open Prayer:** High Priest may leave **exactly one** existing Prepared Prayer slot **unassigned** during regular preparation; on first later eligible chosen-Pillar technique use, it becomes the spell filling that one slot for the rest of the preparation cycle. This is **slot assignment, not Open Invocation**: **no +1 Invocation surcharge** for that first filling when otherwise normally legal, and the slot cannot fill more than once. If a Priest has no empty slot or chooses not to reserve one, nothing changes. On Accord shrink suspend slots before any new use; a no-longer-existing reserved Open Prayer slot gives no free preparation.

## Scholar learning and preparation details

**Starting known techniques:** choose **5 High / 4 Medium / 3 Low distinct individually authored Pillar/Aspect techniques** at L1, within the existing level/tier ceiling. They may cross the 16 Pillars, including outside the eight selected MVP Pillars, **only where an actual technique exists**; knowing an Aspect category does not automatically teach its spells. A character may select **fewer** than the numerical cap if the permitted player catalogue is smaller; record unused known capacity as **empty** and do not invent a spell. No free per-Profile duplicate, no post-creation random technique pickup without an actual learning award/retraining permission.

**Level-up awards:** at **level 2**, gain **exactly one additional individually learned Pillar technique** in each Focus (High **6**, Medium **5**, Low **4**); High receives **one additional prepared composite formula** (L1 **1 → L2 2**); Low's Prepared subset cap grows **2 → 3**. **Level 3 grants no extra known-technique/prepared-count slot**: High instead obtains **Formula Fluency**, Medium **Efficient Routing/Overrouting**, Low **Deep Embodiment**, as already authored. The character may fill the newly awarded learned slot at the L2 level-up by choosing an eligible actually authored Tier-1 technique; a later Tier-2 ceiling at L3 **does not** auto-teach an unlearned or unauthored Tier-2 technique. [TAL-S01 Expanded Repertoire](../rules/10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md#tal-s01--expanded-repertoire) adds **one independent known Pillar technique**, not an extra prepared or formula slot.

**High Scholar formulas at levels 1–2:** A prepared formula records **ordered Primary and Secondary technique identities**, the one **exact shared profile identifier**, and one legal delivery mode in which both authored contributions are compatible. A given known technique can appear in several formulas, but each distinct ordered pair/profile/mode occupies **one formula slot**. Swapping Primary/Secondary counts as a **different formula** where both directions would be legal. Formula capacity may remain unused if no actually compatible authored pair is known. Preparing a formula **does not spend VP or trigger a technique**. When cast, both known techniques pay their full effective ordinary component VP costs and one combined legal action as already defined by Composite Casting, and both still require the other legality checks. **At level 3 Formula Fluency ends this prepared-formula restriction**; it doesn't grant unlearned components, new Secondary contributions, unlimited damage, lower VP cost or extra separate actions.

**Low Scholar preparation at levels 1–3:** The Low Scholar chooses **up to 2/3/3** *distinct known* Pillar techniques in the Prepared subset. Only that subset is available for any ordinary Low Scholar cast/Reserve under the Prepared Arsenal framework; other known techniques still count as known but are not currently ready. One can have multiple profiles without taking extra preparation slots. **Only actually eligible prepared Embodiment profiles** may fill the **one Active Embodiment** cap at L1–3: merely using `Self`, `Weapon Enchantment`, a [Projection] tag or a sustained Reserve does **not** imply compatible Low Embodiment/Switching/Intensification authorization.

**Medium Scholar:** All individually known Pillar techniques remain eligible for ordinary individual casting where their action/target/VP allow. There is **no generic prepared-technique cap**. Existing **max one Active Inward Boost** at L1–3 remains. **Inward Boost** must be explicitly authored with its channel/compatibility; regular self-targeted [Projection] Stone Skin is **not automatically** a reroutable Inward Boost. Medium's **3 Spent VP** L1–2 reroute is payable only when legitimate alternate known Inward Boost expressions exist, not a free ability to reconfigure arbitrary magic.

**Scholar preparation timing at levels 1–2:** To replace the *prepared High formula list* or *Low prepared technique subset*, perform **10 uninterrupted minutes of deliberate technical review/practice**, at most **once per six elapsed in-world hours**, or choose the initial set for free at L1 creation. **This is independent of VP recovery, the Priest's prayers and Talent resets**, and never rewrites the permanently known-technique list. At L2, a new Low prepared slot or High formula slot may be filled on the **next legal preparation**. Level-3 Formula Fluency removes the High formula selection requirement entirely. There is no High/Medium global preparation cap on individually known normal-technique Primary casts.

|---|---|

| L1 **Medium Priest +1 Accord** | Base **2** +1 = **3** capacity; no authored Consecration is invented by having empty slots |
| L1 **Low Priest +1 Accord** | Base **1** +1 = **2** capacity; cannot call every sustained self spell a Devotional Rite |
| L2 Priest all Focuses, Accord still +1 | High **5**, Medium **4**, Low **3** total capacity; L3 same totals before other modifiers |

| Priest with TAL-P01 and Accord +1 at L1 | High **5**, Medium **4**, Low **3** capacity; Talent does not raise Accord |

| L1 High Priest prepared **Basic Heal**, Life Pillar | One normal Primary (2 Spent VP) if legal; no Invocation surcharge |

## Scope still blocked after this decision

Even if the numbers above are merged, the [audit](b04-b05-archetype-access-and-starting-repertoire-audit.md) still reports **0/12 certified complete builds**: the catalogue contains one actual technique per selected Pillar, Medium Priest Consecrations and Low Devotional Rites are unauthored, Medium Scholar reroutable Inward Boosts and explicit Low Embodiment compatibility still need implementation, High Scholar has few legal actual composites, Gifted Amplification/Conduits/Embodiments are not complete, and Anointed Signatures/Authority are missing. The list of currently **authored** spells and these new *capacity* values must not be confused with known magical functionality. B06 enemies, B07 level-up walkthroughs, level-3 Blink affordability and actual balance remain open.
