# B04/B07 — Independent L1–3 Build and Progression Audit v0

> **Independent mechanical review, not a full playable Focus certificate.** This report audits all **12 Archetype × Focus pairings**, **13 physical/source variants** (Low Priest A and B count as one pairing) and **39 L1/L2/L3 snapshots**. It does not claim player/GM role witnesses, verified two-milestone adventures, representative encounter outcomes, or 12/12 independently certified Focuses.

## Audit method and authority

The independent [executable cross-source audit](../../scripts/b04_b07_independent_progression_audit_v0.py) imports each selected original profile(level) but **does not call the original profile's own verify() or trust its source_legal/certified booleans**. Each sheet is checked against separately coded creation and progression rules: the 28-point nine-Ability schedule, two separate Weapon Development points, eight starting Learned Discipline points plus one at L2, the no-extra-L3-training/no-before-L4-Ability gates, 150 sp starting budget with no automatic level-up loot, exact Focus-based Max VP, HP/Vigor and 30/36/42 Turn AP, physical/projection AP tempo, L1 Veil Inspection and L3 Blink (access is **not** VP affordability), L1/L2/L3 regular Tier gates, and unchanged selected skills, equipment, bonds/links as applicable. At L3, the Talent count must respect the authored [B07 early-level Focus awards](../rules/10-advancement/talents-framework-v0.md), including explicit saved-selection accounting rather than inventing a choice.

**Independent Focus pricing and repertoire limits (Codex review hardening):**
The audit derives ordinary Physical/Projection AP from the canonical High **30/20**, Medium **24/24**, Low **20/30** schedule, General Tempo **24**, and computes derived tempo from those independent prices; a matching forged cost+tempo pair cannot pass. Scholar known technique caps are High **5/6/6**, Medium **4/5/5**, Low **3/4/4**, with only an actually earned, eligible **TAL-S01 Expanded Repertoire** adding one known technique. High's L1/L2 formula caps are **1/2** (L3 Formula Fluency removes required formula preparation), Medium has no general prepared-spell cap, and Low prepared caps are **2/3/3** without automatic bonuses. Priest base capacities are High **3/4/4**, Medium **2/3/3**, Low **1/2/2** plus authentic Accord (these initial sources carry **+1**) and an eligible **TAL-P01 Expanded Liturgy** modifier. High L3 has at most one Open Prayer slot *inside* that capacity. The checks separately validate reported capacity **and** actual list size, including tampered sources inflating both together. No capacity is gained from an ineligible or unearned modifier.

**B07 Talent-selection provenance (Codex review hardening):** the audit reads **all 32 catalogue entries** and their minimum character level and explicit Archetype qualifier directly from the authoritative [Talent Catalogue](../rules/10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md), rather than assuming any TAL-P/S/G/A prefix authorizes the selection. Priest-only, Scholar-only, Gifted-only and Anointed-only Talents are restricted to their respective Archetypes; shared Any / Any Archetype/Focus entries remain generally available, subject to their other authored prerequisites. An invalid cross-Archetype Talent is reported as a structured **talent_archetype** issue and is **not** eligible to expand a repertoire limit. A malformed selected Talent ID at L1 is reported as **talent_identity**; an invalid prior ID cannot crash subsequent L2/L3 validation, and the attempted progression is reported as **talent_retraining**. These checks do not retroactively authorize unrelated skill, equipment or narrative prerequisites not independently evidenced.

**Authored High Scholar composite permissions (Codex review hardening):** Formula capacity is distinct from **actual composition authority**. For the audited High Scholar L1–3 source, the only positively authored v0 ordered pairs are **Stone Skin `Self` Primary + Basic Heal `Self` instantaneous Secondary**, and **Stone Skin `Self` Primary + maintained subordinate Vital Shelter `Self` Secondary**. Both require the same exact `Self` profile and **normal on-turn Projection** delivery, with the specified subordinate effect mode; neither may be reversed, reacted with, attached under an invented key, or replaced by a nominally known Primary-only technique such as Phantom Sound. These permissions are independently encoded from [Stone Skin](../rules/07-veil-magic/stone-skin-v0.md#high-scholar-composite--stone-skin-primary--compatible-self-secondary), [Basic Heal](../rules/07-veil-magic/basic-heal-v0.md) and [Vital Shelter](../rules/07-veil-magic/vital-shelter-v0.md), **not** derived from the audited sheet's formula objects. New authorizations need separately reviewed source evidence; this bounded list is not a prohibition on all future authored composites.

**Saved Talent account (Codex review hardening):** At *every* level, the number of actually selected entries plus a nonnegative integer of saved awards must equal the Focus's earned Talent awards. An omitted saved field means **zero only**; it does not hide an L2 or L3 unspent choice, and cannot manufacture extra choices when none remain. Saved booleans, negative balances, decimals and overstatements fail; an invalid saved ledger also cannot grant repertoire-modifying Talent effects.

**Priest chosen-Pillar and Tier provenance (Codex review hardening):** The audited High and Medium Priest pilot builds have independently fixed **Life** as their chosen Pillar. The verifier checks the original and subsequent character sheets preserve that Pillar, then validates **each** prepared prayer against six canonical authored Life technique rule files: **Basic Heal**, **Guided Regeneration**, **Vital Shelter**, **Woodland Rapport**, **Venom Endurance**, and **Gentle Growth**. Their source rule headings establish Life / Restoration, Preservation, or Nature, while their individual identity-table Base Tier fields establish **Tier 1**. The verifier reads and checks those identity and base-tier fields rather than trusting the candidate build's `LIFE_T1` list, technique labels, or reported preparation capacity; a modified canonical identity or unrecognized tier format fails closed. The **L1–2 Tier-1 / L3 Tier-2 ceiling** is independent of a sheet's own `tier_ceiling` claim. **Conjure Flame**, a forged new Life spell, and automatic Universal Veil procedures cannot occupy a chosen-Pillar prayer slot. Open Prayer at High L3 is an optional unfilled slot inside capacity, **not** cross-Pillar permission. This is a bounded audit of the currently selected Life Priest sources, **not** a universal exhaustive catalogue of all Pillars or unsupported higher-level access.

**Scholar known-repertoire provenance (Codex review hardening):** All three Scholar Focuses have an independent positive eligibility check in addition to their **5/4/3 → 6/5/4** capacity check. The authored Tier-1 individual Pillar technique registry covers **Conjure Flame, Stone Skin, Basic Heal, Minor Telekinesis, Phantom Sound, Vital Shelter, Guided Regeneration, Gentle Growth, Venom Endurance, Woodland Rapport, Protective Ward, Spark of Inspiration, Weaken Restraints**, each verified against its executable canonical technique page for its full identity, original Pillar/Aspect and **Base Tier**. Each linked Aspect must belong to the canonical [16-Pillar/48-Aspect matrix](../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md). A technique may occupy a Scholar's individual known slot only if its authored base tier is at most the **independently derived** level ceiling (L1–2 T1; L3 T2). Automatic Universal Veil **Veil Inspection/Blink** are separate knowledge and never consume individual Pillar-technique slots, even after their ordinary unlock. An unknown or merely named but unauthored spell fails. This positive registry covers **all thirteen currently published executable individual Tier-1 Pillar technique rule pages**, not just the techniques already selected in the three pilot sheets. It accepts other actually authored cross-Pillar choices, while newly authored individual techniques require an explicit reviewed registry addition.

**Gifted permanent-link creation (Codex review hardening):** Validate **exactly** the canonical eight emotions (Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, Surprise, Disgust, Trust, Anticipation), sourced from [Gifted](../rules/08-archetypes/gifted.md), rather than only matching the source's separate technique-map keys. Every one of the eight birth links must use an actually canonical Pillar/Aspect pair from the [world matrix](../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md), with **eight different Pillars and eight different Aspects**. Duplicating Fear into Joy, using a noncanonical pair, inventing an Aspect, or replacing an emotion cannot pass even if source-local validators or subsequent level snapshots carry the same invalid links. This is an identity/allocation check, not an assertion that every potential emotion–Aspect compatibility interpretation has been independently proven.

**Anointed birth-bond authentication (Codex review hardening):** The audit independently reads the two positive level-1–3 Signature package headings from the [canonical Anointed Signature and Authority rules](../rules/08-archetypes/anointed-signature-authority-l1-3-v0.md): **Returning Step is Life / Restoration** and **Kindled Flame is Fire / Conflagration**. Both require a canonical Pillar/Aspect pairing. The three selected Restoration pilot sheets must have the exact **Life / Restoration** bond, and must keep Returning Step, their active bond and proper L1–3 Destiny cap; a consistently changed Fire / Conflagration bond fails **anointed_bond** at **each** level even if source-local persistence passes. This does not invent a new Signature, authorize a second bond, or reclassify the existing character as the different Fire pilot.

**Invalid Learned Discipline rank resilience (Codex review hardening):** The independent verifier validates every directly invested rank as an actual **integer from 0 through 2** before computing its sum or level-to-level delta. A malformed string, `None`, boolean, float, or out-of-range rank yields **learned_ranks**, and if the current or prior source is invalid, a level progression requiring arithmetic yields **learned_progression** rather than crashing. The aggregate twelve-pairing/39-snapshot machine-readable audit is still produced; rejecting corrupted source data does not silently award skills or certify an invalid Focus.

Each witness records the original source-file SHA-256 and its independent findings. The **Low Priest A/B** case uses the actual G0 physical proxy actor at each level and cross-checks the separate [authored A/B build record](b05-life-low-priest-l3-build-exaltation-verification-v0.md); this is intentionally **not claimed to be a complete generic Low Priest profile**. It does not convert a physical proxy into proof of unrestricted spellcasting, Chosen Rite legality, or full noncombat play.

The verifier separately checks *hypothetical* GM-declared L1→L2→L3 receipts: exactly two distinct, explicit story-event IDs, correct character, one level at a time, and non-automatic awards. No two events have actually occurred. This validation does not award levels, grant new Training/Talents, refill Available VP, refill Destiny, add known techniques, or infer XP. Only a real GM session/adventure record can establish the milestones happened.

## Findings requiring follow-up

| Source pairing | Level | Finding | Classification / next action |

| **High Priest / Life** | L3 | Sheet includes a second selected Talent, **TAL-M03 Frugal Technique**, although the adopted provisional High Focus schedule awards only the L1 selection before L5 | **Over-award / structural progression inconsistency**. | **Low Scholar / Embodiment** | L2–L3 | The build shows L1 Talent and adds a Talent at L3, without accounting for the distinct L2 award | **Unaccounted earned selection**, not necessarily illegal. These are **source observations**, not silently corrected character choices. The executable audit produces machine-readable pairing/level issue codes. A future corrective PR should reconcile source snapshots, the rule's Talent ledger, associated independent checks, native actor-derived Talent modifiers, and any affected bounded evidence before claiming that the relevant L1→3 structural progression is fully validated. In particular, removing High Priest's unauthorized L3 Talent is more than a documentation change if prior encounters relied on its VP discount.

    python -m unittest -v tests.test_b04_b07_independent_progression_audit_v0
    python -m scripts.b04_b07_independent_progression_audit_v0

The test suite includes negative tampering of earned VP, training, illegal early Blink and Talent access, cross-Archetype Talent substitutions, malformed prior L1 Talent IDs preserved through the full audit, forged Focus AP/tempo combinations, correlated known/prepared/formula-capacity inflation, invalid ordered/form/profile/delivery Composite Casting, unauthorized repertoire modifiers, negative/bogus saved Talent ledgers, **out-of-Pillar/invented Priest prayers, ineligible Scholar known entries, and independently enforced base-tier eligibility**, **duplicated/noncanonical/missing Gifted emotion-to-Pillar/Aspect links**, **consistently wrong Anointed bond/Signature pairings and malformed numeric Learned Discipline allocations**, and illegal/missing GM milestone chronology. The scoped GitHub Actions workflow runs the focused audit regressions plus the full repository unittest suite at the attested PR head. Passing these tests **does not imply a completed adventure or 12 Focus certificates**.
