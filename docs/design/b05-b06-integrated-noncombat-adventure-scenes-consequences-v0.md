# B05/B06 — Integrated Noncombat Adventure Scenes & Consequences v0

> **Runnable, source-accounted three-scene author-fixture, not human/GM play and not a complete 3–5-session adventure.** It connects existing [ordinary core checks](b04-b05-source-legal-noncombat-scene-resolution-v0.md), [bounded source-paid technique actions](b04-b05-focus-noncombat-technique-integration-v0.md), G0 Turn AP/VP and the authentic G4.2 WorldClock. It makes no new canonical check, spell, failure penalty, NPC influence subsystem, XP or GM mission award. Every narrative fact and conditional consequence below is **an explicitly authored GM fixture premise** that a real GM must accept or adjust.

## Playable GM scene: The Supply-Cache Ledger

The group is tracing an ordinary contaminated medicinal-herb shipment to a neutral storekeeper's public supply log. There is a small guarded footbridge and a ford route to a supply cache. The cache contains an accessible herbal sample and a **portable ring held by the investigating character**; the ring's plainly observable Veil pattern is **Life / Restoration**, but its function and any concealed enchantment are *not* revealed. On the far shore sits a **loose, ordinary, unwarded, unattended 3 kg / 3 dm³ / 0.4 m annotated map marker** within a directly visible unobstructed line and a clear two-square retrieval path. The nearby fox is a wary, ordinary nonhostile **12 kg animal** capable of perceiving peaceful cues. A courier may recognize a prearranged knock, but the storekeeper is not thereby magically persuaded.

**GM decisions before play:** confirm the terrain, object attendance, animal state, actually held ring, reasonable consequences and DCs; determine whether all three scenes genuinely require rolls; choose an exact legal L1–3 player sheet and its prepared/known techniques, available VP, player approaches, consequences and response windows. A real GM is free to change those premises; the executable program only covers the frozen example. A successful physical check does not create material knowledge or unlock new magic; a failed request does not create a compulsory NPC disposition change.

### Scene 1 — Crossing the ford

**Goal:** reach the cache before an ordinary patrol. A player can choose a careful **Awareness + applicable Navigation** survey (Challenging target 12, 180 authored seconds) or a direct **Agility + applicable Acrobatics** slippery crossing (Hard target 15, 90 authored seconds). A success reaches the direct crossing. A failure does **not** inflict damage or force an opposed check: the scenario's predeclared **long footpath adds 300 seconds** before reaching the same cache. The detour is a *scenario-specific time consequence*, not a general penalty for failed navigation.

**Priest/High optional focus choice:** genuine prepared **Woodland Rapport** at T1 on the wary fox costs one High Projection Action **20 AP + 2 Spent VP**. Its **one +2 Initial Trust modifier** is consumed on one separate qualifying peaceful **Presence** first-approach check (GM-fixture target 10). The fox **cannot communicate semantic route knowledge, be compelled, guarantee safety or grant an infinite social buff**; the separate navigation check still decides whether the party takes the direct route. Rapport is not automatically available to another Priest Focus.

**Scholar/Medium L2–3 optional focus choice:** a genuinely known **Minor Telekinesis** action (**24 AP + 2 Spent VP**) moves the loose annotated marker, no resistance on this plainly **unattended mundane** fixture. The GM-premised ordinary text physically on the retrieved marker shows the safe bank. This is a *readable physical clue*, not magical object identification, obstacle destruction, creature movement or a free spell. **Scholar/Medium L1 cannot select this option.**

### Scene 2 — Investigation at the cache

The character can inspect the herb batch using **Intellect + actually purchased Medicine**, Challenging target 12, with **240 authored seconds**. A success establishes an *ordinary medical diagnosis of spoiled goods* and makes a documented argument possible later. Failure leaves cause uncertain.

Alternatively, any Focus can choose **Universal Veil Inspection** of the actually held ring. This spends a genuine **Full Action** (all currently untouched Turn AP) and **2 Spent VP**. The basic result is only a visible Life / Restoration Veil signature, not an automatic diagnosis or hidden enchantment, and the GM fixture assigns **120 additional exploration seconds** to processing the observation (not as spell cast duration). No extra Veil Theory roll is silently inserted in this adventure; the separately authored Inspection +2 interpretation check remains an independent optional action question for real play.

**Scholar/High and Scholar/Medium L2–3** can instead use a known **Minor Telekinesis** to move one loose, ordinary sample container through a clear two-square route for **2 VP**, High **20 AP** or Medium **24 AP**. This retrieves **physical material**; it does **not** automatically identify spoilage or provide the medical conclusion. The GM fixture assigns **120 seconds** of ordinary sample sorting. The L2–3 Medium Scholar selecting Telekinesis in both crossing and investigation pays **4 Spent VP across two genuine own turns in the same actor ledger**.

### Scene 3 — The public record request

Ask the storekeeper to show an existing public record. The player may make a **Presence** petition (Challenging target 12, authored 60 seconds) or make a **fact-based Intellect + genuinely learned regional/era History** case (Challenging target 12, authored 120 seconds). Do **not** invent universal Negotiation, Command or Oratory Training.

For this **specific neutral NPC** the scenario author predeclares a **+2 situational modifier** to the request **only if Scene 2 actually produced a medical diagnosis**. That modifier is neither automatic Medicine-to-social Training nor magical influence. A successful GM-fixture roll means *this particular storekeeper* presents the public log; failure means *this specific request* is declined, with other approaches and consequences for a real GM to decide. The earlier detour does not magically change the NPC's interests; its actual consequence is the G4.2 elapsed time.

**Scholar/High optional utility:** first pay a separate known **Phantom Sound** (**20 Projection AP + 1 Spent VP**) for **one short nonverbal knock** at a visible unobstructed square. It sends only the chosen cue; **no guard is compelled to look away**, no spoken message is transmitted, and no persuasion, improved DC, automatic distraction or NPC cooperation is granted. The subsequent petition still needs its independent roll.

## One continuing mechanical record — important

Unlike PRs #185–#187's separate one-action snapshots, this three-scene script creates **one independently source-identified L1–3 G0 actor, one Encounter ledger, one DamageState/G4.2 WorldClock**, and passes the actual **Available/Spent VP** and clue/consequence state sequentially between scenes. Each sourced technique uses a real G4.2 `exploration → initiative → exploration` boundary, a genuine G0 `begin_round/start_turn/spend_ap/spend_vp/end_turn` and independently credited **world seconds only for the stated exploration tasks**. No invented six-second duration for a physical combat round or ghost regeneration is imposed. A normal core check consumes the **authored in-fiction exploration time** but *not* unsupported AP/VP.

All character access is checked against the #184 audited source through the prior `_character` adapter, and source magic access through the #187 `_authorized` check. Low Priest **A/B remains a physical-source proxy with no invented trained noncombat ranks**; a campaign-ready Low Priest must receive a complete reviewed sheet. The same character's VP cannot reset at each story scene, the one-shot Rapport bonus cannot secretly repeat, and the social +2 cannot appear without successful diagnosis. All scripted choices use deterministic supplied d20 faces; there is **no artificial win rate or actual NPC testimony**.

## Pilot handoff and acceptance limitations

These sample scenes are **usable GM prompts**, not a production-ready adventure book, a formal reputation/influence engine, a full combat+noncombat 3–5-session campaign, a certified twelve-Focus sheet or a human GM session. A real GM must select meaningful actual stakes, provide independent players' action/alternative declarations, confirm clue and NPC truthfulness, adapt partial failure without negating player agency, decide whether a roll is needed, keep source-paid AP/VP for other encounters, and document **two separate actually declared group-story milestone awards** if the campaign reaches L2 and L3. None is granted by this runner.
