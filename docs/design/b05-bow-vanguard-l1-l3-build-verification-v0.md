# B05/B07 — Bow Vanguard Level 1–3 Character Build and Verification v0

> **STRUCTURAL BUILD CANDIDATE — runnable L1→L2→L3 ledger and regression gates, NOT a new Archetype/Focus, independent 12-pairing MVP certification, complete encounter, Hunter CR or published balance verdict.** The Bow Vanguard is the [existing Restorative Vanguard A](b05-life-low-priest-l3-build-exaltation-verification-v0.md) re-equipped and trained for Bow instead of Sword. The [Phase 3 Hunter R witness](b06-hunter-tactical-and-party-diversity-v0.md) is the target numeric compatibility profile; its existing combat algorithm and verified 4,096-window outcomes are **unchanged**. No new bow subtype, Veil arrow, special ranged Talent, action discount or automatic technique access is introduced.

| Profile | Legal derived result | Boundary |

| Movement | **12 MP** | Legal occupied-square, threatened-movement and Opportunity procedures apply |
| Range | Bow **7–12 squares Short +0**; 1–6 Close −2, 13–30 Medium +2, 31–60 Long +5, 61–120 Extreme +8 | Range modifies the applicable ranged-defense difficulty, not the listed d20+4 Attack |

**Two-hand/hand boundary:** Attacking with the bow requires two functioning available hands. While the bow is actively wielded two-handed it supplies **no Guard**, and the dagger, shield or another item cannot simultaneously supply their wielded benefits. [Basic Heal](../rules/07-veil-magic/basic-heal-v0.md) requires **one actually functioning available hand**, while [Guided Regeneration](../rules/07-veil-magic/guided-regeneration-v0.md) requires no hand components. Only allow Basic Heal from a demonstrably eligible hand state; do **not** invent a zero-AP combat swap, immediate dagger Guard or free redraw. A normal drawing/readying another weapon has its authored General Tempo timing (24 AP) where applicable. The bow can be fired while threatened, but this provokes a pre-shot **Opportunity**, and a successful Opportunity Attack **interrupts that shot**; see [ranged combat](../rules/05-combat/off-hand-and-ranged-combat.md).

## 3. Derived character ledger (unbuffed, no damage or loot)

| Value | L1 | L2 | L3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Max HP | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| Max Vigor = 12 × Level | 12 | 24 | 36 |
| Max VP = 2 × Level (VA0) | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| Turn AP | 30 | 36 | 42 |
| Physical/Projection/General Action AP | 20 / 30 / 24 | 20 / 30 / 24 | 20 / 30 / 24 |
| Physical / Projection Tempo | 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 | 2 / 1 |
| Normal Guard capacity **if Guard instrument legal** | 1 | 2 (incl. TAL-C05) | 3 (incl. TAL-C05) |
| Ordinary bow shots on pure physical turn | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Ranged Evasion bonus when actively dodging | 0 | 0 | +1 |
| Passive own-turn regeneration throughput | 6 | 6 | 6 |
| If self Chosen Guided is actually maintained | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| Actual Prepared Prayers at Accord +1 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| Total selected Talents | 1 | 2 | 3 |

These are **ceilings/capacities, not free attacks or defenses**. Guard capacity does not override a bow's ineligibility. Turn AP is **one shared pool**: L3 can spend **20+20=40** on two ordinary bow shots or **20+20=40** on Aim followed by a legal shot, but **20+30=50 >42**, so no standard bow attack plus Projection Action on the same L3 turn. At L1–2 even Aim (20) plus firing (20) cannot fit into the turn; Aim must retain its usual legal future-shot duration. Reactive Aim (TAL-C07) is a possible later **alternative L3 Talent selection**, not part of this unchanged build or a bonus conferred by the bow.

### Conditional spell and VP examples

- **Chosen self-maintained Guided:** costs **30 Projection AP + 1 Committed VP** after the actual Chosen -1 commitment discount (ordinary maintained mode costs 2 Committed). Does not immediately heal or create a second Regeneration Step. With TAL-C02, throughput is **6 unbuffed, 7 while genuinely maintained**, always consuming recipient Vigor per HP restored or repair progress.
- **Basic Heal T1:** ordinarily **30 Projection AP + 2 Spent VP** and one functioning free hand at valid application. A full L1 VP2 character who already Committed 1 to Chosen Guided has **only 1 Available** and cannot then cast Basic Heal T1 without ending the host or legally gaining more Available VP. At L2/3 after the same commitment, a full pool leaves **3/5 Available**, respectively, so T1 can be funded if other conditions hold.
- **Woodland Rapport:** prepared from L2; its authored T1 costs **2 Spent VP +30 AP**. Its eligible T2 is available at L3 for **4 Spent VP +30 AP**, with animal/mass, visibility and disposition requirements; it is **not** combat mind control or a summoned animal.
- **Held Renewal at L3:** requires an actually active self Chosen Guided Rite and genuinely **unused throughput at the end of a later Regeneration Step**, with the property's separately paid **1 Spent VP**. Its up-to-2 pending healing later consumes real Vigor and expires under its exact rules. TAL-C10 likewise uses its real Reaction/VP/Vigor/missing-HP restrictions; neither effect is passive free HP.
- **Blink at L3:** known automatically but its ordinary **10-Spent-VP** cost exceeds the build's **Max VP 6** at VA0, even with an untouched pool. No free activation or technique slot.
- On a completed six-hour recovery at L3, normal **25% Max Vigor/VP recovery, half-up** corresponds to **9 Vigor and 2 VP**, subject to usual resource-state restrictions; no automatic resource refresh on leveling.
