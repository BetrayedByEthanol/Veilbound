# B05 — Final Eight-Pillar MVP Authoring Selection and Coverage Validation

> **DESIGN DECISION — 13 October 2026.** Select the **eight-Pillar roster for authoring and testing** in Veilbound's level-1–3 tabletop MVP: **Fire, Life, Earth, Liberty, Mind, Kinship, Secrets, Arts**. The selection is a production-scope decision, **not** evidence that eight complete catalogues, all 12 Archetype × Focus builds, resource costs or an encounter pack are already playable. Those are separate hard MVP exit gates. Reopen this scoped decision only for a demonstrable missing playtest function or a failed technique feasibility test; do not silently add a ninth Pillar.

## Sources and scope

- [Canonical 48-Aspect identities and categories](../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md) (PR #60, merged): **authority for each Aspect's domain**, not the superseded interpretations in the earlier audit.
- [48-Aspect compatibility/coverage audit](16-pillar-48-aspect-coverage-compatibility-audit.md): original 48 × 8 Gifted S/P/— matrix and sets A/B, retained as data; its authoring-role tags remain noncanonical.
- [Gifted Foundation](../rules/08-archetypes/gifted.md): exactly one emotion-linked Aspect in each of **eight distinct Pillars**; no External/Internal/Transform quota; Strong (S) and Plausible (P) equally powerful after legal creation.
- [Technique tiers and expression metadata](../rules/07-veil-magic/technique-tiers-and-expression.md): tier 1 at levels 1–2, tier 2 at levels 3–4; individual-technique [Projection]/Inward/[Embodiment], cadence, defense and Veil Control are **not inferred from category**.
- [MVP roadmap and exit criteria](veilbound-roadmap-and-mvp-readiness.md): 3–5 sessions, levels 1–3, **all 12 Archetype × Focus builds**, combat, exploration, social/investigation, enemies and progression; full crafting, advanced Scrying and flying combat are excluded by default.

**Working pilot assumption:** a short mixed adventure with at least one encounter requiring attacks and defense, one terrain/traversal challenge, one information and stealth challenge, and one social/cooperative interaction. The adventure itself is **not yet authored**, so scenario-specific mandatory Pillar needs remain unproven.

### Capability coverage — qualitative, not executable validation

- **PASS: distinct domain to author** means at least one canonical Aspect legitimately supports the stated playtest function. It does **not** mean a technique or numeric effect is implemented.
- **OPEN: executable proof** applies until a legal, resourced tier-1/2 effect and applicable Focus expressions pass the specific test.

| Level-1–3 MVP function | Canonical identity anchor(s) | Domain coverage | Executable proof |
|---|---|---|---|

| Defense / prevention | Earth Fortification; Life Preservation; Kinship Guardian; Liberty Autonomy | **PASS** | **OPEN** — stacking, Guard/Intervene resources, cadence |
| Injury recovery / wounds | Life Restoration | **PASS, single primary Pillar** | **OPEN, high-risk** — HP/Vigor/Repair Progress, dying, affordability |

| Information / investigation | Mind Insight; Life Nature (animals); Liberty Exploration | **PASS** | **OPEN** — bounded hints and uncertainty, not automatic truths |
| Social / morale / persuasion context | Arts Performance and Inspiration; Secrets Deception; Kinship Unity | **PASS** | **OPEN** — ordinary checks, target resistance, no forced consent |

**Risk note:** Compared with earlier role tags, the newly canonical Mind/Psionics **no longer means generic mind control**. The selected roster still tests hostile protected interior effects through **Mind/Memory**; Secrets/Hallucination can test either manifested illusion or protected-perception Intrusion, depending on its authored technique. **Arts/Inspiration is morale/creativity, not automatic HP/Vigor healing.** Similarly, Fire/Warmth is thermal support, not Restoration.

| Archetype | High Focus test | Medium Focus test | Low Focus test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Anointed** | One chosen bonded Aspect has valid 0-VP Signature, projection/Authority, Destiny limits. | Same bond supports appropriate investment/routing and Authority. | Same bond supports embodied physical play and Signature, without extra actions. | **OPEN** — Signature/Authority profiles missing |

**Baseline requirement:** demonstrate at least one complete, legally affordable character in **each of the 12 Archetype × Focus combinations** at level 1, then L1→L2→L3, with noncombat contribution and effective combat actions. These checks are **not passed** merely by this roster.

**Unrestricted roster requirement:** if *all eight Priest Pillars and all three Aspects per Pillar* are player-selectable in the first MVP, author enough executable tier-1/2 and Focus-compatible techniques to support **all 24 selected Aspects** and each permitted starting package. Do not assume eight sample spells suffice; valid Gifted links, Medium Conduit retuning and Low Embodiment can require additional expression variants.

**Technique acceptance fields:** minimum level/tier (tier 1 at L1–2; tier 2 at L3), action/Turn AP or Reaction cost, VP spent/committed, tags, source Aspect, targets/range, Intrusion/Imposition/Manifestation, defense, HP vs Vigor and Veil Control scaling where relevant, duration, Continuous/Rider/Pulse/Spend cadence, equipment/environment needs and failure modes. No unrestricted per-action full-strength Rider.

## Pilot regression tests and completion gates

1. **Gifted legality (PASS now):** independently recheck all 48 × 8 matrix cells; exemplar eight-S links; at least one P-linked eight-Pillar build without negative post-creation modifier.
2. **Direct physical damage and martial parity (OPEN):** Fire and Mind plus weapon attackers, correct Manifestation HP and standard-scaling Vigor handling; compare High/Medium/Low VP economy.
3. **Healing and Dying (OPEN):** Life/Restoration with HP, Vigor, Repair Progress, overkill, no HP/Vigor loop; Life/Preservation distinct from Endurance and delayed death.
4. **Defense and Interception (OPEN):** Earth/Fortification versus Kinship/Guardian; no free extra Guard, Reaction, reserved MP or dodge.
5. **Positioning and perception (OPEN):** Earth/Quake and Mind/Psionics pushes respect movement/hazards; Secrets/Shadow creates actual darkness, Hallucination apparent stimuli, Deception alters deceiver.
6. **Agency and intrusion (OPEN):** Liberty/Autonomy vs Mind/Memory alteration; hostile protected targets get the correct resistance; Arts/Performance is not mind control.
7. **Noncombat and communication (OPEN):** Mind/Insight provides bounded interpretive clues; telepathic messaging does not imply direct mental domination; Arts/Inspiration gives ideas/courage without free skills.
8. **Persistent effects (OPEN):** Kinship/Unity and Sacrifice, Arts performances/creations and other sustained expressions specify Pulse/Spend/finite budget for strong outputs.
9. **All 12 Archetype × Focus builds (OPEN):** spell access, starting choices, AP/VP affordability, level 1→3 advancement, High Projection, Medium Conduits/Investments, Low Embodiments and Anointed Signatures.
10. **MVP scenario and encounters (OPEN):** adventure content demonstrates each mandatory function and contains level-appropriate opponents; avoid hidden need for Alchemy/flight/corporeal necromancy.

## Scope and next steps

**Decision: selected eight-Pillar authoring roster = C, Fire / Life / Earth / Liberty / Mind / Kinship / Secrets / Arts.**

**Validation outcome: PASS** named-Aspect/domain completeness, eight-distinct-Pillar eligibility, Strong-only matching, conceptual coverage across intended pilot functions. **NOT YET VALIDATED** actual spells, affordability, four-Archetype/three-Focus access, balanced outcomes, NPC/encounters, and gameplay progression.

**Sequence:** (1) author a minimal **Fire + Mind + Life** combat/healing/control slice to exercise native delivery/resource rules; (2) add **Earth + Liberty** terrain, protection and traversal; (3) add **Kinship + Secrets + Arts** protection/social/perception; (4) complete all available tier-1/2 Aspect and Focus expression profiles, repertoire and signatures; (5) test twelve L1→L3 builds against a mixed 3–5 session pilot. If these tests reveal a missing essential domain, raise a specific change PR rather than claiming the roster is already playtested.

**No canonical 48-Aspect identities, Gifted matrix cells, Focus rules, technique mechanics or unrestricted crafting permissions are changed by this decision.**
