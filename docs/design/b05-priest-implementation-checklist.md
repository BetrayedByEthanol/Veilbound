## Status legend and current verdict

- **AUTHORED v0**: Canonical procedure or profile exists and can be executed if the character has legal access and resources. Its numeric balance may remain **PROVISIONAL**.
- **PARTIAL**: A defining Focus mechanic has at least one eligible execution path, but lacks enough alternative choices, level-3 expression, or practical validation.
- **MISSING**: Required technique/property/test has not been authored; a framework or candidate name does not supply it.
- **STRUCTURALLY VERIFIED (scripted)**: Complete legal sheet plus fixed-input L1→3 resource/action/Exaltation traces, as in **two merged PR #82 Life Low Priest variants**. This is a **test-evidence milestone**, not end-to-end Focus certification.
- **CERTIFIED (full MVP Focus)**: An independently reviewed end-to-end character/progression and genuinely opposed combat plus noncombat scene that meets the [graduation criteria](#p5--priest-verification-scenarios--gates). **No Priest Focus yet meets this gate; 0/12 global Archetype×Focus identities certified.**

**Priest state:** Priest starting counts/preparation AUTHORED v0; **two distinct canonical Medium Life Consecrations** (Guided Regeneration, Vital Shelter). The **universal self/equipped-item + Committed VP test (merged PR #80)** makes Guided Regeneration and Vital Shelter continued self-maintenance, **Earth Stone Skin** and **Fire Conjure Flame Reserved Weapon** qualifying Low Priest Rites when prepared and payable (one Active Rite at L1–3); ordinary Life/Nature Prayers (Gentle Growth, Woodland Rapport) and fixed-duration **Venom Endurance v0** remain non-Rites. **Mundane Toxins v0 is merged hazard canon**, not a Life technique or magical antidote. **Two legal Life Low Priest L1→3 variants have structural/ledger verification in merged PR #82; no Priest Focus has opposed/mixed-scene certification** (0/12 global). B04/B05/B07 remain open, and B06 enemy encounter content is next.
