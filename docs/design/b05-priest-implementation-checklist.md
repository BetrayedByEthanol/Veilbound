## Status legend and current verdict

- **AUTHORED v0**: Canonical procedure or profile exists and can be executed if the character has legal access and resources. Its numeric balance may remain **PROVISIONAL**.
- **PARTIAL**: A defining Focus mechanic has at least one eligible execution path, but lacks enough alternative choices, level-3 expression, or practical validation.
- **MISSING**: Required technique/property/test has not been authored; a framework or candidate name does not supply it.
- **VERIFIED**: A complete legal character sheet and relevant L1→L3 action/resource test have actually been demonstrated. **None of the three Priest Focuses currently meets this test.**

**Priest state:** Priest starting counts/preparation AUTHORED v0; **two distinct canonical Medium Life Consecrations** (Guided Regeneration, Vital Shelter). The **universal self/equipped-item + Committed VP test** in this review change makes Guided Regeneration and Vital Shelter continued self-maintenance, **Earth Stone Skin** and **Fire Conjure Flame Reserved Weapon** qualifying Low Priest Rites when prepared and payable (one Active Rite at L1–3); ordinary Life/Nature Prayers (Gentle Growth, Woodland Rapport) and fixed-duration **Venom Endurance v0** remain non-Rites. **Mundane Toxins v0 is merged hazard canon**, not a Life technique or magical antidote. **No Priest Focus has a certified L1→L3 build**; B04/B05/B07 remain open.
