# B06 — Encounter-Integration Readiness Audit v0

> **AUDIT / EXECUTION PLAN, not an encounter ruleset, new simulation or an MVP certificate.** Source baseline: `main` after merged PRs **#96 (Hunter Phase 3), #97 (Bow Vanguard R), and #98 (High Scholar F)** on 19 October 2026. Compare this audit against the [development roadmap](veilbound-roadmap-and-mvp-readiness.md). Nothing here changes an enemy's stats, a player's sheet, a spell, a response window, the frozen 4,096-window census or the Hunter's **`unrated`** status.

## 1. Decision and status vocabulary

**Decision: begin a small deterministic encounter-state engine, not more first-stop Monte Carlo, and do not attempt a full general-purpose GM or all twelve Focus identities at once.**

These labels apply to *integration readiness*, not numerical balance or final canonical approval:

- **READY (R):** an existing source actually defines the inputs/procedure and the limited test fixture can consume them without inventing a rule. This does **not** imply the full encounter implementation exists.
- **CONDITIONAL (C):** an authored procedure exists, but execution depends on scenario geometry, legal equipment state, action/response choices, timing or explicit GM policy that the current bounded scripts do not implement.
- **BLOCKED (B):** the demanded full-encounter result cannot be produced responsibly until a shared executor, missing scenario input or narrowly unresolved mechanic is addressed. A missing *implementation* is different from a missing *game rule*.
- **DEFERRED (D):** not needed to certify the first three narrow encounter packs; do not fill a gap with a silent assumption.

**Current scoreboard:** **3** executable enemy schema fixtures (Sentry, Soldier, Hunter), **four** structural character variants (A/B/R Priest/Low, F Scholar/High), **zero** fully certified B06 opponent encounter packs and **0/12** independently certified end-to-end Archetype×Focus identities. R/F have real, separately verified L1→3 sheets, but are **not** complete encounter agents. The Hunter remains **unrated**.

## 3. Critical blocking dependency: the 770 unresolved first-contact trials

The [Phase 3 runner](../../scripts/b06_hunter_tactical_diversity_v0.py) explicitly hardcodes an ambush-stage **Hunter head (0,0), rear (−1,0), front target (4,0)**. Its documented **only staged** melee entry is (1,0); a front F may step (4,0)→(3,0), pay **2 MP**, then cast within three squares; R is stationary in a separately assumed clear **7–12-square** lane. These are **staging contracts**, not a dynamic map.

At least four distinct *model stop mechanisms* exist; the 770 census aggregate does **not** supply a published per-reason breakdown:

1. **Nonfront melee body in straight corridor:** moving Hunter's 1×2 head/rear would illegally overlap the actor at (1,0); any alternative needs legitimate pivots, swept clearance, MP cost and possible Opportunity Response.
2. **Second melee actor attempts the same entry:** cannot occupy the first actor's square or reuse an invented free route.
3. **Front F attempts Thrown Flame through a newly occupied living screen:** cannot assume magical projectile passage; must apply exact LOS and Unified Firing Lane, if a legal shot still exists.
4. **Other fallback approach with no established contact route:** no arbitrary Hunter teleport, frontal retarget or automatic Bite.

**Do not assume 770 = 770 Hunter detours.** Attribution requires trace-level instrumentation, e.g. source trial's `contact_route`, `lane_disrupted_by`, `melee_corridor_occupant`, turn order and staged target, with exact seed, prior states and tile occupancy. A trial may terminate at the *first* of several potential problems.

### G1 minimal geometry/response fixture, not a generic pathfinder

Start with a **small explicit gridded map** and one rigid 1×2 Hunter. Each actor has stable world coordinates and current equipment/footprint state. A legal step must check destination, swept body, diagonal blocked corner, terrain per virtual footprint cell, ally surcharge, threat departure and remaining MP. A paid **90° Hunter pivot** is allowed during normal movement but **forbidden in fixed-heading Charge/Pounce**; a pivot does **not** meet Pounce's three translated-step prerequisite. Each entire body step generates at most one normal Movement Opportunity **per threatening creature per segment**, not per footprint cell.

Have the policy choose **a declared legal route or an explicit cannot-proceed terminal**. A shortest-path search is optional and must not silently choose GM tactics, substitute Cautious movement without policy approval, ignore reaction expense or force Hunter attacks into illegal positions.

**G1 acceptance traces, deterministic and inspectable:**
- clean three-step head-forward Pounce with full body/MP legality and exactly one Bite plus eligible post-Bite Balance;
- F's paid 2-MP precontact approach and legal one T1 Flame, with re-checked range and changed Hunter contact path;
- nonfront melee interruption of the direct lane; find and trace a genuinely legal alternate normal/Cautious route **or** explicitly retain unresolved under a classified, missing-scenario-geometry reason;
- multiple melee walkers cannot share the same square; actor may choose a different legal lane and risk consequences, without a free movement grant;
- Hunter pivots 90° using 3 MP in free clear normal terrain; pivot barred during Charge; sweeps/blocked corners reject illegal rotations;
- threatened departure creates correct pre-step Opportunity with one Reaction activation; a valid attack may leave the move legal or change its outcome, **not** automatic Interrupt;
- one living-screen ranged example, one fully blocked line (shot illegal), one cover-only hit allocation and one eligible projectile Guard;
- recorded Attack/Reaction/MP/VP/wound state still conserved when an Interception changes geometry mid-declaration.

**Parity discipline:** new G1 fixtures are new scenarios with new, separately named results. **Do not retrofit Phase 3's 770 into a guessed distribution** or overwrite its signed record. The old first-stop model stays as a frozen baseline; improved continuations must record an explicit before/after seed and route reason.

## 4. G0 reusable encounter state, event order and rules ownership

### Minimum deterministic state (not necessarily a stable published schema yet)

- **Scenario:** map/terrain/passability, start clock, grid coordinates, ordered actor IDs, roster+enemy fixture refs and versions, team, objective, stop policy, policy ID, RNG seed and keys.
- **Actor:** immutable source sheet/fixture reference; ability/Training and legal equipment slots; persistent max/current **HP**, **Vigor** and **Available/Committed/Spent VP** (sum = Max VP), chosen maintained hosts, active/expired effects, wounds and **Repair Progress**, toxins and milestones, posture, 1×1/1×2 indexed occupied squares/orientation, movement mode, used/remaining MP.
- **Turn/round:** initiative and tie-break, Turn AP, eligible physical/projection/general action price, Attack Sequence commitments and fixed target, Guard uses and real instrument, Dodge Pressure, one Reaction state/Opportunity Response capacity, atomic Interceptions, Blink AP Debt, per-own-turn Regeneration Step and end-of-turn effect deadlines.
- **Event log:** declaration→commit→precommitted Interceptions/nested windows→spontaneous reactions/opportunity→legality recheck→one attack/resistance/damage event→wounds/terminal→timed effects. Record selected defense *before rolls*, payment timing, dice keys, damage types and distinct HP/Vigor packets, profile identity and actual state delta.

**Conservation invariants:** no negative available AP, MP, ammunition, Vigor or VP states; **Available+Committed+Spent = Max VP**; restoration spends 1 Vigor per actual HP or Repair Progress; no duplicated damage or extra Attack Sequence after interruption; a full round refreshes capacities **once**; a multi-square creature has **one** HP pool and turn; separate timed toxin packets preserve their actual deadlines.

**GM-policy separation:** source rules are the legality validator, whereas reproducible attacker/defender choices are explicitly **policy inputs**. No rule grants ideal target selection, guaranteed stealth/ambush advantage, optimal movement, automatically maintained Stone Skin, or implicit healing just because the technique is known. For unverifiable or omitted policy, log `unresolved_decision` rather than manufacturing a game-law outcome.

## 7. Source-bound audit acceptance and known uncertainties

- **READY / sourced:** actor/creature baseline, shared action/timing, some defensive/movement rules, six F known spells and two legal exact `Self` composites, bow ammo, VP/damage/wound/toxin rules. All retain their original PROVISIONAL playtest numeric watches.
- **CONDITIONAL / source exists but not generic executable scene:** legal dynamic choices and responses, thrown vs bow geometric LOS, held gear, environmental paths, spell state, death/timed advancement.
- **BLOCKED / missing integration:** shared persistent event/actor state and dynamic 1×2 route execution; actual nested defensive response order; all 0-HP and above-zero wound continuation; complete long toxins/post-combat; win/loss/CR confidence.
- **OPEN rule/content boundary, not a reason to invent mechanics:** multi-square Grapple/mass, other footprint pivots, arbitrary collisions, unapproved spell pairings, general non-Focus NPC progression and unsupported called-shot specifics. First three narrow packs can avoid these if the GM policy explicitly excludes or records them; they cannot quietly treat these as solved.
- **Do not claim:** that 770 cases were fixed by prose, that three executable opponents are three complete encounters, that a valid level-3 character sheet proves all Twelve Focus identities, that Hunter 0HP represents confirmed kill, or that an unexecuted audit is experimental evidence.

**This audit is a dependency/acceptance document only.** No old Phase 3 source, enemy JSON, attack probabilities, damage/VP prices, signed census or gameplay rules need be changed by this PR.
