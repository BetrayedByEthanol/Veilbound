# B06 G1 — Native Movement, Footprint and Physical Contact Integration v0

> **Integration implementation and deterministic tests, not a new combat rule or completed encounter.** Implements the physical grid/contact portion of [B06 readiness audit](b06-encounter-integration-readiness-audit-v0.md#3-physical-contact-g1-the-geometry-obstacle). The canonical authority remains [Movement Foundation](../rules/05-combat/movement-foundation.md), [Multi-Square Creature Footprints](../rules/05-combat/multi-square-creature-footprints-v0.md), [Special Movement/Terrain](../rules/05-combat/special-movement-terrain-and-prone.md), [Rapid Advance/Charge](../rules/05-combat/rapid-advance-and-charge-v0.md), [Pounce](../rules/05-combat/hunter-pounce-and-prey-takedown-v0.md) and [Response windows](../rules/05-combat/initiative-and-round-structure.md). Numerical values and the Hunter's CR remain provisional/unrated.

## Integration surface

- Engine: [`scripts/b06_movement_v0.py`](../../scripts/b06_movement_v0.py), attached as `GridMovement(encounter, width=..., height=..., walls=..., terrain=...)` to the retained [G0 event/state engine](../../scripts/b06_encounter_state_v0.py).
- Tests: [`tests/test_b06_movement_v0.py`](../../tests/test_b06_movement_v0.py) contain the audit's **seven independently labeled acceptance traces**, plus mixed-terrain averages, cutoff/AP conservation, unused Movement Interception expiry and dedicated regressions for five Code Review findings (stale/new post-Interception Opportunities, rejected paid Bite routes, spoofed action labels, impossible start routes and pre-defense frontal retargeting, plus stale/callback-failed Bite response windows and dead-prey refusal). Run with `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v`.
- Explicit finite two-dimensional tiles with impassable walls and **Normal/Difficult/Severe** destination terrain. Grid coordinates are source-immutable anatomical indices mapped to current world coordinates. Unspecified obstacle maps, elevations, special traversal and inherent body compression are **not** inferred.
- Native **1×1 and straight rigid Hunter 1×2** footprints: one actor and one shared HP/Vigor/VP budget, head index1/rear index2 stable. Ordinary 8-neighbor translations validate entire body, diagonal blocked corners, all constituent destination terrain costs, use `ceil(sum(c_i)/N)` once, and add +2 MP **for each newly entered ally square**. Hostiles never overlap. A continuous two-step **ally transit** can be predeclared and atomically paid only where third-party response/interruption policy is absent; ambiguous interrupted transit remains OPEN rather than ending overlapped.
- Hunter's **90° pivots** anchor exactly one selected labeled cell; other cell moves diagonally, requires clear swept outside corner, and costs **3 MP + destination terrain surcharge**, or **6 MP cautious + destination surcharge**. 180° requires two separate legal pivots. Charge/Pounce disallow pivots. Other sizes have no generic pivot.
- Ordinary movement does not spend Turn AP, and G0 `spend_ap` now ends an actor's existing **movement segment** (except the explicitly linked AP commitment of the ongoing Hunter Pounce). Current segment's threatening enemies are recorded by actor, so a creature generates **at most one general Movement Opportunity per threatening enemy per segment**, regardless of the number of body cells. **Cautious** departure costs double the base MP, prevents this ordinary trigger, and never yields free reactive movement.
- Pre-step movement and pivot events use G0's **Interception-before-Reaction response window, with spontaneous eligibility collected after Interceptions and the first original-trigger recheck**. Callers may supply static eligible Opportunity choices (stale offers are dropped without cancelling a valid step), or a callable explicit post-Interception policy choosing only from the **then-current** threatening enemies. No enemy receives a forced Opportunity, and an ineligible response never consumes Reaction/Opportunity capacity. Ordinary movement does **not** automatically stop on a legal Opportunity Attack. **The first valid post-Interception Opportunity window records its then-current threatening sources for that movement segment when G0 commits spontaneous responses, before any response callback can Interrupt the step, move an actor, change MP, or throw an unresolved-effect error.** Even when that original movement attempt is later cancelled or cannot pay/reach its destination, its already-spent Reaction and Opportunity use cannot be duplicated by retrying in the **same** movement segment. If no response was chosen, the completed response window still consumes the one general Movement Opportunity per source/segment; an invalid choice rejected before commitment creates no phantom Opportunity. An actual segment break (own action or qualifying Movement Interception) starts a new source-eligibility window. A successful authored external effect can change geometry/mobility; destination, MP and consciousness are revalidated before committing the whole-body step.
- **Movement Interception** reserves a positive number of remaining MP on the reserver's own turn, creates a native precommitted G0 reservation, cannot trigger on that same turn, resolves one caller-selected route out of turn, and consumes at most that reserved MP (no Reaction). It breaks the active mover's segment **only after one real legal Interception step**. Unused MP is forfeited and expires at the reserver's next Start Expiry. Committed reservation does not magically include an Attack, Sprint or an Intervene effect. Unexpected nonmovement Interception needs its own later ability/effect resolver.
- **Hunter-local Pounce** is source-bound to head-forward cardinal 1×2 translation: its initially declared **living (not dead), standing one-square PC must already be reachable by a plausible, unblocked, straight frontal route requiring ≥3 whole-body steps and ≤24 actual terrain MP**; otherwise declare fails before changing AP, MP or action state. No prior own-turn MP expenditure; **24 total MP**, a **minimum of three completed translations** and the source-authored **18-MP Rapid cutoff**. The single **24 Turn AP** is committed only when first exceeding that cutoff, or just before its one Bite if attacking earlier. G1 verifies final frontal square and legal target and permits **one expressly chosen eligible replacement one-square PC already in the final frontal cell before AP/defense commitment**, retaining the declared heading/Bite profile. It assigns the **one** final defender's pre-roll defense, executes its real response window, and logs exactly **one Bite pending G5** and the **conditional post-Bite Balance rider pending G5**. A released Movement Interception with an invalid route forfeits unused MP and still completes the paid Bite legality recheck; it cannot strand the Pounce state. **After Bite AP/defense/sequence commitment, any rejected spontaneous choice or raised response callback records exactly one terminal `g1_pounce_contact_result` with indeterminate (not falsely cancelled) Bite/Balance outcomes, clears the active Pounce and high-speed MP, and re-raises the validation error.** The committed AP/defense/Reaction and sequence lock remain spent: no automatic retry or fabricated hit. Prevalidating all later responses before Bite declaration is deliberately not assumed valid because Interceptions can change which responses are relevant. No invented hit, toxin, forced Prone or completed Hunter victory follows. Aborting below cutoff logs an explicit no-attack policy and retains at most the remaining Rapid allowance **on the original fixed heading for the remainder of that turn**; voluntary pivots, Cautious detours, further MP reservations and a new Pounce are illegal until the next turn. Unrelated own actions cannot be taken during an unresolved Pounce; they become possible only after contact or explicit abort. Abort after AP commitment discards unused high-speed MP and does not refund AP.

## Seven deterministic acceptance traces

| Trace | Source-backed result | Not claimed |
|---|---|---|
| **01 Clean Pounce** | Three translated Hunter steps, 6 MP, frontal one-square contact, one committed Bite and **conditional** post-Bite Balance, 24 AP paid | A Bite hit, venom, contested Balance result or forced Prone |
| **02 F precontact** | F physically approaches 1 square for **2 MP**, ending 3 direct squares from Hunter head, with original VP/AP untouched | Authored Thrown Flame cast, lane, any VP spend or hit |
| **03 Nonfront obstruction** | Hostile soldier blocks a direct lane; caller supplies and pays an alternative physically legal diagonal/orthogonal route | Automatic optimal pathfinding or universal body grappling |
| **04 Multiple walkers** | Prevents illegal shared stopping square and requires a legal **continuous +2-MP allied transit** | Free overlap, squeezing or interrupted transit topology |
| **05 Hunter pivot** | Stable head/rear labels, **3-MP/6-MP** pivots, swept corner test and **no pivot during Pounce** | Arbitrary creature rotation or new size bonuses |
| **06 Threat departure** | One pre-step optional Opportunity per enemy/segment with correct Reaction/Capacity use, no invented automatic Interrupt; Cautious exclusion | Actual attack outcome |
| **07 Interception** | MP reservation released before spontaneous responses, real interceptor occupies intended destination, original movement cancelled before entry, preserved mover AP/MP/HP/Vigor/VP | Automatic defensive Intervene, a hit, or a legal path through a now-occupied square |

## Explicit G1 stop conditions

**Supported here:** G0 turn/resource integration; map initialization and grid passability; movement step/pivot cost; whole-body indexed occupancy, ordinary threatened departure; explicit MP interception; bounded Hunter Pounce geometry and compound-action **declaration**.

**Not yet executable:** all projectile lines, cover and ranged attacks (**G2**); spells, Blink execution and VP effect attribution (**G3**); actual injury, hazard and status application, mobility impairments/Prone state transition and complete Intervene (**G4**, future movement extension where required); Bite/Balance/damage dice and complete encounters (**G5–G6**); numerical CR (**G7**). Generic Rapid Advance/Charge/Sprint, full imposed terrain/exploration transitions, climbing, swimming, arbitrary 1×N pivots, nested conflicting reactive repositioning and fully-interrupted allied transit are **not silently treated as solved**; callers must supply a later legal executor or retain `UnresolvedDecision`.

**Historical parity:** No change to signed **4,096** Hunter Phase 3 windows or its **770** `contact_path_unresolved` stops. Seven trace results are **new, separate deterministic G1 scenario evidence**, not redistribution or purported repair of the frozen first-stop census. There are still **0 completed B06 encounters**, **0/12** certified full Archetype×Focus pairings, and Hunter remains **unrated**.
