## Native execution surface

- **Code:** `scripts/b06_ranged_v0.py` attaches `RangedCombat(encounter, cover=...)` after `GridMovement`. Source-Audited **R, Bow Vanguard L1–3** is the first executable weapon source: Bow must actually be wielded, uses the source's one Physical Action at **20 AP** per committed shot and the **one real physical arrow per launched shot** count. A Level-3 R can commit two consecutive bow profiles in one Attack Sequence (all attack profiles/defenses locked before the first die), not open a second sequence against the same target. No free reload, magical arrow, or bonus action.
- **Authoring the scene:** A G1 finite grid and coordinates are required. This **bounded, level, tile-center** ray traverses crossed tiles (and detects ambiguous solid-wall corner crossings). Full solid walls along the ray make a direct attack illegal before AP is spent. For every creature actually crossed as an intervening G1 footprint, an explicit **exposure source +2/+4/+6/+8** is required from the authored firing corridor; being adjacent/engaged is not enough. A 1×2 Hunter contributes **one creature source**, not two. To aim/shoot at a 1×2 Hunter, the actual stable head/rear **target cell** must be chosen explicitly and retained for an aimed shot; a conditional collision with its body calculates range from the **nearest interposed body cell**. No exposure is inferred from sprite size, allegiance or melee engagement.
- **Directional partial cover:** `CoverSurface(cell, difficulty, facing)` is an author-provided partially exposed obstruction along the ray, with a declared direction of incoming fire and source tier **2/4/6/8**. A barrier on a different side does not become universal cover. More than one overlapping physical cover surface, inaccessible wall-corner opening, non-tile-center silhouettes, multi-body shot origin without a named anatomical cell, an unknown screen exposure or other ambiguous geometry remains **`UnresolvedDecision`** rather than an invented percentage/penalty. Cover is a single source `C`, not the sum of pieces. Full physical blockage is **illegal** irrespective of Aim or natural 20.
- **Range and defense:** Ordinary R bow uses authored Close -2 at 1–6 squares, Short +0 at 7–12, Medium +2 at 13–30, Long +5 at 31–60 and Extreme +8 at 61–120. Source L3 R accuracy is `d20+4`. Static ranged Take Hit uses `10+Range+Obstruction`; Dodge uses capped Agility, Awareness, ranged Evasion = Physical Tempo−1, Range, Obstruction and **already accumulated** Dodge Pressure. Projectile Guard is legal **only for a ready eligible shield/buckler with remaining capacity**, uses existing `guard_value+Obstruction` (no Range addition), and consumes one normal Guard. An R's wielded **Bow cannot Guard**, even with Guard Reserve capacity. **All intended-target Guard/Dodge assignments and their resource costs are committed for the entire on-turn Attack Sequence before its first roll**, using a per-shot Dodge Pressure snapshot; each shot subsequently rechecks numeric defense against its actual post-Interception lane without paying that Guard/Dodge twice. A lost Guard instrument falls back to Dodge/Take Hit with the committed Guard charge retained. Active target and incidental Dodge/Guard require explicit awareness; no invisible threat earns a free active response. An unwielded R Dagger cannot threaten; a paid **24 AP General Action** changes readied R Bow/Dagger state.
- **Mixed obstructions:** Apply exactly one effective physical modifier `P=max(C,L_1,...,L_n)`; add `P` once to the selected target Defense. With an actual Living Screen, preallocate **failed natural faces 2–19** (natural 1 is an automatic ordinary miss; natural 20 enters G5 confirmation). Cover receives the highest failed faces up to residual `C`; physically ordered allied creatures occupy the lowest remaining faces up to their individual `L_i`, nearest ally first; enemy creatures occupy the highest remaining below cover, nearest enemy first. No overlapping faces. A nonaligned third-party screen's allied-side/enemy-side allocation must be explicitly declared by the GM before AP payment; G2 never silently treats neutral parties as hostile. Unlike **cover-only** shots, this allocation replaces the `B<A<=B+C` test even when physical Cover does not set `P`.
- **Cover-only:** When no effective Living Screen intervenes, the one primary roll resolves `A>D` intended hit, `B<A<=D` surface impact and `A<=B` normal miss/eligible Guard instrument (with natural 1 and 20 precedence). A mixed allocated **creature** face authorizes exactly **one conditional accuracy d20** against that unintended creature's own predeclared legal ranged defense, *never* a new attack/critical opportunity/AP/arrow or a chain into other screens. Its Guard/Dodge resources apply only if it is actually selected. Before committing a multi-shot sequence, preflight the maximum **possible** conditional Guard uses against each shared collateral creature's remaining capacity; do not promise two eligible Guard interceptions from one remaining Guard. A Guard becoming unusable after declaration but before an incidental face is selected follows the normal Dodge/Take Hit fallback without consuming a Guard use that never activated. Failed secondary accuracy never replaces original primary Attack total in any downstream projectile-area scatter. The original primary `max(0,B-A_primary)` is preserved as **scatter margin metadata only**; G3 owns actual Manifestation/area point scattering.
- **Responsiveness:** G0's standard ordered Interception → original attack legality recheck → spontaneous response → final legality stage applies. The G2 callback consumes correctly reserved **G1 Movement Interception MP** without a free Reaction, then rechecks the actual lane before projectile release. A bow fired **while genuinely threatened** offers normal source-authorized Opportunities; a hit supplied by an independently resolved attack **Interrupts this bow shot specifically**. An interruption, newly blocked firing line or loss of viable launch **after Attack AP has committed but before arrow release** loses the shot and AP **without consuming an unfired arrow**. An attack whose projectile really launches debits exactly one arrow even if it misses, strikes cover, or is stopped by a Guard.
- **Aim:** One named observed target, Bow attack profile and one named difficulty source (Range, directional Cover, or a particular actually exposed Living Screen) costs **one additional R Physical Action (20 AP)**. The one-source reduction is `min(4,max(0,applicable_source))`: never a universal +4 or immunity to collateral, full blockage or independent modifiers. Aim at declaration additionally requires a **currently positive, applicable source**; Aim cannot be prepared against nonexistent Range or Cover difficulty. Moving the shooter, changing the ready weapon, firing an aimed shot, or the start of the **second subsequent own turn** ends that preparation. The Aim expires **when its shot begins**, including an Interrupted shot; an unaimed regular shot does not inherit or spend it.
- **F High Scholar Thrown Flame:** `preview_thrown_flame` validates the independent physical projectile's **1–3-square** legal path and its explicit **+0 Range Difficulty**; it rejects a blocked path or unknown Living Screen. This is **only a geometry preview**. It spends **no VP or projection AP**, launches no spell and never applies Fire HP/Vigor, Composite casting or a ranged Opportunity trigger. The full F magic executor, legal access, timing and effects are **G3**, not inferred from G2.
