# B06 G3 — Projected Actions, Spell State, VP and Maintained Effects v0

> **Bounded, source-backed execution gate — not a new ruleset, new spells, new VP costs, global caster interpreter, G4 wound adjudicator, G5 full encounter or Hunter CR.** The canonical rules remain [Scholar](../rules/08-archetypes/scholar.md), [Priest](../rules/08-archetypes/priest.md), [Conjure Flame](../rules/07-veil-magic/conjure-flame-v0.md), [Stone Skin](../rules/07-veil-magic/stone-skin-v0.md), [Basic Heal](../rules/07-veil-magic/basic-heal-v0.md), [Vital Shelter](../rules/07-veil-magic/vital-shelter-v0.md), [Guided Regeneration](../rules/07-veil-magic/guided-regeneration-v0.md), [VP states](../rules/07-veil-magic/volition-points.md), and [G0–G2](b06-encounter-integration-readiness-audit-v0.md). No historical first-stop result is rewritten.

## Source, spell and state boundaries

- **Executor:** `scripts/b06_spells_v0.py`, `SpellState(encounter)` attaches after existing native G0 actors and optional G1/G2 physical geometry. It uses unchanged source-bound VP ledgers; an `SpellEffect` records its identity, caster, recipient, committed reservation, parent/child dependency, remaining prevention budget and meaningful expiry. It never creates a VP pool or refreshes spent VP. **Available + Committed + Spent = Max VP** after every legal transition. An effect whose host/channel ends returns only its remaining **Committed**, not its initial undiscounted base or prior **Spent**.
- **High Scholar Fire F (L1–3):** The exact B05 known list (Conjure Flame, Stone Skin, Basic Heal, Minor Telekinesis, Phantom Sound, plus Vital Shelter at L2) is checked through its authored `profile`. The two and only two selected ordered `Self` Composite Formulae are **Stone Skin Primary + Basic Heal Secondary** and **Stone Skin Primary + Vital Shelter Secondary**. L1 has only the first prepared; L2 both; L3 **Formula Fluency** removes their preparation requirement, not knowledge or compatibility. Reversing roles, attaching Conjure Flame to an unrelated `Thrown Projectile`, reusing a Rider, or reactive Composite Casting is not authorized. There is no second Stone Skin instance.
- **Composite Heal:** one normal High **20 AP Projection Action**, Stone Skin **ceil(MaxVP/3) Committed**, and Basic Heal **2×T Spent**. The independent Heal Used Tier is T1 at L1/2, at most T2 at L3. A functioning available hand and explicit legal d6 are required. On a successful `Self` application the one-time **Secondary** restores up to **1 HP + (2+ceil(d6×T/6)+VC) Vigor** (VC0 in F), constrained by actual missing amounts, never recipient Vigor/Regeneration-throughput debit. **Dying/incomplete wound repair remains G4**, and such application fails explicitly here rather than silently restoring blocked HP.
- **Composite Shelter:** one normal High **20 AP Projection Action**, Stone Skin **ceil(MaxVP/3) Committed** + subordinate Vital Shelter **2 Committed + 1 Spent**. Snapshot finite **S4** prevention at VC0, not the independent Primary **B8**. The **one-time** budget prevents only *delivered direct Vigor packets* while the living recipient has positive current Vigor; HP/Armor/typed resistances and damage application remain independent. It does not spend budget on regeneration Vigor or create Vigor. Depletion releases only its 2 Committed VP; the Stone Skin host remains. Ending or losing host consciousness ends child too; reserve releases but the 1 Spent never refunds. Stone Skin resistance R2 is typed and highest-only with other eligible resistance (never Armor and never direct Vigor).
- **Standalone Stone Skin and authorized Reaction:** F knows normal standalone Stone Skin at one High **20-AP** Projection Action and **ceil(MaxVP/3) Committed VP**, with no stack. The separately authored Reaction may resolve only before a **perceived** incoming attack/hazard; the choice is prevalidated, passed through the real G0 Interception-then-Reaction window, and G3 verifies that G0 actually committed the exact labeled Reaction on that trigger. The reaction is **not transferable**: preparation stores one source/GM-validated claim bound to that exact original trigger instance and issues a unique response label, which is consumed once after an authentic G0 Reaction commitment. A manually constructed generic Response, a response intended for another trigger, or a clone with the same trigger key cannot activate Stone Skin. **The preflight claim and historical Reaction ledger record do not suffice after the response window ends**: G0 explicitly scopes live authority to the exact matching `on_spontaneous` callback, with restoration after nested response windows and guaranteed invalidation on exceptions. A deferred response cannot grant retroactive pre-hit protection. G0 commits all spontaneous capacities before invoking any callback, so if an earlier initiative-ordered response incapacitates this caster, their already-paid Reaction is consumed and the exact preflight claim ends with a **zero-VP interrupted reaction**; it does not raise mid-window, charge Committed VP or block final trigger recording. It costs **0 immediate Turn AP and no Blink AP Debt**, grants no reactive Composite Heal/Shelter and cannot be retroactively chosen after an earlier Interception cancels the threat.
- **Standalone Basic Heal:** F's learned and Low Priest R's actually prepared Primary Self Basic Heal each cost one ordinary **20/30-AP** Projection Action and **2×T Spent VP**, require a genuinely free functioning hand and restore **up to 2 HP + 4+ceil(d8×T/3) Vigor** once. G4 owns incomplete wound repair, Dying conversion, non-self delivery and resisted Intrusion.
- **Thrown Flame:** F uses a real authored **1–3 square G2 physical projectile lane with +0 range difficulty**, **20 AP** plus **2×T Spent VP** at successful release, d20+5 from Throwing (not Training/Veil Control), with explicit primary d20/d8. On direct qualifying hit the executor emits **separate pending packets**: Fire HP **2**, and Vigor **2+ceil(d8×T/3)**; target's HP/Vigor are *not directly changed* in G3. A legitimate miss/cover impact/Guard has no normal hit packets, though VP remains Spent. Critical 20 is **pending G5 confirmation**, not a forged full hit or extra Vigor critical. Illegal solid-wall line/tier/hand/access/VP fails before AP; post-action newly blocked lane loses AP but spends **no VP** and creates **no projectile**. Source-exposed Living Screens, area scatter, explicit Spontaneous/Interception opportunities and precise opposed damage continuation cannot be implemented by treating a magical projectile as a Bow arrow. The bounded G3 executor **refuses** these unresolved interactions rather than simulate free safe casting, especially if F is currently threatened. It uses G2's actual **+2 Prone Position Defense at 3+ squares** for static and explicitly feasible Dodge defenses and requires G2's **independent/absorbed** position policy where authored Cover already measures the same prone silhouette. Before charging **Spent VP** after committed response effects, G3 rechecks the final legal projectile defense and G2 position: a newly incapacitated Dodger or newly unusable Shield Guard **cancels before launch** and loses committed AP but **not** spell VP or target defense capacity. No phantom projectile is logged. G2's separately verified full Bow/Living Screen algorithm remains intact.
- **Low Life Priest R:** R's actual `Guided Regeneration` Prepared Prayer supports maintained `Self` Chosen Rite (**1 Committed VP**, normal **2 Committed non-Chosen**) and separately **fixed 2 Spent VP**. Both pay a Low **30 AP Projection Action**. Only a *specifically declared successful Chosen instance* receives the −1 reservation; fixed mode is not a Rite and never consumes the Chosen slot. It adds **+1** throughput only to the beneficiary's **real existing G0 Regeneration Step**, with ordinary recipient Vigor debits and no extra Step; Round Begin R+10 expires the fixed instance. Low A/B remain G0 **physical proxies** with no independently loaded Prepared Prayer sheets; this deliberately refuses invented source spells. At L3, G3 verifies only **EX-LR-01 Held Renewal** host eligibility on an actual *Chosen, self-maintained Guided Regeneration* Rite; non-Chosen, fixed and lower-level hosts fail. The R sheet cannot legally anchor **EX-LP-01 Vital Interposition** without a separately sourced finite Chosen self Vital Shelter host. Neither property is armed and neither spends its 1 VP without the genuine G4 timing/payout procedure. The separate L3 Exaltation Held Renewal and Vital Interposition event triggers, comprehensive other Priest spellbooks and long duration conversions are further G3/G4 integration, **not** automatically granted merely by Rite qualification.
- **Firebound Sentry EN-01:** Exact **cold_start** and **prepared_start** states are loaded without rebuilding its VP. Cold actual sword-wielding **Chosen Conjure Flame Reserved Weapon** pays Low **Projection30 AP** and commits **3 VP**, leaving **12 AP**, insufficient for same-turn **Physical20** sword attack. Prepared starts with exactly **3 Available / 3 Committed / 0 Spent** and the existing source ID `B06-FIRE-RESERVE-01` already attached; no double payment. Enchantment permits only the existing enhanced sword attack profile **B06-AT-02** while the sword remains wielded. Disarm/drop/sheathe/stow requires a **separately legitimate equipment state event** and immediately ends that source effect, returning precisely **3 Committed**; re-wielding alone does not recreate it. Actual sword physical/Fire/Vigor attack outcome stays G5.
- **Event timing:** The caller's optional `on_committed` response hook represents independently resolved responses after AP is paid but before spell application. Successful magical application must still be affordable/valid. If invalidated, AP remains paid, **no Committed or Spent VP** is charged before magical application. Existing G0 response windows remain separate; this hook is *not* a universal permission for an NPC/GM to force a Reaction or guarantee spell success. Voluntary maintained release occurs **only at actual own-turn legal decision points**; no public bypass flag can end it from an incoming packet, spontaneous response or other actor's turn. Channel break, exhaustion, expiration, unconsciousness and death use dedicated forced termination paths, not a pretend voluntary action. Each ledger event uses a unique **event-kind** field distinct from spell/effect-kind details, preventing post-VP commit logging exceptions or partial effects. `sync_consciousness` must be called immediately by the future G4 damage executor when that event actually changes consciousness/death, and ends Stone Skin but not still-valid maintained Guided Regeneration merely on unconsciousness. G0 Round Begin calls G3 expiry processing. G3 never schedules free extra Guard/Dodge, critical, wound repair, six-hour recovery or area ignition.
