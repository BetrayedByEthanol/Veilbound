> **Audit and execution plan, not a rules amendment, implementation, or G4 certificate.** Reviewed against `main` on 20 October 2026 after merged **PR #104**. Canonical authority remains in [HP/Vigor](../rules/06-vigor-wounds-death/hp-and-vigor.md), [Dying/Wounds](../rules/06-vigor-wounds-death/dying-and-wounds.md), [Mundane Toxins](../rules/05-combat/mundane-toxins-v0.md), [Hunter Bite/Venom](../rules/05-combat/hunter-bite-venom-and-horn-v0.md), [Venom Endurance](../rules/07-veil-magic/venom-endurance-v0.md), [Basic Heal](../rules/07-veil-magic/basic-heal-v0.md), [Physical Hazards](../rules/05-combat/physical-hazards-v0.md), [initiative timing](../rules/05-combat/initiative-and-round-structure.md), and [VP](../rules/07-veil-magic/volition-points.md). Do not alter authored mechanics to make tests pass.

## Decision and terminology

**Verdict: G4 is a bounded executable substrate, not a completed gate.** It can support the core injury portion of the first narrow Soldier/Sentry pack, provided unavailable rules and runtime paths are explicitly excluded or recorded `unresolved_decision`. It cannot yet certify the full G4 acceptance gate or Hunter pursuit.

- **DONE / verified bounded primitive:** source and regression tests implement the stated limited procedure.
- **PARTIAL / integration gap:** canonical procedure exists, but the implementation supports only a subset.
- **PARITY / correctness follow-up:** canonical formula or order differs from existing executor; fix without changing the rule.
- **DEPENDENT:** requires G5/G6 hit qualification, scene policies or actual chronological events rather than new G4 rules.
- **OPEN rule:** canonical authoring intentionally defers a case; do **not** fill by implicit game logic.
- **DEFERRED for I1/I2:** not required on specifically restricted starter scenes; becomes mandatory if the scene uses it.

The previous [encounter-readiness audit](b06-encounter-integration-readiness-audit-v0.md#6-incremental-delivery-gates-and-stopgo-criteria) says G4 must replay the **22** historically unresolved Phase 3 wound stops in **new** legitimate traces. It also requires real HP repair, wound repair, survival cancellation with **1 throughput + 1 Vigor but no HP/repair**, a zero-Vigor control, and no free healing regeneration. **Those 22 are not replayed** merely because focused unit tests pass.

## B. Source rules that must be preserved, not generalized

1. **A wound is not an automatic property of every hit.** Trigger on independently qualified Called Shot, Devastating Critical, or first positive→nonpositive damaging event. Already-Dying later ordinary damage is not another 0-HP wound. An exceptional actual injury needs its own authority. A falling impact is not a weapon critical.
2. **Dying is not death.** All incomplete repairable and unreconstructed Irreparable wounds block natural HP restoration. Natural Dying repair visits oldest unfinished first, whereas non-Dying allocation is player-chosen. Basic Heal chooses one wound and does **not** spill extra repair to another.
3. **TX-HM is not a universal toxin engine.** Full five and Reduced three one-HP future ticks are source constants. Printed-grade nonstacking and a Ward can prevent **acceptance**; treatment/suppression may stop **future effects** but never retroactively refund damage.
4. **Hunter poison remains one delayed, continuous impairment.** New accepted Bite at T=0 has no immediate penalty. Full applies M−2/Strength-exertion−1 at +10m; *replaces* with M−4/Strength-exertion−2 at +30m; ends +120m. Reduced has stage 1 at +10m and expires +60m. Neither mode deals toxin HP/Vigor or alters generic Attack, AP, Guard or all Strength checks.
5. **Clocks never create turns.** Short toxin-local phases retain `0..5` seconds; actual target Step 2 precedes Step 3, with no extra regeneration. Long toxin and Hunter milestones use source-specific *conservative* Round Begin mapping, not an asserted global six-second initiative round. The six-hour recovery phase exists independently of toxin exposure.
6. **Venom Endurance is not permanent immunity.** An eligible Preventive Ward stops the first genuine nonignored application before the natural save without undoing Bite damage; Active Suppression spends finite units only against specified future packets/penalties, with its own protected six-second-equivalent cadence. Neither mode cures or resets the toxin.
7. **Resisted healing is not free and is not prohibited by default.** A valid application costs VP even if an ensuing +8 Intrusion resistance defeats it; unavailable contest rolls/policies must remain unresolved. Self and Composite use the same authored `+VC` Vigor rule. A prohibition on external Vigor does not automatically prohibit separately authored HP/Repair Progress.

## E. Gate and MVP statement

- **Ready now:** G0–G4 foundation plus bounded source-verified damage/regen/healing for carefully specified initial I1/I2 cases.
- **Not yet G4-certified:** outstanding original 22 wound-stop continuation, chronological long clock and toxin-state completeness, and listed healing/source parity; G4 completion claim requires reviewed passing fixture evidence, not simply a green old CI.
- **Not an automatic global blocker:** extraordinary trauma effect tables, generalized Medicine/bleeding, Called Shot pricing, arbitrary collision physics, all eleven toxins in every starter scene, generic other-size Grapple, unrelated Focus spells. Keep those as **OPEN/DEFERRED** when an I1/I2 or I3 scene does not require them.
- **Still unchanged:** **0** fully certified B06 encounters, **0/12** independently certified end-to-end Archetype×Focus identities, Hunter **`unrated`**, **4,096** frozen Phase 3 one-round trials (2,673 Hunter-first 0 HP; 0 front-first; 22 wound; 770 contact; 631 completed round), checksum `c02c9830d10f1bcaf7d80ccedfe81102c1d4d9b28f4f004e9f715560b184ca5b`.
