## Deliberately unresolved, not silently granted

- **Resisted/refused Basic Heal Intrusion**: +8 barrier, real source/target contest and VP payment on an otherwise valid attempted application remain G5+ policy/integration work. G4 still rejects `unopposed=False`; no free success, immunity, natural-20 bypass or phantom die rolls are introduced.
- **Authenticating a dynamic restriction**: G4.1 does not invent a universal healing-condition schema or automatic scan for hidden wards. The caller must provide **authoritative live restoration policy** whenever committed responses may occur, even if there were no blocks at declaration. The policy must report current blocked components after responses; otherwise the cast is unresolved before AP. The tests' `Actor.conditions` labels are **explicit test-only policy witnesses**, not a universal ward registry or evidence of an authored new ward. In G3-only scenes, a dynamic ward active at declaration may be removed by a committed response; passing the second live check is what makes the otherwise valid direct Heal legal. In contrast, a static declared block cannot be removed by simply altering the dynamic test state.
- **Exceptional injuries**: Called Shot AP/location qualification, Catastrophic or physically Irreparable/Fatal structures, bleeding, medical stabilization, severed anatomy and independent restoration suppression are **not** generated by the default 10/20/30 wound overkill band. Actual blocked wound repairs can still preserve Dying.
- **Full G4 chronology**: TX-HM is a narrow short-clock primitive. General toxin gating, long TX-NP/EN/HE clocks, Hunter's delayed T+10/30/60/120 and Venom Endurance, and replays of the frozen **22** wound-first-stops remain pending under G4.2–G4.5. No generic time, extra natural regeneration or world recovery cadence has been created.
- **Unchanged baseline**: the 4,096 verified Hunter Phase 3 one-round trial census, full-pack certification **0**, end-to-end Focus certificate count **0/12**, Hunter challenge rating **unrated**.
