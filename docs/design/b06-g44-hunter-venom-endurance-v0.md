## Hunter's Lingering Venom — exact source

The [G4.4 executor](../../scripts/b06_hunter_venom_endurance_v0.py) accepts Hunter `B06-TX-HUNTER-SLOW-MY` **only** from an actual native G4 `g4_profile_hit` ledger event with `B06-EN-03` as attacker, the same target, and actual `B06-AT-04` Bite or `B06-AT-06` Pounce Bite profile. A given hit ledger index and exposure key can be processed **once**; even **not-delivered, ignored and ward-prevented Hunter exposure IDs** are reserved in the shared G4.3 exposure ledger and cannot later be reused by generic toxin application. **The source-backed application must consume the most recent encounter ledger event synchronously while it is still the verified Bite/Pounce Bite hit.** Any intervening logged action, Round Begin, or exploration-time progression makes the historical hit ineligible: its original Ward/nonstack state and T+0 deadlines cannot be reconstructed by scheduling new milestones from a later application time. `B06-AT-05` Horn Bash, `B06-AT-07` Horn Drive, a claimed hit, an avoided attack or another creature's bite never creates this toxin.

The post-hit result's `injection_eligible` must reflect **surviving real Piercing HP after the actual effective Armor/Penetration and separately authored event-wide prevention**, or the GM must independently attest actual canine-secretions delivery through a pre-existing physical breach. Mere nonpiercing HP, Vigor loss, visual contact, or normal unconfirmed damage does not qualify. The physical Bite damage already applied to the source G4 event is **not reversed** by an ignored/prevented poison application, and neither poison acceptance nor a failed Pounce Balance creates a second bite.

The Hunter profile is a **Moderate DC12** member of the existing *one active mundane toxin* slot. Source-backed target Vitality uses uppercase player/lowercase JSON enemy key. One valid resistance roll selects Full or Reduced; equal-grade repeat injection is ignored **before** any Ward or new resistance. Hunter's pending milestones are source-owned **members of the same record**, not short toxin ticks:

| Resolution | Exact source schedule | Penalties |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** | Exposure T+0; **T+600s onset**, **T+1800s escalation**, **T+7200s expiry** | Silent before onset; stage 1 **−2 normal M / −1 muscular Strength checks/contests**; stage 2 *replaces*, not adds to, stage 1 with **−4 M / −2 muscular Strength**; clean expiry. |
| **Reduced** | Exposure T+0; **T+600s onset**, **T+3600s expiry** | Silent before onset; stage 1 **−2 M / −1 muscular Strength** only; no T+30m escalation. |

No direct toxin HP/Vigor packet, additional Dying loss, paralysis, wounds, rerolls, free turns, Movement AP loss, Attack/Guard/Dodge penalty, or altered source Strength is introduced. G0 reads the established, currently effective ordinary MP impairment at real turn allocation. A stage change while the target is moving never adds a per-tile penalty or refunds prior spent MP; named muscular Strength physical contests must query the **exact** G4.3 modifier, not alter all weapon attacks.

At exposure during initiative round R the distinct milestones map to **R+100/300/1200**, or **R+100/600** for Reduced. Entering initiative from exploration maps each remaining `s` to **R+floor(s/6)**, including immediate `q=0` resolution; leaving uses **`6×max(0,E−C−1)`** from the current next-active exit label. Ordinary G4.2 milestones and genuine Recovery preserve their original chronological order if conversion coalesces deadlines, **except a fixed Venom Endurance expiry marked `before_toxin` takes precedence over toxin pulses at that same Round Begin even when the expiry's original second was later**. Exploration still uses actual due-second order; no early expiration away from a coalesced initiative boundary. Finished stage events are not replayed; the underlying instance is never recreated or shifted to the victim's next Step 3. Suppression does not move these dates.

## Venom Endurance — fixed Primary spell

The same executor supports two separately chosen profiles (`Self` or `Single Target`) and two **non-switchable** modes:

**Preventive Ward:** A successful effect carries exactly **one** intercept until spent or its fixed 60s / R+10 expiry. It can intercept a *new, actually delivered and non-ignored* Minor/Moderate mundane toxin, including the Hunter's named venom, **before** the toxin's Vitality roll. A failed puncture, blocked attack, equal/lower printed grade candidate or non-toxin never spends Ward. An intercepted higher-grade application leaves the earlier live toxin intact and consumes no victim resistance roll.

**Active Suppression:** Binds one already-active eligible toxin instance, if any, with a one-time `min(5,3+floor(max(0,raw VC)/2))` budget: VC0–1 = 3, VC2–3 = 4, VC4+ = 5. The spell does not diagnose an absent toxin: legal attempts without useful remaining consequences **still pay ordinary action + VP**, but create no functional protection. Fixed-duration expiry or actual toxin expiry/cure/replacement ends the bound protection without refund.

Each actual discrete eligible toxin HP/Vigor packet consumes one unit if there would be damage; TX-NP at current Vigor0 consumes none. **Legacy G4 `schedule_hemotoxin` TX-HM is still supported**: when there is no active unified toxin, Active Suppression binds its existing `DamageState.short_toxins` recipient/key and intercepts each actual Step 3 or exploration HP tick directly, spending one unit per prevented packet. Its legacy `left`, `phase`, `applied` and own source IDs are not migrated or duplicated. Depletion resumes subsequent legacy packets normally; completion of the legacy toxin ends an otherwise funded bound suppression without refund. TX-HE consumes one only at its pre-existing genuine six-hour Vigor Recovery boundary, never creates a new checkpoint, and leaves Spent VP Recovery untouched. A continuous short profile spends one unit immediately for its current toxin-local interval, then at each completed genuine Step 3 or six exploration seconds as long as the source penalty and budget remain; a paid interval suppresses **all** eligible checks/movement, not one check. Delayed TX-NC consumes nothing during its first two harmless intervals. TX-CY's harmless three-interval residual marker has no future suppressible consequence.

For **TX-EN and Hunter's long continuous staged impairment**, Active Suppression has a separate exact **six-second-equivalent local protection interval**. If penalty is already active, one unit purchases the current interval immediately. Hunter's silent T+0…10m onset initially uses **0 units**; if Stage 1 arrives during the fixed spell's lifetime, one unit purchases the first local interval *before* the stage becomes effective. At each completed six exploration seconds, or at the next genuine affected-recipient Step 3 after protection began, that interval ends and one new unit is automatically bought if both spell and toxin remain effective. A Hunter stage 2 transition inside a paid interval changes the **underlying** magnitude but uses **no additional unit**, and the stronger penalty resumes when the final protected interval ends. Partial elapsed exploration seconds survive mode changes. **When a genuine recipient Step 3 closes an already paid long suppression-local interval, its existing 1–5 elapsed exploration-second remainder carries into the newly purchased interval; Step 3 spends a unit but adds zero world seconds.** Thus 5 exploration seconds + one genuine Step 3 leaves **1** exploration second until the next interval boundary, not 6. A fully completed six-second **exploration** interval instead starts its successor at phase 0. Depletion resumes the original impairment on the correct next boundary; neither interval transition freezes, delays, resets, or cures the original toxin.

### Source, cost, Intrusion and delivery boundary

Because current G0 A/B Low Priest fixtures are **not proof that Venom Endurance was actually prepared**, G4.4's spell casting port requires an **explicit independent source authorization** and **identified access reference**; it grants no new repertoire to any fixture. The cast is **own-turn only**, one legal source Focus Projection Action (High20 / Medium24 / Low30), or an independently authorized High Priest Open Invocation Full Action (never a free Reaction), and **2 Spent VP**, optionally locked at 1 by an externally attested eligible cost discount with an explicit consumed-use callback. Open Invocation adds an independent non-discountable **+1 Spent VP**. Both AP and Available VP, **sourced raw VC for suppression**, and static access/discount gates are checked **before commitment**; available VP/capability/range and caster/recipient exclusivity are rechecked after the committed response callback: interruption retains AP but spends zero VP. Valid application spends locked VP even on resisted or useless protection. Beneficial unopposed recipient needs no Intrusion roll; a consciously refusing recipient requires an independently attested **+8 barrier** contest, with tie resisting, rather than guessed attributes or automatic success. Contested invalid inputs reject before AP spend.

Nonself `Single Target` uses the exact within-3 square-equivalent source reference-distance metric, visible and unobstructed open-air line asserted at declaration and checked again at application. **Range uses each actor's stable, body-local reference cell ID—not `min(occupied)` or a post-pivot coordinate sort**. The Hunter's JSON declares `footprint.reference_cell_id=head`; its head remains index 0 across lawful pivots, even if the rear acquires the lexicographically smaller coordinate. For legacy actors **without** an authored reference, the fallback lowest-x/y/z occupied square is converted **once at initial validation** into a retained body-local ID, and subsequent moves reuse that identity. Unknown reference IDs fail validation rather than authorizing a new point. When real map/path source adjudication is complex, a live independently authored `delivery_check` is the caller's responsibility; no fictitious wall-passing permission is created. No fixed spell is canceled merely because the caster becomes unconscious later. **The protected recipient's actual death ends Ward or Suppression immediately** from the G4 damage or native Step 2 Dying death transition, cancels the fixed-expiry marker, and releases caster/recipient exclusivity without waiting for a later toxin query. A caster with remaining AP/VP still needs a new legal own-turn casting to protect a different living target; spent VP is never refunded. No maintained, Composite Secondary, Rite, Consecration, free detox, invented generic poison immunity or automatic talent discount is granted. Caster/recipient exclusivity prevents budget stacking or a no-cost reset.

**Acceptance tests:** The exact-head regression suite also includes **Hunter head-versus-rear range reversals (both accepted and rejected), a post-commit pivot changing range, and stable one-time legacy-reference fallback**, plus **rejection of stale Bite ledger events after exploration, Round Begin or another same-time ledger action**, and **immediate Endurance cancellation on both independent lethal G4 damage and actual Dying Step 2 death**, in addition to the original G4.4 cases.
