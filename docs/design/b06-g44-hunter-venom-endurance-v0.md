## Hunter's Lingering Venom — exact source

The [G4.4 executor](../../scripts/b06_hunter_venom_endurance_v0.py) accepts Hunter `B06-TX-HUNTER-SLOW-MY` **only** from an actual native G4 `g4_profile_hit` ledger event with `B06-EN-03` as attacker, the same target, and actual `B06-AT-04` Bite or `B06-AT-06` Pounce Bite profile. A given hit ledger index and exposure key can be processed **once**. `B06-AT-05` Horn Bash, `B06-AT-07` Horn Drive, a claimed hit, an avoided attack or another creature's bite never creates this toxin.

The post-hit result's `injection_eligible` must reflect **surviving real Piercing HP after the actual effective Armor/Penetration and separately authored event-wide prevention**, or the GM must independently attest actual canine-secretions delivery through a pre-existing physical breach. Mere nonpiercing HP, Vigor loss, visual contact, or normal unconfirmed damage does not qualify. The physical Bite damage already applied to the source G4 event is **not reversed** by an ignored/prevented poison application, and neither poison acceptance nor a failed Pounce Balance creates a second bite.

The Hunter profile is a **Moderate DC12** member of the existing *one active mundane toxin* slot. Source-backed target Vitality uses uppercase player/lowercase JSON enemy key. One valid resistance roll selects Full or Reduced; equal-grade repeat injection is ignored **before** any Ward or new resistance. Hunter's pending milestones are source-owned **members of the same record**, not short toxin ticks:

| Resolution | Exact source schedule | Penalties |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** | Exposure T+0; **T+600s onset**, **T+1800s escalation**, **T+7200s expiry** | Silent before onset; stage 1 **−2 normal M / −1 muscular Strength checks/contests**; stage 2 *replaces*, not adds to, stage 1 with **−4 M / −2 muscular Strength**; clean expiry. |
| **Reduced** | Exposure T+0; **T+600s onset**, **T+3600s expiry** | Silent before onset; stage 1 **−2 M / −1 muscular Strength** only; no T+30m escalation. |

No direct toxin HP/Vigor packet, additional Dying loss, paralysis, wounds, rerolls, free turns, Movement AP loss, Attack/Guard/Dodge penalty, or altered source Strength is introduced. G0 reads the established, currently effective ordinary MP impairment at real turn allocation. A stage change while the target is moving never adds a per-tile penalty or refunds prior spent MP; named muscular Strength physical contests must query the **exact** G4.3 modifier, not alter all weapon attacks.

At exposure during initiative round R the distinct milestones map to **R+100/300/1200**, or **R+100/600** for Reduced. Entering initiative from exploration maps each remaining `s` to **R+floor(s/6)**, including immediate `q=0` resolution; leaving uses **`6×max(0,E−C−1)`** from the current next-active exit label, with original order retained. Finished stage events are not replayed; the underlying instance is never recreated or shifted to the victim's next Step 3. Suppression does not move these dates.

## Venom Endurance — fixed Primary spell

The same executor supports two separately chosen profiles (`Self` or `Single Target`) and two **non-switchable** modes:

**Preventive Ward:** A successful effect carries exactly **one** intercept until spent or its fixed 60s / R+10 expiry. It can intercept a *new, actually delivered and non-ignored* Minor/Moderate mundane toxin, including the Hunter's named venom, **before** the toxin's Vitality roll. A failed puncture, blocked attack, equal/lower printed grade candidate or non-toxin never spends Ward. An intercepted higher-grade application leaves the earlier live toxin intact and consumes no victim resistance roll.

**Active Suppression:** Binds one already-active eligible toxin instance, if any, with a one-time `min(5,3+floor(max(0,raw VC)/2))` budget: VC0–1 = 3, VC2–3 = 4, VC4+ = 5. The spell does not diagnose an absent toxin: legal attempts without useful remaining consequences **still pay ordinary action + VP**, but create no functional protection. Fixed-duration expiry or actual toxin expiry/cure/replacement ends the bound protection without refund.

Each actual discrete eligible toxin HP/Vigor packet consumes one unit if there would be damage; TX-NP at current Vigor0 consumes none. TX-HE consumes one only at its pre-existing genuine six-hour Vigor Recovery boundary, never creates a new checkpoint, and leaves Spent VP Recovery untouched. A continuous short profile spends one unit immediately for its current toxin-local interval, then at each completed genuine Step 3 or six exploration seconds as long as the source penalty and budget remain; a paid interval suppresses **all** eligible checks/movement, not one check. Delayed TX-NC consumes nothing during its first two harmless intervals. TX-CY's harmless three-interval residual marker has no future suppressible consequence.

For **TX-EN and Hunter's long continuous staged impairment**, Active Suppression has a separate exact **six-second-equivalent local protection interval**. If penalty is already active, one unit purchases the current interval immediately. Hunter's silent T+0…10m onset initially uses **0 units**; if Stage 1 arrives during the fixed spell's lifetime, one unit purchases the first local interval *before* the stage becomes effective. At each completed six exploration seconds, or at the next genuine affected-recipient Step 3 after protection began, that interval ends and one new unit is automatically bought if both spell and toxin remain effective. A Hunter stage 2 transition inside a paid interval changes the **underlying** magnitude but uses **no additional unit**, and the stronger penalty resumes when the final protected interval ends. Partial elapsed exploration seconds survive mode changes, but real Step 3 does not add fictional world seconds. Exhausting units never freezes, delays, resets, or cures the original toxin.

### Source, cost, Intrusion and delivery boundary

Because current G0 A/B Low Priest fixtures are **not proof that Venom Endurance was actually prepared**, G4.4's spell casting port requires an **explicit independent source authorization** and **identified access reference**; it grants no new repertoire to any fixture. The cast is **own-turn only**, one legal source Focus Projection Action (High20 / Medium24 / Low30), or an independently authorized High Priest Open Invocation Full Action (never a free Reaction), and **2 Spent VP**, optionally locked at 1 by an externally attested eligible cost discount with an explicit consumed-use callback. Open Invocation adds an independent non-discountable **+1 Spent VP**. Both AP and Available VP are checked before commitment, and available VP/capability/range are rechecked after the committed response callback: interruption retains AP but spends zero VP. Valid application spends locked VP even on resisted or useless protection. Beneficial unopposed recipient needs no Intrusion roll; a consciously refusing recipient requires an independently attested **+8 barrier** contest, with tie resisting, rather than guessed attributes or automatic success. Contested invalid inputs reject before AP spend.

Nonself `Single Target` uses the exact within-3 square-equivalent source reference-distance metric, visible and unobstructed open-air line asserted at declaration and checked again at application. When real map/path source adjudication is complex, a live independently authored `delivery_check` is the caller's responsibility; no fictitious wall-passing permission is created. No fixed spell is canceled merely because the caster becomes unconscious later. No maintained, Composite Secondary, Rite, Consecration, free detox, invented generic poison immunity or automatic talent discount is granted. Caster/recipient exclusivity prevents budget stacking or a no-cost reset.

**Acceptance tests:** [G4.4 source/timing/ward fixtures](../../tests/test_b06_hunter_venom_endurance_v0.py) verify Full and Reduced timeline, legitimate source/armor qualification, normal Bite and Pounce Bite, repeated grade, exact initiative and exploration deadlines, Ward interception and ignored delivery, 0-unit pre-onset Suppression, stage escalation inside purchased interval, finite-unit exhaustion, timed expiry, TX-NP and TX-HM packet suppression, contested/interrupt AP and VP, and non-free casting.
