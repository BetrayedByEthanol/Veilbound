## Purpose and authority boundary

The new `WoundContinuation` is a **separate continuation and evidence layer** over the unchanged G0/G3/G4 actor state. It does not invent physical contact, weapon attack totals, Guard/Dodge response order, legal critical confirmation, movement, survival choices, repair abilities, resistance, or treatment. Its accepted source-profile hit requires:

1. A real **already-committed current-attacker G0 Physical Action** in the in-scene ledger, with a stable and once-consumed action index; no free attack or action refund.
2. Separately attested **actual legal hit/contact and already-resolved defense**, with non-empty auditable contact/defense source references and two explicit Boolean attestations. These are **caller claims requiring outside G5 evidence**, not proof generated by this bridge.
3. A source-backed Soldier/Sentry/Hunter JSON attack profile, independently supplied exact damage die/critical tier and critical d4 faces. `G4.apply_profile_hit` remains the single source for physical Armor/Penetration, typed damage, HP/Vigor packets, one-event wound location/overkill severity and actual death. Missing qualifying actual wound-location d20 rejects before G4 mutation.
4. An independent, nonreusable hit source-event ID. The record contains actor source digests, committed action index, declared G5 external evidence references, G4 native hit result, and exact resulting wounds. **The action must belong to the present round and active attacker turn and remain the last live Physical Action; intervening defense/response logging is legal but another committed action or already-resolved profile hit closes that window.** Subsequent applications cannot spend the same G0 action again, including after a later round refresh.

This does **not** certify the G5 attestation merely because an API caller asserted `True`; only G5 with a real map, defense stack and provenance may produce that evidence. Nor does it turn an already Dying target into a dead/certified-defeated opponent. `complete_encounter_certified=False`, `winner=None`, and `challenge_rating=None` are deliberate.

## Native turn and wound continuation

`start_next_turn(choice)` delegates strictly to the next genuine G0 Initiative position. G0 Step 1, **one genuine Step 2** `DamageState.regenerate`, ordinary scheduled Step 3 toxin/hazard, and Step 4 action resources remain owned by their original runtime. The bridge verifies and stores the actual Step 2 ledger ID and before/after source actor state; it never advances a virtual turn. If actual player allocation is needed, an omitted `RegenerationChoice` stays **unresolved** and the native pending Step 2 is resumed with the eventual explicit choice; Step 1 cannot repeat. `end_current_turn` and `begin_next_round` delegate unchanged native G0 behavior.

The source actor remains alive, Dying or dead **according to real HP**, not a declared winner. Wounds carry unique IDs, original source event IDs, severity, location, trauma, repair requirement, cumulative Repair Progress and repairability. Tests require at least one genuine L3 Soldier → L3 Low Life front-Priest **sustained Critical wound** followed by five actual 6-throughput repairs costing 30 recipient Vigor, with no automatic HP refund, healing spell, synthetic regeneration, victory or recovery. Other bounded fixtures demonstrate second genuine Dying repair with oldest-first allocation, 1-throughput/1-Vigor survival cancellation and zero-Vigor uncancelled Step 2 loss, plus the fatal `HP ≤ −MaxHP` threshold with **no extra repairable wound**.

Actual independently paid Basic Heal and its external repair component are still owned by **G4/G3**; no new generic Medicine, surgery, Irreparable reconstruction, recovery-rate modifier or miracle is introduced.

## Endings and unresolved results

`mark_unresolved` records a reproducible, source-referenced **missing-authority stop**, selecting one of the narrow authored categories: missing G5 contact/defense, critical confirmation, wound-location roll, exceptional injury rule, G5 retreat geometry, G6 policy or response, or another sourced decision. It does not silently advance combat.

`attest_withdrawal` accepts a living actor's independently proven departure **only when accompanied by an actual actor-linked in-scene G1 movement ledger entry and explicit independent G5/G6 exit policy evidence**. Without that, exit geometry remains unresolved. A genuine withdrawal may be recorded as an event but does not itself decide encounter winner, CR or objective. Death is observed only at the existing G4 threshold. Result reports include source fingerprints, event-index range, wounds, native regeneration count, verified-hit ledger and explicit unresolved/withdrawal outcomes.

## Frozen Phase 3 wound-stop register (G4A-13)

The function `historical_phase3_wound_witnesses()` re-executes the *original* immutable [Phase 3 source trial](../../scripts/b06_hunter_tactical_diversity_v0.py) over all **32 authored cells ×128 seeds**, selects its **22** `wound_unresolved` **first stopping events**, and emits **22 separate keyed evidence records** with the original six-dimensional scenario/seed tuple, the original trial result digest, source terminal and HP/contact-route projection. Each entry retains the explicit classification:

`unresolved_G5_critical_confirmation_and_wound_location`

and lists independently required hit/contact/defense confirmation, an actual precommitted wound-location d20, player defense and recovery choices, and new legal turns before any true G4 continuation can be claimed. **None is marked genuinely replayed or certified**. The old Phase 3 `trial`, `census`, signed 4,096 trials and checksum are not modified. Aggregate first stops or attacker/defender state cannot substitute for the absent G5 input needed to replay real wound consequence events. These are 22 *identified classification records*, **not** 22 repaired or completed encounters.
