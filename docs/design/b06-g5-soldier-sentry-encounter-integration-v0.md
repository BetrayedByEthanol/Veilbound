## Source and procedure

This v0 supports **one-square ordinary melee** from the existing verified Soldier `B06-EN-02` sword `B06-AT-03`, and Sentry `B06-EN-01` normal/enhanced swords `B06-AT-01/02`. No independently invented PC offensive attack profile, Hunter manoeuvre, Fire Exaltation, NPC Focus progression, offensive AI policy, unknown terrain, or new damage rules. A generic `SourceMelee` also permits these source attackers against another benchmark creature, including a meaningful Sentry vs shield Soldier Guard check.

**Source-and-time pipeline:**

1. G1 **current actual melee contact**, 1×1 footprint and explicit opposing teams are preflighted before consuming AP or defenses. G3 checks that the actual Sentry Reserve matches the declared normal/enhanced profile.
2. G0 `declare_sequence()` commits **all** paid Physical Action slots, names every profile and target, assigns each Guard/Dodge/Take Hit **before** dice, and stores exact source action, defense and declaration indices. **Every resolution, including natural-1 misses and Guard/Dodge misses, must match an action and declaration from the current round and attacker turn and the original paid G0 sequence slot** (validated against G4.5's shared source-bound slot registry *before* a response window or roll is logged). A stale prior-round miss cannot consume old AP as if the attack were current. A held shield Guard is an actual consumed per-round resource; Dodge pressure is recorded in order. No separate or free G5 AP.
3. G5 binds the **exact G0 `defense_assigned` event for every slot**, in canonical declaration order, including the event's `attack_key=${attacker}:${target}:${slot}`, declared defender, chosen defense, and actual recorded Defense. Even when two slots both say `dodge`, swapping their indices is invalid because the earlier and later Dodge Pressures differ. A reordered action index is likewise rejected despite identical attack profile labels. Only after those checks does G5 accept an explicit **d20 source attack face**, the printed fixture's `attack_roll.total_bonus`, the **live Prone melee accuracy modifier** (−2 for a Prone attacker; +2 for a standing attacker against a Prone target; two Prone combatants give only −2), and that exact G0 defense value. This modified attack total applies to the ordinary attack and its confirmation. **Natural 1 misses**; melee **Take Hit** receives damage unless natural 1; an attack against Guard or Dodge must **beat** the chosen defense. Natural 20 follows the canonical **one confirmation** ladder: failed Guard/Dodge confirmation Strong +1d4, successful Critical +2d4, confirmation natural20 Devastating +2d4 plus separately qualified wound; Take Hit starts Critical and upgrades to Devastating only on confirmation natural20.
4. **Natural 1 is also an automatic miss on the confirmation attack**, even when the Sentry's +7 bonus exceeds a pressure-reduced Dodge Defense; such a failed Guard/Dodge confirmation is **Strong**, never Critical. Natural 20 on the confirmation is Devastating after legal initial confirmation opportunity. The genuine G0 `response_window()` logs trigger rechecks, with a source-attested **explicit** `decline_all` spontaneous policy for these narrow fixed scenes. A matching reserved Interception is **unresolved**, not silently suppressed or consumed; a changed Guard instrument requiring a new physical fallback remains unresolved, as do cancelled geometry and missing player decisions. **Previously committed Dodge/Guard against a target that became Dying between sequence hits instead resolves as Take Hit**: the original Guard/Dodge expenditure is retained and an explicit fallback event is recorded. **G5 slots resolve in their predeclared order**: slot N cannot proceed until every earlier slot is genuinely resolved or has an explicitly recorded lost-action cancellation.
5. On predicted hits **before any response window or attack roll is logged**, G5 uses G4's shared **`preflight_profile_hit`** to validate the exact source damage d6, required enchanted-sword Vigor d6, all additional typed packets, effective mitigation/HP-crossing and required wound-location d20. Invalid inputs leave no new dice or damage events; correcting them is permitted only before the action is actually rolled. On **real hits only**, G5 delegates to G4.5's already source-and-AP-bound `verified_profile_hit` and G4's real typed Armor/Penetration, Fire HP, direct Vigor, wound and terminal mechanics. Misses do not apply damage or fabricate wounds. Every action has one `g5_melee_resolved` result, and a consumed G0 action can never damage twice.
6. Each repeatable scene retains the exact source digests, AP/VP/Vigor/HP and selected dice, defense, critical, contact, result and terminal movement evidence. Missing decisions remain unresolved rather than winning by fiat. Report snapshots are detached from the authoritative ledger.

## I1 — Soldier vs L3 Low Life Priest (bounded exit objective)

`run_i1(seed=...)` initializes the ordinary human Soldier against a verified L3 Low Priest A physical proxy in a fixed 8×5 open corridor. An actual source sword sequence tests Guard and natural-1 failure. The author binds an immutable `SceneExit` before the encounter adapter is opened: **actor `front`, border square `(0,2)`, exact `G5:open-corridor:exit-front` policy**, encounter/map IDs and declared exit objective. The Priest has a genuine G0 Step 2 / movement turn and reaches this exit through two G1 legal movement steps under the explicit policy that the Soldier **declines eligible Movement Opportunities**. G5 checks the actor, still-matching authored scene, actual current border position, **current consciousness/HP above zero**, and **last completed G1 movement step** before asking G4.5 to attest withdrawal and logging `g5_terminal_exit`. A previous valid move **does not secure a terminal exit** if subsequently resolved G4 harm drops the actor to **0 or negative HP** before withdrawal is attested: G5 and the reusable G4.5 gate both reject it without `withdrawal_attested` or `g5_terminal_exit`, keeping the encounter unresolved. `SourceMelee.withdraw(actor_id)` accepts **no caller-selected square or policy**; an unconfigured general `SourceMelee` cannot create an exit, and arbitrary interior movement remains nonterminal. It is a **true narrow scene exit terminal**, **not** a Soldier death, a victory based on HP reaching zero, an assertion that the actor outran a Hunter, or a certificate for a multi-person party fight.

Further G5 tests independently run a shield-bearing Soldier's Guard18 against a Sentry attack, maintaining actual armor and Guard capacity rather than treating the Soldier only as an attacker. Regression coverage also verifies declared sequence order, loss of active Guard/Dodge when a target becomes Dying mid-sequence, pre-roll Vigor/wound-location dice gating without contradictory roll histories, and standing/Prone accuracy for both initial attacks and confirmation.

## I2 — Firebound Sentry cold vs prepared (two separately run source starts)

`run_i2(state="cold_start"|"prepared_start",seed=...)` instantiates the source-approved Sentry's actual actor/VP snapshot, opposed by the same L3 Low Priest A physical proxy in the open corridor.

- **Cold:** real own-turn **Projection30** and **3 Committed VP** establish the Chosen Sword Reserve; only **12 AP remain**, less than the **20 AP** sword strike. The Priest's intervening real turn, Round End, Round Begin and next Sentry Step 2 must occur **before** any enhanced attack. No instant no-cost Fire strike.
- **Prepared:** starts with the exact authored **3 Available / 3 Committed / 0 Spent VP** and source-linked enhanced sword; the first own turn may make a normal **20 AP** attack immediately without a second projection cost.
- Both modes use `B06-AT-02` with one source sword packet (Armor after Penetration), **separate Fire HP** without physical Armor, and a separately rolled direct-Vigor rider; there is no free persistent Burning or Fire Exaltation. A scripted victim withdrawal later provides a narrow terminal objective with genuine G1 exit evidence.

Separate tests prove Guard versus sword, two precommitted Dodge defenses (Dodge pressure), natural-20 Take Hit Critical/Devastating and Guard Strong confirmation, **a natural-1 failure versus pressure-reduced Dodge 6 under the printed +7 attack bonus, a successful confirmation-2 control**, full VP/AP payment, profile-state mismatch rejection, independent packet mitigation, and rejection of fabricated interior exits or unauthored generic withdrawals. Additional invariants reject **a prior-round miss on the same attacker** without changing the ledger, **reversed Dodge14/Dodge12 source indices** that would turn an attack total 13 from miss into hit, and **swapped paid attack slots** with identical profile labels. Valid current-round attacks still resolve and retain exact native G0 evidence.

## Certified evidence vs open gates

This PR provides **repeatable bounded I1/I2 terminal-exit witnesses** and **actual source-verified G5 ordinary melee opposed hits/misses** rather than G4.5's earlier externally assumed Boolean contacts. It does not certify full I1/I2 diverse-party fights or imply success rates, tactical AI, a full Guard-loss fallback, loaded G1 Interception attack effects, a genuine enemy defeat, a general opponent escape/pursuit contest, all High Scholar/Life Priest spells in dynamic opposition, or any Hunter G6/G7 calibration. **An HP0 actor is Dying, not defeated by automatic terminal policy.**

To close the wider G5 pack gate, add more legal player offensive profiles and response policies, completed harm/death/retreat branches for the full required party roles, and genuine spell/protection + repeatable exit/failure branches. Until then `complete_party_pack_certified=False`, and **0/12** Focus identities remain end-to-end certified.

**Frozen historical census remains untouched:** 32 Phase 3 cases × 128 seeds = 4,096 first-stop trials, 22 original `wound_unresolved` records unchanged. All Phase 3 / Hunter CR claims remain separately pending.
