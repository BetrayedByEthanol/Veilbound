## Source and procedure

This v0 supports **one-square ordinary melee** from the existing verified Soldier `B06-EN-02` sword `B06-AT-03`, and Sentry `B06-EN-01` normal/enhanced swords `B06-AT-01/02`. No independently invented PC offensive attack profile, Hunter manoeuvre, Fire Exaltation, NPC Focus progression, offensive AI policy, unknown terrain, or new damage rules. A generic `SourceMelee` also permits these source attackers against another benchmark creature, including a meaningful Sentry vs shield Soldier Guard check.

**Source-and-time pipeline:**

1. G1 **current actual melee contact**, 1×1 footprint and explicit opposing teams are preflighted before consuming AP or defenses. G3 checks that the actual Sentry Reserve matches the declared normal/enhanced profile.
2. G0 `declare_sequence()` commits **all** paid Physical Action slots, names every profile and target, assigns each Guard/Dodge/Take Hit **before** dice, and stores exact source action, defense and declaration indices. **Every resolution, including natural-1 misses and Guard/Dodge misses, must match an action and declaration from the current round and attacker turn and the original paid G0 sequence slot** (validated against G4.5's shared source-bound slot registry *before* a response window or roll is logged). A stale prior-round miss cannot consume old AP as if the attack were current. **Guard availability is fully preflighted before any sequence payment:** both G5 and native G0 verify a real `guard_instrument` remains present, in addition to `ready_guard`, Guard Defense and sufficient capacity. A ready flag without a usable instrument rejects the entire proposed sequence **before AP, target sequence locks, Guard/Dodge debits, or `attack_sequence_committed`**, even when Guard is a later slot. No untracked paid attack can be silently discarded on turn end. The caller can restore the instrument through separate legal equipment handling and retry a fresh normally paid sequence. A held shield Guard is an actual consumed per-round resource; Dodge pressure is recorded in order. **When a target is Prone, the scene must independently attest that real repositioning is physically possible (`prone_dodge_feasible=True`) before calling native G0 `declare_sequence` with any Dodge.** Missing/false attestation leaves AP, Guard, Dodge Pressure and sequence slots untouched. If a previously standing Dodge recipient becomes Prone before its roll, G5 requires the original declaration's affirmative attestation and otherwise stops before response/roll logging. This matches G2's bounded Prone-Dodge physical-feasibility requirement; it does not infer feasibility from the status alone. A Prone Guard likewise requires an **independent, affirmative `prone_guard_feasible=True` attestation** that the specific shield/weapon can physically orient and operate from the actual posture, before committing G0 AP/Guard; neither a ready-Guard flag nor Prone status alone proves feasibility. A newly Prone defender must also have this affirmative attestation in the original sequence evidence before the Guard is applied; otherwise the unresolved posture case stops **before dice or damage**, retaining already paid resources until supported fallback is adjudicated. These checks do not apply a generic Prone Guard Defense penalty. **The attacker also requires affirmative `prone_attack_feasible=True` for each source sword attack when Prone**, independently establishing that its current weapon, required limb, swing and leverage can actually operate from that posture. Otherwise G5 rejects the entire sequence *before* G0 commits AP or the defender's Guard/Dodge, rather than merely applying the legal **−2** accuracy modifier to an impossible strike. The declaration records this pre-roll attack-feasibility attestation; if a previously standing Soldier/Sentry becomes Prone before resolution, G5 requires that same affirmative evidence and stops before the attack roll/response window when it is absent. This is the bounded posture gate from [Attacking while Prone](../rules/05-combat/special-movement-terrain-and-prone.md#attacking-while-prone), not a free assumption that holding a sword makes it usable. No separate or free G5 AP.
3. G5 binds the **exact G0 `defense_assigned` event for every slot**, in canonical declaration order, including the event's `attack_key=${attacker}:${target}:${slot}`, declared defender, chosen defense, and actual recorded Defense. Even when two slots both say `dodge`, swapping their indices is invalid because the earlier and later Dodge Pressures differ. A reordered action index is likewise rejected despite identical attack profile labels. Only after those checks does G5 accept an explicit **d20 source attack face**, the printed fixture's `attack_roll.total_bonus`, the **live Prone melee accuracy modifier** (−2 for a Prone attacker; +2 for a standing attacker against a Prone target; two Prone combatants give only −2), and that exact G0 defense value. This modified attack total applies to the ordinary attack and its confirmation. **Natural 1 misses**; melee **Take Hit** receives damage unless natural 1; an attack against Guard or Dodge must **beat** the chosen defense. Natural 20 follows the canonical **one confirmation** ladder: failed Guard/Dodge confirmation Strong +1d4, successful Critical +2d4, confirmation natural20 Devastating +2d4 plus separately qualified wound; Take Hit starts Critical and upgrades to Devastating only on confirmation natural20.
4. **Natural 1 is also an automatic miss on the confirmation attack**, even when the Sentry's +7 bonus exceeds a pressure-reduced Dodge Defense; such a failed Guard/Dodge confirmation is **Strong**, never Critical. Natural 20 on the confirmation is Devastating after legal initial confirmation opportunity. The genuine G0 `response_window()` logs trigger rechecks, with a source-attested **explicit** `decline_all` spontaneous policy for these narrow fixed scenes. A matching reserved Interception is **unresolved**, not silently suppressed or consumed; a changed Guard instrument requiring a new physical fallback remains unresolved, as do cancelled geometry and missing player decisions. **Previously committed Dodge/Guard against a target that became Dying between sequence hits instead resolves as Take Hit**: the original Guard/Dodge expenditure is retained and an explicit fallback event is recorded. **G5 slots resolve in their predeclared order**: slot N cannot proceed until every earlier slot is genuinely resolved or has an explicitly recorded lost-action cancellation. **All earlier and selected action IDs are reconstructed from the native current-round G0 `attack_sequence_committed` event and its ordered `sequence:{profile}:{index}` Physical Action payments**, not the caller's mutable sequence receipt. The full receipt must match those canonical IDs before checking earlier resolutions, defense pressure, opening response windows, rolling dice or applying damage. A historical already-resolved action ID cannot be substituted for a new unresolved slot, even if slot N itself names a genuine current action. **While any G5 slot remains unresolved, G1 rejects ordinary attacker translation, continuous route movement, AND `reserve_movement()` before MP expenditure, route simulation, MP reservation serial advancement, native G0 Interception registration or new ledger events. In particular, observing the result of a first strike cannot unlock movement reservation while a second paid slot remains outstanding. Once every slot has a genuine resolved/cancelled entry, normal G1 MP reservations are again legal; G1-only encounters without G5 keep their original reservation behavior. G5 separately rejects any attacker `g1_movement_step` / completed allied-transit evidence added after declaration even if its current contact remains legal. Movement resumes only after every declared slot has a genuine resolved/cancelled entry.** G5 v0 authors no response-authorized attacker movement exception; later legal response movement must be explicitly tied to that response's live authority rather than silently interleaved.
5. **Committed-slot completion gate and sequence exclusivity:** every `g5_melee_sequence_attested` payment remains pending until an exact later `g5_melee_resolved` or source-authorized `g5_melee_cancelled` record for that action index. **No new attack sequence against *any* target may be declared while even one already-paid G5 slot for that attacker remains pending.** **Every subsequent own-turn AP commitment is also forbidden until all that actor's paid G5 slots are actually resolved or explicitly canceled.** **Voluntary G3 effect release is gated independently:** `SpellState.release(caster, kind)` also refuses to release any maintained effect while that caster has pending G5 slots. Unlike casts or reserves, voluntary release spends no AP and would bypass `Encounter.spend_ap()`; releasing a Sentry's Sword Reserve after declaring `B06-AT-02` would otherwise invalidate the paid attack and permanently block turn completion. Failed release leaves the effect, Committed/Available VP, AP, pending slots, and ledger untouched. Once every slot has genuinely resolved or been legally canceled, the normal voluntary release and committed-VP refund become legal again. Mandatory source-driven effect loss remains distinct and is not silently converted to voluntary cancellation. G0's central `Encounter.spend_ap()` now checks G5's encounter-ledger pending-slot registry **before** any Physical/Projection/General/Full Action debit or new `action_committed` log. This covers direct `spend_ap()`, `Encounter.reserve()` (all reservation AP classes), and G3 casts without forcing each subsystem to invent its own partial-sequence guard. The canonical initial `Encounter.declare_sequence()` payments remain legal because `g5_melee_sequence_attested` is emitted only **after** all original paid slots and defenses are committed; a failed competing spend does not mutate AP, MP, VP, reservations, Guard/Dodge pressure, sequence locks, or the ledger. Once every slot is resolved/canceled, any remaining AP is again available for a separately legal action. **Attachment itself is fail-closed:** when the encounter already contains a native `attack_sequence_committed` event without G5 tracking, `SourceMelee` refuses installation **before opening a G4.5 bridge or logging a G5 event**. This holds even after a G0-only turn or round ends, since neither a native paid sequence nor its defenses can be reconstructed as fully and legally resolved from the mere absence of a G5 record. G5 must be attached **before the encounter's first native Attack Sequence**; importing historical G0 payments is deferred until an independent provenance importer exists. **When G5 is attached, direct calls to the native `Encounter.declare_sequence()` are rejected even for the first sequence**, before AP, Guard/Dodge, or the sequence ledger changes: only `SourceMelee.declare()` can temporarily enter the native declaration window and then immediately register `g5_melee_sequence_attested`. Its synchronous authorization is reset in a `finally` block even on an invalid native declaration. This closes the prior bypass where a direct first G0 sequence spent AP and Guard, but registered no pending G5 slot and could be skipped before withdrawal. `SourceMelee.declare()` and the native `Encounter.declare_sequence()` entry point enforce this before changing AP, Guard/Dodge, sequence locks or the event ledger; resolving the first sequence genuinely restores eligibility for a distinct second target's ordinary sequence when AP remains. **Independent incoming G4 harm to a previously committed attacker is a separate terminal loss path:** the native `DamageState.apply_damage` event immediately notifies G5 after recording its source damage, if the recipient becomes **Dying (HP ≤ 0) or dead**. All still-unresolved G0-paid actions for **that attacker** receive a distinct `g5_melee_cancelled` entry, `reason="attacker_dying_before_resolution"` or `"attacker_dead_before_resolution"`, tied to the genuine `damage_event_key`; **no AP or pre-assigned Guard/Dodge is refunded**. The cancellation is idempotent and completed **before any later contact/attack-roll attempt or native end-turn check**; the already dead attacker's invalid `melee_contact` must not leave permanent pending slots. Native G0 turn end now succeeds after those source-backed cancellations. Actual death is reported as `death_attested`, whereas 0-HP Dying remains **unresolved**, never upgraded to a terminal victory. Invalid inputs/missing dice while the attacker remains conscious never cancel paid slots. **If a first/middle hit actually kills its original target at G4's death threshold**, the G5 adapter **retains every later already-paid slot as pending**, without refunding AP or previously assigned Guard/Dodge resources. The dead target cannot receive another live attack, but no automatic `g5_melee_cancelled` event is emitted for target death. The attacker must explicitly **retarget** the still-funded ordered suffix using `retarget_remaining()` to another newly eligible enemy (with new, separately paid defensive assignments), or **decline** those slots using `decline_remaining()` when the original target is genuinely unavailable; only this explicit decline emits `g5_melee_cancelled` with `reason="explicit_decline_target_unavailable"`. Until either choice resolves the pending suffix, native turn-end and terminal withdrawal remain blocked. **Dying (HP ≤ 0 but not dead) alone is not target unavailability**: while the original target remains legally in contact, its committed Guard/Dodge falls back to Take Hit and neither redirection nor decline is permitted. Missing dice, lost contact and unresolved responses do not silently refund or auto-cancel paid attacks. This target-loss rule is distinct from the **attacker** becoming Dying or dead from incoming harm, which legitimately cancels that attacker's pending attacks through the dedicated G4-to-G5 hook described above. `SourceMelee.pending_attack_slots()` reconstructs every pending slot from the authoritative encounter ledger **across the full scene**, including previous turns and rounds. Native `Encounter.end_turn()` calls the optional G5 validation hook **before** changing the actor's AP/MP, initiative cursor, event ledger or round, so a caller cannot circumvent this rule via direct G0 or G4.5 turn-end entry points. Ending an attacker turn with any outstanding paid G5 slot is unresolved and rejected. The independently authored scene exit also refuses `withdraw()` while **any** earlier sequence remains outstanding, even if a border movement has already been completed. The report records detached `unresolved_attack_slots` and never labels an encounter terminal while pending slots remain. A missing roll, failed preflight, or skipped call is **not** a cancellation and does not grant a free terminal. Explicitly canceled G5 response triggers remain distinct from genuinely resolved hits or misses.
6. On predicted hits **before any response window or attack roll is logged**, G5 uses G4's shared **`preflight_profile_hit`** to validate the exact source damage d6, required enchanted-sword Vigor d6, all additional typed packets, effective mitigation/HP-crossing and required wound-location d20. Invalid inputs leave no new dice or damage events; correcting them is permitted only before the action is actually rolled. On **real hits only**, G5 delegates to G4.5's already source-and-AP-bound `verified_profile_hit` and G4's real typed Armor/Penetration, Fire HP, direct Vigor, wound and terminal mechanics. Misses do not apply damage or fabricate wounds. Every action has one `g5_melee_resolved` result, and a consumed G0 action can never damage twice.
7. Each repeatable scene retains the exact source digests, AP/VP/Vigor/HP and selected dice, defense, critical, contact, result and terminal movement evidence. Missing decisions remain unresolved rather than winning by fiat. Report snapshots are detached from the authoritative ledger.

## Already-paid follow-up redirection after target loss

A **fatal or otherwise target-invalidating first strike never automatically cancels the remaining paid Attack Sequence slots**. The attacker must finish the sequence through either:

- **`retarget_remaining(original_sequence, new_target, defenses=...)`:** resolve the original slot(s) in declaration order against another currently legal 1×1 opponent in genuine G1 Reach who has **not** been the target of an Attack Sequence from that attacker this turn. All original action indices, attack profiles, prices and source effects stay fixed. The newly targeted defender receives one actual new pre-roll G0 Guard/Dodge/Take Hit assignment per remaining paid slot, including ordinary Guard capacity/Dodge Pressure costs; old target's already-committed defenses are **not** refunded. The encounter-wide `g5_melee_retarget_committed` evidence binds the new target and defense-index mapping to the same original AP action indices. G4.5 checks that evidence before applying any redirected damage; there is no new AP spend, second free profile, or reinitialization of the ordinary sequence.
- **`decline_remaining(original_sequence)`:** an explicit, ledger-recorded decision after the current target is unavailable. It closes only still-open paid actions with `explicit_decline_target_unavailable`, returning **zero AP** and **zero old defensive resources**; without either redirect or decline, the turn and terminal report remain blocked by unresolved paid slots.

**A living Dying target (HP = 0 or negative but above the death threshold) remains legally attackable while actual G1 melee contact holds.** Its previously committed Guard/Dodge becomes Take Hit at the G5 resolver; this defense fallback does **not** make it an unavailable target or authorize changing which creature receives the remaining paid damage. Both `retarget_remaining()` and `decline_remaining()` share the same availability rule: only **actual death, loss of contact, invalid 1×1 target geometry or other genuine opposing-target illegality** unlocks a paid suffix. An HP crossing alone never unlocks either branch; a failed attempt must leave the ledger, AP, defender resources and outstanding slots unchanged.

**The same Dying rule applies to a *new* target after a legitimate original-target loss.** When the original target is genuinely unavailable (such as actual death), a *distinct*, adjacent opponent at **HP 0 or below, but not dead**, is a lawful replacement for the already-paid suffix. However, **every newly assigned defense must be `take_hit`**; `guard` or `dodge` is rejected before any G0 defense call, defense-resource debit, AP payment, target-sequence lock or retarget event. Once the caller selects Take Hit, the ordinary printed source profiles, existing AP payments, original-defense no-refund rule, G4 wound and HP/death thresholds and G5 one-slot-once resolution apply normally. Regression fixtures cover replacement HP **0 and −1**, reject illegal Guard/Dodge without any state change, then finish the same paid suffix with legitimate Take Hit. A still-legal Dying **original** target remains ineligible for redirection as before. The existing fatal-first-hit redirect remains legal.

A caller cannot redirect while its current target remains legally attackable, redirect to an already-attacked creature, add attacks, change profile, or turn an earlier miss into a free second declaration. Multi-slot redirections preserve the source slot order; defenses on the replacement creature are paid only when that target is genuinely declared.

## I1 — Soldier vs L3 Low Life Priest (bounded exit objective)

`run_i1(seed=...)` initializes the ordinary human Soldier against a verified L3 Low Priest A physical proxy in a fixed 8×5 open corridor. An actual source sword sequence tests Guard and natural-1 failure. The author binds an immutable `SceneExit` before the encounter adapter is opened: **actor `front`, border square `(0,2)`, exact `G5:open-corridor:exit-front` policy**, encounter/map IDs and declared exit objective. A paid Soldier attack must first have an actual resolved hit/miss or valid G5 cancellation; skipping `resolve()` cannot end the Soldier's turn or produce a terminal. The Priest has a genuine G0 Step 2 / movement turn and reaches this exit through two G1 legal movement steps under the explicit policy that the Soldier **declines eligible Movement Opportunities**. G5 checks the actor, still-matching authored scene, actual current border position, **current consciousness/HP above zero**, and **last completed G1 movement proof**—either a `g1_movement_step` translation or the atomic `g1_allied_transit_finished` record whose immediately preceding actor-linked transit step reaches the exit; the reusable G4.5 bridge additionally verifies the complete consecutive paid transit route. Intermediate allied-transit steps, earlier completed routes followed by a later move away from the exit, canceled steps and MP reservations are never sufficient before asking G4.5 to attest withdrawal and logging `g5_terminal_exit`. A previous valid move **does not secure a terminal exit** if subsequently resolved G4 harm drops the actor to **0 or negative HP** before withdrawal is attested: G5 and the reusable G4.5 gate both reject it without `withdrawal_attested` or `g5_terminal_exit`, keeping the encounter unresolved. `SourceMelee.withdraw(actor_id)` accepts **no caller-selected square or policy**; an unconfigured general `SourceMelee` cannot create an exit, and arbitrary interior movement remains nonterminal. It is a **true narrow scene exit terminal**, **not** a Soldier death, a victory based on HP reaching zero, an assertion that the actor outran a Hunter, or a certificate for a multi-person party fight.

Further G5 tests independently run a shield-bearing Soldier's Guard18 against a Sentry attack, maintaining actual armor and Guard capacity rather than treating the Soldier only as an attacker. Regression coverage also verifies **turn-end rejection while any single- or multi-slot G5 sequence remains unresolved, and independent terminal-exit rejection even after actual border movement until the enemy's outstanding attack is adjudicated**; declared sequence order, loss of active Guard/Dodge when a target becomes Dying mid-sequence, pre-roll Vigor/wound-location dice gating without contradictory roll histories, and standing/Prone accuracy for both initial attacks and confirmation.

## I2 — Firebound Sentry cold vs prepared (two separately run source starts)

`run_i2(state="cold_start"|"prepared_start",seed=...)` instantiates the source-approved Sentry's actual actor/VP snapshot, opposed by the same L3 Low Priest A physical proxy in the open corridor.

- **Cold:** real own-turn **Projection30** and **3 Committed VP** establish the Chosen Sword Reserve; only **12 AP remain**, less than the **20 AP** sword strike. The Priest's intervening real turn, Round End, Round Begin and next Sentry Step 2 must occur **before** any enhanced attack. No instant no-cost Fire strike.
- **Prepared:** starts with the exact authored **3 Available / 3 Committed / 0 Spent VP** and source-linked enhanced sword; the first own turn may make a normal **20 AP** attack immediately without a second projection cost.
- **Ownership parity:** the enhanced sword is legal only while the single live Chosen `Sentry Sword Reserve` remains linked to the actually maintained sword and still owns **3 of the caster's Committed VP**. G0 `Encounter.release_vp` now refuses to refund any share still owned by an active G3 effect; this closes direct raw-refund bypasses even when a paid G5 strike is pending. G3 effect termination removes the effect first, then refunds its 3 VP under the existing legal timing (including when all pending G5 slots have resolved for voluntary release). `SpellState.sentry_profile` also fails closed on an unfunded or internally inconsistent active Reserve rather than granting Fire/Vigor because a stale effect record exists. Both cold-cast and prepared starts, a pending declared strike, normal refund after genuine release, and a corrupted-but-VP-conserving actor snapshot are covered by regression tests.
- Both modes use `B06-AT-02` with one source sword packet (Armor after Penetration), **separate Fire HP** without physical Armor, and a separately rolled direct-Vigor rider; there is no free persistent Burning or Fire Exaltation. A scripted victim withdrawal later provides a narrow terminal objective with genuine G1 exit evidence.

Separate tests prove Guard versus sword, two precommitted Dodge defenses (Dodge pressure), natural-20 Take Hit Critical/Devastating and Guard Strong confirmation, **a natural-1 failure versus pressure-reduced Dodge 6 under the printed +7 attack bonus, a successful confirmation-2 control**, full VP/AP payment, profile-state mismatch rejection, independent packet mitigation, and rejection of fabricated interior exits or unauthored generic withdrawals. Additional invariants reject **overlapping two-target declarations while an earlier G5 slot is pending, including direct G0 calls, and distinguish genuine G4-confirmed death cancellation from a merely Dying defender's still-live follow-up**, and reject **attacker G1 movement (including allied route entry) immediately after declaration and between unresolved sequence slots**, accept ordinary movement after all slots genuinely finish, require **pre-declaration Prone-Dodge physical-feasibility attestation** without any invalid payment, and stop a newly Prone target's unaudited Dodge before rolling. They also reject **a prior-round miss on the same attacker** without changing the ledger, **reversed Dodge14/Dodge12 source indices** that would turn an attack total 13 from miss into hit, and **swapped paid attack slots** with identical profile labels. Valid current-round attacks still resolve and retain exact native G0 evidence. The two-round regression additionally substitutes a previously resolved natural-1 attack ID into a fresh two-Dodge sequence while preserving the genuine later-slot ID; this is rejected before any ledger change, and the genuine first Dodge14 miss must resolve before the Dodge12 hit. A second control rejects the tampered prior ID even after the current first slot has genuinely resolved.

## Certified evidence vs open gates

This PR provides **repeatable bounded I1/I2 terminal-exit witnesses** and **actual source-verified G5 ordinary melee opposed hits/misses** rather than G4.5's earlier externally assumed Boolean contacts. It does not certify full I1/I2 diverse-party fights or imply success rates, tactical AI, a full Guard-loss fallback, loaded G1 Interception attack effects, a genuine enemy defeat, a general opponent escape/pursuit contest, all High Scholar/Life Priest spells in dynamic opposition, or any Hunter G6/G7 calibration. **An HP0 actor is Dying, not defeated by automatic terminal policy.**

To close the wider G5 pack gate, add more legal player offensive profiles and response policies, completed harm/death/retreat branches for the full required party roles, and genuine spell/protection + repeatable exit/failure branches. Until then `complete_party_pack_certified=False`, and **0/12** Focus identities remain end-to-end certified.

**Frozen historical census remains untouched:** 32 Phase 3 cases × 128 seeds = 4,096 first-stop trials, 22 original `wound_unresolved` records unchanged. All Phase 3 / Hunter CR claims remain separately pending.
