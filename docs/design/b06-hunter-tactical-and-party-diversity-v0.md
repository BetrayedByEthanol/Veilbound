## 2. Pre-staged Hunter tactics and geometry contract

The matrix is **L2/L3 × 3/4 members × original/mixed × warned/ambush × armored/exposed lead = 32 conditions**. Each condition has **128 matched SHA-256-keyed seeds**, giving **4,096 configured one-round trials**. This is a staged, conditional set of opening encounters, not a pathfinder or a generic initiative/surprise AI.

The Hunter's **armored** front contact is B1. Its **exposed** contact is A1 in the original party or F1 in the mixed party. These are **independently positioned starting states**, not free in-round target switching, impossible 1×2 rotation, teleports or retroactive weapon targets.

In **warned**, the Hunter is already head-forward adjacent to the chosen standing one-square lead; it can pay for an ordinary Bite, **not** an instant Pounce without its three-step approach. In **ambush**, the Hunter starts head (0,0), rear (−1,0) facing east, and the selected front lead is at (4,0). Its three cardinal whole-body steps would put its head at (3,0), adjacent to the standing target. **Pounce depends on whether that physical three-step corridor remains legal at the Hunter's own turn, not whether it wins Initiative outright.** An earlier stationary Bow attack from a separate clear lane, or an independently ranged Fire throw by a non-front caster, **does not move a body into that corridor** and leaves the Pounce available if the Hunter survives. An earlier melee participant uses the separately staged forward approach into the contested lane and disrupts Pounce; an earlier *frontal* Fire caster's explicit range-entry movement also disrupts it. **Disrupted Pounce does not by itself establish legal Bite contact.** The only supported ambush Bite fallbacks are: (a) the **front melee target itself** has reached square (1,0), already adjacent to the stationary Hunter head at (0,0); or (b) the **front F** has walked from (4,0) to (3,0), leaving squares (1,0) and (2,0) empty so the Hunter may advance its full 1×2 footprint two cardinal steps and Bite F without leaving a separately threatening melee actor. If a **different melee actor** reaches (1,0) first while the designated front stays at (4,0), the Hunter **cannot traverse the occupied square to Bite that lead**. A detour, Cautious route or Movement Opportunity is not authored, so the sample stops as **contact_path_unresolved** before declaring any Hunter Bite. An uninterrupted surviving Hunter may therefore Pounce **after** R or another stationary ranged participant acts. Ambush **does not provide surprise attack bonuses, denied defenses or automatic first turns**.

Other party roles require separately pre-established **nonoverlapping legal attack lanes at action time**: A/B can walk to melee without crossing threatened-exit squares, but the **only actually staged pre-Hunter melee entry** is square (1,0). A second pre-Hunter melee mover **cannot share that square**; the script stops before its unsupported strike rather than invent a different legal adjacent approach. If a front F attempts to fire from (3,0) while such a melee actor already occupies (1,0), F has an **unmodeled Living Screen** and the script stops before movement/casting, rather than claiming a clear projectile lane; R has an unobstructed bow lane **7–12 squares away** (Short Range Difficulty +0) with no ally or enemy Living Screen; F has a clear **1–3-square Thrown Flame** lane (+0 on its own authored technique), without cover, intervening creatures or a forbidden inside-target Manifestation. Real maps **must confirm these assumptions** before a played encounter; the runner does not prove every simultaneous placement or moving shot is legal.

**Exposed F — actual contact versus initiative:** In a **warned** setup the Hunter is head-adjacent from the start, so the front F is threatened and the runner conservatively skips an attempted Thrown Flame until a real Movement/Opportunity procedure is supplied. In an **ambush** setup F at (4,0) is *not threatened yet*, but the Hunter's head at (0,0) is **four squares away**, beyond the flame's authored **three-square maximum**. If F acts **before the Hunter**, F makes **one ordinary cardinal step toward it** to (3,0) for **2 of its available 12 MP**, entering the clear straight corridor. F now casts at legal three-square distance, pays its ordinary **20 Projection AP + 2 Spent VP per launch**, and **prevents the pending three-step Pounce**. **Provided no earlier melee actor occupies (1,0)**, the surviving Hunter can then approach only two head-forward squares to (2,0) and use an ordinary Bite against F at (3,0); no free turn or new target is created. If instead the Hunter acts first, it may finish the original three-step Pounce and establish contact, after which the exposed F's offensive spell is **skipped** by this conservative model rather than being granted a free Withdrawal/Opportunity response. These are scenario-specific movement choices, not a universal ban on threatened magic. This runner records **frontal contact established, lane disruptors, F's pre-contact cast count and its 2-MP range entry** explicitly.

## 3. Actual first-round procedures modeled

- Initiative is independent **d20+4** per participant, ties by Awareness, Agility, then a keyed roll. Hunter has AWA2/AGI2; A/R/F use AWA1/AGI3; B uses AWA0/AGI2.
- Hunter pays for **one** normal Bite or **one** eligible 24-AP Pounce, with its natural Bite+6 / 3+d6 Piercing Pen1, post-mitigation puncture-only DC12 toxin, and an independent d20+4 Pounce Balance when Bite damage has not already triggered the run's stopping boundary. **Eligible Pounce checks actual pre-turn lane disruption**, never initiative ranking alone. **A blocked Pounce never magically turns into a Bite against the same distant front lead**: if non-front melee occupancy makes the fallback illegal/Opportunity-dependent, the trace stops **contact_path_unresolved** before assigning Guard, rolling Bite, applying toxin or generating Pounce Balance. A tied Balance fails to knock Prone.
- A/B select actually wielded **Guard17/18**; F has no Guard instrument. Hunter always selects Dodge. First Hunter ranged and melee Dodge both begin at **14**: Hunter Agility2+Awareness2, and Physical Tempo1 grants ranged Evasion bonus0. Every further chosen Dodge reduces its Defense by 2 **including misses**. No free Guard, armor-stacking, cover, size or flank modifiers.
- A prone lead commits **4 MP** to Stand and remains Prone throughout the Hunter's first eligible Standing Opportunity, granting Hunter **+2 melee attack**; Standing completes only if responses leave it legal. If the Opportunity produces terminal wound damage, no subsequent toxin/Stand completion is generated.
- Natural 1 misses; natural 20 gets the authored **single confirmation**. Strong +1d4, Critical/Devastating +2d4 apply **once to the primary hit HP packet before Armor or typed resistance**. R's Bow rolls d4 damage, not d6; Strength never adds to Bow damage. Flame has **Fire2 primary HP** with no invented physical Armor mitigation, plus independent **Vigor burn** on a hit even where Fire HP is zero. No critical Vigor multiplier.
- An unthreatened F uses **one Projection20 + 2 Spent VP** per thrown flame, *even on a miss*. When F is the ambush lead and acts before Hunter contact, reaching authored three-square range first costs **2 ordinary MP** and disrupts the would-be Pounce. A **stationary** R uses a paid Physical20 and **one physical arrow** per actual shot without disturbing Hunter's separate approach lane. At L2, the 36-AP pool buys one such action; at L3, the 42-AP pool buys two. No extra automatic composite or reload benefit.
- The Hunter's genuine own-turn Step2 restores **up to 4 missing HP for matching Vigor**, even when higher-Initiative PCs hit it before that Step. The lead's own-turn Step also spends recipient Vigor when repairing missing HP. No Round-Begin restoration or extra Step is invented.
- Entire HP and Vigor packets of **one legal hit** resolve before its terminal check. The bounded trial ends on **Hunter's first HP≤0**, **lead's first HP≤0**, or **a Devastating unresolved wound**; it does not process subsequent toxin/Pounce/other actions. A distinct **contact_path_unresolved** category stops when a necessary approach or shot requires unmodeled occupancy, Living Screen, Cautious movement or a Movement Opportunity; it is **not** a hit, wound or win. If the Hunter's legitimate own-turn Regeneration Step has begun, that HP/Vigor restoration is still accounted for before testing its unresolved Bite route. Otherwise the trace stops at **one round**. A fallen Hunter is Dying, not automatically dead; a fallen lead is not a wiped party.

From repository root, the following script reports the terminal-category counts (including **contact_path_unresolved**), Pounce declarations/actual Balance resolutions, **how often an earlier moving actor disrupted the lane, F's pre-contact casts and MP spent entering flame range**, Full/Reduced accepted toxin instances, Spent VP, arrows fired, Vigor burn and mean Hunter HP for every one of the 32 case records:

**No numerical 32-cell census is asserted yet**: the new 4,096-trial matrix is *configured*, but the actual GitHub checkout and its runner need Codex/CI verification before any figures are published as results. Fourteen standard-library tests check sourced stats and damage, natural-20 Fire/Bow packet separation, terminal-Bite precedence, **front F acting before contact and actually moving/casting, a prior unmoving archer/Fire turn preserving Pounce, earlier melee approach breaking it, a non-front corridor block preventing illegal Hunter Bite, a front melee target enabling a legal stationary Bite, a second melee mover being denied the occupied square, a front Fire shot being halted by the intervening melee screen, and warned contact withholding the unresolved threatened cast**, seed replay, 32-cell conservation and bounded AP/ammunition/VP. Their presence does not by itself establish a passing CI run.

**Not simulated:** true map collision/cover and mixed Living Screen, multiple turns, actual Guard/Intervene of other PCs, accurate threatened-caster Withdrawal, adaptive Hunter redirection, actual Dying/wound repair, post-combat venom T+10/30/60/120, real Priest Rite/Exaltation or non-Conjure Scholar spells, VP six-hour Recovery, and player GM negotiation/flight. **No Hunter numeric CR or party victory probability is justified, and B06 remains an MVP blocker.**
