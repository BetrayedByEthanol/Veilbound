# October 2026 Mechanical Watchlist

This record explains why several review findings are being **watched rather than immediately changed**. It is design material, not player-facing canon.

## M1 — Intrusion and Manifestation balance

**Status: WATCH / intentional baseline**

Hostile Intrusion is intentionally difficult against a comparable unwilling target. The +8 Intrusion Barrier protects effects that can bypass ordinary physical interaction, including mind control, memory alteration, forced sleep, and internal transformation.

Manifestation does not universally bypass defense: manifested projectiles and pointed beams use ordinary ranged-defense procedures. Broad area Manifestations intentionally do not gain a generic spell-save or area-Dodge subsystem merely because they are magical.

Watch for whether Intrusion-focused characters have enough practical setup routes—capability advantage, deception, technique-specific leverage, teamwork, or changed circumstances—to make the playstyle useful without making hostile Intrusion routine.

## M3 — Agility usage

**Status: WATCH during subsystem completion**

Agility currently appears in many mature combat procedures: most weapon attacks, Guard pairings, Dodge, Initiative, thrown attacks, and manifested projectiles.

Do not rebalance ability usage from that snapshot alone. Social play, exploration, investigation, hazards, medicine, crafting, techniques, and other non-combat systems remain less developed. Reassess once the wider ability ecosystem exists.

Primary question: **can a character deliberately deprioritize Agility and still produce a strong coherent build?**

## M4 — Weapon balance

**Status: WATCH pending weapon identities**

Several current baseline profiles appear numerically dominant over others, but the weapon catalogue is still incomplete.

Do not finalize balance until Reach, Brace, handedness, Guard traits, close-range constraints, trauma distinctions, and likely family splits are developed. Expected future distinctions may include short/long swords, spear/pike families, halberds and other polearms, and additional one- versus two-handed profiles.

Current numerical imbalance is evidence to retest, not yet sufficient reason to flatten weapon profiles.

## M5 — Armor overlap

**Status: WATCH pending equipment systems**

Some armor profiles currently share Armor and Dodge-cap values.

This is acceptable provisionally because final differentiation may also come from:

- rune space;
- coverage;
- price;
- rarity and availability;
- maintenance;
- social acceptability;
- environmental behavior;
- future trauma or equipment interactions.

Reassess once those systems are sufficiently defined. Identical values in two columns do not by themselves require different Armor numbers.

## M6 — Flat HP and increasing attacks

**Status: VERIFY by simulation**

Flat HP combined with rising Physical Tempo could increase lethality with level, but attack count cannot be evaluated in isolation.

Simulation must include the defensive side of progression, including:

- increasing Guard capacity;
- Dodge and Dodge Pressure;
- ranged Evasion Bonus where applicable;
- defensive off-hands and shields;
- Interception and other defensive options;
- armor and future rune effects;
- Vigor scaling and regeneration;
- later defensive techniques, cover, and positioning rules.

The intended durability model allows weapons to remain physically dangerous at high level. The key test is whether expected successful damaging hits and burst lethality outpace the defensive and regenerative tools available at the same level.

## M7 — Miracle Debt and Pillar Accord Advances

**Status: WATCH / intentional tradeoff**

A Pillar Accord Advance deliberately presents a choice:

- **deepen the relationship** by increasing Pillar Accord; or
- **repay what is owed** by reducing Miracle Debt.

Repaying 1 Debt improves the next Petition modifier by 3, while increasing Accord by 1 improves that modifier by 2. This asymmetry is acceptable because Accord also improves other Priest benefits.

The important calibration control is **Advance frequency**. Advances should be uncommon and meaningful enough that repeated Miracle Petitions accumulate real consequences instead of being routinely erased. Watch for both extremes:

- Debt that is so easy to repay that Petition use has little long-term cost;
- Debt that accumulates so aggressively that Priests stop using their defining exceptional-intervention mechanic.

Exact Pillar Accord Advance cadence remains provisional.

## M9 — Ranged Evasion Bonus

**Status: WATCH**

Evasion Bonus currently applies to ranged Dodge rather than melee Dodge. This may be a deliberate expression of higher Physical Tempo improving the ability to read and evade ranged attacks without making melee Dodge scale twice with martial progression.

Do not broaden it automatically. Reassess during combat simulation and battlefield-defense work.

## Separate editorial follow-ups

The following review findings are not balance watch items:

- Physical Tempo can be expressed by its equivalent formulas as well as the existing table.
- VP Recovery needs an explicit .5 rounding rule.

They can be handled as ordinary documentation cleanup without waiting for the balance watchlist.
