# October 2026 Mechanical Watchlist

This record explains why several review findings are being **watched rather than immediately changed**. It is design material, not player-facing canon.

## M1 — Intrusion and Manifestation balance

**Status: WATCH / intentional baseline**

Hostile Intrusion is intentionally difficult against a comparable unwilling target. The +8 Intrusion Barrier protects effects that can bypass ordinary physical interaction, including mind control, memory alteration, forced sleep, and internal transformation.

Manifestation does not universally bypass defense: manifested projectiles and pointed beams use ordinary ranged-defense procedures. Broad area Manifestations intentionally do not gain a generic spell-save or area-Dodge subsystem merely because they are magical.

Watch for whether Intrusion-focused characters have enough practical setup routes—capability advantage, deception, technique-specific leverage, teamwork, or changed circumstances—to make the playstyle useful without making hostile Intrusion routine.

**Calibration target, not a locked rule:** a Veil-Control-focused character facing a comparable unwilling target should remain near the current low parity baseline without setup, but should be capable of reaching roughly **40–50% success after a meaningful setup advantage**. Playtest whether that target produces useful control play without trivializing the Intrusion Barrier.

## M3 — Agility usage

**Status: WATCH during subsystem completion**

Agility currently appears in many mature combat procedures: most weapon attacks, Guard pairings, Dodge, Initiative, thrown attacks, and manifested projectiles.

Do not rebalance ability usage from that snapshot alone. Social play, exploration, investigation, hazards, medicine, crafting, techniques, and other non-combat systems remain less developed. Reassess once the wider ability ecosystem exists.

Caster builds belong in this check as well: Veil Control now scales authored magical output, while projected attacks may also care about Agility and/or Precision. Standard Veil Control scaling currently increases authored **Vigor** damage, not ordinary HP damage from physical Manifestations; HP-scaling with Veil Control must be explicitly authored. Reassess whether this leaves Manifestation-focused casters with the right incentive to invest in Veil Control while also needing Agility and/or Precision.

Primary question: **can a character deliberately deprioritize Agility and still produce a strong coherent build?**

## M4 — Weapon balance

**Status: WATCH pending weapon identities**

Several current baseline profiles appear numerically dominant over others, but the weapon catalogue is still incomplete.

Do not finalize balance until Reach, Brace, handedness, Guard traits, close-range constraints, trauma distinctions, and likely family splits are developed. Expected future distinctions may include short/long swords, spear/pike families, halberds and other polearms, and additional one- versus two-handed profiles.

Current numerical imbalance is evidence to retest, not yet sufficient reason to flatten weapon profiles.

Explicit retest cases once the missing traits exist:

- Spear vs Sword;
- Mace vs Axe;
- Greatsword vs Mace + shield.

## M5 — Armor overlap

**Status: WATCH pending equipment systems**

Some armor profiles currently share Armor and Dodge-cap values.

This is acceptable provisionally because final differentiation may also come from:

- rune space;
- coverage;
- price;
- rarity and availability;
- maintenance;
- social acceptability;
- environmental behavior;
- future trauma or equipment interactions.

Reassess once those systems are sufficiently defined. Identical values in two columns do not by themselves require different Armor numbers.

Explicit retest cases:

- Chain vs Reinforced Leather;
- Brigandine vs Scale;
- Breastplate against both once Coverage is available.

## M6 — Durability across level

**Status: LOW-LEVEL RECOVERY INTENT LOCKED / HIGH-LEVEL VERIFY**

Wound repair is now **incremental and cumulative**. A low-level character may naturally repair a Critical wound whose total Repair Requirement exceeds Max Vigor, but doing so can require multiple Vigor-recovery cycles. The intended low-level consequence is therefore prolonged vulnerability and dependence on stabilization, safe recovery time, external healing, or exceptional reconstruction—not a requirement to level up before healing becomes possible.

This still requires a **content verification** once healing techniques exist: a typical level 1–3 party should have a plausible route to stabilize a Critical wound and survive the recovery window. External healing should meaningfully shorten or secure that recovery without making severe wounds trivial.

High-level lethality remains a simulation item. Flat HP combined with rising Physical Tempo could increase burst lethality with level, but attack count cannot be evaluated in isolation.

Simulation must include the defensive side of progression, including:

- increasing Guard capacity;
- Dodge and Dodge Pressure;
- ranged Evasion Bonus where applicable;
- defensive off-hands and shields;
- Interception and other defensive options;
- armor and future rune effects;
- Vigor scaling and regeneration;
- later defensive techniques, cover, and positioning rules.

The intended durability model allows weapons to remain physically dangerous at high level. The key test is whether expected successful damaging hits and burst lethality outpace the defensive and regenerative tools available at the same level.

## M7 — Miracle Debt and Pillar Accord Advances

**Status: WATCH / intentional tradeoff**

A Pillar Accord Advance deliberately presents a choice:

- **deepen the relationship** by increasing Pillar Accord; or
- **repay what is owed** by reducing Miracle Debt.

Repaying 1 Debt improves the next Petition modifier by 3, while increasing Accord by 1 improves that modifier by 2. This asymmetry is acceptable because Accord also improves other Priest benefits.

The important calibration control is **Advance frequency**. Advances should be uncommon and meaningful enough that repeated Miracle Petitions accumulate real consequences instead of being routinely erased. Watch for both extremes:

- Debt that is so easy to repay that Petition use has little long-term cost;
- Debt that accumulates so aggressively that Priests stop using their defining exceptional-intervention mechanic.

The Petition threshold itself is now explicit in canon: a final total of **12 or higher succeeds**. Exact Pillar Accord Advance cadence remains provisional.

## M9 — Ranged Evasion Bonus

**Status: WATCH**

Evasion Bonus currently applies to ranged Dodge rather than melee Dodge. This may be a deliberate expression of higher Physical Tempo improving the ability to read and evade ranged attacks without making melee Dodge scale twice with martial progression.

Do not broaden it automatically. Reassess during combat simulation and battlefield-defense work.

## Open equipment dependency — dual-wield weapon classes

**Status: OPEN / WAIT FOR WEAPON PASS**

Dual wield currently uses **Light** and **Medium** weapon-handling classes, but final assignments are not yet defined. These classes are separate from the existing **Light Physical Contribution** damage category.

Do not assign the dual-wield classes until the weapon-family, handedness, Reach, and related identity pass is far enough along to make the classifications meaningful. Consider renaming the Physical Contribution category during that pass to remove the terminology collision.

## Open wound calibration — 0 HP overkill

**Status: OPEN / VERIFY WITH DAMAGE SIMULATION**

Every 0-HP event creates a wound, and the current ladder allows Serious, Severe, Critical, Catastrophic, Irreparable, and Fatal outcomes.

Exact **overkill thresholds** for mapping a normal 0-HP event into Serious / Severe / Critical / Catastrophic severity remain unresolved. Calibrate these only after ordinary weapon damage, armor, Physical Tempo, and defensive progression have been simulated together.

Irreparable and Fatal outcomes should continue to depend on the actual physical injury/location and circumstances rather than being produced solely by crossing a numeric HP threshold.

## Veil Control and HP damage

**Status: WATCH DURING TECHNIQUE AUTHORING**

The Standard Veil Control packet currently scales authored **Vigor damage**. Physical Manifestations and other ordinary HP-damage packets do not receive Standard Veil Control damage automatically.

This is intentional as a baseline, but technique authoring must verify that:

- Veil Control remains worthwhile for Manifestation-focused casters;
- physical HP-damage techniques do not double-dip by receiving both strong physical damage and the full standard Vigor-scaling packet;
- techniques that should scale HP damage with Veil Control explicitly author a smaller or otherwise calibrated coefficient.

## Action-Unit technique mixing

**Status: WATCH / possible rework**

Physical Tempo formulas now define the current martial Attack/Guard cadence, but they do not settle how many Attack-Unit Veil techniques should be repeatable in one turn or how freely martial attacks and techniques should be mixed at high Tempo.

Watch for cases where rising Physical Tempo unintentionally becomes a multiplier on full technique output. Technique cadence, Attack-Unit permissions, and mixed martial/magical turns may need a dedicated action-economy pass once authored techniques exist.
