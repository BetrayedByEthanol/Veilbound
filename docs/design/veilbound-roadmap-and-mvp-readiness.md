# Veilbound — Development Roadmap and Level 1–3 MVP Readiness

> **Design status review — 17 October 2026, after merged PRs #78 (Mundane Toxins) and #79 ([Venom Endurance v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/venom-endurance-v0.md)). Universal Low Priest Devotional Rite eligibility **merged in PR #80**. The universal Level-3 Exaltation framework, two Life properties and cross-Pillar paper tests are **merged in PR #81**. Two complete legal Low Priest Life L1→3 build fixtures and deterministic Exaltation ledgers are documented in the **current verification change**, pending review; full 12-build/encounter certification stays open.** This roadmap tracks definition readiness, not proven numerical balance, character-sheet completion, or certification. Use the [Rules](../rules/README.md) for executable v0 authority.

## What changed in this checkpoint

- **PR #78 merged — Mundane Toxins v0:** [Mundane Toxins](../rules/05-combat/mundane-toxins-v0.md) now canonically supplies **11 real-material biological/chemical families**, real exposure delivery, **one d20+Vitality** natural check (DC10 Minor / DC12 Moderate; Reduced effect rather than immunity on success), one active instance per recipient, toxin-only short exploration clocks, 600-round-per-hour Round Begin mapping for long events and independent TX-HE six-hour Recovery handling. No magical Death/Plague toxins, species or alchemy recipes follow; TX-01–19 remain provisional. Separately merged **PR #79 [Venom Endurance v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/venom-endurance-v0.md)** provides one incoming-application prevention or finite suppression of an existing instance; its merged profile raised the **Tier-1 Pillar total from 12 to 13**, Life **5/6 → 6/6**, still **10/24 MVP Aspects**, and **0/12 certified builds**.
- **PR #77 merged — Woodland Rapport v0:** [Woodland Rapport](../rules/07-veil-magic/woodland-rapport-v0.md) is the second canonical Life/Nature Tier-1 technique, with authored normal Used **T1–3**, target mass **25/125/625 kg inclusive**, ordinary **2/4/6 Spent VP**, 10–30-minute VC-scaled noncoercive sensory cues, and **+2 once** to genuine first peaceful animal-contact check. Animal control, semantic communication, Companion, Secondary/Rider and new Consecration/Rite remain unauthored. **WR-01–06** balance watches continue. **At this PR #77 milestone** there were 12 merged Tier-1 Pillar techniques / 10 of 24 MVP Aspects / 5 of 6 candidate T1 Life techniques; **the sixth, Venom Endurance, subsequently merged in PR #79**.
- **PR #76 merged — first Life / Nature executable profile, Gentle Growth v0:** [Gentle Growth](../rules/07-veil-magic/gentle-growth-v0.md) is canonical one-minute permanent ordinary development of living vegetation (one flexible 0.5 m shoot / three mature buds / one 5 cm seedling); **1 Spent VP** with genuine botanical viability and resource conservation. **No combat entanglement, biomass conjuration, Tier-2 upcast, Nature-derived Consecration or Rite**. Life now has **3/3 canonical Aspect identities represented by actual Tier-1 techniques**, though one Tier-1 Preservation candidate and a second Nature candidate remain for breadth; GG-01–04 remain watch items.
- **PR #75 merged — B05 Life / Preservation, Vital Shelter v0:** [Vital Shelter](../rules/07-veil-magic/vital-shelter-v0.md) is **canonical executable v0**: **8+VC finite Vigor-damage prevention**, fixed **2 Spent VP / 1 minute** or maintained **2 Committed +1 Spent**, with a **4+floor(VC/2)** composite-compatible Secondary for specific Basic Heal/Stone Skin High Scholar cases. It establishes a second distinct **Medium Priest Consecration** and alternative **Low Priest `Self` Devotional Rite** (Chosen **1 Committed +1 Spent**); one Active Low Rite at L1–3, two only at L4. **VS-01 recast**, **VS-02 composite efficiency** and **VS-03 specialist Conflagration stress test** remain balance watches; merger does **not** certify L1–3 builds.

After merged PR #75, both Guided Regeneration and Vital Shelter are legal but different Medium Consecrations and Low Devotional Rite alternatives, not verified Focus builds. Starting counts from merged PR #72 and all numerical balance remain provisional.

- **PR #63 merged — first executable Conflagration technique:** [Conjure Flame v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/conjure-flame-v0.md) supplies all three delivery profiles, Primary/Secondary/Rider formulas, Focus AP, Throwing accuracy, Reserve/disarm/drop rules, and spontaneous/prepared arrows; [elemental resistance](../rules/07-veil-magic/elemental-damage-resistance-v0.md) and [ammunition](../rules/05-combat/prepared-and-spontaneous-ammunition-v0.md) foundations define separate Fire HP/Vigor and discounted 24-hour prepared ammo. **All spell damage and preparation prices/timing remain provisional playtest values; casting still requires Archetype-specific access and B05's eight-Pillar catalogue is incomplete.**
- **PR #64 merged — Basic Heal v0 + universal Intrusion clarification:** [Basic Heal v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/basic-heal-v0.md) establishes instant Life / Restoration HP/Vigor replenishment and small +2/+1 Dying wound progress by conversion of its own HP component. [Intrusion rules](../rules/07-veil-magic/resonance-and-intrusion.md#intrusion-barrier) distinguish **harmful or actively resisted** (+8), **genuinely deceived harmful acceptance** (+4), and **genuinely beneficial and unopposed** (no contest, even unconscious). Values and resisted-cast Ability pairs are v0 playtest numbers. **Neither automatic spell access nor broad restorative content is granted.**
- **PR #67 merged — Weaken Restraints v0:** [Liberty / Defiance](../rules/07-veil-magic/weaken-restraints-v0.md) authors a touch-only **`Touched Object` Primary** requiring **one Full Action**, **base 2 Spent VP** for every Focus (eligible **TAL-M03** can lock the effective cost at **1 VP**, rechecked before application), **no free hand**, and temporary weakening for **60 seconds outside initiative** or until **Round Begin R+10** when cast during round R in combat, affecting one nonmagical restraint. The reduced DC has **Continuous Expression Cadence** (one static effect usable by separately paid break actions, no repeat magic or upkeep), and any selected **TAL-M03** use is **consumed on legal declaration even if the cast later fails before VP application**. Transitions use a technique-only conservative floor conversion that may shorten the effect rather than extend it. Material break DC caps are **12/10/8 − raw signed VC**, never increasing the original DC; the caster does not automatically break the restraint or gain a free physical escape check. Attended restraints use **Strength + Vitality** Direct Veil Resistance and highest applicable non-waived attendee, with no contest for an ordinary unattended mundane restraint. It is the fifth **merged** representative Pillar technique rather than automatic Archetype acquisition; numeric balance and Low-Focus escape viability remain provisional.
- **PR #68 merged — Protective Ward v0, Kinship / Guardian:** [Protective Ward](../rules/07-veil-magic/protective-ward-v0.md) adds the sixth representative executable Pillar technique: **Tier 1**, a single pre-roll **Reaction + base 2 Spent VP** creating a one-attack **magical shield at another visible ally's occupied space within six direct 3D square-equivalents (nominally 9 m)** using a technique-local sorted-axis distance metric `a + 0.5b + 0.5c` (no rounding), with clear magical targeting path and perceived attack, **no Guard capacity, Turn AP or AP debt**. An ordinary attack must first be a hit against the ally; then the **same attack total** is compared to caster's **standard-shield Guard Defense = 10 + Strength + Agility + eligible shield-style Training + 2 (+ expressly applicable general Guard modifiers)**. A legal **natural 20 cannot be blocked**; one Ward Defense confirmation sets original ally's Strong/Critical/Devastating severity. Once successfully applied, the Ward **follows the named ally during later same-window Reactive Blink or other movement only while the originally committed attack remains unresolved**; after all spontaneous/nested responses including attacker Blink-follow, recheck attack legality and the Guardian's **6-square 3D range/perception/straight targeting path**. Loss ends Ward **without refunding applied VP or Reaction**. A successfully Ward-blocked area-bearing projectile **impacts the original ally's final occupied square after these responses**; for multi-square allies, use the **precommitted target cell's stable footprint index mapped into the final footprint** (or deterministic final reference-square fallback), without post-roll impact choice. Original attacker/cover/area-bearing projectile interactions, one-protector exclusivity and response-window priorities are retained. **No physical shield required, no free dodge/movement, no direct magical Intrusion protection or attack transfer to caster.** Explicitly provisional range/cost/defense calibration, not automatic Archetype repertoire acquisition.
- **PR #69 merged — Phantom Sound v0, Secrets / Hallucination:** [Phantom Sound](../rules/07-veil-magic/phantom-sound-v0.md) authors the **seventh distinct selected MVP Pillar representative technique** (Tier 1, Transform Aspect): **`Localized Sound` Primary** costs one Focus-priced Projection Action and **1 Spent VP** to make **one ≤2-second localized nonverbal external sound** at a directly visible/open-line source within **12 local 3D square-equivalents**; **`Ammunition Shot` Primary** integrates **one 1-VP sound** with a normal physical arrow/bolt (0 additional spell AP; VP paid on **launch**, sound on **first actual physical contact** including a real missed-projectile collision, not a guaranteed chosen scatter location). **No forced distraction, stealth bonuses, mind Intrusion, damaging/volume scaling, prepared ammunition, stackable second enchantment, independent weapon enchantment or freely activated Rider**; the optional Rider contribution awaits separate authorization. Actual audibility/investigation use ordinary noncombat mechanics. **Phantom Image stays a separate unauthored technique**; at the time of PR #69, **Arts** was the last selected Pillar without a representative, subsequently supplied by merged PR #70. All values are provisional; no automatic Archetype knowledge is implied.

Pay **one Focus-priced Projection Action (20/24/30 Turn AP) + base 2 Spent VP**; eligible own-turn Frugal Technique can lock 1 VP. The effect supplies **+2 exactly once** on the **first genuine creative/expressive noncombat Ability check** for **one declared undertaking**, including a specifically invoked one-time passive check, **not general Presence/Persuasion/Performance, a second social outcome, invented expertise or forced audience behavior**. Benefit expires upon use/completion/abandonment, **600 elapsed seconds outside combat or Round Begin R+100 in initiative**, with conservative local transition conversion; one pending Spark per recipient. Beneficial knowingly accepted **Intrusion** has no Direct Veil contest, **no hostile/resisted mode**. VP/bonus/duration provisional; eight sample techniques **do not** complete legal starting repertoires, all 12 Archetype × Focus builds, or L1–3 playtesting.

## Level 1–3 tabletop MVP — target and exit criteria

**Target:** a **3–5 session** short adventure, levels **1–3**, able to exercise **all twelve Archetype × Focus combinations** through valid example builds and representative encounter/noncombat tests. This does **not** require every combination to be played through an entire campaign, but it does require each to be **constructible and capable of meaningful contribution**, with representative coverage in actual playtest.

**MVP exit test:**

1. **Create:** independently build a legal character for each of **4 Archetypes × 3 Focuses** from current instructions, with explicit starting gear, costs, available techniques/features and derived stats.
2. **Act:** each representative character has usable attacks/defenses, meaningful noncombat choices and, where their identity relies on magic, an **authored playable Pillar/Aspect or Archetype technique set**, not only Inspection/Blink.
3. **Resolve:** run combat with turn economy, defense, critical damage, wounds, Dying, basic movement/terrain, cover and protective intervention, using fixed native rules rather than defaulting to D&D.
4. **Advance:** take the representative builds from **level 1 to 2 to 3**, applying all changes in attributes/resources, action/Guard/Tempo, Talent eligibility and spell/technique repertoire. **Blink unlocks at level 3**, but Low Focus may still be unable to pay its base 10 VP; the unlock is **not free VP**.
5. **Challenge:** run several prebuilt enemies, 1–3 encounter types, a short exploratory problem and at least one social/investigation interaction using the authored check engine. Benchmark challenge and healing/recovery plausibility.
6. **Observe:** record unclear interactions, time spent resolving turns, combat length, survivability, Focus tradeoffs, technique affordability and outcomes. Mark v0 balances provisional until the test evidence exists.

## Scope and status discipline

- **LOCKED** means the authored procedure or structural choice exists in current canonical rules, **not** that every related content record or number is complete.
- **V0 / PROVISIONAL** means an executable playtest baseline exists, with explicit calibration work still open.
- **MISSING / OPEN** means do not treat unimplemented content as automatically granted or import D&D/5e mechanics to conceal the absence.
- Track future changed statuses against **merged PRs and canonical rule files**. Historical audits can remain as history; they are not a competing live progress tracker.
