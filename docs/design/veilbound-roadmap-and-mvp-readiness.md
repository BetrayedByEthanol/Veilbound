# Veilbound — Development Roadmap and Level 1–3 MVP Readiness

> **Design status review — 14 October 2026, following merged PR #63 (Conjure Flame), #64 (Basic Heal), #65 (Minor Telekinesis), and proposed Stone Skin v0 on this feature branch.** This is a **progress tracker and prioritization analysis**, **not** a rules authority. For executable rules use [Canonical Rules](../rules/README.md), not this document, the historical [Core Rules Audit v34](audits/Veilbound_Core_Rules_Audit_v34.md), or past roadmap estimates. The 17 rows retain the existing development-roadmap area taxonomy; progress describes **rule readiness**, not percentage of content, automated implementation, balance, or playtest completion.

## What changed in this checkpoint

- **PR #63 merged — first executable Conflagration technique:** [Conjure Flame v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/conjure-flame-v0.md) supplies all three delivery profiles, Primary/Secondary/Rider formulas, Focus AP, Throwing accuracy, Reserve/disarm/drop rules, and spontaneous/prepared arrows; [elemental resistance](../rules/07-veil-magic/elemental-damage-resistance-v0.md) and [ammunition](../rules/05-combat/prepared-and-spontaneous-ammunition-v0.md) foundations define separate Fire HP/Vigor and discounted 24-hour prepared ammo. **All spell damage and preparation prices/timing remain provisional playtest values; casting still requires Archetype-specific access and B05's eight-Pillar catalogue is incomplete.**
- **PR #64 merged — Basic Heal v0 + universal Intrusion clarification:** [Basic Heal v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/basic-heal-v0.md) establishes instant Life / Restoration HP/Vigor replenishment and small +2/+1 Dying wound progress by conversion of its own HP component. [Intrusion rules](../rules/07-veil-magic/resonance-and-intrusion.md#intrusion-barrier) distinguish **harmful or actively resisted** (+8), **genuinely deceived harmful acceptance** (+4), and **genuinely beneficial and unopposed** (no contest, even unconscious). Values and resisted-cast Ability pairs are v0 playtest numbers. **Neither automatic spell access nor broad restorative content is granted.**
- **Stone Skin v0 (this proposed PR):** [Earth / Fortification](../rules/07-veil-magic/stone-skin-v0.md) defines maintained **Physical/Fire/Electrical HP resistance**, unaffected by mundane-versus-magical origin, with **highest applicable resistance source only** and separate ordinary Armor; no Vigor mitigation. Normal activation is one Focus-priced Projection Action (High 20/Medium 24/Low 30 AP), while Reactive activation is **one Reaction with no AP debt** before an incoming attack roll or perceptible imminent hazard. Both commit **ceil(Max VP/3)** until unconsciousness/release. Stone Skin is `Self` Primary only; a normal on-turn High Scholar composite can deliver Basic Heal's matching `Self` Secondary once on cast, not as a reactive/free repeat. These are executable proposed playtest mechanics, not automatic spell acquisition.
- **PR #65 merged — Minor Telekinesis v0:** [Mind / Psionics](../rules/07-veil-magic/minor-telekinesis-v0.md) now has an executable `Single Object` **Imposition** profile with 20-square target range, 6-square instant displacement, caster-elevation ceiling, simultaneous mass/volume/dimension limits and **Strength check against Force DC `6 + VC`**. An unwilling item holder receives the existing Imposition contest first and then a free existing-grip Strength check; a creature already obstructing the declared object path has a free Strength check (success stops object, failure pushes aside if legal, otherwise Prone), while a new passing-object **Grab** consumes one Reaction. No extra damage, creature-targeted telekinesis, persistent hold or free Rider. Numerical force/object capacities and ability pairs remain v0 provisional.
- **PR #62 merged — Technique Structure v0:** [Technique Structure v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/technique-structure-v0.md) established independent **Primary/Secondary/Rider contributions**, technique-specific profiles, default Focus-priced single-spell AP, Spent versus Committed VP, Reserved Weapon Disarm channel break, and the retained existing High Scholar Composite Casting authority. PR #63 refined exact profile matching and explicit integrated shot exceptions.
- **PR #61 merged — eight-Pillar MVP content scope selected:** [B05 Eight-Pillar Selection and Coverage Validation](b05-eight-pillar-mvp-selection-and-coverage.md) chooses **Fire, Life, Earth, Liberty, Mind, Kinship, Secrets, Arts** after reviewing the new canonical identities and verifying **278 Strong-only one-to-one Gifted emotion/Pillar matchings** (historical A: 202; prior B: 188). The roster has **domain coverage potential** for combat, healing, defense, traversal, information, social support, stealth and cooperation; **no executable Aspect catalogue or twelve-build gameplay test has passed**. Alchemy/reagents and advanced crafting are excluded from the first pilot unless later required.
- **PR #60 merged:** [Canonical Aspect Identities and Boundaries](../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md) established all **48 named Aspects**, External/Internal/Transform categories and positive/negative boundaries. The previous PR #59 E?/I?/T? hypotheses are historical; **48 × 8 Gifted ratings remain untouched**. This is **lore/authoring guidance**, not 48 executable techniques. Do not conflate the **B05 identity milestone** with the **B05 MVP technique-content blocker** below.
- **PR #59 merged:** The [mechanical coverage and Gifted compatibility audit](16-pillar-48-aspect-coverage-compatibility-audit.md) checked 16 Pillars/48 Aspects and retained two valid but **unselected** eight-Pillar MVP candidates. Its provisional category assignments have since been superseded by the **merged** canonical 48-Aspect lore reference.
- **PR #57 merged:** [Universal Veil Fundamentals](../rules/07-veil-magic/universal-veil-fundamentals.md) now grants **Basic Veil Inspection automatically at character level 1** and **Blink automatically at level 3** to **Priest, Scholar, Gifted, and Anointed**, independent of Focus. No Talent, Learned Discipline, Prepared Prayer slot, Scholar Pillar-technique slot, Gifted emotion link or Anointed bond is spent on acquisition. The level gate does **not** provide free VP, actions or special Archetype compatibility. The Archetype and metaphysical guides are synchronized.
- **PR #56 already merged:** [Veil Inspection v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/veil-inspection-v0.md) is a **Full Action**, **base 2 Spent VP**, one **physically held item**, observable magical presence and discernible **Pillar/Aspect signatures**, plus **+2 to one relevant Veil Theory interpretation check** during the examination. Concealed/dormant effects, full enchantment function, and **Veil Analysis** remain separate/not executable.
- **PR #55 already merged:** [Blink v0](../rules/07-veil-magic/blink-v0.md) is a **base 10 VP** Universal Translocation operation with normal Focus-priced Projection Action, valid Reactive Blink with next-own-turn AP debt, live arrival view, straight unobstructed path, safe arrival, no fixed range and constrained counter-Blink. Its cost respects legal reductions such as on-turn **Frugal Technique**; failed attempts expend committed AP/Reaction but no VP.
- The previous first three MVP combat blockers have **authored deterministic v0 rules**: [Dodge Pressure](../rules/05-combat/defensive-responses-and-criticals.md#dodge) is a nonnegative penalty subtracted once; [Strong/Critical damage](../rules/05-combat/defensive-responses-and-criticals.md#strong-hit-critical-hit-and-devastating-critical-damage--v0-playtest-baseline) adds v0 dice; and [0-HP wound severity](../rules/06-vigor-wounds-death/dying-and-wounds.md#wound-severity) uses deterministic overkill bands. **The procedures are closed as definition blockers; numerical calibration and simulation remain open.**
- **B07 is an overloaded label across previous work:** the merged **B07 Talents v0** work is **not** the same as **MVP blocker B07 — a complete level-1-to-3 advancement path**. Keep those scopes separate rather than declaring the advancement blocker done because the Talent catalogue exists.

## Level 1–3 tabletop MVP — target and exit criteria

**Target:** a **3–5 session** short adventure, levels **1–3**, able to exercise **all twelve Archetype × Focus combinations** through valid example builds and representative encounter/noncombat tests. This does **not** require every combination to be played through an entire campaign, but it does require each to be **constructible and capable of meaningful contribution**, with representative coverage in actual playtest.

**MVP exit test:**

1. **Create:** independently build a legal character for each of **4 Archetypes × 3 Focuses** from current instructions, with explicit starting gear, costs, available techniques/features and derived stats.
2. **Act:** each representative character has usable attacks/defenses, meaningful noncombat choices and, where their identity relies on magic, an **authored playable Pillar/Aspect or Archetype technique set**, not only Inspection/Blink.
3. **Resolve:** run combat with turn economy, defense, critical damage, wounds, Dying, basic movement/terrain, cover and protective intervention, using fixed native rules rather than defaulting to D&D.
4. **Advance:** take the representative builds from **level 1 to 2 to 3**, applying all changes in attributes/resources, action/Guard/Tempo, Talent eligibility and spell/technique repertoire. **Blink unlocks at level 3**, but Low Focus may still be unable to pay its base 10 VP; the unlock is **not free VP**.
5. **Challenge:** run several prebuilt enemies, 1–3 encounter types, a short exploratory problem and at least one social/investigation interaction using the authored check engine. Benchmark challenge and healing/recovery plausibility.
6. **Observe:** record unclear interactions, time spent resolving turns, combat length, survivability, Focus tradeoffs, technique affordability and outcomes. Mark v0 balances provisional until the test evidence exists.

**Excluded from the tabletop MVP by default:** Veil Analysis/Scrying, long-range/advanced translocation and portals; levels 4–20 Specialization/Mastery; full magical crafting/runes; campaign-scale faction and travel simulations; comprehensive bestiary; production PWA or automated character builder. Include one only if the chosen pilot adventure actually requires it.

## Scope and status discipline

- **LOCKED** means the authored procedure or structural choice exists in current canonical rules, **not** that every related content record or number is complete.
- **V0 / PROVISIONAL** means an executable playtest baseline exists, with explicit calibration work still open.
- **MISSING / OPEN** means do not treat unimplemented content as automatically granted or import D&D/5e mechanics to conceal the absence.
- Track future changed statuses against **merged PRs and canonical rule files**. Historical audits can remain as history; they are not a competing live progress tracker.
