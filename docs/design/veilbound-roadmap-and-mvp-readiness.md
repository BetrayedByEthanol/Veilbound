## Status and evidence policy

- **Delivered:** merged implementation or authored design. This does **not** certify a full playable feature.
- **Structural build:** legal sheet, level/resource/repertoire checks, not an independent Archetype × Focus certificate.
- **Native evidence:** actual source actors/actions, costs and G0 event receipts; **bounded** evidence has an explicitly narrow policy, geometry, round limit or unresolved branch.
- **MVP accepted:** all six exit gates below pass independently; no result may be inferred from an attractive scenario label or a test's pass status.
- **Historical:** original report scope and cryptographic digests are frozen; do not repurpose early-stop censuses into win rates.
- **Stable B06 milestones:** G0–G8 are the existing implementation gates (with G4.1–4.5 and G7-A–H subphases). New source-specific integrations such as PR #148 belong **within G1/G5**, not new G9-style milestones. A delivered mechanism is not a closed B06 MVP blocker or complete Focus certification.
- **Counting:** one Archetype × Focus pairing counts once regardless of alternate builds, individual spells, test numbers or enemy policies. Separate *implemented*, *bounded native*, *independently certified*, and *representative-playtest* states.
