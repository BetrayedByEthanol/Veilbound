# Core Resolution

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34. Only rules already marked locked are promoted here; unresolved critical-check and inherited combat formulas remain in the audit backlog.

## When to roll

Do not roll for routine or trivial actions that the character can reasonably perform automatically. Training may also make an action routine for one character even when it would be uncertain for another.

For uncertain actions, use one of three procedures.

### Passive

Use:

```
10 + Ability + applicable Training + situational modifiers
```

Use passive resolution for things a character does or notices naturally without deliberately spending time on the task.

### Active with sufficient safe time

When repeated attempts are possible and failure itself carries no serious consequence, the player may:

- roll normally;
- Take 10; or
- Take 20.

The GM determines the fictional time required. Take 20 represents exhaustive effort; it is not literal guaranteed success.

### Active under pressure or limited time

Roll:

```
d20 + Ability + applicable Training + situational modifiers
```

Take 10 and Take 20 are unavailable.

## Difficulty

General checks compare against:

```
10 + Difficulty Modifier
```

Difficulty is set from the circumstances rather than being an immutable property of an object.

| Difficulty | Modifier | Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Easy under pressure | -3 | 7 |
| Favorable | -1 | 9 |
| Standard | +0 | 10 |
| Challenging | +2 | 12 |
| Hard | +5 | 15 |
| Exceptional | +8 | 18 |
| Extreme | +10 | 20 |

Tasks below this range are normally automatic rather than rolled. Higher targets remain possible for genuinely extraordinary circumstances.

## Player skill and character skill

Players choose approaches and make decisions; character abilities determine what the character knows, notices, communicates and executes.

A player may deduce something from clues already available even while playing a low-Intellect character, but information the character would need to know can still depend on Intellect and relevant Training. Conversely, a high-Intellect character may receive deductions or contextual clues that the player personally missed.

The same principle applies socially: players do not need to personally deliver an eloquent speech to use a high-Presence character. Strong or weak approaches primarily change the situation and difficulty rather than replacing character statistics.

## Natural 1

On an attack roll, a natural 1 is an automatic miss. It carries no universal fumble or critical-failure effect.

Attack-roll natural-20 handling is defined in [Defensive Responses and Criticals](../05-combat/defensive-responses-and-criticals.md#natural-20-and-confirmation). Non-combat natural-1/20 handling remains unresolved unless a specific rule states otherwise.
