# Abilities and Training

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34.

## Native abilities

Veilbound uses nine direct ability modifiers.

- **Strength** — raw muscular power.
- **Agility** — gross motor control, movement, balance and bodily coordination.
- **Precision** — fine motor control and delicate manipulation.
- **Intellect** — reasoning, memory and learned understanding.
- **Awareness** — observation, instinct and environmental notice.
- **Presence** — social force, self-expression, persuasion and command. Physical attractiveness is not part of Presence and may instead be represented by traits.
- **Vitality** — physical resilience and the primary survivability ability.
- **Veil Affinity** — magical capacity: how much Veil power a character can contain and channel.
- **Veil Control** — magical execution: how precisely, efficiently and forcefully a character shapes Veil power.

Abilities use their modifier directly; there is no legacy 3–18 score plus derived modifier.

No whole Archetype is tied to a mandatory mundane casting ability. Mundane abilities may contribute to specific magical actions when the action genuinely depends on them.

## Level-1 ability generation

All nine abilities begin at **-2**. A level-1 character receives **28 ability points**.

| Final score | Total cost |
|---:|---:|
| -2 | 0 |
| -1 | 1 |
| 0 | 2 |
| +1 | 3 |
| +2 | 5 |
| +3 | 7 |
| +4 | 10 |

The normal level-1 maximum is **+4**.

A balanced default of two abilities at +3 and the remaining seven at 0 costs exactly 28 points.

## Ability advancement

At levels **4, 8, 12, 16 and 20**, increase one ability of the player's choice by +1.

There is no special catch-up rule for negative abilities. Normal permanent natural ability advancement is capped at **+5**. Extraordinary Veil enhancement, transformation, relics or similar effects may exceed that natural cap.

## Training structure — B04

> **LOCKED design choices:** distinct Weapon Training and Learned Disciplines budgets; one point buys +1 directly invested rank; no generic Perception/Persuasion-style umbrella Training; earned/background familiarity is not purchased expertise. **PROVISIONAL v0 values:** starting points, +5 ceilings, advancement level gates, and schedules. See [Weapon Training and Style Refinements](../05-combat/weapon-training-and-refinements-v0.md) for weapon-family mapping, melee half-transfer and shield Guard.

Training represents **specific learned practice or expertise**; ordinary ability, judgment, presence, perception, personal experience and decisions remain on the relevant **Ability** or the fiction. Training is **not permanently tied to one Ability**: Medicine may pair with Intellect for diagnosis or Precision for surgery; Climbing and Swimming can pair with Strength, Agility or Vitality according to the task. Knowledge that would be routine for a genuinely trained character can be provided without a roll.

| Learned rank | Bonus |
|---|---:|
| None / Untrained | +0 |
| Familiar | +1 |
| Trained | +2 |
| Expert | +3 |
| Master | +4 |
| Grandmaster (**v0 extension**) | +5 |

The previous general Training catalogue stopped at +4; this **provisional B04 extension** allows +5 in **both** Weapon Training and Learned Disciplines at high levels. These are competence bonuses, not automatic level bonuses: each directly purchased +1 costs **one corresponding development point**, and the character must meet the level cap. No Training may add its bonus twice to one check, and a Talent/Refinement cannot invent additional universal Training bonuses.

## Learned Disciplines — practical, professional and scholarly

**Learned Disciplines** use a separate budget from Weapon Training. They can be useful in or out of combat, but they **do not** automatically modify weapon Attack/Guard or direct Veil attack rolls. A discipline includes the **ordinary techniques and supporting knowledge intrinsic to that trade**: Medicine includes medical anatomy and standard diagnosis; Smithing includes everyday relevant materials and forge practice. Do not require five microscopic sub-disciplines merely to practice one profession.

Organize the catalogue into **four descriptive families** for character-creation guidance. For the standard **4 / 2 / 2 starting allocation**, each listed discipline belongs to **exactly one family** below; its points may count toward that family's primary or secondary allocation, **never both**. The families are **not** separate permanent advancement pools.

### Complete starting-family assignment

| Family | Canonical disciplines assigned to this family |
|---|---|
| **Physical and Fieldcraft** | Climbing; Swimming; Acrobatics; Riding; Throwing; Navigation; Tracking; Hunting; Survival (**bounded fieldcraft**) |
| **Professional and Technical** | Lockwork; Sleight of Hand; Trapwork; Smithing; Carpentry; Tailoring; Engineering; Medicine; Herbalism; Alchemy; profession-specific Trades |
| **Scholarly** | Veil Theory; Veil Practice; Pillar Lore; regional/era History; regional Law; Religion & Cults |
| **Cultural and Specialized** | Disguise; Acting; cultural Etiquette; regional Politics; Emotional Discipline; Oratory*; Negotiation*; Interrogation*; Command* |

**The starred labels are legacy broad social entries, not general-purpose purchasable bonuses.** A player may spend points under **Cultural and Specialized** on one of them only after naming a **bounded trainable practice** (for example, formal oratory, diplomatic protocol, interviewing methods or military command procedure) rather than buying a universal social modifier. This family assignment does not relax the [social interaction boundary](#social-perception-and-investigation-boundary).

**Custom narrow Trainings** remain allowed. Before allocating points, the player and GM identify **one fixed primary family** for each new discipline from its actual learned subject or practice. A custom discipline counts toward **only that family** throughout starting allocation; it cannot be reclassified between primary and secondary points to meet the 4 / 2 / 2 split. The character may instead use the already-authored **unrestricted eight-point specialization alternative**. A discipline's learned subject can be used with different Abilities without changing its family.

Examples of newly scoped disciplines: a defined heraldic tradition or language falls under **Cultural and Specialized**; a specific historical research field under **Scholarly**; a particular learned craft under **Professional and Technical**. A skill's **scope must be meaningful**: a whole profession should not be arbitrarily fragmented, but one broad `Survival` skill must not replace Tracking, Hunting, Navigation, Herbalism and every other expedition discipline. If retained, Survival is **fieldcraft for shelter, exposure and practical camp conditions**, not an all-purpose wilderness bonus. Knowledge fields should cover meaningful eras/regions/institutions, not individual conversations or one village's gossip.

### Social, perception and investigation boundary

- There is **no universal Persuasion, Negotiation, Interrogation, Command, Perception, Investigation, Athletics, Deception or Stealth bonus**. Existing catalogue labels such as Oratory, Negotiation, Interrogation and Command are **legacy broad labels** until a concrete **bounded learned practice** (e.g. formal rhetoric, diplomatic protocol, military command procedure, interrogative interviewing technique) is specified. They are **not** general-purpose bonuses to Presence, Awareness or every social encounter.
- Social outcomes derive from the player's **approach, stated objective, available evidence, relationships, opponent's interests and situation**; use **Presence** when a check is necessary, or another Ability where the actual approach warrants it. A player need not personally deliver eloquent speeches to roleplay a high-Presence character; they may describe the character's argument or intent.
- A chosen action can work because of a good idea or established fact without a Search/Investigation roll. Awareness notices; Intellect interprets; relevant **narrow technical expertise** is added only when genuinely material to the task.
- **Veil Practice** remains a trained practical procedure, **not** a universal casting or Direct Veil Pressure bonus. **Emotional Discipline** remains its specifically authored learned emotional-regulation practice rather than a generic social bonus.

See [Non-Combat Play](../04-non-combat/non-combat-play.md) for the established Ability-first resolution.

### Starting allocation — 8 points, standard 4 / 2 / 2

At level 1, receive **8 Learned Discipline points** and spend one point for each +1 directly invested rank (normal creation cap **+2 per discipline**). The **standard guided allocation** is:

| Allocation | Points | Requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Primary family | 4 | Distribute among one chosen Learned Discipline family |
| Secondary family | 2 | Choose a **different** family from the primary |
| Flexible | 2 | Invest in any family |
| **Total** | **8** | Every point buys +1 |

This **encourages** coherent broad preparation without guaranteeing every character is well-rounded. **Deliberately specialized alternative:** a player may assign all 8 without the family split when a coherent history or education supports it; the **same eight-point total and +2 creation cap** still apply. No additional GM-provided numerical bonus is attached to either method.

### Background familiarities are not Trainings

Choose or describe **two or three ordinary background familiarities**—e.g. a hometown and its inhabitants, a previous trade community, or a cultural environment. A character also knows their own normal lived experience and things learned during play without buying them with points. Familiarity gives **contextual knowledge, not +1/+2 numerical Training, secret facts or guaranteed connections**. A local may know the quartermaster without regional Politics Training; systematic analysis of the institution can still benefit from a relevant narrow Learned Discipline.

### Learned Discipline progression — provisional v0

**One Learned Discipline point** is awarded at **levels 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 and 20**; no automatic points at other levels. The purchasable rank cap is +2 at levels 1–3, +3 at levels 4–7, +4 at levels 8–11, and **+5 at level 12 onward**. Total points available by level 20 are **18 including the starting 8**. A character may broaden their knowledge or keep specializing; points can be held for an eligible investment.

A **Discipline Refinement** is a future, **individually authored benefit linked to one nominated discipline** and purchased with points instead of another +1; it must not act as a free-floating, universal social/skill bonus. No individual benefits, prerequisite numbers or purchase rules beyond this framework are currently implemented. Unspent development points can be retained; do **not** invent refinements to fill an advancement gap.

## Current discipline catalogue and boundaries

Use the [complete starting-family assignment](#complete-starting-family-assignment) above as the **single authoritative mapping** of the existing discipline catalogue for character creation. All previously listed disciplines are assigned there, including **Throwing (Physical and Fieldcraft)**, **Sleight of Hand (Professional and Technical)**, and **Disguise and Emotional Discipline (Cultural and Specialized)**.

The legacy contextual labels **Oratory, Negotiation, Interrogation and Command** are classified under Cultural and Specialized only once their specific trainable practices are named. They never act as blanket modifiers for ordinary social interactions.

**Emotional Discipline** is the learned practice used by Gifted rules that explicitly test trained regulation, deepening or stability of emotion.

**Veil Theory** is theoretical/analytical expertise concerning Veil principles, magical structures and technique theory where the knowledge applies.

**Veil Practice** is hands-on expertise in deliberate Veil procedures only when an authored rule, technique or situation calls for it; it is **not** a universal casting bonus.

## Status and outstanding content

The **two-pool architecture, family-guided (but not mandatory) initial allocation principle, social roleplay-first boundary, and background-familiarity boundary are design decisions**. Starting-point numbers, +5 ceilings and scheduled progression are **v0 playtest values**, to calibrate against Attack/Guard and task-difficulty progression. **Weapon/Discipline Refinement effects, exact character social/background subsystems, and advanced knowledge/practice content are not yet authored**. Do not import legacy 5e skill/proficiency defaults.
