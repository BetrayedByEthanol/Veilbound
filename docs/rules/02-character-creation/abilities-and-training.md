# Abilities and Training

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34.

## Native abilities

Veilbound uses nine direct ability modifiers.

- **Strength** — raw muscular power.
- **Agility** — gross motor control, movement, balance and bodily coordination.
- **Precision** — fine motor control and delicate manipulation.
- **Intellect** — reasoning, memory and learned understanding.
- **Awareness** — observation, instinct and environmental notice.
- **Presence** — social force, self-expression, persuasion and command. Physical attractiveness is not part of Presence and may instead be represented by traits.
- **Vitality** — physical resilience and the primary survivability ability.
- **Veil Affinity** — magical capacity: how much Veil power a character can contain and channel.
- **Veil Control** — magical execution: how precisely, efficiently and forcefully a character shapes Veil power.

Abilities use their modifier directly; there is no legacy 3–18 score plus derived modifier.

No whole Archetype is tied to a mandatory mundane casting ability. Mundane abilities may contribute to specific magical actions when the action genuinely depends on them.

## Level-1 ability generation

All nine abilities begin at **-2**. A level-1 character receives **28 ability points**.

| Final score | Total cost |
|---:|---:|
| -2 | 0 |
| -1 | 1 |
| 0 | 2 |
| +1 | 3 |
| +2 | 5 |
| +3 | 7 |
| +4 | 10 |

The normal level-1 maximum is **+4**.

A balanced default of two abilities at +3 and the remaining seven at 0 costs exactly 28 points.

## Ability advancement

At levels **4, 8, 12, 16 and 20**, increase one ability of the player's choice by +1.

There is no special catch-up rule for negative abilities. Normal permanent natural ability advancement is capped at **+5**. Extraordinary Veil enhancement, transformation, relics or similar effects may exceed that natural cap.

## Training

Training represents specific learned expertise, experience or knowledge. Generic human capability remains on the base ability.

Veilbound deliberately avoids mandatory broad umbrella skills such as generic Perception, Investigation, Athletics, Persuasion, Deception or Stealth.

| Training | Bonus |
|---|---:|
| None | +0 |
| Familiar | +1 |
| Trained | +2 |
| Expert | +3 |
| Master | +4 |

Training is not permanently tied to one ability. Medicine might pair with Intellect for diagnosis and Precision for surgery; Climbing might pair with Strength or Agility depending on the obstacle.

Custom narrow trainings and knowledge fields are allowed. Knowledge training may grant information automatically when the character would simply know it; not every use requires a roll.

## Character-creation allocation status

The Training scale is locked, but the audit does **not** yet define how many Training selections/points a new character receives.

Do not import a 5e proficiency count or background proficiency package to fill that missing rule.
