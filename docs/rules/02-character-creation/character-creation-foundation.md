# Character Creation Foundation

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34. Veilbound does **not yet have a complete executable character-creation procedure**. This page records the portions that are currently defined and makes the remaining gaps explicit instead of filling them with 5e defaults.

## Current character framework

A player character is defined by these major mechanical layers. **B04 resolves starting Training and equipment purchases at v0; B07 adds a separate provisional starting Talent selection**. Other creation gaps below remain.

1. **Archetype** — Priest, Scholar, Gifted, or Anointed.
2. **Focus** — High, Medium, or Low.
3. **Nine Abilities** — Strength, Agility, Precision, Intellect, Awareness, Presence, Vitality, Veil Affinity, Veil Control.
4. **Trainings** — independent [Weapon Training](../05-combat/weapon-training-and-refinements-v0.md) and [Learned Disciplines](abilities-and-training.md#training-structure--b04) budgets; provisional maximum +5 after advancement.
5. **Resonance** — the universal -3 to +3 state describing internal coherence of Volition; new player characters normally begin at **0**.
6. **Archetype-specific access choices** — such as a Priest's chosen Pillar and Pillar Accord, a Scholar's learned techniques, a Gifted's emotion-to-Aspect links, or an Anointed's bonded Aspect/Mission/Birth Imprint.
7. **Universal Veil** — automatically know [Veil Inspection v0](../07-veil-magic/veil-inspection-v0.md) at **level 1**. [Blink v0](../07-veil-magic/blink-v0.md) unlocks automatically at **level 3**. Neither consumes a Talent, Training or Archetype technique slot.
8. **Talents** — one **provisional level-1 Talent choice** under the [B07 Talent Framework](../10-advancement/talents-framework-v0.md), selected from actual eligible [level 1–5 entries](../10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md); this is separate from both Training point budgets and may instead be saved.
9. **Derived resources and combat values** — HP, Vigor, VP, Physical Tempo, and other values whose rules are already defined.
10. **Equipment** — [150-sp v0 starting purchasing](../11-equipment/economy-and-starter-equipment-v0.md) from provisional armor, weapon, kit and ammunition prices.
11. **Background/origin/social starting position** — currently missing as a complete subsystem.

## Step 1 — Choose Archetype

Choose one of the four base Archetypes:

- [Priest](../08-archetypes/priest.md)
- [Scholar](../08-archetypes/scholar.md)
- [Gifted](../08-archetypes/gifted.md)
- [Anointed](../08-archetypes/anointed.md)

Archetype defines the character's **relationship to and access route into the Veil**.

## Step 2 — Choose Focus

Choose:

- **High — Projection**
- **Medium — Routing**
- **Low — Embodiment**

See [Focus: Veil Expression](../09-focus/focus.md).

Focus does not replace Archetype. The two choices combine to produce one of twelve base foundations.

## Step 3 — Allocate Abilities

Use the level-1 generation rules from [Abilities and Training](abilities-and-training.md).

All nine abilities begin at **-2**.

Spend **28 ability points** using the cumulative costs:

| Final score | Cost |
|---:|---:|
| -2 | 0 |
| -1 | 1 |
| 0 | 2 |
| +1 | 3 |
| +2 | 5 |
| +3 | 7 |
| +4 | 10 |

The normal creation cap is **+4**.

## Step 4 — Choose Trainings and background familiarities

**B04 v0 allocations (numbers provisional; structure agreed):**

1. **Weapon Training:** receive **2 Weapon Development Points**; one point buys +1 direct rank in a [weapon family](../05-combat/weapon-training-and-refinements-v0.md#weapon-families), starting cap +2. Melee half-transfer applies only between melee categories; Bows and Crossbows remain independent. Shields use the equipped one-handed melee weapon's effective Training for legal shield Guard rather than requiring Shield Training.
2. **Learned Disciplines:** receive **8 points**, one for each +1 directly invested rank (starting cap +2). The **standard guided split** is **4 primary-family / 2 different secondary-family / 2 flexible**, but a coherent deliberately specialized character may use an unrestricted allocation with the **same budget and cap**.
3. **Background familiarities:** establish **two or three** normal contextual familiarities. They provide lived knowledge and context, **not Training bonuses, secrets, or guaranteed contacts**.

Use [Abilities and Training](abilities-and-training.md#training-structure--b04) for four descriptive Learned Discipline families and the narrow-skill/social-resolution boundaries. General Presence/Awareness/Intellect checks remain available without buying umbrella Persuasion, Search or Perception skills. Both development tracks are independent of Archetype and Focus. Complete high-level refinements and final point calibration are still later advancement content, not level-1 blockers.

## Step 5 — Set Resonance

New player characters normally begin at:

```
Resonance 0
```

Archetype does not change this universal starting value.

See [Universal Resonance and Direct Veil Magic](../07-veil-magic/resonance-and-intrusion.md).

## Step 6 — Make Archetype-specific choices

### Priest

Choose one Pillar. **Pillar-technique** access is constrained to that Pillar's three Aspects and its Prepared Prayer structure. This does **not** exclude [Universal Veil](../07-veil-magic/universal-veil-fundamentals.md#universal-eligibility-and-automatic-acquisition--locked): all four Archetypes automatically know **Inspection at level 1** and **Blink at level 3** without using Prepared Prayer capacity; normal procedure VP/action limits apply.

The Priest also uses the separate **Pillar Accord** state defined in the Priest foundation. Pillar Accord is not universal Resonance.

**L1 v0:** Start at **Pillar Accord +1 (Stable)**, separate from Universal Resonance 0. **Base Prepared Prayer capacities** at L1 are **High 3, Medium 2, Low 1**; Accord +1 yields actual **High 4, Medium 3, Low 2**, before an explicitly eligible capacity Talent. Slots may remain empty when only one chosen-Pillar spell is authored; **do not invent additional techniques**. Effective capacity has a **minimum of one** after signed Accord, and preparation follows the authored **10-minute / once-per-six-elapsed-hours** procedure. High L1 Open Invocation permits an otherwise eligible **unprepared chosen-Pillar Tier-1** technique with **one Full Action + normal VP +1 separate Spent VP surcharge**. Its surcharge never becomes a Talent-discounted 0-VP tax. Detailed rules: [Priest Foundation](../08-archetypes/priest.md#prepared-access) and [Priest/Scholar repertoire v0](../../design/b04-b05-priest-scholar-starting-repertoires-v0.md).

### Scholar

Choose individually learned **Pillar/Aspect** techniques from allowed Pillars/Aspects. [Universal Veil operations](../07-veil-magic/universal-veil-fundamentals.md#universal-eligibility-and-automatic-acquisition--locked) are a separate automatically level-unlocked family (**Inspection 1, Blink 3**) and do **not** consume the Scholar's individually learned Pillar-technique capacity.

**L1 v0 distinct known Pillar techniques:** **High 5 / Medium 4 / Low 3**, selected individually from actually authored eligible techniques and potentially across Pillars. **High:** prepare up to **1 legal Composite Formula** from known compatible technique pairs (a formula is **not** a new known technique). **Medium:** no general prepared-technique cap; the **one Active Inward Boost** at L1 still requires explicitly compatible authored Inward profiles. **Low:** prepare **up to 2 of 3 known** techniques as its ready subset; an Active Embodiment additionally requires explicit compatible expression. A technique is one known/ready choice regardless of number of authored profiles. Normal Universal Inspection/Blink slots are separate. Technical High-formula/Low-prepared setup is **10 minutes, at most once per six elapsed hours** after the creation-time initial set. See [Scholar Foundation](../08-archetypes/scholar.md#l13-individually-learned-repertoire--all-scholar-focuses) and [Priest/Scholar repertoire v0](../../design/b04-b05-priest-scholar-starting-repertoires-v0.md).

### Gifted

Create exactly eight permanent emotion-to-Aspect links:

- one for each core emotion;
- each link uses a different Aspect;
- each link comes from a different Pillar.

The full compatibility matrix remains to be migrated into structured content.

### Anointed

Define:

- one bonded Pillar/Aspect;
- a broad lifelong Mission;
- a Birth Imprint shaped by the surge that created the bond.

Exact Signature/Authority packages remain Aspect-specific content work. The Anointed's bonded-Aspect limit applies to bonded techniques and Authority; **Inspection at level 1 and Blink at level 3** unlock automatically as Universal procedures, with the same costs and restrictions as for all other Archetypes.

## Step 7 — Calculate derived durability/resources

Use the currently promoted native formulas.

### HP

```
Maximum HP = 10 + Vitality
```

### Vigor

```
Maximum Vigor = (10 + Vitality) × Level
```

At level 1, this is equal to Maximum HP.

### VP

Use Focus:

- High: `max(1, 6 + Veil Affinity) × Level`
- Medium: `max(1, 4 + Veil Affinity) × Level`
- Low: `max(1, 2 + Veil Affinity) × Level`

See [Volition Points](../07-veil-magic/volition-points.md).

### Turn AP and Tempo

Turn AP and derived Tempo come from level plus Focus.

At levels 1–2, all Focuses have:

- **30 / 36 Turn AP** respectively;
- **Physical Tempo 1**;
- **Projection Tempo 1**;
- **Guard Capacity 1**.

At level 3, Turn AP is **42** and Focus begins to differentiate cadence:

| Focus | Physical Tempo | Projection Tempo | Guard Capacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| High — Projection | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| Medium — Routing | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Low — Embodiment | 2 | 1 | 2 |

Guard Capacity equals Physical Tempo unless modified by equipment or another explicit rule.

See [Turn Action Points and Tempo](../05-combat/action-categories-and-physical-tempo.md).

## Step 8 — Buy starting equipment

Every level-1 character receives **150 sp (15 gp) of starting possessions** under [Equipment Economy and Starting Loadouts v0](../11-equipment/economy-and-starter-equipment-v0.md) (**provisional numerical baseline**). Spend that single budget on ordinary listed weapons, **eligible starter armor (Leather through Full knightly harness, subject to budget)**, shields, ammunition, professional kits and supplies, or retain unspent coins. **The clothing/light-robes and heavy-padded/battle-robes price rows are market references, not legal starting armor purchases.**

- **10 copper = 1 silver; 10 silver = 1 gold** for consistent game accounting.
- **Combat playstyle shopping lists** are **optional examples**, not Archetype/Focus packages or free equipment. Mix categories freely to create an armored healer, lighter healer, ranged defender, and so on.
- Prices represent usable mundane equipment. A fitted armor configuration includes necessary underlayers, **without Armor Rating stacking**.
- No separate starting Armor cap or mundane-armor spellcasting restriction. Armor 6 plate and Armor 7 full harness exceed the ordinary 150-sp budget **by price**, not by level rule.
- Ordinary clothing (including cosmetic robes) is **assumed worn for free** at creation, granting Armor 0 and no rune properties. **Neither ordinary clothing/light robes nor heavy padded/battle robes may be purchased or chosen as starting armor**, despite their listed future market prices. Heavy padded/battle robes retain their Armor 3 rules for later acquisition.
- Standard adventuring packs, professional kits and ammunition are priced. **Arrows/bolts are tracked per fired shot** under the v0 supply baseline. No unlisted equipment bonus is implied by a tool.
- Exceptional inherited or patron-provided equipment over budget needs explicit campaign agreement; no automatic privilege from social background.

**Deferred:** regional economies, discount/salvage systems, full adventuring catalogue, detailed ammunition recovery, numeric encumbrance, equipment wear/durability, equipment runes and enchanted items. Do **not** fill those with 5e defaults.

## Character-creation systems still missing

The audit explicitly leaves these areas incomplete:

- background/origin mechanics;
- social origin and starting contacts;
- final numerical calibration of v0 Training/starting equipment and later Weapon/Discipline Refinement content;
- complete native defense/save mapping, including non-spell hazards and spell defenses;
- some spell-derived formulas;
- **repertoire-count balance** and **focus-specific compatible expressions** remain unresolved, even though Priest and Scholar **L1–3 v0 counts and preparation rules are now authored**;
- a final shared procedure for every remaining derived sheet value.

The **B04 Training, mundane starter-equipment and Priest/Scholar repertoire quantity** steps are executable at level 1 using v0 values. Character creation still cannot be certified end-to-end across all twelve builds: Priest Consecrations/Devotions, Scholar Inward Boost/Embodiment profiles, Gifted Focus expressions and Anointed Signature/Authority packages need individually authored content. Do not count available prayer/known-technique slots as playable Focus effects.
