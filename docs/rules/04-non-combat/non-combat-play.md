# Non-Combat Play

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34. Veilbound currently has a native core-check engine for ordinary non-combat actions, but several extended subsystems remain missing.

## Core procedure

Non-combat actions use the same core resolution rules as other uncertain actions.

Under pressure:

```
d20 + Ability + applicable Training + situational modifiers
vs
10 + Difficulty Modifier
```

Use [Core Resolution](../01-core-resolution/core-resolution.md) for:

- when not to roll;
- passive resolution;
- Take 10;
- Take 20;
- the difficulty ladder.

The chosen Ability depends on **what the character is actually doing**, not on a fixed skill/ability pairing.

Training is narrow and situationally paired with the relevant Ability.

## Social interaction

Ordinary social execution generally uses **Presence** plus a relevant narrow Training where one applies.

Possible Trainings include:

- Oratory;
- Negotiation;
- Interrogation;
- cultural Etiquette;
- Command;
- Acting;
- Disguise;
- profession- or culture-specific fields.

There is deliberately no mandatory generic **Persuasion**, **Deception**, or equivalent umbrella skill.

### Player approach and character ability

The player chooses the approach.

The character's Presence and Training determine how effectively the character carries it out.

A player is not required to personally deliver an eloquent speech to benefit from a high-Presence character.

A particularly strong, weak, appropriate, inappropriate, risky, or well-supported approach primarily changes:

- the fictional situation;
- what outcomes are plausible;
- the difficulty modifier;
- whether a roll is needed at all.

It does not replace the character sheet.

### Still missing

Veilbound does not yet define:

- extended social conflicts;
- influence tracks;
- reputation/faction procedures;
- formal opposed social rules;
- settlement/institutional political procedures.

## Investigation and observation

Use **Awareness** for noticing, sensing, searching the immediate environment, or recognizing something present.

Use **Intellect** for reasoning, interpretation, reconstruction, memory, or technical understanding.

Relevant narrow Trainings may contribute.

Examples include:

- Medicine;
- Engineering;
- Veil Theory;
- Pillar Lore;
- regional/era History;
- regional Law;
- regional Politics;
- Religion & Cults;
- profession-specific expertise.

There is deliberately no mandatory generic **Perception** or **Investigation** skill.

### Knowledge

If a trained character would simply know something, the GM may provide that information without a roll.

Roll only when uncertainty is meaningful.

A high-Intellect character may receive contextual deductions the player missed. A clever player may also make deductions from clues already available even if the character has low Intellect; however, information the character must actually know can still depend on Intellect and relevant Training.

### Still missing

Veilbound does not yet define a formal extended clue/progress-clock investigation procedure.

## Stealth

Ordinary quiet or careful bodily movement generally uses **Agility**.

Narrow contextual Training may apply where the character has learned a specific relevant practice.

There is deliberately no mandatory generic **Stealth** skill.

The current rules do **not** yet define a complete opposed stealth/detection procedure, so do not silently import 5e passive Perception or Stealth-vs-Perception rules.

## Physical non-combat actions

Choose the Ability from the actual task.

Examples:

- **Strength** — force, lifting, direct muscular exertion.
- **Agility** — balance, gross movement, bodily coordination.
- **Precision** — delicate manipulation and fine control.
- **Vitality** — endurance/resilience when the task is primarily bodily tolerance.

A Training may pair differently depending on the approach.

For example:

- Climbing + Strength for hauling upward through raw force;
- Climbing + Agility for technically difficult movement;
- Medicine + Intellect for diagnosis;
- Medicine + Precision for surgery.

## Non-combat systems not yet implemented

The current audit marks the following as missing:

- group checks/helping;
- opposed-check procedure;
- extended tasks/progress clocks;
- travel/navigation expedition procedure;
- discovery/hazard exploration procedure;
- downtime;
- crafting/research projects;
- faction/influence mechanics;
- settlement/institutional change;
- extended social/investigation conflict.

These are design gaps, not invitations to assume 5e procedures.
