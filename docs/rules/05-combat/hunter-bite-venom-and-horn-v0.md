# Hunter of the Woods — Venomous Bite and Horn Bash v0

> **B06-EN-03 CREATURE-LOCAL ATTACK/TOXIN LOCK CANDIDATE.** This page independently authors **one normal Bite**, a distinct **slow pursuit venom** and **one defensive Horn Bash**, with a mutually exclusive **Horn Drive (Shove)** option. The concrete attack numbers are **provisional B06 encounter-test values**, not general natural-weapon, poison or NPC progression rules. Hunter remains **CONCEPT_ONLY** until its other physiology/defenses, combatant resources and a full executable enemy fixture are authored and opposed encounters calibrated. This source does not invent a biological weapon crafting/dose recipe.

## 1. Biological delivery source and roles

**B06-SRC-HUNTER-GROOVED-CANINES** is the Hunter's real biological **natural Bite source**: powerful ordinary jaws with two **reinforced upper canines**, each bearing a protected groove fed by a paired **venom-producing modified salivary gland and duct**. During an ordinary puncturing Bite, venomous saliva may enter the actual tooth wounds. This is **not** a separate pair of fangs, automatic contact poison, hollow hypodermic injection, ranged secretion, weapon coating, cloud or additional attack. Biting can still injure and hold prey when no venom is delivered. Grooves/salivary anatomy are fictional creature-lore implementation, not a statement that this arrangement is identical to any real animal's.

**B06-SRC-HUNTER-FRONTAL-HORN** is a **single central frontal-cranial horn**, reinforced with a bony core and keratin sheath, on the frontal skull rather than the nasal epithelium. Its **primary ecological jobs** are rutting/competition, intimidation and discouraging competing predators, and loosening dirt/roots/weak material before the front limbs enlarge a den. It can bash/push during defense, but is **not** the primary prey-killing weapon, is **not venomous**, and receives no special armor-piercing property, Guard instrument, free fear effect or magical protection merely by existing.

Both sources are **natural, mundane, bodily, corporeal** attacks. Neither is an independent Veil technique and neither spends or generates VP. Regaining breath after a hunt does **not** spend/recover Vigor, which remains the distinct regenerative reserve under [HP and Vigor](../06-vigor-wounds-death/hp-and-vigor.md).

**Hunter local AP anchor:** use the existing provisional **42 Turn AP, 24 AP Physical Action** and normal **M = 12 MP** until the full physiology is authored. Both attacks and Horn Drive spend the **same** Turn AP pool and fit the existing one Attack Sequence per target per turn rule where ordinary melee attacks are involved. **One** Bite or Horn Bash is one attack, not two grooved-canine attacks or two horn strikes. Other ordinary attacks this turn must be independently paid and committed in the same Attack Sequence against that target **before any attack rolls**. The 42-AP sample budget only pays **one** 24-AP Physical Action. No extra free off-hand, additional attack or arbitrary combo is granted.

**Bite reach** is the Hunter's ordinary anatomically plausible melee Reach from its **head**. For this v0 profile, against a one-square PC it is the **one immediately head-forward adjacent grid square**; a lateral/rear target first requires a legal paid 1×2 pivot on an ordinary movement mode to face it. **Horn Bash/Drive** use the same direct head-forward adjacency. This is a *source-specific anatomy limitation*, not a change to the general footprint rule or a universal facing restriction.

**Normal Bite** uses the ordinary one-attack declared [Attack Sequence](initiative-and-round-structure.md#attack-sequence). The target chooses Take Hit, Dodge or one eligible Guard before the roll; open/respect Interception/Reaction/Blink and Attack legality windows. A natural 1 and Critical tier are handled by existing rules. After a hit, subtract `Effective Armor = max(Armor - Penetration, 0)`, then `HP damage = max(rolled damage - Effective Armor, 0)` using the [ordinary Armor procedure](weapons-and-armor.md#weapon-damage). Apply all legal separately authored event-wide prevention **before** testing injection; never treat raw die damage or pre-Armor damage as a puncture.

**Pounce compatibility:** Pounce is still one paid **24-AP Charge-like Bite + one separately resolved free force/Balance rider**, **not** a free Bite from moving. Use this **same natural Bite d20+6 and 3+d6 Pen1** at [Pounce stage D](hunter-pounce-and-prey-takedown-v0.md#3-one-declaration-one-response-window-two-independent-resolutions). Perform Bite damage, any real injection gate, initial Vitality resistance and the immediate **toxin instance** establishment, then resolve Pounce stage E Balance under its existing post-Bite prone/incapacitation rule. The new venom's delayed impairment has **no instant mechanical penalty** and therefore cannot rewrite the Balance roll of that same Pounce. No new Bite attack, extra response assignment, second venom resistance, Grapple Control or automatic fear results from a Pounce.

## 3. Bite injection gate (one possible application per actual successful Bite)

The **grooved canine source** can deliver the named toxin `B06-TX-HUNTER-SLOW-MY` through **injection only**, and only at a physically valid **canine puncture**:

1. **First** resolve the one normal Bite's attack and defense, natural 1/critical, legal armor/damage/packet transformations, and **all real event-wide HP prevention**. A miss, completely successful Guard, Dodge avoiding the Bite, blocked natural attack, or fully prevented puncture yields **no injection** and **no resistance roll**.
2. **Exposure succeeds** only if the Bite has a **positive surviving Piercing HP packet** actually caused by its grooved canines after **all applicable mitigation**, or an **independently authored and established breached route** into which those actual venomous secretions were delivered. **Other typed HP** (Fire, Crush, etc.) never substitutes for a surviving Piercing puncture. Ordinary Armor is applied to the physical Bite **once**, not to any future internal venom consequence again.
3. Check the recipient's existing one-active-toxin gate [**before** natural resistance](mundane-toxins-v0.md#4-nonstacking-re-exposure-and-ending). A valid **equal-or-lower-grade** repeat Bite while one ordinary toxin instance is active is **ignored for toxin purposes** (but Bite's physical damage still applies): **no new Vitality roll, stacking, onset reset, duration refresh or other penalty**. A legitimately higher printed grade replaces only as the general toxin rule permits; no higher-grade Hunter version is authored here.
4. If eligible, allow an **actually applicable** [Venom Endurance](../07-veil-magic/venom-endurance-v0.md) preventive effect under its own rules before the natural resistance check. A separately authored cure/suppression may affect one active instance when allowed. **No general medical antidote or passive immunity** is created by this profile.
5. Roll **one** `d20 + target Vitality + explicit eligible modifiers ≥ DC 12`. A tie **succeeds** and receives the **Reduced** effect below; a failure receives **Full**. Natural 1/20 are not automatic poison criticals. Record one instance and its **original exposure time**. The result is not rechecked at later onset/milestones.
6. The Bite deals its ordinary Piercing HP damage whether venom exposure was accepted, ignored by nonstacking, reduced, prevented, or the recipient later recovers. Successful Pounce Balance is **not** the injection trigger and a failed Pounce Balance does **not** cancel a real Bite puncture.

**Explicit damage examples (normal hits with a d6=4):** Bite damage `3+4=7 Piercing`. Against Leather Armor3, Pen1 → effective2 → **5 HP**, injection eligible. Against Brigandine Armor5, Pen1 → effective4 → **3 HP**, injection eligible. Against full harness Armor7, Pen1 → effective6 → **1 HP**, injection eligible if not otherwise prevented. With a low d6=1 against Armor5, `4-4=0 HP`, **no injection**, regardless of a visually impressive bite. With any event-wide prevention of the remaining **Piercing** packet to zero, **no injection**, even if other packets still injure. These are eligibility examples, not extra attack sources.

## 4. Pursuit venom profile — `B06-TX-HUNTER-SLOW-MY`

**Name:** *Hunter's Lingering Venom* (fictional secretions from grooved upper canines).

| Field | Authored v0 value |
|---|---|
| Source | `B06-SRC-HUNTER-GROOVED-CANINES` only; not horn or unarmed contact |
| Dominant toxin family | **Myotoxin-style neuromuscular fatigue/weakening** (mundane fictional taxonomy) |
| Potency / resistance | **Moderate; Vitality DC 12** |
| Route | **Actual Bite injection**, after the puncture gate above |
| Result | **Full** on failed resistance; **Reduced** on a successful resistance |
| First onset | **At T+10 minutes after accepted exposure**; no immediate penalty or damage at T=0 |
| Full stage 1 | At **T+10 minutes:** **−2 to ordinary Movement Allowance (M)**, **−1 to eligible Strength-based muscular exertion checks/contests** |
| Full stage 2 | At **T+30 minutes:** replaces stage 1 with **−4 to M**, **−2 to eligible Strength-based muscular exertion checks/contests** |
| Full expiry | At **T+120 minutes after exposure**, both penalties end; no extra last pulse |
| Reduced effect | At **T+10 minutes:** **−2 M**, **−1 muscular Strength check/contest penalty**, **no stage 2**; expires **T+60 minutes after exposure** |
| HP/Vigor impact | **0 direct HP, 0 direct Vigor damage**; no poison-induced wound, Dying loss, permanent paralysis or automatic death |
| Re-exposure | Existing **one active ordinary toxin instance** rule; profile occupies that slot **from accepted exposure even before onset** |
| Clock | Absolute **long-duration milestone** deadlines; [mundane long toxin clock/initiative mapping](mundane-toxins-v0.md#3-ticks-timekeeping-and-healing) |

**Scope of the muscular penalty:** Only actual **Strength-based physical exertion checks and contests** where muscle force matters, including a legitimate Strength-based **Shove Pressure/Hold Ground, Trip Pressure/Balance Resistance, Grapple Contest, physical lifting/hauling or strenuous traversal**. It does **not** lower **Strength** itself, ordinary weapon Attack rolls, weapon damage, Armor, Dodge Defense, Guard Defense/capacity, Turn AP, Physical Tempo, Projection, Reaction, VP, Vigor, wound recovery or every skill check merely because Strength was named. Against Pounce, the penalty applies to the PC's **Strength-based** Balance Resistance only if its stage is already active; a PC may choose a legitimately plausible **Agility**-based balance method without that *Strength-only* modifier. Existing external conditions and actual limb limitations apply separately.

**Movement penalty:** Subtract only from the target's otherwise-current **normal Movement Allowance M**, floor at **0**, then recalculate mode allowances (normal, Rapid Advance, Charge or Sprint) through their **already existing impairment procedures**. Do not subtract it again separately per tile, pivot, terrain entry or 1×2-footprint cell. MP already spent stays spent; no refunds or extra movement pool from toxin expiry. No automatic one-square slow, no mandatory halving of all speed, no AP loss and no impossibility of standing unless the real current movement/physical rules make an action impossible.

### Long clock and exploration ↔ initiative transitions

Track an accepted instance as **one toxin record** with `grade=Moderate`, `resolution=Full|Reduced`, `start=exposure`, its fixed **milestones** and active stage. **No six-second short toxin ticks, HP packet ticks or per-turn regeneration entitlement** are created. Both onset and expiry remain scheduled even when the victim withdraws from combat.

- **Exploration:** count actual elapsed in-world seconds; at T+600s (10m) apply stage 1, at T+1800s (30m) apply Full stage 2 (Reduced has no stage 2), and expire Full at T+7200s (120m) or Reduced at T+3600s (60m). Advance in chronological order, including when the GM presents a one-hour travel summary. If a milestone may change pursuit, a physical challenge or a decision, resolve it at its actual event before continuing time. **Each milestone changes the same instance**, not a new toxin or a second resistance roll.
- **New exposure during initiative round R:** schedule long events at **Round Begin R+100** (10m), **R+300** (Full escalation, 30m) and **R+1200** (Full expiry, 120m) or **R+600** (Reduced expiry, 60m). This uses the toxin-local **100 initiative-round boundaries per 10 minutes** long-clock surrogate (**6 seconds per boundary for scheduling only**); it does not assert global 6-second rounds, grant turns/regeneration, or begin at the target's next Step 3.
- **Entering initiative from exploration:** for **every pending milestone** with `s` whole seconds until due, map independently using `q=floor(s/6)` to **Round Begin R+q**, or apply at entry before actions if `q=0`. When multiple milestones collapse onto the same mapped boundary because of rounding, resolve in original chronological order; a stage 1 that is immediately superseded by stage 2 must not leave two additive penalties. Do not extend a deadline to get an extra full interval.
- **Leaving initiative in round C:** for every pending Round Begin event E, use **`s=6×max(0,E−C−1)` seconds** as the new exploration countdown; when `s=0`, resolve it at transition. Preserve already processed milestones and their order, and do not restart the clock or regenerate the poison instance. This is the existing conservative [long-to-exploration conversion](mundane-toxins-v0.md#3-ticks-timekeeping-and-healing), applied to **each distinct milestone** of this one profile.
- **While initiative continues:** apply milestone stage changes and expiry at their registered **Round Begin**, not at each injured target's Step 2/3. A saved range/proposed move already legal before milestone resolves is not retroactively removed; future movement costs/allowances use current actual impairment rules. Never grant a second full combat turn from the clock.
- **Same-boundary ordering:** if a future active stage and expiry genuinely coincide, **expire without leaving a lingering penalty**; if rounding merges originally distinct stage/expiry events, resolve in original time order, concluding with expiry. A separately authored cure/neutralization cancels the pending future milestones of that **one** active instance.

**Why not immediate poison HP loss?** This is a *pursuit* venom, not a copy of short `TX-HM` hemotoxin or an instant victory toxin. A resisted Bite can be dealt with, a wounded PC can reach shelter, and an uninjured/strong PC can still resist a later attack. Larger prey may become easier to approach and physically overpower after an interval, but a moose is **not automatically killed** and its actual scale/contest rules are deferred.

### Specific timeline examples

1. **Fail DC12, leave combat immediately:** At T+0, Bite punctures for 3 HP after Armor → accepted **Full** venom instance, no immediate movement modifier. At **T+10m**, M12 → **M10** and Strength-based exertion −1. At **T+30m**, M12 → **M8** and exertion −2 (not −3 cumulatively). At **T+120m**, penalties end and M returns to its independent current value, unless another real impairment remains. **No added HP loss**.
2. **Make DC12 exactly:** **Reduced** instance. At T+10m, **−2 M / −1 eligible Strength checks**, no stage 2; at T+60m expires. No immunity from an ordinary successful Vitality resistance, no second poison save.
3. **Repeated biting at T+15m:** Physical Bite damages as rolled. A second **Moderate** Hunter injection is ignored while the first active Moderate instance persists; its T+30m/T+120m schedule remains unchanged. No automatic dose increase, refresh, second Vitality check or separate poisoned wound.
4. **Pounce in the first 10 minutes:** Bite hit may envenom even if the PC remains standing; any *linked* Pounce Balance is resolved before onset, so neither Full nor Reduced applies an immediate Balance penalty. **If Bite takes the PC to 0 HP**, [Pounce's Unable-to-Contest](hunter-pounce-and-prey-takedown-v0.md#3-one-declaration-one-response-window-two-independent-resolutions) already resolves independently; venom is not a cause of the instantaneous incapacity.
5. **Regeneration/rest:** Normal HP/Vigor/wound rules resolve on their own authorized clocks. This toxin has **no direct Vigor packets and no invented new recovery checkpoints**, and resting for ten minutes does not remove it by default.

## 5. Horn Bash — defensive natural weapon (no venom)

**Normal Horn Bash:** one **head-forward** melee **Crush** attack (`d20+5`, `2+d6` HP, **Pen 0**) against one eligible defender. It costs the Hunter **one 24-AP Physical Action**, declares a usual Attack Sequence/standalone response where actually permitted, and offers defender **Take Hit, Dodge or Guard** under the standard procedure. A hit is subject to normal Armor once and general critical rules. This Bash does **not** automatically trigger Shove, Prone, stun, fear, collision, grapple, a second attack, Venom exposure, or added body weight/penetration damage. A blocked/avoided Bash inflicts neither damage nor a free Shove.

**Defensive timing:** Horn Bash is an ordinary melee attack profile: if the Hunter has an **independently valid Opportunity Attack** or has already made a **valid atomic attack Interception reservation** with the usual resource commitment, it may use Horn Bash as that one melee attack when head-forward contact still exists. It does **not** create a free Opportunity, Reaction, pre-emptive strike, Guard or automatic Riposte. **Horn Drive is a separately paid Shove maneuver, not an Opportunity Attack substitution**; reserve it as an out-of-turn AP action only if the existing reservation rules independently permit that specific maneuver and trigger. No Horn Bash hit is needed to threaten or warn opponents narratively.

**Horn damage example:** d6=4 gives `2+4=6 Crush`; vs Armor5, Pen0 → effective Armor5 → **1 HP** if a normal Bash actually hits. Against Guard16, a `d20=10` plus 5 totals **15**, so the Bash is blocked for **0 HP**. Neither result creates a free Shove.

**Alternative Horn Drive (defensive displacement, no hit roll/damage):**

1. On its own turn, with one facing-forward **standing one-square PC in the single adjacent head-forward square**, the Hunter may declare **Horn Drive** for **one 24-AP Physical Action** **instead of** Horn Bash/Bite. Before rolling or responses, choose its intended opponent and fixed **straight head-forward one-square displacement** (no sideways choice). Do not declare Bash and then switch to Drive after a Guard/Dodge, or vice versa.
2. **Preserve [canonical Shove v0](universal-maneuvers.md#shove-v0)**: the attacker physically drives with head, horn and powerful shoulders; if the target threatens the Hunter, trigger the usual **Close-Contact Opportunity** before the contest. An Opportunity hit imposes the normal **−2 Shove Pressure** (and its normal damage), subject to continued legality. Horn geometry grants no immunity to this response and no free Interception. If the PC's chosen opportunity attack and subsequent effects displace or incapacitate the Hunter, recheck legality and lose committed AP when appropriate.
3. Resolve the **ordinary Shove opposed roll**, with the canonical defender's **+2 Hold Ground**: `Hunter d20+Strength+applicable Training+Shove modifiers` vs `PC d20+Strength+applicable Training+2+Anchoring`. A tie holds the position. **Provisional Hunter Strength +4** is available **only as the same B06 testing contribution used for Pounce Pressure**; do not infer a universal +4 bonus for any horn or monster.
4. On Hunter strict win and a **physically legal unobstructed forward destination**, move the **PC one square directly away from the Hunter's head**, then perform the canonical **no-MP built-in advance** of the **entire rigid 1×2 Hunter footprint** by one square along its already-head-forward orientation: old rear → old head square, head → vacated former PC square. **Do not** place the rear in the PC's vacated square, drop the Hunter's body length, pivot, collapse into a shared Grapple square or charge a separate movement step for the built-in advance. This is the **one canonical Shove entry**, not two steps. Respect a full-body clear path and all third-party Opportunities that the built-in advance actually provokes. The displaced PC spends 0 MP and gets no general Movement Opportunity from this forced move.
5. If the PC cannot enter the destination because it is blocked, **the entire Shove displacement and built-in Hunter advance do not occur**, even on a winning contest. No Crush damage, automatic Prone, collision or re-targeting is substituted. If it moves over unsupported ground, use actual [Falling/Catch Grip](physical-hazards-v0.md) rules rather than treating a lack of floor as impassable. If an existing environmental condition independently requires a Stability check, use [Shove's](universal-maneuvers.md#stability-after-displacement) actual rule; the horn itself creates no new generic check.
6. No poison exposure, fear status, free intimidation modifier or combat advantage is generated by threatening with the horn. The Hunter may use warning displays/territorial posture as **ordinary roleplay**, not automatic mental control or a guaranteed successful social check.

**Example:** Hunter rear `(0,0)`, head `(1,0)`, PC `(2,0)`; the creature faces east and declares Horn Drive. Its Pressure `d20 13 + provisional Strength4 =17`. The PC's Hold Ground `d20 8 + Strength2 + 2 base=12`. With `(3,0)` clear and no successful response invalidating contact, the PC goes to **(3,0)** and Hunter becomes **rear (1,0), head (2,0)**. Neither the horn nor its advance inflicts HP damage. If `(3,0)` is solidly blocked, **no one moves**; use the blocked Shove rule.

## 6. Remaining limits / certification

- These attacks are *locally source-authored* for encounter tests and the linked Pounce, **not** a full `b06-enemy-v0` enemy or verified CR. The Hunter still needs actual Strength/Agility derivations/Training, HP, Armor, Dodge/Guard disposition, Vigor, physical action resources, senses and full opposed tests. Treat fixed attack totals as tentative **self-contained** local values, not modifiers stacked onto final attributes.
- **Venom Endurance** can prevent one valid new exposure, or apply **Active Suppression** to the existing `B06-TX-HUNTER-SLOW-MY` instance under its [authored long continuous six-second-equivalent suppression-local cadence](../07-veil-magic/venom-endurance-v0.md#b-active-suppression--one-existing-toxin-instance). A VC0 cast has **3 finite units**, not 3 hours or 3 stage removals: spend the first unit immediately if the T+10/T+30 penalty is already active, then one per successive protected local interval; in the pre-onset silent period spend **0** until a penalty actually starts during the spell. The original T+10 onset, Full T+30 escalation and T+60/T+120 expiry continue unchanged even during paid protection; escalation within a protected interval needs **no extra unit** and does not reset the interval. When protection ends before toxin expiry, the **current unsuppressed stage resumes**, without a new Vitality check or reinjection. A suppression-local counter is **not** a short venom toxin tick or a source of virtual regeneration. Basic Heal/normal HP regeneration **does not** independently purge this toxin; an antidote, medicine treatment or detox technique is **not** authored here.
- **No direct toxin damage** means the venom's gameplay threat is gradual impairment, not automatic poisoning death. Rarity, hunting success on large prey and encounter difficulty require actual session testing and an eventual size/mass system.
- **Horn Bash is not a Guard:** it does not produce a shield/parry capacity, automatic Reaction, free counterattack or automatic knockback when attacked. Horn Drive is a **separate paid maneuver**, not a free Bash rider. Horn anatomy can be narrated for excavation without granting mining or siege attack damage.
- **Pounce** retains its own head-forward minimum three-step approach and free balance rider. Horn Bash/Drive cannot be substituted as that Pounce's committed Bite profile.
