# Blink v0 — Live-View Translocation and Reactive Pursuit

> **LOCKED B05 Blink operational design.** Blink is an authored Universal Veil **Translocation** procedure, not a Pillar/Aspect technique, physical movement step, portal, or automatic Dodge. This page owns its cost, timing, live-view condition, attempted-failure consequences and Blink-follow counterplay. The [Translocation spatial foundation](veil-translocation-foundation.md) remains authoritative for open straight-line paths, safe arrival, momentum, bars/glass and no telefragging. **Universal procedure acquisition, tier assignment and learning/preparation route remain deferred:** this profile is not automatically known by everyone eligible to use Universal magic.

## Profile

| Parameter | Blink v0 |
|---|---|
| Family | Universal Veil — Translocation |
| Subject | Self, including carried/worn equipment that fits the destination |
| Normal activation | **One Focus-priced Projection Action** paid in Turn AP on the caster's turn |
| Reactive activation | **One Reaction**, on an eligible live trigger; successful relocation incurs **one Projection Action's AP Debt** on the caster's next own turn |
| VP cost | **Base 10 Spent VP**, reduced by any independently applicable, declared VP-cost modifier; pay the **effective cost** only on a valid, completed relocation |
| Fixed distance/range | **None**; constrained by live destination view, accurate own-arrival visualization, straight unobstructed direct path and full arrival clearance |
| Movement | Instant position change, **no traversal** of skipped squares; no MP spent or gained |
| Momentum | Current velocity **and direction preserved** |
| Failed attempted Blink | **No VP spent**, but the committed normal-action AP **or Reaction is lost**; **no AP Debt** for an invalid Reactive Blink |
| Acquisition/tier | Not yet authored; eligibility for Universal magic does not grant Blink automatically |

**Effective VP cost and availability:** Blink's **base Spent VP cost is 10**. At declaration, apply only cost reductions **actually eligible for this activation** and commit the choice to use each such limited benefit, before testing available VP:

```text
Blink effective Spent VP cost = 10 - applicable legal Spent VP cost reductions
(subject to each modifier's own minimum cost and stacking restrictions)
```

**[TAL-M03 — Frugal Technique](../10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md#tal-m03--frugal-technique)** qualifies for this **authored Universal Veil procedure** when its **once-on-the-character's-own-turn** condition is met and its benefit has not been used on that turn: **10 → 9 VP**. It may apply to an ordinary on-turn Blink and to an otherwise legal Reactive Blink **during that character's own turn**; it **does not** apply to a Reactive Blink **on someone else's turn**, even when that character has an unused Frugal Technique from their previous turn. Blink is not automatically eligible for discounts restricted to a specific Pillar, Prepared Prayer, separate surcharge, or otherwise inapplicable category.

**Before committing AP or Reaction**, the character must have at least the **declared effective cost in Available VP**, not necessarily 10. A 9-Available-VP character with an unused, eligible Frugal Technique can declare an on-turn Blink; one without that discount cannot. **Lock the eligible modifier(s) and effective cost at declaration**; a limited once-per-turn discount selected for an attempted Blink is **used for that turn even if Blink fails**. Do not retroactively move a discount to a different activation or gain a discount after seeing the Blink's outcome.

**At effect resolution**, recheck that sufficient Available VP remain to pay the **locked effective cost** (intervening responses may have changed the VP ledger). Spend that cost **only if Blink actually completes a valid relocation**; on an invalid, interrupted or otherwise unsuccessful attempt, spend **zero VP** while retaining the committed AP or Reaction loss. If remaining Available VP are insufficient to pay the effective cost at resolution, relocation fails and no VP is charged. **Committed VP and Spent VP cannot fund the cost.**

## Live-view arrival visualization — LOCKED

Blink requires a **currently live visual understanding** sufficient to reconstruct **the caster's own whole-body arrival pose and the surrounding scene as it would be perceived from that pose**. This is **not** simply recognizing a distant place or imagining a remembered scene.

- **Direct sight** of the relevant presently existing geometry can support mental reconstruction of a nearby destination's arrival perspective. The caster does not need to stand there first; they must be able to infer that perspective from sufficiently clear current information.
- **Active Scrying** or another *separately authored, genuinely live viewpoint* can supply the needed destination-perspective information, including for distant endpoints. Blink **does not grant Scrying**, permit a free viewpoint or authenticate a misleading image. A valid live magical view meets the information condition only; its spell costs, access, range and effects remain separate and must eventually be authored.
- **Memory, an old recording, map, description, drawing or mere familiarity** cannot by themselves satisfy the **live** requirement. They may help interpretation but cannot substitute for real-time spatial information.
- Merely **seeing a mountain summit from a valley** does not automatically mean the view from the summit and the exact arrival pose can be reconstructed. If independent live observation/Scrying supplies that perspective accurately, it can qualify **only if the open-path and clearance conditions below also qualify**.
- No fixed maximum in metres is added. Beyond-the-horizon destinations ordinarily fail because the planet's surface blocks the **straight spatial path**, **even with Scrying**; intervening solid terrain, walls and glass similarly block it.

### Independent physical eligibility — LOCKED

All of these must be satisfied **at resolution**, not only when the destination was first chosen:

1. **One continuous direct, straight unobstructed spatial path** connects the actual current origin and chosen destination. Solid matter interrupts it. Narrow genuinely open gaps through bars/grilles/slits may suffice even when a body could not pass through normally; solid **glass** never qualifies merely because it is transparent. No bent/around-the-corner path, curved planetary-horizon bypass or through-wall travel.
2. **Complete arrival volume** must be free for the caster's body and all carried/worn gear. No intersecting matter, teleporting into an enemy, material slicing or automatically shifting to the nearest free square.
3. **Express anti-Translocation protections** may prohibit an otherwise spatially valid Blink. Neither live Scrying nor knowing a ward exists provides an automatic bypass.

The caster must name the **intended arrival position and pose** before committing their action/Reaction; it cannot be changed after failure as part of the same attempt. Re-evaluate actual path and clearance after intervening responses. A Blink may fail because the live scene was insufficiently known or conditions changed. **Failure does not diagnose why**, grant a free obstruction scan or reveal hidden wards.

## Normal on-turn Blink

1. On the caster's turn, declare a specific arrival destination and pose, choose Blink as **one Projection Action**, determine and lock any **eligible VP-cost modifier** (including unused on-turn Frugal Technique), confirm **Available VP ≥ effective cost**, and commit the Focus-priced **Turn AP** cost (High **20**, Medium **24**, Low **30** AP). The VP are not yet spent.
2. Resolve ordinary action response/Interrupt timing. At the effect's resolution, validate the **live perspective**, straight open path, complete clearance and applicable wards.
3. If valid and the action is not cancelled, and enough Available VP still remain, spend the **locked effective VP cost** (**10 normally, 9 with eligible TAL-M03**) and instantly relocate. Do not traverse intermediate squares; apply preserved momentum and any actually encountered landing/falling hazards normally.
4. If any required condition is invalid or the locked effective cost cannot be paid at resolution, **do not relocate and spend 0 VP**. **The already committed Projection Action AP stays spent**. The attempt cannot redirect to a replacement location or silently fall back to ordinary movement. If an unrelated response cancelled the action, use the normal committed-AP/response rules; the effective VP cost is charged only on completed relocation.

Blink itself creates no Attack Sequence, attack, Guard/Dodge response, free movement, resistance bypass, change of heading or automatic hazard immunity.

## Reactive Blink and next-turn AP Debt — LOCKED

**Valid Reactive Blink trigger:** a character who can perceive a **declared incoming attack against them** may use Blink as their **one Reaction** in its **pre-roll spontaneous-response window**, or may react to a clearly imminent, unresolved physical danger they can perceive **before its effect is applied**. A previously completed hit, damage packet or other resolved event cannot be undone by retroactive Blink. A special **Blink-follow trigger** for a suspended committed attack is defined below. These are **explicit timing permissions**; merely having spare Reaction does not permit arbitrary off-turn relocation without a trigger.

**Canonical priority is mandatory:** for the **same incoming attack or hazard trigger**, [Response Windows](../05-combat/initiative-and-round-structure.md#response-windows-and-nested-responses) resolve all released **precommitted Interceptions and their nested responses first**, **then re-check whether the original incoming event is still legal/relevant**, and **only then collect spontaneous Reactions**. The defender does **not** commit a Blink destination, Reaction or VP during the earlier Interception phase. A possible future Blink does not reserve the Reaction while the Interceptions resolve.

1. **Finish the original trigger's Interception phase and re-check it.** If an Interception has cancelled the incoming attack or made the hazard irrelevant, and **no independently valid new trigger** remains, **no Reactive Blink can be declared for that cancelled event**: spend **0 Reaction, 0 VP, and incur no debt**. Any separate nested event still uses its **own** complete response procedure.
2. **At the normal spontaneous-Reaction declaration step, if the threat remains pending**, declare one specific Blink destination and pose, determine and lock any **legally applicable VP-cost reduction** (Frugal Technique applies **only if this is on the Blink user's own turn**), verify **Available VP ≥ the locked effective cost**, and **commit the one Reaction**. Other uses of that Reaction (including activating Opportunity Response or Reactive Intervene) are then unavailable this round. Resolve same-trigger responses in canonical **Initiative order**, including nested triggers; do not put Blink ahead of precommitted Interceptions.
3. **When Blink itself resolves**, check path, live perspective, clearance, protections and whether the **locked effective VP cost remains payable** under the then-current circumstances. **Success:** spend the **effective cost (normally 10 VP)**, relocate instantly, and record **next-own-turn AP Debt equal to one Projection Action** (**High 20 / Medium 24 / Low 30 AP**).
4. **Invalid, interrupted or otherwise unsuccessful attempted Blink after Reaction commitment:** relocate nowhere, spend **zero VP**, and **lose the committed Reaction**. **Do not incur AP Debt.** No free alternative destination or explanation of the failed eligibility condition is granted. A subsequently cancelled *original attack* does not retroactively refund a Reaction or VP from an otherwise legally resolved Blink.
5. **After Blink and all nested responses finish**, re-check the suspended attack/hazard under its current legality and circumstances. Blink does **not** explicitly Interrupt or automatically cancel the whole Attack Sequence. An attack that is still legal proceeds with its **already committed** profile and defense assignments. An attack made impossible by the new position fails with committed costs lost; other already committed attacks are not automatically cancelled.

### AP Debt payment

**The debt is due at Step 4 of the first own turn that *begins after* the successful Reactive Blink resolves**, not at the next occurrence of Step 4 regardless of which turn it belongs to. Record which own turn (if any) was already in progress when the Blink succeeded. If the caster reacted in **Steps 1–3 of their own current turn** (such as to an imminent start-of-turn hazard in Step 3), the still-upcoming **Step 4 of that same turn is NOT the payment point**: the debt is due at Step 4 of their **following own turn**. If Blink occurred during another creature's turn (or at Round Begin before the caster's turn has started), pay at Step 4 of the caster's **next own turn**.

At the **eligible** Step 4, calculate the usual Turn AP allotment and derived Tempo normally, then **commit/pay the due Projection Action AP Debt before voluntary AP expenditures**. Treat the debt as **prepaid AP consumption**, **not** a reduction of the character's underlying Turn AP allotment or derived Physical/Projection Tempo, Guard or Opportunity capacity. Remaining AP may be spent normally. The debt is **not VP**, does not accumulate interest, and is cleared by that payment. Merely passing through Step 4 of the **same** own turn in which Blink succeeded does **not** erase, accelerate, or pay the debt.

A character **cannot attempt another Reactive Blink while an earlier successful Reactive Blink AP Debt is still outstanding**, even if a new round refreshes their Reaction before their next own turn. This does not prohibit an otherwise legal ordinary on-turn Blink after outstanding debt has been paid.

**Exceptional case left for calibration:** if a separate effect reduces the next turn's available AP below the debt amount, that character cannot spend AP on any voluntary action until the debt is settled; the exact handling of an exceptional residual shortage is not yet locked. Under the ordinary current Turn AP progression every character has **at least 30 Turn AP**, so one Blink Debt of **20/24/30** can normally be paid in full.

## Blink-follow: Reaction pursuit of a suspended attack — LOCKED

**Trigger:** while a creature's **already declared and committed attack** against a target remains **suspended and unresolved**, that target **successfully completes Blink**, potentially escaping the attack's reach or line of fire. The completion creates a **new nested Blink-follow trigger** *after relocation* and **before the original pending attack is re-checked or marked lost**. Resolve **any matching precommitted Interceptions on this new trigger first**, including their nested responses, and re-check that a suspended attack and eligible follow trigger still exist; **only then** may the attacker declare a spontaneous Reactive Blink with an **available Reaction**, naming their own independently legal destination. This is not permission to precommit follow before an Interception resolves, or to follow an attack already cancelled before the target Blinks. The pursuer sees/learns the resulting position only to the extent ordinary perception and live magical viewpoints allow, and gains **no automatic Scrying or perfect targeting knowledge**.

- The attacker must independently have **Available VP equal to or greater than their own applicable effective Blink cost** (**normally 10**), **live destination-perspective visualization**, **one straight unobstructed spatial path from their own origin**, destination clearance and ward restrictions. A follow on an enemy's turn **cannot use TAL-M03**, because Frugal Technique works only on its owner's turn. **Follow does not copy the target's path**; it calculates a new path from the follower's present location to their proposed arrival position. They may arrive in a valid nearby position, **not** in the target's occupied volume.
- Following consumes the pursuer's **Reaction**. On a valid completed follow it spends the **pursuer's locked effective VP cost** (**normally 10**) and incurs normal next-turn AP Debt; on an invalid attempt it spends **0 VP**, loses the Reaction, incurs **no debt**, and does not move.
- Once the follow response and its nested responses finish, re-check **each pending attack as it comes due** against the **originally declared target**, using the **original committed attack profiles, remaining AP commitments and defender choices**. If reach, line, weapon/profile and other prerequisites are still legal, the suspended attack resolves normally; otherwise that attack is lost. **No fresh attack, new Attack Sequence, new target selection, changed weapon/profile, additional attacks, reset of Aim, or fresh defense assignment** is granted just because follow succeeded.
- **Nested Blink responses remain legal only with an unspent Reaction and a valid trigger.** The target that already spent their single Reaction to escape cannot Blink again in the same round. A chain cannot repeatedly spend one Reaction, or reopen an attack already resolved or ruled cancelled.
- **Counter-follow is not automatic Interrupt**. The first Blink has already succeeded; follow may preserve the *attacker's* ordinary attack legality but does not undo the displacement or force the target back to their prior square.

This special follow window is available for an individually suspended standalone attack or an attack in a committed **Attack Sequence**. It does not revive an attack that has already completed resolution, including earlier attacks in the same sequence. Precommitted Interception priority, ordinary response order and one-attack-sequence-per-target restrictions in [Initiative and Round Structure](../05-combat/initiative-and-round-structure.md) otherwise remain intact.

## Regression scenarios

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| 20 m courtyard with accurate live view, straight open path and clear arrival | Blink may succeed despite no authored fixed metre limit; normal 10 VP cost |
| Scrying supplies live mountaintop viewpoint; direct line from valley is clear | Blink may succeed if arrival volume, wards and all other restrictions pass; separate Scrying access/cost still required |
| Same Scrying view, but solid ridge/planetary curvature interrupts the line | Invalid Blink; **0 VP**, committed AP **or Reaction lost**, no reactive AP Debt |
| Transparent closed window between caster and visible room | Invalid (solid glass blocks); **0 VP**, action/reaction lost |
| Target Blinks beyond melee reach; attacker spends unused Reaction to follow within reach | If both Blinks are legal, original committed pending melee attack can continue; no new attack |
| Attacker tries to follow through a solid wall | Follow invalid; attacker loses Reaction, spends **0 VP**, attack still fails if now out of reach |
| Target responds to follow with another Reactive Blink but already spent their Reaction | Illegal; no second Reaction or free escape |
| Reactive Blink succeeds on another creature's turn | Pay **20/24/30** AP Debt at Step 4 of caster's next own turn; base Turn AP / Tempo and Guard capacity unchanged |
| Reactive Blink succeeds at **Step 3 of caster's own turn** against an unresolved start-of-turn hazard | The **current turn's Step 4 does not pay** the debt. Pay at Step 4 of the **following own turn** |
| Precommitted Interception cancels an incoming attack before spontaneous Reactions | Defender cannot declare Reactive Blink on the cancelled original attack: **0 Reaction, 0 VP, no debt** |
| Precommitted Interception changes but does not cancel the attack | Re-check original event first; defender may declare Blink **after** Interceptions if still a valid threat |
| Interception is triggered by a completed Blink-follow event | Resolve it **before** the pursuer's spontaneous follow Reaction, and re-check the new trigger |
| Reactive Blink is declared after Interceptions, but a later nested response invalidates the Blink | **Reaction remains spent**; **0 VP, no debt** without completed relocation |
| Reactive Blink fails to find a legal destination | Lose Reaction, spend **0 VP**, **no AP Debt** |
| Normal Blink fails | Lose prepaid Projection Action AP, spend **0 VP** |
| Normal on-turn Blink with unused **TAL-M03** and **9 Available VP** | **Legal to declare**; Frugal Technique is committed; if successful, spend **9 VP** and normal Projection Action AP |
| Normal on-turn Blink with **9 Available VP** but no applicable discount | **Cannot declare**; no action/AP or VP is committed |
| Reactive Blink during **another creature's turn**, with **TAL-M03** and **9 Available VP** | **Cannot declare**: Frugal Technique's own-turn condition does not apply, so cost is **10** |
| Reactive Blink during **own turn**, unused **TAL-M03**, and **9 Available VP** | **Legal if an independent Reaction trigger exists**; success costs **9 VP**, Reaction and next-own-turn AP Debt |
| Frugal Technique is selected for an attempted Blink that fails due to blocked path | **0 VP** spent; normal AP or Reaction lost, but the once-on-own-turn Frugal use is still consumed |
| Another effect spends VP before a declared discounted Blink resolves, leaving **less than 9 Available VP** | Relocation fails, **0 VP** spent on Blink; its committed AP or Reaction stays spent |
| Falling traveler Blinks immediately before impact | Original downward velocity persists; no free fall-damage cancellation |

## Compatibility and open content

**LOCKED:** live perspective rather than remembered destination; no fixed distance; straight open path including barred openings and exclusion of glass; whole-body clearance; velocity conservation; normal Projection Action / reactive Reaction, **10 VP base cost with independently applicable cost modifiers (including on-turn TAL-M03)** paid only on completed relocation; reactive AP Debt on success only; invalid attempt costs committed AP/Reaction but **no VP**; Blink-follow counterplay before suspended attack's final legality re-check.

**STILL DEFERRED:** Universal learning/preparation and exact technique-tier eligibility; Scrying acquisition, action and VP mechanics; complex AP-reduction/debt edge cases; exceptionally complicated physically moving camera viewpoints and vehicle reference-frame calibration; explicit anti-Translocation wards. This page does not authorize free or otherwise unauthored Scrying, nor turn Blink into higher-level Personal Teleportation.
