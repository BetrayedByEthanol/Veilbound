# Gentle Growth v0 — Life / Nature

> **Executable Tier-1 proposal; numerical and ecological utility balance PROVISIONAL.** Gentle Growth encourages **one small, natural developmental change in already-living vegetation** over one minute. The result is ordinary, persistent biological growth; this spell **does not conjure vegetation, create food/wood from nothing, restore damaged tissue, preserve it against decay, compel animals, or grant battlefield entanglement**. Its canonical **Life / Nature Aspect is External (E)**; Aspect category is independent of whether a particular technique actually uses bodily Intrusion. Publication does not itself grant technique access to any character.

## Rooted Plant acquisition and biological target limits

**Eligible individual:** one real living plant rooted in its suitable substrate—soil, moss, a viable bed, or an ordinary pot/container—or one viable seed **already placed in a plausible suitable germination substrate** with at least part of its seed coat actually visible. Ordinary potted plants count as rooted even if a person carries the pot; do not require that roots visibly protrude. The caster targets the **living organism**, not the pot, soil, plant patch, entire connected forest, garden bed, colony or several seeds. A clump of individually rooted plants is **multiple plants**, even when leaves overlap. If a plant consists of a larger shared root system, the one cast is still limited to **one selected shoot or up to three selected buds** and never gains distributed output from the system's total size.

**Invalid:** dead branches and timber, detached cuttings without a viable rooted organism, harvested seeds with no suitable planting medium, seeds wholly hidden under soil, dry/inert seeds that are not viable, fungi unless separately classified/authorized by a later ecological technique, plant remains, mere illustrations, and **sapient/animated plant creatures, treants or plant-based monsters**. A planted seed can legally begin germination but **cannot** simultaneously receive Shoot or Bloom before such organs actually exist. Flowers attached to a harvested dead stalk are not living growth targets. This profile grants **no creature-targeting route** and therefore cannot be used to bypass living-creature Intrusion defenses.

**3D technique-local distance:** Select the plant's stable visible exposed target point or the exposed part of the planted seed **before** declarations/responses; place that point in its containing 1.5 m grid cell. Determine cell-coordinate offsets `dx,dy,dz` between caster's recorded reference occupied cell and the target cell. Take absolute offsets, sort `a >= b >= c`, and compute **`d = a + 0.5*b + 0.5*c`** square-equivalents, without rounding. `d ≤ 3` is necessary but not sufficient. For example `(2,2,0)` → `3` (eligible); `(3,1,0)` → `3.5` (out of range). For a multi-cell caster use their existing fixed reference cell, or preselect lowest-x then lowest-y then lowest-z occupied cell if absent; never choose the best cell after seeing an outcome. This is **not** an override of ordinary movement distance.

**Line and visibility:** The targeting segment must be **straight through unobstructed open air**, not along a bending route. A solid wall, door, opaque obstacle, or solid transparent glass blocks it, even if the caster can see the plant through glass. An open window or actual opening can qualify if the line reaches the selected plant tissue. A wholly buried seed is not visually exposed and is not a target through soil; the caster cannot acquire it by guessing where it lies. A visible seed resting partly exposed on suitable moist growing medium can qualify. Once the magical application has succeeded, the plant can later move with its pot or disappear from sight; range and sight are **not continuously maintained**.

**Interaction:** Life/Nature is classified External, but this particular effect **directly accelerates a living target's internal development** and therefore uses **beneficial bodily Intrusion** rather than environmental Imposition or manifested plant creation. An eligible ordinary rooted plant or viable seed is not a conscious hostile person and does **not** roll a fabricated Intelligence/Resonance contest. If a magical ward or other specifically authored protection applies, resolve its actual rule; a failed valid application still costs the ordinary Spent VP. **Mere ownership/attendance of a mundane plant or pot does not confer an invented owner's Direct Veil Resistance**: this is not an object-moving Imposition effect. Sapient/animated plant creatures are invalid here, not treated as unopposed ordinary plants. No default Shaping/Resistance Ability pair is needed, since this v0 creates **no ordinary resisted-plant mode**; a future creature-compatible mode must author its own defense.

## Primary effect — choose exactly one expression

Declare the **one selected expression, the specific plant/seed and the concrete organ(s)** before responses, including any preferred biologically plausible growth direction. On a legal successful application, growth begins gradually over **60 seconds** and produces **at most** that one expression's output. A caster cannot divide one activation among multiple expressions, repeatedly select another branch/seed later, move the spell to another plant, or choose an outcome retroactively.

### A. Encourage Shoot — one flexible growing organ

Cause **up to 0.5 metres (50 cm)** of *new*, **flexible, naturally plausible tissue** along **one existing viable growing shoot, vine tip, branch tip or root tip** of the plant over the full minute. The caster may indicate a **general direction or nearby available support** at declaration, but cannot precisely puppeteer the tip, specify each bend after application, make rigid architectural shapes, rapidly thicken a trunk, or create a free-standing object. Species with no eligible shoot/root tissue for this change are not legal for this expression; no anatomical conversion is granted. Growth ends early at a hard obstruction, if resources/viability fail, or if its remaining length budget is exhausted; unused length is lost.

The new tissue cannot exert mechanically significant force against a creature, worn/carried equipment, a binding, a lock, stone, a door, a wall or another barrier. **It cannot grow into/through an occupied cell, wrap or fasten around a creature, displace objects, produce a trip/grapple, or create load-bearing climbing gear.** Its root can extend only through suitable loose substrate or an actually open gap; it cannot push solid objects aside, break masonry or penetrate an unwilling person's body.

### B. Encourage Bloom — existing mature buds only

Cause **up to three already sufficiently developed flower buds on the same one living plant** to open gradually during the minute. Choose the actual buds at declaration, and choose no more buds than exist and are biologically ready. The technique **does not create buds, new flowers, pollen with authored mechanical effects, seeds, fruit, food, harvest yield, medicines, poison, or guaranteed reproductive success**. Species with no appropriately mature buds are ineligible for this expression. The open flowers have their natural species properties; the magic does not make a target adopt a new colour, scent, toxin or breed. Ordinary downstream pollination/fruiting, if ever possible, follows natural circumstances **after** this spell, not a magical yield bonus.

### C. Encourage Sprouting — one planted viable seed

Cause **one already viable and visibly accessible planted seed** to begin naturally plausible germination, developing **up to 5 centimetres of young seedling** over the minute, including such initial root/shoot development as the species supports. The seed needs **viable biological potential, suitable growing medium and enough water/nutrients for that modest change** at application. Dormancy can be encouraged only when the seed is actually viable and the conditions could support its germination; this does not fix missing germination prerequisites by producing nonexistent matter or making a dead seed alive. It does not turn one seed into multiple seedlings, produce an adult plant, edible fruit, lumber, edible mass or a harvest.

A seed that has germinated becomes an ordinary young plant; later **separately paid** casts can target an actually qualifying shoot only after one exists and remains supported by its environment. Neither the first activation nor later activations provide free biological reserves.

## Material conservation, mundane outcomes and ecological boundaries

The supernatural contribution is **encouraging biological development**, not creating matter or energy without bounds. The resulting organism uses **resources already reasonably available to it**: water, nutrients and viable living tissue/seed reserves, with whatever environmental conditions actually support its species. No automatic fixed number of food units, magical fertilizer, water conjuration, guaranteed photosynthesis, genetic mutation, repair of damaged structures, or instant conversion of sand/stone into soil follows.

Before successful application the GM checks the **established biological circumstances**, not an invented universal plant-nutrient numerical pool. An ordinarily viable plant in adequate conditions can grow within the small limit **without a fabricated botanical skill check**. A dead/diseased plant that cannot support normal growth, or a depleted/unsuitable medium, may yield **no eligible expression**, as stated before paying a valid application where observable. Repeated successful **new, paid casts are allowed** if normal life-supporting resources plausibly remain, but they consume real plant reserves. Subsequent casts cannot pretend the substrate, organism and available resources are unchanged after cumulative growth. No hidden per-day spell limit is introduced; also no unlimited growth from a small pot, exhausted seed reserves or starved cutting.

**Nature vs other domains:**
- **Restoration** repairs damaged biology; Gentle Growth **cannot repair wounds, rotten tissue, dead seedlings, injured plant creatures, HP/Vigor, Dying states or damaged reproductive organs**. Simply naming the growth a "repair" does not authorize it.
- **Preservation** prevents deterioration; Gentle Growth **does not prevent disease, drought, withering, age, rot, poisoning or death**.
- **Bounty** changes land fertility, harvests, yield and broad resource productivity; Gentle Growth **does not grant a farm/crop productivity multiplier, instant harvest or material surplus**.
- **Future Nature control** (e.g. Entangling Growth) may author forcing vegetation into binding/defense, but **this Tier-1 spell supplies no binding, difficult terrain, guard/cover, grapple, displacement, battlefield hazard, enemy restraint or attack**. Existing normal plant matter may have ordinary environmental consequences in later fiction, but no new combat statistics arise solely from this technique.
- **Animals and plant creatures** receive no communication, obedience, growth, control or friendly-attitude modifier; Woodland Rapport and future plant-creature expressions require separately authored profiles.

**Persistence:** Once produced, tissue/buds/seedlings are **real ordinary biology**. They survive after the 60-second magical expression completes and do not disappear at dispel/caster death. Environmental needs and normal injury still apply. The technique provides **no guaranteed lifespan, hardiness, perpetual flower or extra growth beyond the authored small change**.

## Casting, payment, completion, and interruption

1. **Declare:** Confirm legitimate access to the individual technique, one legal `Rooted Plant` target and exactly one expression with selected organ(s), eligibility/resources, caster's action capability, direct range/sight/open magical path, no other still-active Gentle Growth process from this caster or on the chosen plant, and **≥1 Available VP** after any genuinely applicable effects. **TAL-M03 Frugal Technique does not discount this standalone 1-Spent spell**, as it requires a normal Spent VP component of at least 2. If this is an eligible unprepared **High Priest Open Invocation**, also lock its **separate +1 Spent VP surcharge** and its L1–4 Full Action requirement (all Turn AP unspent), or normal Projection Action at L5+; need **2 Available VP** for that invocation. No legal declaration means no action/VP spent and no response window.
2. **Commit activation:** Pay one own-turn **Projection Action** at the appropriate Focus AP (High 20 / Medium 24 / Low 30), except eligible L1–4 Open Invocation spends a **Full Action consuming the entire Turn AP allotment**. Handle ordinary responses, interruptions, and changing legal circumstances. This spell provides no Reaction or automatic growth before the application.
3. **Recheck before application:** Caster still conscious/capable; selected plant/seed still eligible, living/viable and exposed; its actual location still within range and unobstructed direct sight/line; selected botanical organ still exists; single-instance cap unoccupied; entire locked **ordinary 1 Spent + any independent Invocation surcharge** available. If an interrupted or otherwise ineligible attempt **never reaches a valid magical application**, paid AP/Full Action remain lost, but **no VP** are Spent; this is not a free replacement cast.
4. **Apply and spend:** At a valid application **spend 1 ordinary VP**, plus the **separate 1 Spent Invocation surcharge** if used, even when an applicable independent magical ward/suppression defeats the application. No routine Direct Veil contest on unopposed ordinary plants/viable seeds, no improvised owner contest, and no material creation. If the target is eligible and no actual ward defeats application, establish **one** 60-second process with the selected finite output; **no Committed VP** are created.
5. **Complete or terminate:** Progress occurs gradually during the valid window, using only available physical clearance and resources. On completion or prior interruption by **plant death, loss of biological viability/substrate, severe obstruction, special dispel/suppression before completion, or exhaustion of the eligible output**, end the process. Already physically developed tissue persists normally; **any unused promised output is lost**, not banked or reassigned. Voluntarily changing the target, expression, direction, buds, or seed is not authorized after successful application; there is no refundable reservation or caster concentration to release. An independently completed process is **not automatically canceled** because the caster walks away, becomes unconscious or dies.
6. **Follow-on actions:** Identification, botanical care, transplanting, climbing, constructing, knotting, tracking and interpretation use ordinary noncombat procedures (and actions/AP when relevant). **No bonus or free check** from Gentle Growth itself; if a genuine uncertain independent task arises use established Ability/Training rules rather than manufacturing a check to use the spell.

### One-minute completion through initiative boundaries

Outside initiative, record the **whole seconds remaining** from successful application to 60 seconds later. In initiative, a cast successfully applied during round **R** completes at **Round Begin R+10**, assuming still valid. Effects before full completion are **visible incremental ordinary development** but supply **no guaranteed mechanical partial-length threshold, new combat obstacle, terrain condition or early completion**. The actual biological tissue already developed remains real if the process ends early, but its length/output cannot exceed a proportional fictionally plausible part of the one declared cap; do not award a second free full output after conversion.

Use the same **technique-local conservative transition** as Guided Regeneration and Vital Shelter, not a universal round-duration rule:
- **Exploration → initiative:** With `s` whole elapsed seconds remaining at transition, start initiative in round R and set `q=floor(s/6)`. If `q=0`, the process completes **immediately at the transition** (without extra combat benefit); otherwise completion is **Round Begin R+q**. Discard any remainder rather than lengthening the process.
- **Initiative → exploration:** Leave in current round C with scheduled completion Round Begin E. Set `n=max(0,E-C)` and `s=6*max(0,n-1)` whole seconds remaining; if `s=0`, complete at transition. Give no credit for partial round C. Track only **remaining** time across later transitions, never start a new 60-second growth timer.
- **Expiry vs failure:** Scheduled completion yields at most the declared expression's fully supported biological growth. A target made dead/ineligible before then ends the process early, with no VP refund or magically preserved viability. Outside initiative no free per-round growth ticks or a blanket "six seconds per combat round" rule are created.

## Explicit compatibility and scope limits

- **No Secondary contribution or Rider contribution is authored.** `Rooted Plant` does not exactly match Basic Heal/Guided Regeneration/Vital Shelter's `Self` or `Single Target` profiles; knowing those spells supplies **no High Scholar Composite**. A future rooted-plant-compatible spell would still need genuinely compatible delivery, one valid host and current High Scholar authorization; do not invent it from matching names alone.
- **No Medium Priest Active Consecration:** A one-minute, nonreserved biological process is not a caster-maintained transferable blessing; no Reassign or Intercession.
- **No Low Priest Active Devotional Rite:** The effect is not an Inward `Self` or worn-equipment maintained Reserve; Chosen Devotion -1 Committed and Exaltation do not apply.
- **No reactive casting, persistent Rider, plant attack, repeat activation, free harvesting action, multi-seed field or multi-target.** Future high-tier nature growth/animate/entangle/animal speech and magical crafting remain unauthored. **Used Tier is exactly 1**, regardless of general character ceiling.
- **High Priest access:** An eligible **prepared** Gentle Growth costs the ordinary **1 Spent VP + Focus-priced Projection Action**; unprepared chosen-Pillar **Open Invocation** costs **2 Spent VP total** (1 ordinary + 1 separate surcharge), **Full Action L1–4**, normal action L5+, if the Priest has real access to that Tier-1 technique. At L3 Open Prayer an eligible prepared-slot fill avoids the Invocation surcharge but does not grant an extra technique or free VP.

## Calibration and deferred work

- **GG-01 — utility and range:** Does **0.5 m** of flexible new shoot and **5 cm** of seedling feel meaningfully useful compared with 1-Spent VP, without becoming a structural or control spell? If not, test an explicit **1 m** alternative in a later balance revision; do not silently enlarge the present cap.
- **GG-02 — biological feasibility:** Track how often genuine resource/viability requirements make the spell unusable or enable excessively repeated growth; do **not** introduce arbitrary hidden numeric soil meters or unlimited matter creation.
- **GG-03 — exploration timing:** Check 60-second completion and conservative initiative transitions in play, and whether gradual permanent natural output enables creative but noncombat environmental solutions.
- **GG-04 — Aspect separation:** Nature encourages existing organism development; keep **Restoration** as repair, **Preservation** as damage prevention, **Bounty** as land/crop yield. Treat future higher-tier Entangling Growth and Woodland Rapport as separately authored content, not implied Tier-1 riders.
- Distinct `Rooted Plant` Secondary or Rider, other compatible High Scholar composite pair, plant creature target/defense, broad agricultural alteration, poison/sap/medicinal effect, growth-based hazards, Tier-2+ variants, plant animation/treant forms and animal communication are **deferred**.
