# High Gifted — Amplification, Projected Surge and Genuine Miscasts v0

> **Canonical level-1–3 High Gifted rules and universal Gifted Instability resolution.** This authorizes only the stated techniques/profiles, not new Pillar access, extra actions, automatic extra reactions, free damage, native encounter or full 12-Focus MVP certification. Read with [Gifted Foundation](../08-archetypes/gifted.md), [Veil Expression](technique-tiers-and-expression.md), [Technique Structure](technique-structure-v0.md), [Turn AP](../05-combat/action-categories-and-physical-tempo.md), and [Volition Points](volition-points.md).

## Conservative Amplification — six categories

One Amplification Step buys **one explicitly supported option** on **one exact technique profile**. A technique may offer only a subset, or none. Declare selected options with the spell and consume the corresponding available Steps from the **current Emotion Window**, never per attack or per damage packet. No repeated category on the same cast unless the technique explicitly permits it. None change Used Tier, create new technique access, actions, attacks or reactions, bypass targeting/defenses, or spend extra VP unless a distinct feature says so.

| Category | Exact modifier | No implicit applicability |
|---|---|---|
| **Potency** | For **one** eligible original damage die, roll a second die with the same sides and use the higher face **before** the spell's existing division/rounding. | Not every hit, attack, target, critical bonus die, periodic tick, healing roll, or fixed packet; source must authorize its die and finite eligible instance. |
| **Reach** | Increase one authored maximum delivery range by **1 square (1.5 m / approximately 5 ft)**. | Does not change minimum range, line of effect, cover, legal targets or trajectory. |
| **Extent** | Increase one authored existing **area radius** by **1 square (1.5 m)**. | No area is created for single-target/line spells; existing targeting/hazard rules still apply. |
| **Persistence** | **Double an originally finite duration**, using its actual source clock and end anchor. | Not instantaneous, event-only, indefinite, condition-maintained or Committed-VP-duration effects; no extra pulses per interval. |
| **Penetration** | **+1 to the caster's applicable Direct Veil Pressure roll** made against resistance. | Not +1 attack, -1 typed resistance, Armor Penetration, Dodge or Guard. |
| **Precision** | **+1 to one eligible technique attack roll**. | Not two d20 rolls/advantage, no extra attack, no change to critical rules. |

**Expression/type conversion is not an Amplification category.** Fire / Conflagration cannot become Lightning / Strike through this feature. All source packets retain their own HP/Vigor/resource/type properties.

**Amplification and timing:** The Evoked state grants no natural Step, Heightened grants one safe Step, and Overwhelming grants a second **risky** Step. These Steps refresh/expire only at the Gifted Emotion Establishment Step. A character can use a compatible purchased Surge Step while Evoked; Surge does not Heighten the emotion. The **first intensity-generated Step is safe**; actually spending the Overwhelming second Step on a spell calls for its Instability Check **once in that Emotion Window**. If the roll fails by 1–4, the risky Step has no effect; if it fails by 5+, the risky Step has no effect and the miscast table applies. The purchased Surge Step is independently paid and can survive Instability unless a miscast specifically removes Amplification or cancels the spell. A successful risk check permits the risky Step. Unused Steps expire; a different emotion does not restart the Window.

## Projected Surge — High Gifted L1–3

Only an individually authorized **[Projection]** technique/profile with a **positive effective VP activation price** is eligible. Merely costing a Projection Action does **not** confer the expression tag. The legal on-turn or explicitly authored Reactive timing must exist first.

**Extra Spent VP = `ceil(effective technique VP price / 4)`**; use the positive, actually payable base technique amount (including legal discounts), excluding the Surge surcharge itself. For a legally available ongoing Reserve, calculate from its payable initial Committed VP, but pay the surcharge as **Spent**, not Committed; its normal maintenance/release still obeys the source. On an immediate cast all base Spent VP and Surge VP are Spent. A cast cannot begin if the combined AP/VP commitment is unaffordable. Normal fizzle forfeits the already paid **Spent** VP; no effect/Reserve is established, and an unestablished reserve cannot remain Committed. Do not automatically make 0-VP Anointed Signature Expressions eligible for Gifted Surge.

At L1–3 there is **one purchased Surge Step per Emotion Window**. It must buy a distinct legal Amplification option on one eligible technique/profile. It can be reserved for a separately authorized Reaction in the same Window, but does not itself grant a Reaction or defer AP. The L4 and L5 established progression remains separate and requires future numeric verification; this v0 does not amend L4 efficiency or L5 quantity.

**Concurrency:** The same spell may receive safe, risky and Surge categories if separately paid/available, with no duplicates. If the miscast fizzes or replaces the spell, paid Surge VP and the allowance remain consumed. This is not a resource-refund mechanic. Normal VP recovery is unaffected.

## Genuine Instability — replaces old severe Manifestation Spill restriction

When the **Overwhelming second intensity-generated Step is actually attempted**, roll `d20 + Veil Control + Emotional Discipline Training + Resonance` vs `12 + ceil(Used Tier / 2)`; Tier 1–2 DC 13. Only **one** Instability Check per Emotion Window. A successful check preserves the first and second intensity Steps. Failure by **1–4** loses the risky second Step, with the original technique otherwise resolving. Failure by **5+** loses the risky Step and requires **one d20 genuine miscast**. The latter explicitly **supersedes** the Gifted Foundation's older protection of the original spell and old prohibition on random original-target changes. No unbounded d100 tables or free spells.

**Sequence:** Source preflight (known/linked Aspect, active emotion, Used Tier, source-legal profile, AP/VP, targets, tags, options) → declare/pay action and applicable VP/Surge → check Instability **before the first affected attack/contest roll and responses** → select the one miscast outcome and any required recorded uniform draw → resolve final legal target/profile/effect with the normal attack, cover, Guard, Dodge, Intrusion, damage and wound rules. An originally legal target cannot be voluntarily changed after seeing the d20. Native effects still require actual source-ledger bindings. Never invent a hit or a second action.

### Universal Miscast Table (roll d20 **exactly once**, no table rerolls)

| d20 | Outcome | Deterministic result / inapplicable fallback |
|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **Complete Fizzle** | No spell effect; committed action and already Spent VP are lost. |
| **2** | **Dissipation** | Fizzle plus a harmless visible/audible same-Aspect discharge; no independent mechanical effect. |
| **3** | **Lost Amplification** | Suppress **all** selected Amplification on this cast, including Surge; ordinary spell otherwise resolves **only if the original target remains legal without those modifiers** (otherwise fizzle). Spent VP is not refunded. |
| **4** | **Random Retarget** | Uniformly select one **other** legal target, enemies **or allies**; normal defenses apply. No other eligible target → original target. |
| **5** | **Nearest Retarget** | Choose the nearest **other** eligible target, enemies **or allies**, uniformly breaking ties. None → original target. |
| **6** | **Scatter** | Displace the applicable physical manifestation by one uniformly determined adjacent grid direction; apply real grid/line/delivery legality and any ordinary impact defenses. If its new square is illegal → fizzle; if this profile has no position to scatter → resolve normally. **No automatic hit.** |
| **7** | **Reduced Reach** | Decrease the **current effective maximum range** (including surviving safe/Surge Reach) by 1 square, minimum 0; retain the profile's authored minimum range. If the original target is outside the resulting legal range → fizzle. No range property → resolve normally. |
| **8** | **Reduced Extent** | Existing area radius −1 square, minimum 0. No radius → resolve normally; do not turn an inapplicable minor penalty into fizzle. |
| **9** | **Reduced Persistence** | Existing finite duration halved, rounding up to one source clock unit. No finite duration → resolve normally. |
| **10** | **Unsteady Aim** | −2 to the technique attack roll. No attack roll → resolve normally. |
| **11** | **Weakening Pressure** | −2 to the applicable Direct Veil Pressure roll. No contest → resolve normally. |
| **12** | **Weak Manifestation** | One eligible original damage die takes its minimum face. No damage die → resolve normally. |
| **13** | **Unstable Potency** | Roll one eligible original damage die twice; take lower. No damage die → resolve normally. |
| **14** | **Vigor Backlash** | Original spell otherwise resolves; caster loses Vigor equal to **this cast's actually Spent VP** (base plus Surge, not unrelated prior expenses/Committed VP), through existing Vigor rules. |
| **15** | **Typed Backlash** | Original spell otherwise resolves; caster takes **1** damage of the spell's **actual primary damaging packet's resource and type**, subject to normal defenses/mitigation that apply to such self-damage. If spell deals no damage → **fizzle**. |
| **16** | **VP Leakage** | Spend **1 additional VP** (no action). If not available at miscast resolution → **fizzle**; do not create VP debt. |
| **17** | **Uncontrolled Exposure** | Spell resolves with an obvious same-Aspect manifestation at caster; no unsupported Stealth/defense penalty, attack or guaranteed revelation through barriers. |
| **18** | **Altered Manifestation** | Uniformly draw **one already-authored same-Aspect technique with Base Tier ≤ original Used Tier, including the original**. Use its predetermined legal **Primary** profile against **the original target**; must meet the original paid activation AP/VP budget and the replacement's own Used Tier, target, range, delivery and access rules. If it cannot legally resolve → **fizzle**; no second draw, refund, free technique or extra damage. |
| **19** | **Perfect Manifestation** | Maximize one eligible **original damage die** (not critical, every hit, fixed packet or every recurring pulse). No eligible die → resolve normally. |
| **20** | **Unexpected Amplification** | Apply one distinct remaining authored compatible option to **this spell** without further VP; exclude **every option already selected on this cast** (safe, attempted risky even if lost, and purchased Surge). None available → resolve normally. No new action, attack, tier or spell. |

**No reroll policy:** Inapplicable *minor* drawbacks and unavailable *benefits* resolve normally, not as a harsher fizzle. Explicitly listed serious failure results **do** fizzle. Miscast outcomes never recursively trigger another Instability Check, Surge allowance, or miscast table. When a new legal target is chosen, all its ordinary responses remain available; the old defender's reactions do not automatically transfer.

**Altered Manifestation source ordering:** Build the uniformly drawn pool from **actual individually authored techniques**, not headings for shared profile collections or universal operations. Sort by unique technique identity for stable audited draws. The original is included exactly once. For a selected different technique, use the lowest originally legal **Primary** profile keyed in source order that supports the original target, with **Used Tier no higher than original Used Tier** and no unaffordable cost/greater AP; do not reselect a more convenient profile after the random draw. If no legal resolution exists, fizzle. A replacement does not get a second new Amplification selection. For Conjure Flame at Tier 1, the only currently authored Fire / Conflagration technique is **Conjure Flame**: result 18 is thus effectively the original Thrown Projectile profile, subject to all other lost Steps/costs and normal hit/defenses.

**Scatter and retarget validation:** Alternate creatures may include allies only if normally eligible to receive this exact spell. Require actual **minimum and maximum range** (including surviving safe/Surge Reach, not a lost risky Reach), sight/open path, target classification, cover and equipment restrictions; static candidate examples are not battle certificates. A self-only effect has no alternative creature target. Uniform selection uses an independent recorded draw within the **legal** candidate set, never a reroll of the d20 table. A spell that cannot resolve on an accidental target **does not auto-hit** it.

## Explicit L1–3 initial compatibility and unresolved evidence

**Only [Conjure Flame](conjure-flame-v0.md) `Thrown Projectile` Primary is newly tagged `[Projection]` and authorized for `Potency`, `Reach` and `Precision` in this slice.** Its Potency modifies the single `d8` in the **primary** Vigor expression `2 + ceil(d8×T/3) + VC`, not its fixed Fire HP packet, other contributions or critical dice. Reach expands its legal 1–3-square maximum to 4 squares, retaining +0 source Range Difficulty in the added square. Precision adds +1 to its existing Throwing-based attack roll. It remains one attack, costs 20 AP and `2×T Spent VP`; Surge adds `ceil((2×T)/4)` Spent VP. `Weapon Enchantment` and `Ammunition Shot` are **not** thereby marked compatible; neither their damage dice nor physical bow hits acquire Gifted Amplification by resemblance. Fire HP and separate Vigor burn remain distinct; no Lightning conversion/automatic burning area.

For the restricted pilot, the 8 linked Aspects use the previously source-checked **6 Strong / 2 Plausible** character-creation menu. This document authors no automatic amplification for its other seven techniques; add those individually when needed. Universal Veil Inspection and Blink remain separate unamplified operations.

**Action-economy caveat:** At L1–3, Turn AP is **30/36/42**, Deepen/Overload each cost **24 AP**, and High Projection costs **20 AP**: `24+20=44`, unaffordable in one normal own turn even at L3. There is therefore **no authored same-turn Overload→Thrown Flame miscast witness**. A real Reactive/ongoing/emotion trigger needs separately sourced legality; this v0's pure model verifies the miscast decision table but does **not** invent a naturally Overwhelming opening or a free cast. Missing native G0/G3/G4/G5 integration, complete High Gifted sheet/equipment/training and observed encounter/noncombat play remain strict downstream acceptance work. **Do not change the active 6/12 structural, 0/12 certified, or six MVP gate counts on this slice alone.**
