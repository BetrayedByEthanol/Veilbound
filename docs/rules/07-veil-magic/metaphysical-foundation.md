# Metaphysical Foundation: Desire, Will, Volition, and Resonance

> **LOCKED metaphysical foundation.** This page defines what Veilbound's core metaphysical terms mean and how the four Archetypes relate to the same underlying system. Numerical procedures for universal Resonance and magical resistance remain deliberately unresolved unless an existing Archetype rule already says otherwise.

## The common metaphysical chain

Veilbound distinguishes four related concepts.

### Desire

**Desire** is the raw direction of a person's wants, needs, emotions, convictions, impulses, and longings.

Desire does not have to be rational, moral, conscious, or internally consistent.

Collective human desire is one of the forces from which Pillars emerge and change.

### Will

**Will** is the act of selecting, organizing, sustaining, and acting upon desire.

Will is not identical to stubbornness or certainty. A person may possess strong will while changing their mind, accepting a contradiction, or deliberately choosing between competing desires.

### Volition

**Volition** is the usable metaphysical force produced when desire and will are directed into coherent intention and action through the Veil.

Volition is not the same thing as character level, Veil Affinity, Veil Control, or Resonance.

Those concepts describe different properties of the same wider relationship:

- **Level** describes depth of integration with the Veil.
- **Focus** describes where that integration is predominantly expressed.
- **Veil Affinity** describes how much Veil power/Volition the character can contain and channel.
- **Veil Control** describes how precisely, efficiently, and forcefully the character can shape it.
- **Resonance** describes the internal coherence of the person producing and directing that Volition.

## Universal Resonance

**Resonance** is a universal character state on a **-3 to +3** scale.

It measures how coherently the character's desires, emotions, beliefs, intentions, self-understanding, and actions reinforce rather than oppose one another.

Resonance is **not morality**.

It is also not loyalty, obedience, confidence, fanaticism, or ideological purity.

A cruel person may possess high Resonance if their internal state and actions are genuinely coherent. A compassionate person may possess low Resonance while deeply self-deceived or internally fragmented.

Changing one's beliefs does not inherently lower Resonance. Honest recognition and integration of conflict may strengthen it.

Temporary doubt, fear, sadness, anger, or ordinary indecision do not automatically change Resonance. The eventual universal procedure for gaining and losing Resonance must focus on meaningful internal contradiction, integration, self-deception, and resolution rather than encounter-by-encounter optimization.

### Mechanical boundary

The universal numerical procedure for Resonance is **not yet locked**.

Still unresolved include:

- starting Resonance;
- exact gain/loss cadence;
- whether changes are normally limited to one step at a time;
- recovery or stabilization procedures;
- the exact universal effects of positive and negative Resonance;
- the final Intrusion-resistance formula;
- whether Resonance affects VP recovery;
- any special rules at +3 or -3.

Existing Gifted rules that already include **Resonance** refer to this universal Resonance state.

Resonance does not automatically add to all spell attacks, damage, healing, technique output, Max VP, or ordinary checks.

## Volition Points are freely available Volition

**Volition Points (VP)** represent the portion of a character's Volition that remains **freely available for deliberate allocation** to techniques and sustained effects.

VP does **not** represent the character's total metaphysical integration or total Volition.

A character may have substantial Veil integration already expressed persistently through body, reflexes, equipment relationships, or other developed structures. That integration remains real even though it is not represented as freely spendable VP.

No separate numeric "Total Volition" pool is tracked.

## Level and Focus

**Level represents deeper integration with the Veil.**

**Focus determines the predominant direction of that integration.**

This explains the different VP pools by Focus without implying that a High-Focus character possesses more total will or metaphysical worth than a Low-Focus character.

### High Focus — Projection

High Focus leaves more of the character's growing Veil integration freely available for deliberate outward shaping and projection.

It therefore has the largest freely available VP reservoir.

### Medium Focus — Routing

Medium Focus develops the ability to redirect Veil integration between body, equipment, persistent effects, and outward techniques.

It therefore retains a moderate freely available VP reservoir while specializing in allocation and reconfiguration.

### Low Focus — Embodiment

Low Focus expresses a larger share of growing Veil integration persistently through the character's body, reflexes, physical capability, equipment interaction, and other embodied development.

Less of that integration remains freely available as VP.

This is not lesser Veil integration. It is differently expressed Veil integration.

## Priest: Resonance and Pillar Accord are separate

The Priest-specific state previously named **Priest Resonance** is renamed **Pillar Accord**.

**Pillar Accord** measures coherence between the Priest's genuine relationship, intentions, actions, and the principles/pattern of the chosen Pillar.

It remains a **-3 to +3** Priest-specific state.

Universal Resonance and Pillar Accord answer different questions:

- **Resonance:** *How internally coherent am I with myself?*
- **Pillar Accord:** *How coherent is my current life and will with my chosen Pillar?*

They may move independently.

A Priest may become internally coherent while sincerely rejecting the Pillar they once served. Conversely, a Priest may remain strongly aligned with the Pillar while personally fragmented or self-deceived.

### Mechanical migration

This rename is not intended as a balance change.

Current Priest mechanics that previously depended on Priest Resonance now depend on **Pillar Accord**, including:

- Prepared Prayer capacity;
- the small Pillar-specific final-effect bonus;
- Miracle Petition eligibility;
- Miracle Petition probability;
- Miracle Debt advancement/repayment;
- gains and losses caused by sustained alignment or conflict with the chosen Pillar.

Universal Resonance is **not automatically added** to those Priest mechanics.

## Scholar: technical access without Pillar approval

A Scholar does not receive a technique because a Pillar likes, recognizes, selects, or consciously grants power to the Scholar.

Pillars are stable emergent patterns within the Veil. Their Aspects provide real metaphysical structures that can be studied.

A Scholar learns technical methods that reproduce, enter, route, or shape Volition according to those Aspect patterns.

The technique therefore retains its real Pillar/Aspect identity even though the Scholar does not require:

- worship;
- personal approval;
- recognition by the Pillar;
- a Priest-style relationship;
- an Anointed-style permanent bond;
- a Gifted emotional access link.

A Pillar remains metaphysically relevant because the Aspect pattern exists and is reinforced within the Veil, not because the Scholar must obtain permission from it.

## Gifted: natural Veil phenomenon

A Gifted is a **natural phenomenon of human Veil integration**, not a person selected, created, or deliberately shaped by a Pillar.

The Gifted's eight emotion-to-Aspect links are innate.

Human emotion/desire and Pillars arise within the same wider Veil metaphysics. Pillars are collectively shaped by recurring human desire and practice; a Gifted individual naturally possesses unusually strong pathways in which particular genuine emotional states match particular Aspect patterns.

Therefore:

- an emotion can be genuinely linked to a Pillar Aspect;
- that link can exist from birth;
- the Pillar does not need to choose or approve the Gifted;
- emotion remains the actual access mechanism;
- changing regional Pillar influence does not retroactively mean the Pillar authored the Gifted's personality or emotions.

Current Gifted rules that reference Resonance use the universal internal-coherence state defined above.

## Anointed: Mission is not Resonance

An Anointed's **Mission state** and universal **Resonance** are separate.

Mission state describes the Anointed's relationship with the enduring Mission imprinted by the birth surge.

Resonance describes the Anointed's internal coherence as a person.

An Anointed may therefore coherently reject their Mission and have high Resonance while becoming Forsaken under the Mission rules.

Likewise, an Anointed may remain outwardly Aligned with the Mission while suffering severe internal contradiction and low Resonance.

Mission, Destiny, Aspect Authority, and bond Dormancy are not replaced by Resonance.

## Magical interaction vocabulary

To avoid collision with the existing Pillar Aspect names **External / Internal / Transform**, magical resistance uses a separate vocabulary.

### Intrusion

**Intrusion** is magic that crosses into, originates within, overlaps, or directly alters the protected internal self of a living target.

Examples include:

- mind control;
- forced sleep;
- directly altered perception;
- direct internal bodily alteration;
- creating water inside a target's lungs;
- manifesting matter inside or overlapping living tissue.

Intrusion receives the strongest form of magical resistance.

The exact formula is not yet locked.

### Imposition

**Imposition** is magic that directly acts upon a target from outside without first becoming an independent manifested phenomenon.

Examples include:

- directly telekinetically restraining a creature;
- magically pushing or pulling a creature;
- directly igniting worn clothing;
- another external magical force applied directly to the target.

At equal capability, the intended baseline is approximately an even contest.

The exact formula is not yet locked.

### Manifestation

**Manifestation** creates an independently existing external phenomenon that then interacts with the world.

Examples include:

- a manifested fireball;
- a conjured stone;
- a physical wall of ice;
- a manifested chain that subsequently restrains physically.

Once validly manifested outside a protected living target, the phenomenon itself is not resisted as magic merely because magic created it.

The target instead uses whatever ordinary defenses apply: Dodge, Guard where valid, cover, armor, movement, physical resistance, or the phenomenon's authored consequences.

A supposed Manifestation that originates **inside or overlapping a living target** is an **Intrusion** and must overcome Intrusion resistance. Calling an effect a Manifestation cannot bypass the protected internal boundary.

## Locked separation of concepts

The following distinctions are canonical:

- **Desire** gives direction.
- **Will** selects and sustains direction.
- **Volition** is the usable metaphysical force produced through that directed relationship with the Veil.
- **VP** is freely available Volition, not total integration.
- **Resonance** is universal internal coherence.
- **Pillar Accord** is Priest-specific coherence with the chosen Pillar.
- **Level** is depth of Veil integration.
- **Focus** is the direction in which that integration is predominantly expressed.
- **Veil Affinity** is capacity.
- **Veil Control** is execution.
- **Mission state** is Anointed-specific and separate from Resonance.
- **Emotion-to-Aspect links** are Gifted access pathways, not evidence of Pillar selection.
- **Scholar techniques** access real Aspect patterns without requiring Pillar approval.
