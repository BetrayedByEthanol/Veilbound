# Phantom Sound v0 — Secrets / Hallucination

> **B05 Tier-1 representative technique; authored executable v0, numerical balance provisional.** Phantom Sound creates **one brief localized apparent audible event** as an external phenomenon. It can signal, communicate a simple agreed cue, or give someone a reason to look more closely; **it does not force attention, belief, distraction, movement, a lost action or a Stealth bonus**. It is **separate from Phantom Image**, which remains unauthored. Publishing the technique does not automatically grant it to any Archetype, Pillar user or Gifted emotion link.

See [canonical Aspect identity](../../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md#secrets), [Technique Structure](technique-structure-v0.md), [Tier and cadence](technique-tiers-and-expression.md), [Manifestation](manifestation-resolution.md), [non-combat Awareness/Intellect and Stealth](../04-non-combat/non-combat-play.md), [core checks](../01-core-resolution/core-resolution.md), [Attack Sequences](../05-combat/initiative-and-round-structure.md#attack-sequence), and [integrated ammunition](../05-combat/prepared-and-spontaneous-ammunition-v0.md#integrated-on-shot-activation-0-additional-ap).

## Shared sound limits and observation

**Allowed:** One short tap or sequence of a few knocks, metallic clink, twig snap, soft animal call, brief footstep/noise, dropped-coin-like sound, short whistle, or similarly compact simple **nonverbal** event. A **brief agreed coded cue** is possible if recipients already know its meaning; this does not teach a new language, transmit hidden data or verify sender identity. The caster declares the **specific sound pattern**, source and supported profile at activation or at shot declaration, **before** observing any listener's response. The event is localized in **one square** (or the projectile's actual impact/contact location) and does **not** wander, track listeners or move from place to place. The manifested sound has the character of a normal nearby audible noise; it does not create physical objects, reflect sonic force, deal structural damage, vibrate mechanisms, grant concealment, reproduce an orchestra or flood an area with masking noise.

**Not allowed in Tier-1 v0:** Intelligible words/conversation, an individually recognizable **perfect voiceprint**, a convincing complex soundscape, sustained footsteps, continuous music, repeat-on-command playback, a terrifying supernatural compulsion, a gunshot/explosion with unnatural volume, deafening/damaging shockwaves, a sonic strike, or the ability to project sound in a vacuum. Repeated casts are individually paid ordinary actions; one event is **not** an indefinitely reusable signal. The sound's character is not automatically indistinguishable from a real object making noise: **normal investigation and world context can reveal inconsistencies**. Those limitations describe this technique's expressive budget, not universal limits on Hallucination.

**External phenomenon:** The sound is present at its **authored external location** for anyone physically capable of hearing it. It is not imposed into a particular creature's internal senses; do **not** roll Direct Veil Pressure versus every listener. Conversely, a deafened creature cannot be made to hear it through their protected mind. **Ordinary acoustic propagation applies**: distance, ambient noise, walls, doors, sound insulation, medium and other conditions can prevent or change audibility. In vacuum there is no normal propagating sound for a listener to hear; a legal paid manifestation does not override that. A magical sound does not become inaudible merely because someone recognizes it as illusory.

**Behavior is not compelled:** A guard may notice a knock, turn, investigate, call to colleagues or ignore it as their orders and fictional circumstances suggest. **Noticing** uses **Awareness** where uncertainty needs a check (ordinary passive **10 + Awareness + applicable narrow Training + situation**, or pressured active **d20 + Awareness + applicable narrow Training + situation**). **Interpreting** the sound's cause or recognizing it as magical uses **Intellect**, with relevant narrow Training if genuinely applicable, when uncertain. A clearly audible sound may simply be heard with **no roll**; a trained observer may recognize an inconsistency without one. Do not automatically roll against a fixed spell DC, impose an attack defense or assert an observer believes the sound to be real. The fiction may create an ordinary opportunity for a separately resolved **Agility** stealth action; there is **no automatic distraction condition, guaranteed facing change, bonus/re-roll, lost Guard, reduced Awareness, skipped Reaction or forced movement**. These results are independent of caster Veil Control.

## A. `Localized Sound` — independently cast Primary

**Site and metric:** The caster nominates **one fixed external source square at an existing location in the visible environment**, not a moving creature or object as a tracking anchor. It can be ordinary open air in an unoccupied square or exposed air just outside a physical surface/creature; it **must not originate in or overlap a living creature's bodily volume** (which would call for an independently authored Intrusion). The caster must **directly see the intended source location** and have **one straight unobstructed magical line through open air** to the selected point at legal declaration **and actual magical application**. Solid walls, closed doors and **solid transparent glass** block the magic; a real open doorway/window/gap may pass a direct line. The sound may later travel physically around/across ordinary obstructions by normal acoustics; the spell's **targeting line itself does not bend** around a corner. The sound source stays at the fixed chosen world location even if the caster or a nearby creature moves after application.

For this profile alone, use a **1.5 m per-axis 3D grid**: for caster and source coordinate offsets `dx, dy, dz`, sort absolute values into `a ≥ b ≥ c` and compute:

```text
Direct sound-placement range = (a - b) + 1.5 × (b - c) + 2 × c
                             = a + 0.5 × b + 0.5 × c
```

**≤12 square-equivalents** qualifies numerically; **do not round** half-squares, use Euclidean distance, or substitute walked/curved path distance. A 12-square straight offset qualifies; `(8,8,0)` costs **12** and qualifies; `(9,9,0)` costs **13.5** and does not. Pure vertical `(0,0,12)` costs 12; `(8,0,8)` costs 12. Measure from the caster's occupied-square centre (for a multi-square caster, fixed predesignated map-reference square, deterministic lowest-x/y/z fallback) to the **centre of the selected sound-source square**. This is a **Phantom Sound-specific metric**, using the same math as [Minor Telekinesis's authored 3D grid calculation](minor-telekinesis-v0.md#three-dimensional-distance-metric--targeting-and-movement); neither this technique nor Minor Telekinesis creates a general direct-range convention for other magic.

**Declaration/payment/resolution:**
1. Declare a known/accessible Phantom Sound `Localized Sound` **Primary**, Tier 1, the **fixed source square**, the **one nonverbal sound pattern**, and one Focus-priced **Projection Action**. Verify range, direct sight, open-air magical line, ordinary action legality and **Available VP ≥1**. If illegal, the cast cannot be declared; no Turn AP or VP is spent.
2. Commit the Projection Action's **20/24/30 Turn AP** and resolve all legal response windows/nested responses. Recheck actual caster position, live sight, fixed source location/path and **Available VP ≥1** before magical application. If invalidated or underfunded **after** action commitment, the AP is lost but **0 technique VP** is Spent and **no sound** occurs; no new source square may be chosen for free.
3. If the cast remains legal, spend **1 VP** on successful magical application and **produce exactly one event**, with **no attack roll, no target creature's Direct Veil resistance contest and no extra Physical Action**. Listeners respond through ordinary observation/decision rules. A later failure to attract attention, vacuum, distance, noise or soundproofing does **not** refund VP. There is no Repeat/Rider output after the event ends.

**Components:** An outward magical action must be possible, but this technique has **no extra mandatory held implement or weapon**, and does not conjure a physical sound-producing instrument or unlock forbidden equipment actions. No special somatic hand requirement is authored for this v0.

## B. `Ammunition Shot` — Primary integrated on one physical arrow/bolt

This is an **explicit authored integrated-shot permission**, not a universal license to combine any spell with every attack, nor a generic Rider on every hit.

- **Caster and carrier:** The caster must be the **actual shooter** making one otherwise legal on-turn ordinary bow or crossbow attack with **one physical arrow/bolt**. Do **not** summon magical ammunition, enchant someone else's already airborne arrow, apply this profile to a thrown stone, use it for a melee strike, or choose a different projectile after declaration. **Ammunition Shot** matches the exact shared identifier but does **not** independently grant High Scholar Composite Casting.
- **Timing/AP:** Before the entire declared Attack Sequence's **first attack roll**, identify the particular attack, arrow/bolt, `Ammunition Shot` **Primary**, its nonverbal **sound pattern**, and **1 VP for each chosen Phantom Sound shot**. Require enough total Available VP to cover all declared spontaneous enchantment costs (including other shots). Pay the **ordinary weapon Physical Action/Full Action**, with all ordinary attack sequence, defense assignment, cover, Aim, ranged Opportunity, reload and range rules. **0 extra Projection AP** for the attached sound, but **no extra attack, Reaction or reload**.
- **At launch:** On actual legal launch, **spend 1 VP** immediately **before** the one normal projectile attack roll; subsequent miss, Guard, cover, obstruction or interception does **not** refund it. If attack/launch is cancelled or made illegal **before launch**, committed weapon AP remain spent, **0 Phantom Sound VP spent**, and this attack's sound does not occur. Base VP 1 means the Level-3 **Frugal Technique** discount (**requires a normal Spent component ≥2**) **does not apply**; no zero-VP shot.
- **Trigger:** The enchanted arrow/bolt emits **exactly one** declared brief nonverbal noise **at its first actual resolved physical impact/contact in that shot**, lasting at most 2 seconds. This includes a normal direct hit (contact at the struck creature/object's exposed position), a projectile Guard's physical shield/instrument impact, a collision with Cover or an actual Living Screen, or a miss whose physical landing/collision is **already established by the ordinary shot/environment rules**. Resolve cover/Living Screen/Guard destination **before** the sound; **do not change the collision to suit the sound**. It works even if a contacted creature suffers **0 HP through Armor**, and does not require a damaging hit. It is a localized impact cue, not a new attack or audible explosion with damage.
- **Ordinary miss without established contact:** If an arrow misses and its actual physical contact **has not been determined**, Phantom Sound does **not** invent a wall, a hit on a bystander, a landing location, or a new projectile scatter roll merely to guarantee a favorable noise. The effect ends with that shot if no actual impact location becomes established within its normal resolution; **no sound event, no VP refund**. If ordinary rules already determine an **actual Miss Impact Point**, use that point **only if a physical impact occurs there**; no extra collision roll. The magic does not make a missed arrow home in on the caster's chosen point.
- **At exposed contact point:** The source is at the **actual exterior contact point/square**, never formed inside the living target or target's mind. The sound radiates under normal acoustic conditions; no area damage, propagation through vacuum, automatic recipient, global hearing range or fixed Perception penalty is granted.
- **Expiry:** The effect is consumed by its first actual resolved impact, or expires at the end of the one projectile shot's resolution if no contact occurs. It has **no persistent tracking, attached ongoing enchantment, later secondary trigger, stored ammunition or repeat-on-hit behavior**. Another later hit with a retrieved arrow cannot replay the same paid sound.
- **Stacking/preparation:** Under the existing **one major ammunition enchantment per physical projectile** default, this independently integrated spell **occupies the projectile's single available magic-enchantment slot**, even though its output is modest. Do **not** stack it on a Conjure Flame or other already enchanted projectile, combine two sound-carrying enchantments on one shot, or assume automatic Composites. **No Prepared Ammunition mode** is authored for Phantom Sound; normal pre-shot integrated casting only.

### Rider-contribution boundary

Phantom Sound's `Ammunition Shot` **Rider contribution** is the **same one short nonverbal impact sound, no physical/damage packet and no extra repeat cadence**. It is **metadata for a later independently authorized compatible host**, such as an expressly authored magical item or feature; **it cannot be selected alone, cast at zero AP/VP for every attack, stacked onto an already enchanted arrow, or applied to a melee weapon merely because the technique is known**. No **Secondary** has been authored, so **High Scholar Composite** has no current Phantom Sound Secondary to pair with another `Ammunition Shot` Primary. This paragraph does **not** authorize an independent item, extra magic slot, reserved weapon, new channel, or another shooting mode. The **Primary `Ammunition Shot`** above is the *executable* arrow-riding version for v0.

## Status

**Proposed Tier-1 v0 MVP technique** with **two independently defined Primary profiles**: short external `Localized Sound` **1 VP + ordinary Projection Action** within **12 direct 3D squares**; and **1 VP integrated `Ammunition Shot`**, paid on launch, resolved at the **actual physical projectile impact** with no guarantee on a miss. The **potential Rider contribution is not currently an independent cast/composite/weapon authorization**. **No Intrusion, automatic deception, visual illusion, auditory damage, forced distraction, stealth bonus, Remote Scrying, area blanket sound or spell-specific opposed detection engine**. Range, volume, event budget and cost are provisional for levels 1–3; other Secrets/Arts content and complete Archetype repertoires remain B05 MVP work.
