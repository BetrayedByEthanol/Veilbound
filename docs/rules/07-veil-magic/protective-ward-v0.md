# Protective Ward v0 — Kinship / Guardian

> **Executable Tier-1 B05 technique; approved design direction, provisional level 1–3 playtest range, price and defensive effectiveness.** Protective Ward manifests a **brief magical shield beside one ally** to intercept **one specifically declared incoming physically interceptable attack**. It grants a **separately paid Reaction defense** at range, not free Guard/Dodge capacity, an attack redirect to the caster, an additional target defense assignment, or blanket prevention of harm. The canonical **Kinship / Guardian** Aspect is **External**; it protects others rather than assuming their wounds (the latter belongs to **Kinship / Sacrifice**). Technique publication does **not** grant automatic access to every Priest, Scholar, Gifted or Anointed.

See [Aspect boundaries](../../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md#kinship), [Technique Structure](technique-structure-v0.md), [Technique Tiers](technique-tiers-and-expression.md), [Initiative response windows](../05-combat/initiative-and-round-structure.md#response-windows-and-nested-responses), [Protective Intervene](../05-combat/protective-intervene-v0.md), [Guard Defense](../05-combat/weapon-attack-and-guard-math.md#guard-defense), [Shield equipment](../05-combat/off-hand-and-ranged-combat.md#defensive-off-hands-and-shields), [Critical confirmation](../05-combat/defensive-responses-and-criticals.md#natural-20-and-confirmation), [Manifestation](manifestation-resolution.md), and [VP rules](volition-points.md).

## Target acquisition, range, and eligible threats

### Protective Ward 3D range metric — local to this technique

Use the 1.5 m grid along three orthogonal coordinate axes **x, y, z**. For the caster-to-ally displacement, count absolute offsets in squares `dx = |x_ally − x_caster|`, `dy = |y_ally − y_caster|`, and `dz = |z_ally − z_caster|`. Sort them as `a ≥ b ≥ c` and calculate:

```text
Protective Ward direct 3D range (square-equivalents)
  = (a - b) × 1 + (b - c) × 1.5 + c × 2
  = a + 0.5 × b + 0.5 × c
```

This **explicitly adopts the same grid arithmetic** as [Minor Telekinesis's local 3D metric](minor-telekinesis-v0.md#three-dimensional-distance-metric--targeting-and-movement), **but defines it independently for Protective Ward**; neither spell establishes a universal distance rule for other techniques or creature flight. One-axis separation counts **1 per square**, two-axis diagonal **1.5**, three-axis diagonal **2**. **Retain half-square values**; do not round down, replace this with Euclidean distance, count only the longest axis or use walked/curved path MP. The resulting number must be **≤6** (nominally 9 m at six orthogonal square-equivalents) and **the unobstructed straight targeting line is a separate requirement**. Measure direct caster-to-ally displacement once; walls may block a numerically in-range ally. Recalculate range at valid magical application after intervening movement and **once more at the final pre-roll Ward eligibility check after every remaining spontaneous/nested response (including Blink and Blink-follow)**. An already paid Ward may then fail without refund; this grants no second activation or new range allowance.

For ordinary **one-square creatures**, use the centre of their occupied grid square at their current elevation. For **either creature** (caster or ally) occupying **multiple squares**, use that creature's **single predesignated map-reference square**, selected when it is initially positioned (and changed only with its actual position), **not whichever part is nearest the other creature**. To make an existing multi-square creature executable without an already recorded reference square, choose its **lowest-x occupied square; among ties lowest-y; among ties lowest-z**, before any Ward declaration. The same reference-square rule governs this Ward's range checks at declaration, application and final pre-roll verification, using the **creatures' then-current positions after legal relocation**; **do not retarget a closer part of either body after seeing a range outcome**. This reference square is purely Protective Ward bookkeeping and does not redefine occupied area, ordinary Attack reach or targeting for other rules.

|---|---:|---|
| `(6, 0, 0)` | 6 | **Yes** |

| `(4, 4, 0)` | 6 | **Yes** |

| `(4, 2, 2)` | 6 | **Yes** |

**Ally and range:** Choose one **other** friendly creature as the original, declared target of the triggering attack. The caster cannot use this profile on themself or on an arbitrary structure. The ally may be incapacitated or unaware of the incoming attack; the **caster** must perceive both the ally and the threat before the attack roll. **No ally consent/Direct Veil Resistance contest is needed**: the effect creates an external barrier in the attack path, rather than intruding on the ally's body or mind. Ally status does not grant sight, clairvoyance or immunity to other targeting restrictions.

**Targeting line:** At declaration, at actual magical application and **at the final pre-roll Ward check after all spontaneous and nested responses**, the caster must have **direct perception of the actual ally**, **direct 3D distance ≤6 square-equivalents under the Protective Ward metric above**, measured between the **centre of the caster's one-square or fixed multi-square reference square** and the **centre of the ally's one-square or fixed multi-square reference square**, and a **single straight unobstructed line through open air** from their position to some exposed part of the ally. Walls, closed doors and **solid transparent glass** block the line even where sight would otherwise be possible. Open gaps, windows and grilles are legal only when an actual unobstructed line passes through. **No curved line around corners**, distant ally sensed solely via a magical bond, or automatic target lock from a warning about the ally. This is a technique-specific targeting requirement, not a new universal line-of-effect rule.

**Threat awareness:** The caster must perceive an identifiable incoming attack before its roll, whether by observing the attacker, perceiving the weapon/projectile or receiving a timely independently credible warning. A hidden attack not perceived until after resolution cannot trigger a retroactive Ward. Knowing an ally is *generally* in danger is insufficient. The caster need not physically stand in, reach or move into the incoming attack's lane: the ward is manifested at the ally's **occupied space at the approach of that attack**, with no free movement and no requirement that the caster share the attack's physical path. **After application, the Ward follows its one named ally through legal same-window movement or Blink while the original attack remains suspended; it is not left protecting the ally's old square.** This describes where the magical shield intercepts; it does **not** let the caster select an adjacent square or area impact origin.

**Eligible attacks:** One **individually declared physical strike, projectile, pointed beam or independently manifested physical attack** whose authored delivery can plausibly contact a shield-like magical barrier at the ally. Mundane and magical origin alone does not decide eligibility; the attack's physical/delivery nature does. **Melee, arrows/bolts and physical Manifestation projectiles** may qualify when their particular direct hit would be interceptable. A pointed beam can qualify only if the underlying attack actually uses the physical attack/beam-defense architecture and can meet an intervening protective surface. A magical ward cannot automatically intercept **Direct Veil Intrusion/Imposition on a person, an already-resolved hit, ambient damage, an attack lacking a physically interceptable impact, environmental hazards, or a free-form area without a separately incoming interceptable carrier**. A carried area attack may have an interceptable projectile, but intercepting that projectile **does not erase the area**.

**One attack:** Select **one** particular attack within a declared multiattack Attack Sequence. No effect on the remaining attacks, no delayed pre-declaration of a different attack, no blanket protection until next turn. The ward is **not a physical cover object or Living Screen** and does not create Cover Difficulty, line-of-fire blocking, collision faces, shield HP or a new target for ordinary attacks before its conditional block is evaluated.

## Magical Shield Guard Defense — locked v0 expression

Use the caster's **Shield Guard Ability pair Strength + Agility** and a **standard shield's +2 Guard Defense bonus**, without requiring the caster to physically hold, wear or operate a shield:

```text
Protective Ward Defense = 10 + caster Strength + caster Agility
                            + eligible shield-style effective Weapon Training
                            + 2 (virtual standard-shield Guard bonus)
                            + other expressly applicable general Guard modifiers
```

**Training:** Follow the existing **shield** Training selection, **not** the ally's own Training, the caster's best arbitrary weapon Training or a new Shield Training skill. If the caster **currently wields a legal one-handed melee weapon** that could contribute its effective Weapon Training to shield Guard, count that effective Training **once**; otherwise **+0**. This adapts the shield's ordinary 'one-handed melee weapon in the other hand' rule to the virtual shield, which has **no physical hand**. The spell does not conjure a trained weapon, make an otherwise illegal weapon legal, or enable unrelated two-handed weapon/bow Training as shield Training.

**Bonuses and constraints:** The **+2 virtual shield bonus replaces**, rather than stacks with, a buckler (+1), equipped shield (+2), large shield (+3), physical defensive off-hand bonus, two-handed leverage, or other equipment-specific shield bonus. Other **general Guard Defense modifiers** applying to the caster's **shield-style Guard** can apply to this virtual shield **only if their authored conditions are genuinely satisfied without a physical shield and their own rule does not exclude magical wards**. A modifier requiring a held shield, specific equipment, shield arm, bodily interception, weapon leverage, personal impact position or extra Guard capacity does **not** qualify merely because the virtual barrier resembles a shield. A bonus explicitly authored to affect **magical Ward Defense** also applies according to its own rule; do not import unrelated equipment reach, ordinary shield positioning, Guard capacity or riposte modifiers. **No Veil Control, Intellect, Resonance, Tier or caster Level term** is silently added to this formula. The ally's own Armor, Guard/Dodge, Cover and existing defenses remain independent and are not copied into Ward Defense.

The **caster** supplies Ward Defense, even when the ally has much higher Guard/Dodge. Compute it **at valid ward application** and retain it for this one attack including any critical confirmation; no recalculation from the ally's state. Against a non-natural-20 otherwise-successful attack compare **the original attack total** to Ward Defense: **attack total ≤ Ward Defense → intercepted**; **attack total > Ward Defense → the original ally is hit**. No second attack roll, no magical contest against the attacker and no extra VP payment or Guard use at this check.

## Exact Reaction, VP, and attack order

1. **Original declaration:** The attacker commits the particular attack and original target and appropriate profile; the ally assigns the usual **Take Hit, Dodge or Guard** according to [Attack Sequence](../05-combat/initiative-and-round-structure.md#attack-sequence). An ally's Guard resource and Dodge Pressure remain governed by that assignment; the Guardian cannot change that defense after seeing an attack roll.
2. **Earlier responses:** Resolve all matching **precommitted Interceptions and nested responses first**, then recheck that the original attack remains legal/relevant. If it has been cancelled, made impossible, or no longer targets the ally before the spontaneous-response step, **no Protective Ward Reaction/VP may be declared for that original attack**. An independently triggered new attack gets its own proper response window.
3. **Declare Ward:** At the **spontaneous pre-roll Reaction window**, choose one eligible original incoming attack and ally. Verify range, direct sight/line, threat awareness, Reaction availability, access and physically interceptable delivery. Calculate **base 2 Spent VP**, then apply any **independently eligible cost reduction**, e.g. [TAL-M03 Frugal Technique](../10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md#tal-m03--frugal-technique) **only on the Guardian's own turn**, if unused; **lock the effective cost** (minimum 1) and require **Available VP ≥ locked effective cost**. A failed legality/affordability check **cannot legally declare the Reaction** and consumes **no Reaction, VP or Talent use**. A **legal discounted declaration immediately consumes TAL-M03's once-per-own-turn use** before responses, even if the attempt ultimately fails.
4. **Commit Reaction:** Spend **one Reaction**, with **no Turn AP or future AP Debt**. This competes with the same Reaction used for Reactive Protective Intervene, Reactive Stone Skin, Reactive Blink, or activating Opportunity Response. There is **no Interception reservation mode for this technique**, no free movement, and no separate Guard use. Resolve contemporaneous valid spontaneous responses in the canonical initiative order with all nested response windows; the ward does not leapfrog a precommitted Interception.
5. **Magical application:** After intervening responses, recheck the chosen ally, original unresolved attack, its interceptability, caster consciousness, range, direct sight/path and **Available VP ≥ locked effective cost**. If the attempt became invalid or underfunded **before valid magical application**, it fails with **Reaction spent, no ward and 0 technique VP spent**; any TAL-M03 use selected at legal declaration **remains consumed**. If valid, spend **exactly the locked effective ordinary Spent VP amount** (**normally 2**, **1** for eligible Frugal Technique); the spell succeeds in placing its one conditional barrier automatically, with **no additional activation roll or willing-ally Intrusion contest**. Once magically applied, VP are **not refunded** because a later response cancels/misses the attack, moves the ally outside Ward range or blocks the caster-to-ally magical line.
6. **Final pending-attack/Ward check after all responses:** **After all same-trigger spontaneous responses, their nested response windows, and any [Blink-follow](blink-v0.md#blink-follow-reaction-pursuit-of-a-suspended-attack--locked) finish**, perform the canonical final legality check on the **same original attack, original ally and committed attack/defense profiles**. If the original attack is cancelled or no longer can lawfully reach that ally, **no Ward comparison occurs** and any applied Ward ends; the already Spent VP and Reaction remain spent. If the same attack remains legal, the applied Ward **follows the ally's current position for this attack only**, including a successful Reactive Blink; it is **not anchored to the world square at initial declaration/application**. Re-evaluate the **caster-to-ally 3D distance ≤6, direct perception of the ally, straight unobstructed magical targeting line, and physical interceptability of the same still-unresolved attack** from the **then-current caster/ally positions**, before rolling the original attack. **If any Ward eligibility fails**, the Ward ends without intercepting; the attack resolves normally against the original ally, and **no Reaction, VP or consumed TAL-M03 is refunded**. A later return within range during an already-resolved attack cannot revive the Ward. **A move within range and line keeps the Ward active without a new declaration, Reaction, VP, defense assignment or targeting choice.** The original attacker **cannot change attack target/profile** under this permission.
7. **Resolve the original attack:** Apply the original target's committed defense, actual attack/cover/Living Screen rules and critical procedure with **one** initial attack roll. Ordinary original miss, cover impact, incidental Living Screen impact, natural 1 or now-illegal attack → the ward makes **no extra Guard comparison** and protects nobody else; **the Reaction and VP spent on a valid magical application remain spent**. If an ordinary (non-natural-20) attack would directly hit the ally, compare that **same attack total once** to Ward Defense. A ward block means the attack physically contacts the ward **in the original ally's occupied space**, using the fixed impact anchor defined below; an unsuccessful ward check leaves the original hit on the ally with their normal Armor/resistances/wounds and relevant on-hit effects. **Do not roll or select the ward as an additional creature target.**
8. **Natural 20 — fails to stop:** A legal initial **natural 20** is an otherwise-successful attack regardless of Ward Defense; the ward **cannot stop it**, even if its static Defense would exceed the original total. Resolve **one** existing natural-20 confirmation against **Ward Defense instead of the ally's originally assigned Dodge/Guard/Take Hit confirmation**: failed confirmation → **Strong Hit** on ally; successful → **Critical Hit** on ally; confirmation natural 20 → **Devastating Critical** on ally. No confirmation against the ally *and* the ward, no second initial roll, no second hit, and no cancellation of the original natural-20 attack. Preserve the original attack's actual damage and other on-hit consequences, subject to the resulting severity and ordinary mitigation.
9. **End:** The ward vanishes after that attack's resolution or cancellation. It does not persist to another attack in the sequence, create a Shield Guard use or Riposte Opening, alter the caster's Guard capacity or Dodge Pressure, transfer the hit to the caster, or provide cover after the triggering attack.

**Protective Intervene priority/nonstacking:** A given attack may have **at most one protective interception**. An already successfully established **Prepared Protective Intervene** has priority. Otherwise use canonical **initiative-ordered spontaneous responses** to determine the first **validly committed** Protective Intervene or Protective Ward; once one is selected for that attack, the other cannot be added as another layer or declared after viewing the roll. A caster cannot use both their own Reaction techniques on the same trigger. A protector with no legal ward/Intervene cannot occupy the slot merely by proposing an illegal declaration. Normal personal Guard/Dodge and physical cover remain unaffected, and the original target receives **no extra defense reassignment**.

**Projectile area and beams — deterministic impact anchor after responses:** When Protective Ward successfully blocks an otherwise-hitting projectile, **the impact point is in the original protected ally's *current* occupied space after all spontaneous and nested responses**, exactly as the default for [successful projectile Guard](manifestation-resolution.md#successful-guard-against-an-area-projectile). The ward **follows the creature, never a previously occupied world square**. For a standard one-square ally, the impact is **that final ally-occupied square at its current elevation**, never a chosen adjacent/front/back square, old Blink departure square or arbitrary barrier edge.

**Multi-square deterministic cell assignment:** Resolve the original attack's precommitted specific target occupied square (if any) **before the Ward can be declared**, otherwise assign the ally's predesignated map-reference square (or lowest-x/y/z fallback) **before spontaneous Ward declaration**. Convert the chosen occupied square into a **stable footprint index**: sort the ally's occupied grid squares by x, then y, then z (ascending) at that initial assignment, and record the selected square's **1-based index**. This index identifies the Ward's eventual impact cell **relative to the target's footprint**, not an absolute old world-coordinate and not new attack-target selection. If the ally legally changes position/pose through movement or Blink, take the **same indexed square in the ally's final occupied footprint**, sorted by the same rule, for the Ward's impact **only if the original attack is independently still legal**. **No post-declaration reindexing, changing to a closer square, new attack target, choice after seeing the attack/ward roll, or discretionary relocation of area origin.** If an intervening effect changes the footprint cell count so the original index no longer exists, use the final footprint's fixed map-reference square (or lowest-x/y/z fallback); this is an authored deterministic fallback, **not** permission to change the committed attack profile or ignore its separate target legality.

**Order and cancellation:** Determine the final occupied impact cell only **after all spontaneous Reactions, nested effects and attacker Blink-follow have finished**, immediately before the original attack roll and **only if** the original attack and this Ward remain legal under Step 6. If the original attack became impossible because the ally Blinked out of reach and no follow preserves it, **no Ward impact** occurs and the applied Reaction/Spent VP are not refunded. If the attack survives Blink-follow and the Ward's **final range/sight/path check** passes, resolve any successful interception at the mapped **final ally-occupied square**. The chosen approach side is narrative only and cannot reposition the blast. The original attack's physical cover, Living Screen, legality and post-movement targeting rules remain fully in force. **No new scatter roll or radius shift occurs on a successful Ward block.**

Its carried explosion/cloud/area resolves from **this fixed occupied-space point**, and may still affect the ally (or Guardian if inside the area) under the independently authored area rules; Protective Ward provides **no immunity to the carried area**. A successfully blocked pointed beam contacts the ward for that direct hit, but **does not create a generic beam reflection, wall, area shield, or permanent obstruction**. No unapproved projectile damage to, destruction of or extra HP for the virtual shield is inferred. An ordinary non-hit that struck physical cover or a Living Screen stays with that original impact; the magical ward neither moves nor steals it.

| Protecting an ally does not add Guard capacity | No shield +1 capacity, no free Dodge, Riposte, additional Cover or incidental Living Screen |
| Gifted sample **Trust → Kinship / Guardian** | Grants an eligible **Aspect link**, **not** automatic technique knowledge or a free slot; independent eligible access still necessary |
| Sacrifice-style damage transference to caster | **Not** a Guardian Protective Ward permission; reserve for future Kinship / Sacrifice authoring |

## Design status and deferred boundaries

**Locked as a provisional v0 playtest profile:** Tier 1 Kinship / Guardian **`Other Ally — Single Incoming Attack` Primary**; one threatened **other ally** within **6 direct 3D square-equivalents (nominally 9 m)**, measured by this technique's explicit sorted-axis **a + 0.5b + 0.5c** metric; live sight of ally, threat awareness, straight unobstructed magical line, **one spontaneous pre-roll Reaction**, **base 2 Spent VP at valid application**, **0 Guard capacity/Turn AP/AP debt**, one virtual **standard-shield** comparison using **caster Strength + Agility + legal effective shield-associated Training + 2 + individually eligible general Guard modifiers**, conditional on the original attack otherwise hitting. **Natural-20 attack always bypasses ward stopping**, with **one confirmation against Ward Defense** setting the original ally's Strong/Critical/Devastating severity. **A successfully applied Ward follows the named ally through later legal same-window movement/Blink while the same attack stays suspended, but its range/sight/line and attack legality are rechecked after all spontaneous/nested responses; failure ends it without VP/Reaction refund. Any area-bearing projectile blocked after Blink impacts the ally's final occupied square (multi-square: precommitted footprint index), never its old location or an opportunistic square.** **No extra physical movement, full-Sequence blocking, independent Dodge, self ward, Direct Veil Intrusion interception, unbounded areas, damage transfer to caster, Secondary or Rider**. Range/price/effectiveness and edge-case balancing await L1–3 playtests; this technique does not itself solve Archetype initial acquisition or the full eight-Pillar catalogue.
