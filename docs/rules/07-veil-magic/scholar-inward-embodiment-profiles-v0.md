# Scholar Inward Boost and Embodiment Profiles v0 — Earth / Life

> **New, explicitly authored Scholar-only Tier-1 profile permissions; numerical balance provisional.** This record extends *particular modes* of the already-authored [Stone Skin](stone-skin-v0.md) and [Guided Regeneration](guided-regeneration-v0.md) techniques. It **does not** convert arbitrary self-targeted [Projection] spells or Continuous Reserves into Inward Boosts/Embodiments. It does not teach either technique or establish a source-legal Medium/Low Scholar character. See [Scholar Foundation](../08-archetypes/scholar.md) for repertoire, Focus limits, action and tier ceilings.

## Profile identity and source-law boundary

A Scholar must have **individually learned the named actual Pillar technique**. The Medium uses the explicitly listed **Inward** alternative mode; the Low must have learned **and prepared** the technique and uses its explicitly listed **Embodiment** mode. No other Archetype gains these Focus-only modes by knowing the ordinary technique. The ordinary spell's own Self, Reactive, maintained or projectile profiles retain their original independent permissions and costs; none gain Focus switching, temporary overroute output, automatic discounted commitment or an extra Active Boost/Embodiment.

| **MS-GUIDED-REGEN-T1** | Medium, **Inward** | Life / Restoration: Guided Regeneration | Regeneration | **24-AP Projection**, own ready turn | **1** | **+1 existing self Regeneration Step throughput**, applied only at the caster's real G0 Step 2 and paid from ordinary Vigor constraints |

**T1 only.** A level-3 Tier-2 ceiling does **not** enhance these outputs or produce invented T2 variants. No profile has a free upfront activation. Each is **Self** only and maintained while its reservation and ordinary applicable consciousness/target conditions hold. Medium and Low both have at most **one active compatible Focus configuration at L1–3**; the channels above identify the slot occupied. A different normal Stone Skin or Guided Regeneration instance cannot stack on the same recipient through a differently named Focus profile. Release returns only currently **Committed VP** to Available, never earlier Spent VP. If the casting character is unconscious or dead, follow the base technique's actual termination behavior; do not keep a Stone Skin channel that must have ended.

### Why Medium's numerical alternative differs from ordinary Projection

An **MS** Inward Stone Skin does not use ordinary Stone Skin's `ceil(Max VP/3)` reservation or R2 baseline: its **distinct lower-output R1** alternative expressly costs 1 Committed. A Medium Guided Inward Self profile reserves 1 instead of the ordinary 2, as the basis for tactical **rerouting**, and cannot be delivered to another recipient as an Inward. These are **provisional Focus-specific prices**, requiring independent later balance testing; they do not retroactively reduce normal spells for High/Low/Priest/other builds.

## Medium Scholar — source-specific rerouting and overroute

- Activate one **individually learned** compatible Inward profile normally on your own turn; this is a paid Projection action.
- **Reroute** only at a legal **own-turn decision point**, when an active Medium Inward exists, to a **different learned compatible profile** in this list. Existing Committed VP return to Available, then pay the replacement's **1 Committed VP** and rerouting's **3 Spent VP at L1–2**, or **2 Spent VP at L3**. The switch costs **0 AP** and grants neither an extra spell activation, out-of-turn interrupt nor restoration of finite output. Validate **entire replacement cost before release**. The original effect ends and the replacement begins as an atomic successful transition; if the full combined payment is unavailable, original state is untouched.
- The two authored candidates intentionally occupy **different Body and Regeneration channels** and are compatible replacement choices for this v0. A no-op replacement of the same instance does **not** refresh or bypass any cost.
- L3 **Overrouting** may increase either current active Inward's output by **one unit per paid step**, maximum **two steps**. In Body, R1→R2→R3. In Regeneration, **+1→+2→+3** throughput at the **actual** natural Step. **Sacrifice per step:** reduce the character's **available ordinary movement capacity by 2 MP**, from the base 6 MP/turn to 4 or 2; this is an explicit competing Locomotion function loss, **not** free speed. No retroactive restoration of previously spent MP or free movement. Only allow a step while actual remaining/consumed MP can reconcile to the reduced cap.
- Pay **1 additional Spent VP per step**. Rerouting straight into an overroute pays its normal **L3 2 Spent reroute fee plus 1/2 Spent** for the chosen steps; Overrouting an already active unchanged Boost pays only incremental surcharge. Dropping entirely to normal output is **free** and does not refund Spent VP. Entering Overroute again requires payment again; neither rearming nor changing mode refreshes any effect or grants a Reaction.

**L1 VA 0 affordability witness:** Max VP **4**. Activating either MS profile costs **1 Committed** and **24 AP**; remaining **3 Available** allow **one 3-Spent reroute**, which preserves 1 Committed and leaves **0 Available**, even if an open Turn AP slot remains. This is the *minimum* viable technical loop, not an assertion that repeated rerouting is sustainable.

**Native movement proof remains pending.** The provided pure profile ledger proves VP/AP reservation and calculates the proposed overroute movement cap. It is not yet hooked to a real G1 movement transaction, nor to genuine G3/G4 packet or Regeneration receipts; L3 Overrouting is an **authored candidate**, not a signed combat outcome. A future source-backed Medium Scholar actor must enforce already-spent MP legality before this mode becomes encounter-certified.

## Low Scholar — source-specific Embodiment, switching and Intensification

- Only **Prepared known** Stone Skin or Guided Regeneration in the exact **LS** profile qualifies here. Low Scholar's starting known technique cap is **3/4/4** (L1/L2/L3); Prepared Arsenal cap is **2/3/3** and is selected using normal technical preparation. Authoring a compatible profile **does not grant** a known/prepared slot, new technique, automatic equipment bonus or an empty-slot fill.
- Activating a prepared Embodiment pays an ordinary **30-AP Projection** and the exact profile's **undiscounted** Committed VP. At most **one** is active in L1–3. To change, release the original at a legal own-turn decision, recover its Committed VP, and cast the replacement through **a fresh paid Projection Action**. **No Medium-style free reroute**, no Reactive Stone Skin bypass and no inherited Priest Chosen discount.
- **L3 Deep Embodiment: distinct, provisional paid properties.** A character may pay **1 Spent VP** on a legal own-turn decision for **one** active compatible LS host; no action or Reaction is granted by paying. The price itself requires no AP, but **this is not a spontaneous combat response** and may not be attached to an unprepared or inactive spell.
  - **LS-STONE-BODY-T1 intensified**: the host's original snapshotted Stone Skin resistance increases by **+1** until the *beginning of the caster's next own turn*, then returns to ordinary resistance. No extra ward, armor or Vigor prevention, no duplicate per-round intensification, and no refresh of the old Stone Skin host.
  - **LS-GUIDED-REGEN-T1 intensified**: the **next one actual own G0 Step 2** receives **+1 extra** Regeneration throughput subject to **existing deficit/Vigor/Step** constraints. The paid pending intensification ends only on that real step, or when the host ends; no instant healing, fabricated elapsed Step or outside-turn regen, and no repeated payments while an unconsumed one remains.
- Early baseline: Low VA 0 VP **2 / 4 / 6** at L1/L2/L3. LS Stone's commitment is **1 / 2 / 2**; LS Guided's is **2** at each level. At L1 activating Guided reserves **all 2 VP**. Normal Low equipment/hand limitations remain in force for all outward ordinary techniques. No weapon enchantment is made into an Embodiment by implication.

The optional original Stone Skin reactive **ordinary Self** cast remains governed by [Stone Skin](stone-skin-v0.md); it does not become a new Reactive **Embodiment** or legal out-of-turn LS activation merely because this variant is authored. The same named effect's exclusivity still forbids stacking two Stone Skins.

## Source implementation and proof boundary

[Profile ledger](../../scripts/b05_scholar_inward_embodiment_profiles_v0.py) is a **planning-level executable legality and resource model**, not an independent G0 source Actor or the live spell engine. It covers explicit identity/channel/access checks, L1–3 structural known/prepared limits, focus-limited slots, sourced **baseline VP formula by Focus and supplied VA**, AP costs, paid reroute, mandatory new activation for Low switches, commitment refund, L3 Overroute/Intensification pricing and previewed outputs. The model never applies G4 HP damage, grants a G1 MP move, invokes a G0 Step, or certifies an enemy hit. Its Low Guided next-Step marker **must later be bound to an authenticated source-native Step 2**; a direct model call is not proof of healing.

The [regressions](../../tests/test_b05_scholar_inward_embodiment_profiles_v0.py) exercise affordances, no double reservation/active slot, L1 affordability, L3 progression, invalid access, cancellation and no-free-refund behavior. Existing High Scholar Fire and Priest verified characters are **unchanged**.

**Not in this slice:** independent source-legal Medium or Low Scholar full nine-Ability sheets, purchased equipment, chosen Talents/Training, known/Prepared list attestation against source character, G0/G3/G4/G1 native adapter, positional combat, complete PS-03 Spellblade weapon modes, or representative party balance. This is a **B05 profile authorization plus isolated contract test**, not B04/B07 build completeness, PS-01–PS-08 certification, or MVP exit-gate closure.

**Acceptance disposition:** four exact provisional Focus-specific profiles authored; executable resource/state checks prepared; no numeric balance signoff, no automatic Focus ranking, no rule-generalized Inward/Embodiment qualification, and no change to the existing B01–B07 or six strict MVP gate status.
