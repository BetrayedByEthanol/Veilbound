# Spark of Inspiration v0 — Arts / Inspiration

> **B05 eighth representative Pillar technique; executable Tier-1 playtest baseline with provisional VP price, numeric bonus, targeting range and duration.** Spark of Inspiration kindles confidence or creative clarity **within one knowingly willing creature** for **one specifically declared expressive or creative undertaking**. It offers **+2 on the first actually qualifying noncombat Ability check** made within the active window; it **does not** grant a general Performance/Persuasion skill, permanent Training, automatic social agreement, combat accuracy, new information, extra action, or compulsory belief. Publication **does not** automatically teach the technique to any Archetype, Gifted emotion–Aspect link or character.

See [canonical Arts Aspects](../../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md#arts), [Technique Structure](technique-structure-v0.md), [Technique Tiers](technique-tiers-and-expression.md), [Direct Veil Intrusion and consent](resonance-and-intrusion.md#intrusion), [noncombat resolution and social boundaries](../04-non-combat/non-combat-play.md), [Abilities and Training](../02-character-creation/abilities-and-training.md#social-perception-and-investigation-boundary), [Core Resolution](../01-core-resolution/core-resolution.md), [round structure](../05-combat/initiative-and-round-structure.md) and [VP states](volition-points.md).

## What counts as a declared expressive or creative undertaking?

**Lock one concrete undertaking at the initial casting declaration, agreed with the recipient.** It is a defined piece of expression or creativity, **not** an Ability name, an entire profession, every use of a Learned Discipline, a vague future challenge, an entire social encounter or all of the character's next checks. Examples: **deliver this ceremonial address**, **play this specific song**, **perform this dance**, **act in this particular scene**, **compose this stanza**, **sketch this symbol/design**, **craft the decorative motif on this artifact**. The caster and recipient need enough shared understanding to identify **which completed performance or creative piece qualifies**; no clairvoyance or hidden information is imparted.

**Qualifying check:** The first **actually required** check that measures how well the recipient **expresses, executes or conceives that nominated creative undertaking**, whether its applicable Ability is **Presence, Agility, Precision, Intellect** or another genuinely appropriate Ability. Apply **+2 once**, before the one relevant roll is made, using existing passive/active check procedures and one narrow relevant Learned Discipline only when already possessed/applicable. This is an **explicit magical modifier**, **not +2 Training**, not a new universal **Performance** skill and not a blanket bonus to an Ability. For a performance requiring **several independently paid or resolved checks**, **only the first eligible check receives +2**; the later checks are normal. The caster/recipient cannot skip a qualifying check to bank the bonus for a more attractive later roll or retroactively attach it after learning the outcome.

**Fiction first; no manufactured roll:** When the creative task would ordinarily resolve without a roll because it is routine, automatically feasible, or the character has sufficient knowledge, **do not roll merely to harvest +2**. On completion of the nominated undertaking without a roll, **the pending benefit ends unused**. A genuinely independent expressive check may still be rolled if existing Core Resolution and the fiction warrant it; do not split one social check into a free fabricated 'beautiful speaking' check followed by a second 'convince the duke' check. The bonus **never supplies a missing skill, artistic knowledge, instrument, material, language, audience, opportunity or plausible approach**.

### Domain examples and boundaries

| Undertaking | Eligibility of a specific check |
|---|---|
| Give a prepared ceremonial speech evaluated for rhetorical delivery | **Yes:** expressive **Presence + genuinely narrow formal Oratory** (if trained) or other appropriate check; one +2 |
| Act a defined scene in a play | **Yes:** expressive **Presence + Acting** where relevant; no automatic audience loyalty |
| Play a difficult musical passage | **Yes:** suitable **Precision + instrument-specific Learned Discipline** (if actually possessed) |
| Perform a choreographed dance | **Yes:** suitable **Agility + Acrobatics** (if relevant), for expressive execution only |
| Compose a new stanza, melody or pictorial concept | **Yes:** a genuinely uncertain **Intellect, Precision or Presence** creative check, depending on the method; no information conjured |
| Paint a mural or sculpt one expressive decorative motif | **Yes:** a genuine **artistic execution check**, not automatic item creation, superior craft grade or magical engraving |
| Bargain for a lower price, negotiate a treaty, persuade the duke to pardon someone | **No** to the ordinary **social outcome check** merely because the speaker uses elegant words; the target's interests, evidence and choice govern |
| Give a speech seeking a pardon that requires **one** Presence check solely to change the duke's decision | **No**: do not relabel a social-influence check 'artistic' or invent a separate performance check |
| Forge a convincing identity, fabricate evidence or deceive a guard about a false story | **No** to a general Deception/Disguise/social-lie check: this is not Secrets / Deception |
| Pick a lock, diagnose a patient, build a structurally sound bridge | **No** to the ordinary technical/medical check; incidental aesthetic flair does not make it expressive |
| Shoot an arrow, Dodge, Guard, cast another spell, confirm a critical | **No**: never a combat or Direct Veil accuracy/defense bonus |
| Inspire a crowd to rebel or panic | **No automatic emotional conversion**, compliance, mind control, crowd buff or audience reaction; a separate eligible artistic delivery may be improved only when it has a genuine independent expressive objective |

**Social separation:** A ceremonial address **judged for artistic rhetoric** can benefit. A normal Presence check whose actual success condition is that **another person's opinion, bargain or action changes** cannot gain the bonus merely because the actor calls it a speech. Social outcomes stay with [Non-Combat Play](../04-non-combat/non-combat-play.md#social-interaction). Do not double-count a single performance check as both guaranteed admiration and successful persuasion. A beautiful delivery may be an in-world fact that informs later roleplay, but this technique **does not attach another +2 to the audience's decision**.

**Arts boundaries:** Inspiration improves the **recipient's inner confidence/creative approach**, not outward projection to spectators (**Arts / Performance**), transforming material or fabricating objects (**Arts / Creation**), temporary learned expertise (**Strength / Training**), observing hidden truths (**Mind / Insight**), reshaping emotions without consent or dominating belief. A recipient may feel encouraged or receive an idea within their existing experience but does **not** learn inaccessible facts, automatically overcome a fear condition, gain combat morale bonuses, recover HP/Vigor, alter an Emotion Window, or acquire permanent proficiency.

## Target acquisition and local 3D range

**For this technique alone**, use orthogonal 1.5-metre grid coordinate offsets between caster and recipient: `dx=|x_recipient−x_caster|`, `dy`, `dz`. Sort into `a ≥ b ≥ c` and calculate:

```text
Spark of Inspiration direct range (square-equivalents)
    = (a − b) × 1 + (b − c) × 1.5 + c × 2
    = a + 0.5 × b + 0.5 × c
```

A result **≤3** is numerically eligible; **retain halves** and do not use Euclidean/longest-axis, walked/curved path measurement or rounded-down values. This local arithmetic matches the [explicit Minor Telekinesis 3D distance](minor-telekinesis-v0.md#three-dimensional-distance-metric--targeting-and-movement) but **does not make a universal Veil targeting rule**. Examples: `(3,0,0) = 3` eligible; `(2,2,0) = 3` eligible; `(3,1,0) = 3.5` ineligible; `(0,0,3) = 3` eligible; `(1,1,1) = 2` eligible. For ordinary one-square creatures use square centres; for multi-square caster/recipient use the **fixed map-reference occupied square recorded when positioned**, or the **lowest-x, then lowest-y, then lowest-z occupied square** if no fixed reference exists. Do not opportunistically select nearest edges.

**Non-self targeting:** The caster must directly see the actual recipient and have a **single unobstructed straight magical line through open air** to some exposed part of the recipient at declaration and valid magical application; solid walls, closed doors and **solid transparent glass** block this delivery even if the recipient is otherwise visible through glass. An actual open window/doorway can pass the line. Knowing the recipient's position via a map, bond or report does not waive sight. **Self-target** requires neither seeing oneself nor an external delivery line, and counts at range 0. **No special touch requirement**, but the recipient must be **alive, conscious and capable of knowingly accepting** this specific benefit/undertaking.

Once successfully applied, the one pending benefit **resides within the recipient**; the caster may move away, break line of sight or become incapacitated **without automatically ending** the already applied benefit. Target acquisition is **not continuously rechecked** after valid application. No independent Reserve/Committed VP channel exists. Separately authored magical suppression, dispelling or immunity still applies on its own terms.

## Activation, VP timing, and resistance

1. **Select and declare:** Choose the legal `Single Target` **Primary**, fixed Tier 1, one knowingly willing living recipient (self permitted), the **specific nominated creative/expressive undertaking**, one **Focus-priced Projection Action** and **base 2 Spent VP**. Decide any legal own-turn Spent-VP discount **before committing AP**. [TAL-M03 Frugal Technique](../10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md#tal-m03--frugal-technique), when otherwise eligible and unused on the caster's own turn, reduces **2→1 VP** (minimum 1). **Lock the effective price at declaration** and require **Available VP ≥ full locked price**, a legitimate recipient and purpose, valid action, range/sight/path and informed assent before response windows. If an attempt is illegal, **no AP/VP/Talent use** is consumed and it cannot open a free interruption window. Upon **legal declaration** using TAL-M03, its one use is **immediately consumed**, before responses, even if the attempt later fails.
2. **Commit and allow response:** Spend the normal **High 20 / Medium 24 / Low 30 Turn AP** for a Projection Action, then resolve applicable precommitted Interceptions, spontaneous Reactions and nested responses in canonical order. Do not allow an out-of-turn spontaneous cast, a zero-AP pre-buff, a reaction-time emergency bonus or a second check from this activation. A recipient may withdraw consent before magical application; this spell does not authorize a resisted cast in that case.
3. **Recheck and apply:** At actual magical application after responses, recheck caster ability to complete the action, **recipient still living/conscious/willing and agreeing to the same undertaking**, recipient sight/path/range (except self) and **Available VP ≥ locked effective amount**. If invalid or underfunded before magical application, **the already committed AP is lost, but 0 technique VP are Spent**; a legally selected TAL-M03 use remains consumed. A new target, undertaking or profile cannot be selected retroactively. If legal, move **exactly the locked 2 VP (or eligible 1 VP)** from Available → **Spent VP** and apply the **one pending +2** with **no Direct Veil Pressure roll** because the recipient knowingly accepts an actually beneficial Intrusion.
4. **Single qualifying check:** After valid application the magic is active as a **single pending conditional benefit** on that one recipient and named undertaking until an expiry/consumption event. **At the first genuinely qualifying check whose resolution occurs before expiry**, add **+2 once to its final Ability check total**, announced **before rolling**; end the benefit after that check resolves regardless of success/failure, with **no refund**. If the recipient willingly abandons the undertaking or finishes it without a check, the benefit ends with no further output. An action declared before expiry but whose relevant check resolves after expiry receives **no bonus**. A failed unrelated check, an ordinary social decision roll or a combat roll does **not** consume or receive this benefit; the pending effect still expires on schedule.
5. **Expiry and replacement:** End the instance when its one check resolves, the declared undertaking completes/ends, the 10-minute/round anchor arrives, or a later **successful** Spark of Inspiration on this same recipient replaces it. Only **one instance per recipient** may be pending at any moment; simultaneous different-occasion Sparks do not stack or bank future uses. Neither expiration nor replacement returns **Spent VP**, restores AP, gives a check reroll or makes the recipient repeat a failed task.
6. **Target resistance and protections:** This profile **requires genuine willingness**, not merely absence of objection; unwilling/incapacitated targets are **ineligible**, rather than attacked with the Direct Veil contested-Intrusion rules. A prior express refusal remains operative until deliberately withdrawn. The beneficent and knowingly accepted effect has **no Intrusion Barrier and no Direct Veil roll**; explicit independent protective wards, immunities and suppression can still block it. **If a target withdraws assent after successful application**, end the pending benefit without a refund; it is not mind control. This one-time consensual permission does not waive resistance to any other magic.

## Duration and initiative transitions

**Outside initiative:** Start a **600 in-world second (10-minute)** countdown at valid magical application. The timer runs regardless of actual task activity, rest, delays or interruptions; no pause while speaking, painting, or waiting. The bonus is used only for an eligible check **that resolves before the effect expires**. A creative project taking more than ten minutes receives no automatic bonus at its final check merely because this spell was cast near the beginning; cast close enough to an actually eligible resolution.

**Cast while initiative is active:** Let the casting round be **R**. An unconsumed Spark expires **at Round Begin R+100**, before any refresh/start-of-round action there. There are **100 subsequent round-boundary intervals**, not 100 bonus checks, and no implication that each round is globally an exact six seconds. This technique does **not** provide an emergency Reaction, substitute for another combat action, or allow its bonus on combat checks.

**Exploration → initiative:** If **s** whole in-world seconds remain, compute **`q = floor(s/6)`** and discard any remainder. If **q=0**, the effect expires immediately at transition. Otherwise, if the first initiative round is **R**, schedule expiration **Round Begin R+q**. For example, **37 s remaining → q=6**, expire R+6; **599 s → q=99**, not R+100. Do not award an extra round for fractional remainder.

**Initiative → exploration:** When initiative ends during round **C**, with an existing expiry at **Round Begin E**, count **`n=max(0,E−C)`** future Round Begin boundaries (including the expiry one), and conservatively restore **`6×max(0,n−1)` whole seconds**. If zero, expire immediately. For example **n=4 → 18 seconds**, not 24. The current partially completed round has no additional time credit.

These **conservative floor conversions** deliberately can shorten duration when changing timekeeping modes, never extend it, and do **not** define a universal round length. Repeated transitions carry only the **remaining** time, never reset the initial ten minutes. The calculations mirror the existing [Weaken Restraints technique-specific convention](weaken-restraints-v0.md#duration-and-round-anchoring) rather than silently inventing a global time model. If the effect expires at a Round Begin before a check, that check gets no +2. A caster becoming unable to act after successful application does not by itself stop the pending blessing.

## Status and deferred boundaries

**Proposed Tier-1 v0 Arts / Inspiration technique:** consensual **[Projection] beneficial Intrusion**, one conscious eligible recipient (self included), **3 square-equivalent direct range**, **2 Spent VP + Focus-priced Projection Action**, **one +2 to first specifically nominated creative/expressive noncombat check** before the 10-minute / authored 100-round expiry, no Training substitution or audience control, no Secondary/Rider, no multi-check bonus, no new social/Performance skill, no unapproved composite. Values provisional for L1–3 testing. This **completes one representative technique proposal for each of the eight selected Pillars**, but **does not complete starting repertoires, archetype expression permissions, all Tier-1/2 content, or 12-build MVP playtesting**.
