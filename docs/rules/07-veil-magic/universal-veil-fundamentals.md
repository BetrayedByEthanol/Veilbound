# Universal Veil Fundamentals — Foundation

> **LOCKED design foundation (B05).** Veilbound includes **Universal Veil magic**, separate from Pillar/Aspect techniques. This document locks its scope, shared accessibility, and the **Veil Inspection** family. Individual spell profiles, numeric tiers/costs, and advancement/acquisition details are **NOT YET AUTHORED**; do not fill them by importing D&D spells. [Veil Translocation](veil-translocation-foundation.md) contains its separate spatial rules.

## Where Universal Veil magic fits

The Veil is the underlying source and medium of magic. Pillars and their Aspects are stable patterns formed and sustained by Volition; they are **not** the only structures that can be examined or manipulated through the Veil.

**Universal Veil magic** acts on common Veil structures, interfaces, and spatial relationships **without needing an individual Pillar or Aspect as its source or access identity**. It is neither a **seventeenth Pillar** nor a **fourth Aspect** and does not grant access to arbitrary Pillar-domain effects.

| Family | What it does | Representative operations |
|---|---|---|
| **Veil Inspection** | Detect, identify, examine and interpret Veil structures | Sense magic; recognize a Pillar/Aspect signature on an item; analyze an enchantment |
| **Veil Translocation** | Relocate a subject or establish a connection between locations | Blink; translocate an item; teleport; open temporary or permanent portals |

These are the **initial two shared families**, not an exhaustive future catalogue. New families require a deliberate decision that their subject is truly universal, rather than an existing Pillar/Aspect expression.

### Universal eligibility is not free mastery

All **four Archetypes — Priest, Scholar, Gifted and Anointed — are eligible** to access and develop Universal Veil magic **without changing their Pillar/Aspect choices**:

- **Priest:** Universal Veil operations do not have to belong to the Priest's selected Pillar and do not consume a Prepared Prayer slot merely by being universal.
- **Scholar:** Universal Veil operations are distinct from individually learned **Pillar** techniques; the exact method of learning advanced Universal procedures is deferred, and a Scholar is not automatically granted every one.
- **Gifted:** Universal operations require no extra emotion-to-Aspect link and do not require evoking an arbitrarily assigned linked emotion merely to satisfy Pillar access.
- **Anointed:** Universal operations are not limited to the bonded Aspect; they do not become part of the Anointed's Birth Imprint, Signature or Aspect Authority by default.

**Eligibility alone does not provide a finished spell, bypass a technique-tier limit, grant an unlimited free action, or remove authored costs.** Common/basic procedures may be broadly accessible, while advanced spells and rituals may require tier, study, resources, time, equipment or other prerequisites. Exact knowledge, preparation, learning, VP, action and tier rules remain B05 follow-up work. **Do not count Universal procedures against Pillar-specific known/prepared/bonded catalogues by default; their own acquisition rules must be authored before play.**

**Focus** changes normal VP and Turn AP resources, not who is eligible for these operations. A Universal operation does not automatically become a Priest Consecration, Scholar Composite component, Gifted Conduit, or Anointed Authority effect; such interactions require **explicit authored compatibility**.

Universal operations follow the existing [Direct Veil interaction rules](resonance-and-intrusion.md) and [Manifestation resolution](manifestation-resolution.md) when their actual effect qualifies. Universal status never bypasses a living target's protected internal boundary, an attended object's applicable protections, physical hazards, or the existing [technique-tier ceiling](technique-tiers-and-expression.md).

## Veil Inspection

**Veil Inspection** is deliberate examination of a magical object, active effect, enchantment, Veil structure, or similar accessible phenomenon. Its baseline role is to provide common *magical awareness* and a route to more sophisticated identification **without requiring a matching Pillar**.

### Information boundaries — LOCKED

| Inspection task | Information sought | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Detect | Whether an accessible Veil structure is present | Not omniscient detection of all hidden or distant magic |
| Recognize | Observable Pillar/Aspect signature(s), if discernible | Does not determine the user's religion, loyalty or moral alignment |
| Identify | Understand an accessible effect's recognizable purpose, operation or properties | Does not automatically reveal every hidden function, curse or construction secret |
| Analyze | Study complex, suppressed, damaged, disguised or unknown patterns | May require significant expertise, an authored advanced procedure or equipment |

An item's **Pillar/Aspect signature describes the magical pattern present in the item**. It is **not** a reading of its current owner's beliefs or the item's supposed moral alignment. Multiple signatures, generic/unattributed effects and deliberately obscured patterns are possible; recognition is not guaranteed when a structure conceals or lacks a readable signature.

**Basic inspection should be a common Universal operation**, not a mandatory Talent, not a Priest-exclusive Prayer, and not a purchased all-purpose Perception skill. The **precise baseline result, VP cost (including whether basic inspection is free), action cost under pressure, proximity/range, checks and required equipment are deferred** to its individual executable procedure. **No numeric or combat-time detection power is granted by this foundation alone.**

**Veil Theory** may be relevant when interpreting the *meaning* of a magical pattern; **Veil Practice** may be relevant for an authored hands-on manipulation. Neither is an automatic bonus to every inspection, Direct Veil Pressure or spell attack.

An inspection that directly probes a protected creature, forcibly accesses an attended item, or dismantles an effect must follow the appropriate authored interaction and resistance procedure. Calling an operation `Inspection` does not remove those protections.

## Universal spell / ritual authoring boundary

A complete Universal operation must eventually specify:

- family, name and whether it is a common procedure, activatable spell or prolonged ritual;
- access/learning prerequisite and level/tier, if applicable;
- action or out-of-turn timing, VP spent/committed, material requirements and duration;
- eligible subject, source and destination, range, knowledge and environmental requirements;
- interaction type (when applicable), resistance/protection and failure behavior;
- measurable effect, scaling and any sustained cadence or concentration;
- which Focus/Archetype features, if any, explicitly interact with it.

**Pillar-domain effects** such as creating fire, healing injuries, changing a mind or transforming a creature do **not** become universal merely because their Veil delivery has generic elements. Universal Translocation relocates rather than inventing a new Pillar-domain payload.
