# Venom Endurance v0 — Life / Preservation

> **Executable Tier-1 Life / Preservation proposal; all numbers and the preventive/suppressive power budget remain PROVISIONAL (VE-01–VE-08).** This technique grants one living recipient a bounded defense against **ordinary biological/chemical Minor or Moderate mundane toxins**. Choose **Preventive Ward** against one new application or **Active Suppression** against consequences of one already-active instance. Neither mode heals injury, removes an active toxin, changes its clock, or generally protects against magical Plague, disease or non-toxin damage. See [Mundane Toxins v0](../05-combat/mundane-toxins-v0.md), [Life / Preservation boundary](../../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md#life), [Technique Structure](technique-structure-v0.md), [Intrusion](resonance-and-intrusion.md#intrusion-barrier), [Volition Points](volition-points.md), [Vital Shelter](vital-shelter-v0.md), [Basic Heal](basic-heal-v0.md), and [Priest](../08-archetypes/priest.md). A published technique is not automatically learned or prepared.

**Range and path.** For `Single Target`, calculate relative 3D reference-square offsets, sort absolute values `a >= b >= c`, and require `a + 0.5*b + 0.5*c <= 3`, without rounding. Use each creature's predesignated reference occupied square; if absent, deterministically choose its occupied square with lowest x, then y, then z **before declaration**, not its closest occupied square after a result is known. A non-self recipient must be directly visible and have an unobstructed straight **open-air magical delivery line** at declaration and at actual application. Walls, closed doors, and solid glass block outward delivery. After successful application, protection follows that **same** recipient without later range/sight/path checks. `Self` needs no external sight/path.

**Intrusion and refusal.** Genuinely beneficial, unopposed application (including an unconscious recipient with no still-effective prior refusal) takes **no** Intrusion contest. Conscious refusal or an effective previous express refusal invokes the canonical **+8 Intrusion Barrier**, using shaping **Intellect** against **Vitality + Awareness**: `d20 + caster raw VC + Intellect + caster Veil Integration Bonus + explicit modifiers` versus `10 + target Vitality + Awareness + target Veil Integration Bonus + Resonance + 8 + explicit modifiers`; **tie resists**. Hostile/deceptively mixed future combinations follow universal harmful/deceived Intrusion instead; declaring protection beneficial does not bypass their resistance. No extra save occurs when the later *mundane toxin* is encountered; its ordinary save is skipped **only when Ward actually negates the application**.

## A. Preventive Ward — one application

On successful application, establish **one unused interception** on the recipient. It intercepts the first qualifying **new, valid, non-ignored** application of a **Minor or Moderate mundane toxin** before that application takes its initial `d20 + Vitality` resistance check.

**Application sequence:**

1. Resolve the ordinary incoming source's **real exposure delivery** (injection/ingestion/inhalation/contact) and its normal weapon, armor, Guard, cover or physical-barrier requirements. A missed, blocked or non-exposing source does **not** consume this Ward.
2. Apply the mundane catalogue's **one-active-instance nonstack gate** using the pre-existing toxin's printed grade. An **equal/lower-grade ignored exposure** does not consume Ward. A genuinely eligible **higher-grade replacement candidate** may be intercepted before it replaces the old instance.
3. If the valid, non-ignored new application is printed **Minor or Moderate** and reaches this Ward while it is active, **consume its one interception and end Ward immediately**. The new toxin application is **fully negated**: **no natural resistance roll, Full/Reduced result, active instance, onset clock, immediate toxic HP/Vigor injury, impairment, or three-interval residual marker** is created for that application. An older active toxin remains unchanged if a higher-grade candidate was intercepted.
4. Resolve any **separate physical injury or other non-toxic consequence of the delivery normally**. Preventing an injected venom does **not** undo the puncture's HP damage, an ordinary wound already caused by the strike, or non-toxin attack effects.

A subsequent, independently valid exposure after Ward was spent is **not** protected, even if it has the same source. A **Serious+** toxin (not authored for v0), Death/Plague Intrusion, disease, or unrelated hazard neither benefits from nor consumes this Ward unless later explicit compatibility is written. Ward does not suppress an existing active toxin, including one present when Ward was cast.

## B. Active Suppression — one existing toxin instance

On successful application, the spell can bind to **one already-active, eligible Minor or Moderate mundane toxin instance** on that recipient. It prevents eligible **future consequences** of **that specific instance only**; it **does not end, cure, neutralize, remove or downgrade** the instance, reroll its natural resistance, change its printed grade, freeze its clock, change remaining Full/Reduced ticks, or erase earlier applied loss. The instance's original time remainder, already-scheduled events and one-instance priority remain authoritative.

**No automatic toxin detection.** A caster can attempt this mode on a living recipient without verified diagnosis. If no eligible active instance remains, or an instance is present but has no remaining consequence (for example, an instant cytotoxin's harmless residual nonstack marker), the attempted legal cast still costs the action and **2 Spent VP**, establishes **no useful suppression**, and does not confirm a diagnosis. No eligibility check acts as a free toxin scanner or previews hidden grades/timers.

**Finite unit budget.** Upon successful binding, snapshot **`B = min(5, 3 + floor(max(0, raw VC)/2))`** suppression units. Record `remaining_units`; they are **never regenerated** by reassigning, changing mode, switching initiative/exploration, modifying VC, or the toxin's progression. All eligible unit spending is **automatic**, not player-selected packet skipping. Prevented toxin injury/penalties do not return later. If there is no remaining effect to suppress, no budgeted instance is established.

| Eligible toxin consequence | Unit spending and outcome |
|---|---|
| Scheduled discrete **HP-damage packet** (e.g., TX-HM, TX-NC) | Spend **1 unit at the packet's real scheduled moment**, prevent the **whole eligible toxin HP packet**; not the source attack's independent HP injury |
| Scheduled discrete **direct Vigor-damage packet** (TX-NP) | Spend **1 unit when it would actually lower Current Vigor**, prevent its **whole eligible toxin packet**; if Vigor already 0, no packet reduction and no unit expenditure |
| **Continuous functional penalties** (Dodge, ordinary MP, specified checks) | Spend **1 unit on starting each protected short toxin interval** to suppress **all named penalties from that one toxin profile** during that interval; no cost per incoming attack/check |
| **TX-CA regeneration-throughput impairment** | Spend **1 unit per active short toxin interval protected**; the `−1` does not modify any genuine Step 2 within that protected interval, **even if no Step 2 actually occurs** |
| **TX-EN long-duration continuous impairment** | Use the separate **six-second-equivalent suppression-local intervals** below; spend 1 on the current partial interval and 1 per subsequent protected interval, not 1 per hour or per task |
| **TX-HE one legitimate Vigor Recovery reduction** | Spend **1 unit only if the normally scheduled, genuinely eligible Recovery event actually occurs while suppression is active**; suppress that toxin's `−2/−1` adjustment only; Recovery itself is never created or advanced |
| **Harmless onset-delay interval / no eligible packet** | **0 units**, except if an independently eligible continuous penalty is already active; do not spend merely for the passing delay |
| **Already applied injury/impairment event** | **Never reverse**, heal, refund or backdate |

**Continuous short-clock timing.** At successful binding, if a short-clock toxin has a current, still-active continuous penalty, spend **1 unit immediately** to suppress that penalty for the **remaining portion of its currently active toxin-local interval** (including any existing `phase_seconds` remainder). At the **end of that interval**, after that interval's due toxin consequences and expiry are handled, if the same continuous toxin penalty remains active and the spell has units and duration left, **spend the next unit** for the next interval. In initiative, the recipient's **genuine Step 3** completes one short toxin-local interval; after resolving that interval, the next is protected only if purchased. Outside initiative, use the toxin's **actual six-second clock and retained fractional remainder**; no virtual turn, regeneration, wound repair, Dying loss, AP or Recovery is created. A unit committed to a partially elapsed interval is not prorated or refunded. If the profile expires on that boundary, do **not** purchase a pointless next unit.

**Long continuous clock (TX-EN).** An already-active longer duration has no short toxin-local intervals. At casting, spend 1 unit to protect the **current suppression-local interval**, starting a separate six-second-equivalent counter at **phase 0**. Outside initiative, actual elapsed seconds advance that suppression-local counter; in initiative, each **genuine recipient Step 3 reached after casting** completes one suppression-local interval, preserving any exploration fractional remainder. At each completed interval, if TX-EN and this spell remain active and a unit remains, spend 1 for the next; otherwise its original penalty resumes. A spent interval protects all eligible tasks/checks within it; it does not trigger per task. This counter **never changes** TX-EN's 2/3-hour expiry or creates a general six-second combat-round rule.

**Discrete versus continuous ordering.** A mixed profile, if ever independently authored, must explicitly decide which packet and continuing condition use units; this v0 catalogue has **one dominant family** per profile. Do **not** spend a second discrete-packet unit for a functional penalty already suppressed by the same protected interval. For the eleven existing entries, TX-HM/TX-NC/TX-NP use discrete packets; TX-NE/TX-MY/TX-CA/TX-PN/TX-DE/TX-EN use continuous intervals; TX-HE uses the one Recovery event; TX-CY resolves its only consequence instantly and has no suppressible future effect after application.

**Depletion/ending.** Decrement units at the actual authorized spend. A suppression with **0 remaining units** ends once its just-purchased protection has completed: for a **discrete packet**, immediately after preventing that packet; for a **continuous interval**, at that interval's end or earlier normal spell/toxin expiry, with no next purchase. Any unprotected future toxin consequences follow the original active instance with **no fresh Vitality roll**. If a higher-grade valid unprevented toxin replaces the bound instance, or the original toxin expires or is explicitly cured, **end this suppression immediately** with unused units lost. Equal/lower-grade ignored exposure has no effect. New exposure cannot inherit units. Other spells, such as Vital Shelter, are distinct and cannot borrow units.

## Duration, activation and archetype boundaries

**Fixed duration only.** Successful Ward or Suppression has the same one-minute cap. If applied within initiative round **R**, it expires **at Round Begin R+10 before ordinary events scheduled for that boundary**; an hourly toxin pulse scheduled exactly at R+10 is therefore **not** protected. If applied in exploration, expire at 60 actually elapsed seconds. Converting between modes uses **the same conservative technique-only scheme as Vital Shelter**:

- **Exploration → initiative:** with `s` **whole** actual seconds remaining, set `q = floor(s/6)`; if `q=0`, expire at entry before combat actions, otherwise expire at **Round Begin R+q**. Do not round up or reset to R+10.
- **Initiative → exploration:** exiting during round `C` with expiry at Round Begin `E`, convert to `6 × max(0, E−C−1)` whole remaining seconds; expire immediately if zero. Never extend time by switching repeatedly.
- This is **not** a universal global combat round duration. Short toxin clocks keep their own exact fractional progress; long toxin events retain their **separate existing Round Begin mappings**. Suppression-local clocks and event records retain their own phase across switches. No phantom Regeneration/Dying step or six-hour Vigor Recovery is created.
- **Boundary order:** an effect expires **before** events at its own Round Begin expiry. A continuous interval already purchased remains protected through its normal interval's final Step 3 while the technique is otherwise active, then its purchased protection ends; **no** new interval starts after expiry. A discrete packet due *after* expiry is not prevented. Never resolve an earlier scheduled toxin packet retroactively because a late cast begins.

**Spend and actions.** At declaration choose recipient, exact profile, one mode, and full ordinary VP price; confirm required action/available VP and (for other target) actual sight/path/range. Pay and commit the **one Focus-priced Projection Action** at declaration, resolve normal responses/interruptions, then recheck capability and targeting at valid magical application. **Interrupted or invalidated before application:** AP remains lost; **0 Spent VP**. **Valid application:** spend locked **2 ordinary VP** even if resisted, no useful toxin exists, or Ward expires unused; resolve beneficial/contested Intrusion. On success establish the selected bounded effect. Caster's later unconsciousness does **not** automatically dispel a fixed, already established spell; recipient death and actual expiry end it. Voluntary own-turn dismissal is allowed with no action cost but no refund.

**Ordinary cost reductions / High Priest.** An independently legal once-per-activation ordinary Spent discount (e.g., L3 Frugal Technique/TAL-M03) can reduce this **2 → 1 Spent VP**, with its own declaration-use limitations. For High Priest L1–4 **Open Invocation** of this otherwise eligible *unprepared* Prayer, use **one Full Action**, pay the ordinary **2 Spent VP + separate 1 Spent VP surcharge** on valid application (3 Available required), and do not discount the surcharge. An L3 Open Prayer slot filled at legitimate first use counts as Prepared and owes no Invocation surcharge. A technique selection is never automatically Prepared/known solely by belonging to Life.

**Not an authored maintained Priest mode.** There is **no Committed-VP Reserve, maintained variant, Consecration, Reassign, Intercession extension, [Embodiment] Devotional Rite, Chosen Devotion, Exaltation, Reactive casting or self-only bonus**. Medium/Low Priests may still **prepare and cast this ordinary Prayer** when legal. A corresponding future sustained defensive configuration would require independently authored costs, limits and compatibility. Merely having exact `Self`/`Single Target` identifiers is **not** permission to use High Scholar Composite Casting without a Secondary and explicit compatibility.

## Interaction priority with other effects

1. **Physical delivery first.** Preserve normal armor/Guard/avoidance and the actual exposure route; do not bypass mundane protection with magic.
2. **Existing toxin nonstack/replacement gate.** An equal/lower printed grade attempt is ignored; Ward spends nothing. A higher-grade new application is interceptable before replacement; if Ward negates it, the original active instance stays.
3. **Ward before natural resistance.** When Ward intercepts, remove only the *new toxin application*; no Full/Reduced choice or marker occurs. An unintercepted application then makes **one ordinary** `d20 + Vitality` check and establishes its Full/Reduced instance.
4. **Suppression on already scheduled consequences.** Follow the source's own timer and resolve protection before applying the covered packet/penalty. Suppression never recreates or rearranges an event.
5. **Distinct damage defenses.** For a toxin **direct Vigor packet** already prevented by Suppression, **Vital Shelter spends 0 budget**. If Suppression is absent/exhausted, Vital Shelter may stop eligible **remaining direct Vigor damage** under its own order/capacity rules. Vital Shelter cannot protect toxin **HP** packets, named impairments, reduced Regeneration throughput or suppressed Recovery. Basic Heal may repair existing HP/Vigor loss separately but does not neutralize the toxin.
6. **Wound / Dying timing unchanged.** An actual unprevented HP packet crossing from positive to 0 or below triggers existing wound/Dying checks. Prevented toxin packets cannot produce their own 0-HP crossing or subsequent injury, but earlier unrelated wounds and genuine own-turn Dying processing remain.
7. **No protective oracle or persistence trick.** The ward/suppression does not identify a toxin, reveal a hidden source/grade, or leave an active marker when an intercept was fully negated. Fresh legal casting is needed for any new budget or ward, with new AP/VP cost; no free recast, hand-off, counter refresh, or stacking across casters.

## Calibration / deferred scope

- **VE-01 — short toxin near-cure:** Three units can prevent the last three TX-HM/TX-NC damaging packets. This is temporary prevention of all remaining consequences, **not** removal of the existing instance. Measure encounter impact before reducing to 2 units or changing packet costs.
- **VE-02 — preventive efficiency:** One 2-VP Ward can negate a complete Moderate TX-CY/TX-HM application; compare against Basic Heal's 2 HP plus Vigor and Vital Shelter's 8+VC direct-Vigor pool. An intercepted new exposure must be a **real delivered exposure**, not a declaration of intent.
- **VE-03 — defense density:** One unit protects an entire continuous TX-NE interval, potentially spanning many attacker rolls. Assess benefit against ordinary per-round attack density; no per-attack unit spending in v0.
- **VE-04 — long impairment weakness:** Three short suppression intervals are intentionally weak for TX-EN's multi-hour penalty; future separate long-duration medicinal care/cleansing might fill that role, not an implicit extension of this spell.
- **VE-05 — transition conservatism:** Test partial interval remainders and early fixed expiry across initiative/exploration switches; don't turn mode changes into unit renewals or regen steps.
- **VE-06 — level/VP efficiency:** With increasing VP pools or discounts, repeated legal recasts cost actions and nonrefundable Spent VP. No free immunity from one maintained reserve exists.
- **VE-07 — long event priority:** Preserve TX-NP hourly and TX-HE genuine six-hour Recovery deadlines, including simultaneous actual event and spell expiry order and conservative initiative conversions.
- **VE-08 — one-instance gate:** An ignored same-grade application must not consume Ward; an intercepted higher grade must not replace an earlier Minor; a successful interception never creates a residual marker.
- **Deferred:** Serious+ toxicities, magical Death/Plague poisons/diseases, medical cures/antidotes, toxin discovery and source species, Alchemy ingredient/recipe handling, multi-family stacks, Tier-2+ upcasts, maintained Consecration/Devotional Rite mode, explicit Composite compatibility, Secondary/Rider, and encounter/creature delivery methods.

**Status:** **Venom Endurance Tier-1 Primary profiles AUTHORED v0**, balance provisional. This adds one distinct Life / Preservation technique, not a tested Level-1–3 Priest build, distinct Tier-2/3 Miracle, or a replacement for the mundane toxin catalogue.
