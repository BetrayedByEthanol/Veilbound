# Vital Shelter v0 — Life / Preservation

> **Executable Tier-1 proposal; numeric balance PROVISIONAL (VS-01 / VS-02).** Vital Shelter prevents a bounded cumulative amount of **incoming direct Vigor damage** to one living recipient. It is **not** a new Vigor pool, HP resistance, healing, regeneration, a ward against non-damaging conditions, or indefinite immunity. Fixed-duration and maintained payment modes are mutually exclusive; the latter has a **nonrefundable 1-Spent-VP establishment price** so each new reservoir has a lasting cost. See [Life's Preservation boundary](../../world/aspect-identities-and-boundaries.md#life), [HP and Vigor](../06-vigor-wounds-death/hp-and-vigor.md), [VP states and no-refresh](volition-points.md), [Technique Structure](technique-structure-v0.md), [Direct Veil Intrusion](resonance-and-intrusion.md#intrusion-barrier), [Priest](../08-archetypes/priest.md) and [High Scholar Composite Casting](../08-archetypes/scholar.md#high-scholar--projection-through-synthesis).

## Recipient, targeting, and components

`Single Target`: up to **3 direct 3D square-equivalents** from caster to recipient (nominal **4.5 m** on one axis). For this technique independently define offsets `dx,dy,dz`, take absolute values and sort `a >= b >= c`; range is **`a + 0.5*b + 0.5*c`** without rounding (e.g. (2,2,0) qualifies at 3; (3,1,0) does not at 3.5). Use each participant's predesignated reference occupied square for a multi-square creature; if absent select its lowest-x, then lowest-y, then lowest-z occupied square **before declaration**, not the nearest after a roll. A non-self recipient requires direct visibility and one **straight unobstructed open-air magical delivery line** at declaration and application; wall, closed door, and solid glass block delivery. A legal effect **follows the recipient** thereafter without ongoing sight/range checks. `Self` needs no outward sight/path.

**Components: None** for both exact profiles in either mode, including for an authorized Composite Vital Shelter component. Lack of hand freedom or speech by itself does not block the spell; actual inability to use its selected action or apply magic does. An **eligible Basic Heal composite component separately requires its own functioning available hand**; Vital Shelter's component freedom does not waive Basic Heal's.

For resisted bodily Intrusion, use `d20 + raw Veil Control + Intellect + Veil Integration Bonus + applicable modifiers` versus `10 + target Vitality + target Awareness + target Veil Integration Bonus + target Resonance + 8 + applicable modifiers` with normal Direct Veil rules; a tie fails. An accepted beneficial effect has no such roll. This is **not a pre-hit defense contest** against the later Vigor-damaging attacker.

## Prevention and finite-budget resolution

On **successful application** create a single finite **remaining budget** equal to B (Primary) or S (authorized Secondary), **snapshotted once**. Each eligible direct Vigor-damage packet resolves as follows:

1. Resolve its original legal delivery, hit/defense/contest, type, and existing applicable packet-specific Vigor mitigation; a missed, completely blocked or otherwise ineffective effect yields no eligible damage packet. Do not invent a Vigor-damage packet from HP damage or impose a new attack roll. Fire resistance and physical Armor do **not** reduce independent Conflagration Vigor burn unless separately authorized.
2. If the recipient has **no Current Vigor**, the shelter consumes **no budget** from a packet that cannot reduce Vigor further. Otherwise, if the recipient has Current Vigor **greater than 0**, absorb `min(nonnegative remaining eligible Vigor packet, remaining Shelter budget)`; subtract that absorbed amount from **both** packet and budget.
3. Apply any remaining Vigor damage to recipient's Current Vigor under normal limits (no negative Vigor). Any surplus damage beyond available Vigor never converts into HP damage. **Do not refund Shelter points** used to absorb a large packet merely because the remaining packet exceeds Current Vigor.
4. At **remaining Shelter budget 0**, terminate that shelter effect immediately. If part of a maintained composite, end only that Secondary component and release **its still-Committed VP**; the Primary host remains active if independently legal. A recipient with no Vigor may remain sheltered until a later eligible damaging packet actually threatens a positive Current Vigor pool.

**Excluded from prevention:** HP damage of any type, wounds, Dying HP loss, regeneration/repair Vigor expenditure, active voluntary Vigor spending, casting VP, maximum-Vigor reduction, anti-regeneration throughput/suppression, blocked external Vigor restoration, exhaustion/status effects without a literal Vigor-damage packet, and ordinary six-hour Vigor Recovery. The technique **does not regenerate or add any Vigor**, cause an extra Regeneration Step, or automatically prevent death or wounds. For a mixed hit, protect the **Vigor packet only**; the independent HP packet and any normal Devastating Critical wound trigger still resolve.

**No-refresh:** The original finite budget belongs to the **one successfully created effect instance**, not to its recipient, current caster statistics, attached host, location or whichever creature receives an Intercession. Reassigning, moving, reanchoring, changing situation, releasing a partial component, entering/leaving initiative or changing Form **never tops it up**. A **genuinely new legal cast** after any prior conflicting instance has ended creates a new full budget only after its **new action and complete Spent/Committed price** are paid. Neither the universal no-refresh rule nor an eligible Reassign allows a free recharge.

## Two exclusive Primary payment and duration modes

### A. Fixed-duration — 2 Spent VP

Lock the **effective ordinary Spent price** at declaration (base **2**, or **1** with a legally applicable one-point Spent discount, e.g. TAL-M03); **spend that locked price** on any valid magical application whether the recipient accepts or resists. If interrupted/invalidated before an actual application, lose committed AP but **no VP** (a TAL-M03 selected at legal declaration remains consumed according to its Talent rule).

A successful fixed effect lasts until its **budget is empty** or **60 elapsed seconds** after application outside initiative, or **Round Begin R+10** if cast during combat round R, whichever comes first. A fixed cast is not tied to the caster's reservation: it can persist through caster unconsciousness/death while the recipient lives; no Spent VP are refunded on dismissal. It is **not Consecration or Devotional Rite**.

**Initiative transition (local conservative conversion):** Exploration with `s` whole seconds remaining → start initiative at round R with `q=floor(s/6)`; expire immediately if `q=0`, otherwise **Round Begin R+q**. Initiative ending in current round C with expiry at Round Begin E → `n=max(0,E-C)` and `s=6*max(0,n-1)` whole elapsed seconds remaining; expire immediately if `s=0`. Count no partial-round credit, discard remainders, and apply repeated conversions **only to remaining time**. Never start a new 60 seconds or replenish budget by crossing initiative boundaries. This six-second conservative estimate is **not** a global rule defining round duration.

### B. Maintained reserve — 2 Committed + 1 Spent VP

Lock the ordinary **1 Spent VP establishment cost** and **2 Committed VP** together; check **3 Available VP** for a normal independent cast both at declaration and application. **Spend 1 on a valid magical application even if Intrusion resistance defeats it**, while committing the required **2 only if the effect succeeds**. An interrupt or illegality before application incurs no VP cost but loses committed AP. The maintained effect ends on complete budget depletion, caster's death, recipient death, actual magical-channel termination or own-turn legal voluntary release. **Caster unconsciousness alone does not end this maintained Primary**, although an externally attached host such as Stone Skin may have a stricter end condition. The still-Committed VP return on end; Spent VP never return from release. No upkeep AP/VP or absolute duration limit.

A **Low Priest** whose Prepared Vital Shelter is their **Chosen Devotion**, who actually targets themself with its maintained Primary (authored `Self` or legal self-selected `Single Target`), and who **declares that one new active Rite instance as Chosen while no other Chosen Rite is active** reduces **2 Committed → 1 Committed VP** (minimum 1) for that instance only; another instance remains non-Chosen at the normal 2 Committed; the **1 Spent VP stays**. This requires **2 Available VP** at L1 with VA 0, leaving **0 Available** once successfully activated. **Fixed mode is not eligible** for this discount. There is **no authored Vital Shelter Exaltation property** at L3.

**VS-01 — recast-price watch:** Under these provisional numbers, releasing and starting a genuinely new maintained effect pays only **1 new Spent VP** (plus another full action and a **fresh 2 Committed** reservation, which returns later), versus **2 Spent VP** for fixed mode. This is deliberately **legal for testing**, not concealed as a free renewal or blocked by an invented cooldown. At increasing Max VP, monitor whether action cost and VP opportunity cost still justify the fixed alternative; adjust price/output separately only after evidence. The current budget is **never** refreshed by mere release, Reassign, Intercession or host reconfiguration.

### Open Invocation, Talent discounts, and access timing

For an eligible High Priest using **Open Invocation on unprepared** Vital Shelter, add the **separate +1 Spent VP Invocation Surcharge** to the chosen mode's ordinary locked price. This surcharge is never reduced by TAL-M03 / TAL-P02 or other ordinary-cost discounts. At levels **1–4**, charge a **Full Action consuming the caster's entire Turn AP allotment** (only legal if all Turn AP remain); at level **5+**, use the ordinary **20-AP High Projection Action**. On valid magical application **spend the surcharge whether resisted or successful**. For maintained mode, only commit ordinary VP on success; resistant maintained application spends its **1 ordinary +1 Invocation = 2 Spent VP**, commits 0. Pre-application interruption spends neither ordinary VP nor surcharge but still loses the paid action. At L3 an **Open Prayer** slot fill counts as Prepared and therefore uses normal action/cost **without** an Open Invocation surcharge.

**Spent discounts:** TAL-M03 at L3 may reduce the fixed **2 ordinary Spent → 1** or a legally eligible composite's **aggregate ordinary Spent** component once, minimum 1, following its **own-turn, use-at-declaration and once-per-activation** rules. Vital Shelter's **standalone maintained 1 ordinary Spent** is **not** TAL-M03-eligible because its ordinary Spent component is below 2. TAL-P02 at L5, where genuinely eligible, reduces a Prepared Prayer's normal Spent component ≥2 only; it never discounts the Invocation surcharge or the Committed amount. No simultaneous TAL-M03 and TAL-P02 on one prayer; do not invent extra discount grants.

## Medium Priest — maintained Consecration only

An eligible **Medium Priest** with Vital Shelter Prepared may sustain the normal **maintained Primary** `Single Target` or `Self` expression as **one Active Consecration** (L1–3 cap one), paying **2 Committed + 1 Spent VP** and one Medium **24-AP Projection Action**. The fixed version is **not** a Consecration. No transfer gives a new budget.

**Reassign (24-AP General Tempo Action, own turn):** Choose a valid living replacement recipient satisfying this spell's 3-square range, sight/open-air path and Beneficial Intrusion at declaration and resolution. Transfer the **same** instance with **exactly its remaining budget and 2 Committed VP**, no extra Spent VP or recast. On new recipient resistance, invalid target or failed path, 24 AP remain lost, but the prior recipient retains the effect unchanged and no extra VP are charged.

**Level-3 Intercession (Reaction + 2 Spent VP, provisional):** While a maintained Vital Shelter Consecration exists with **positive remaining budget**, the Priest may respond when **another** eligible living creature faces a **determined qualifying incoming Vigor-damage packet** but **before** applying that packet to their Vigor. The Priest must perceive the imminent damaging event and recipient, with valid 3-square targeting range, line and Beneficial Intrusion access, and have available Reaction/2 Spent VP. The recipient may accept (no Intrusion roll) or resist (normal +8 barrier). On a **legal attempted extension** spend Reaction + **2 Spent VP** whether accepted or resisted; an interrupted/ineligible attempt before actual extension spends the Reaction but not VP. On success absorb this **one packet from the original Consecration's remaining budget**; after the packet the temporary extension ends. Original recipient remains assigned throughout. If the shared budget reaches 0, **the original Consecration ends**, returning its 2 Committed VP, and cannot block a later packet. Intercession provides no second shield, budget, activation, free Reaction, or rearrangement of packet resolution. Reassignment is never a Reactive free move.

## Low Priest — maintained Self Devotional Rite

An eligible **Low Priest** who has **Prepared** Vital Shelter and maintains its Primary on themself (either `Self` or legally self-selected `Single Target`) **automatically uses a Devotional Rite** only while an Active Devotional Rite slot is available: **maximum 1 active Rite at L1–3; maximum 2 from L4** under [Twofold Devotion](../08-archetypes/priest.md#level-4--twofold-devotion--ability-increase). This limit includes the Chosen Rite and every non-Chosen Rite; **VP affordability never overrides the cap**. The Priest may designate this prepared Rite as Chosen Devotion during legal preparation, but the designation by itself does **not** permit an additional simultaneous Rite. The [universal Priest Rite test](../08-archetypes/priest.md#devotional-rites)—actual self-targeting plus maintained Committed VP—is the permission; the `Self` Inward/Embodiment tag is descriptive and grants no generic Low Scholar or Gifted eligibility. As Chosen Devotion, pay **1 Committed + 1 Spent VP** and a **30-AP Low Projection Action** at L1; a non-Chosen maintained Rite still costs **2 Committed + 1 Spent**, unaffordable at **VA 0, L1 Max VP 2**, **but potentially affordable at L2+ only when a Rite slot is vacant**. At L1–3, if another Rite such as Chosen Guided Regeneration is already active, activating Vital Shelter as a second Rite is **illegal even if its full 3 Available VP remain**. The caster must legally release/end the old Rite before declaring a new maintained Rite; this **does not** change which Rite is Chosen or automatically apply its discount to the replacement. Fixed 2-Spent Primary can still be a normal Prepared Prayer if affordable but never a Rite. Depletion ends the Rite, and returning Committed VP does not refund the establishment Spent VP. **Exaltation is deliberately unauthored** here, including any extra bodily condition protection, and must not be inferred from Life/Preservation.

## High Scholar — explicit Composite Casting compatibility

Both `Single Target` and `Self` have a **High Scholar Composite-compatible Secondary** with one finite budget `S = 4 + floor(max(0, VC)/2)`. The caster must **actually know** both techniques and meet all exact-profile, formula (L1–2), tier, AP, VP, components, target, attack/resistance and host-compatibility requirements from [High Scholar](../08-archetypes/scholar.md#levels-12--technical-repertoire-and-prepared-composite-formulae). The **Primary** owns the recipient, range, resolution and host. The Secondary receives **only the specifically stated diminished prevention budget**, not a full extra Primary protection, independent recipient/activation, reactive activation or Rider. **Full ordinary VP cost for each selected mode of both components**, and **one slower composite action**, not two; no second automatic halving of S. `VC` uses the composite caster's applicable Vital Shelter VC when the composite legally applies.

**Authorized composite A — Vital Shelter Primary + Basic Heal Secondary:**
- Exact shared profile `Single Target` or `Self`; the Primary Shelter owns that one eligible living recipient and delivery. **Basic Heal's own one-free-hand component remains mandatory**.
- **Declare Basic Heal's own Used Tier `T_heal` independently**, within [the canonical universal normal tier ceiling](technique-tiers-and-expression.md#universal-technique-tiers), **without any additional composite-specific tier cap**. Examples: **L1–2 max T1; L3–4 max T2; L5–6 max T3; L7–8 max T4; L17–20 max T9**, and intermediate levels follow that same canonical table. Select any otherwise legal integer **`1 ≤ T_heal ≤ current normal tier ceiling`**, subject to Basic Heal's own access and other prerequisites. Vital Shelter itself remains **Used Tier 1**; a higher `T_heal` never raises Shelter's B budget or changes its duration. Basic Heal's **authored Secondary** applies **once on successful composite activation**, using its own independent **1 HP / +1 direct Dying Repair Progress** and **`2 + ceil(d6 × T_heal / 6) + max(0, Basic Heal VC)` Vigor restoration**, before target missing-resource caps. At **VC 0**, `T_heal=1` restores **3 Vigor**, while `T_heal=2` restores **3–4 Vigor** (the d6 is rolled once on successful resolution). It never repeats when Shelter takes damage and never expands target count.
- Vital Shelter **Primary** may be **fixed** or **maintained**, using **Basic Heal's full `2 × T_heal` ordinary Spent VP price** regardless of its Secondary output. **Fixed composite base cost = `2 + 2 × T_heal` Spent VP** (examples: **4 at T1; 6 at T2; 8 at T3; 10 at T4; 20 at T9**); **maintained composite base cost = `2 Committed VP + (1 + 2 × T_heal) Spent VP`** (examples: **2 Committed + 3 Spent at T1; +5 Spent at T2; +7 Spent at T3; +9 Spent at T4; +19 Spent at T9**). Both components share **one Projection Action**, and the **whole base cost** must be Available at declaration and application. Any eligible TAL-M03 composite discount applies at most **once** to the locked **aggregate ordinary Spent** price, not once per component; never reduce Committed VP. Each component retains its access, tier, and eligibility; a resisted whole bodily Intrusion establishes no shelter, but legally paid ordinary Spent components are not refunded. Committed VP establish only on success.
- The instantaneous Basic Heal Secondary does **not** acquire the Shelter's duration or create recurring healing. A healed resource remains healed when Shelter ends. Composite is still a **single legal target**, no reactive cast or separate healing Action.

**Authorized composite B — Stone Skin Primary + Vital Shelter Secondary:**
- Exact shared profile **`Self` only**, **normal on-turn Stone Skin Primary activation only**. Stone Skin's **Reactive** activation **cannot** carry this or any other reactive Composite Secondary; no new Reaction privilege.
- Stone Skin provides its **normal Primary physical/Fire/Electrical HP resistance**; Vital Shelter contributes only **S** finite incoming-Vigor prevention, maintained as a **subordinate Secondary** attached to **that specific Stone Skin Primary host**. The host's own action, self-target Intrusion and termination rules apply. Vital Shelter Secondary is **maintained only**: base **Stone Skin `ceil(Max VP/3)` Committed + Vital Shelter 2 Committed + 1 Spent**. At L1 VA0 High Scholar Max VP 6, that means **2 + 2 Committed and 1 Spent = 5 Available VP** on declaration/application. The combined activation is **one ordinary 20-AP Projection Action**.
- **Host termination ends the Secondary immediately**, returning its still-Committed VP along with Stone Skin's. In particular Stone Skin's unconsciousness channel break also ends its Shelter Secondary, notwithstanding standalone Vital Shelter's more permissive unconsciousness rule. If Shelter Secondary budget depletes first, **only Secondary ends and releases its 2 Committed VP**; Stone Skin Primary persists until its own termination. There is **no post-activation Secondary refill**, independent detached Shelter, or automatic restart by recasting a new host separately without paying a new legal composite price. `S` is not recalculated after host stat changes.

**Not authorized in v0:** reverse **Basic Heal Primary + Vital Shelter Secondary** (an Instant Primary supplies no authored persistent Shelter host); **Vital Shelter Secondary on other unrelated Primary techniques** merely because they use `Self`/`Single Target`; Stone Skin as Secondary (it authors Primary only); duplicate Vital Shelter on one recipient; two reserved enchantments on one weapon; composite Guided Regeneration merely because keys match; independent Rider item/application; non-Scholar Composite Casting. Author these separately if ever wanted. An ordinary Vital Shelter Primary plus Basic Heal Secondary does not change each Aspect's identity: Restoration restores once; Preservation prevents later direct Vigor damage.

**VS-02 — composite efficiency watch:** Compare **full VP cost for reduced Basic Heal Secondary / 4+floor(VC/2) Shelter Secondary** against two separate spells, including action count, available VP and held reservations at **L1, L3 and high VP**. Increased VP reserves do **not** themselves grant Composite Casting; only actual High Scholar authorization does. A component's existing separately authored talent discount may affect the **one combined ordinary Spent VP cost** only as permitted by TAL-M03, never create two discounts within one composite.

## Activation, maintenance and replacement checklist

1. **Declare:** Confirm individual technique access/preparation and valid exact profile, one exclusive payment mode and any specifically authorized Composite/Consecration use; **if a Low Priest is actually self-targeting with a maintained Primary, this is automatically a Rite and the shared cap (L1–3 one; L4+ two) must be checked before AP/VP even when the caster calls it an ordinary Prayer**; select **recipient and mode before responses**. Check components for **all** composite components. Verify action capacity, 3-square direct range/sight/path (unless `Self`), living recipient, nonstacking, and **sufficient Available VP for total locked ordinary Spent + Committed + any High Priest Open Invocation surcharge**. Lock one-time VC/used tier as the profile requires. For a legally selected TAL-M03, consume its use **at legal declaration**, even if interrupted.
2. **Commit action and resolve responses:** Normally one Focus-priced Projection Action (High **20**, Medium **24**, Low **30**). Authorized composite uses **one slower legal action**; authorized Priest Open Invocation at L1–4 uses **one Full Action only with the entire Turn AP allotment still available**, consumes it all even if interrupted/resisted, and at L5+ uses the ordinary action. Resolve ordinary reactions/interceptions and recheck afterward. No free attack, Regeneration Step, or Reactive cast arises.
3. **Recheck at magical application:** Recipient still living; actual valid components, range/sight/path, caster capability, instance cap, **Low Priest Active Rite cap if applicable**, and **all locked VP components available together**. If invalidated/interrupted before application: action lost, **0 ordinary Spent, 0 Invocation surcharge, 0 Committed**, notwithstanding TAL-M03 consumption on legal declaration.
4. **Spend and contest:** On a **legal magical application** spend **locked ordinary Spent VP for every component** (fixed Primary 2 or maintained Primary 1, plus **full `2 × T_heal` Basic Heal ordinary Spent when that Secondary is selected at its own legal Used Tier**) and **any separate +1 Open Invocation surcharge**, even if the direct beneficiary resists. Resolve one **full-effect** beneficial Intrusion acceptance/contest for the shared recipient; refusal of a harmful or unwanted component cannot be bypassed by combining with a beneficial one. **Only on success** commit the locked maintained VP component(s) and establish the applicable snapshotted B/S reservoir and any legal host. A resisted attempt leaves no maintained reservation. No secondary application after a host's failed activation.
5. **Track:** Maintain one remaining budget and original expiry/channel on the actual instance; apply eligible packet prevention before Current Vigor damage, without stacking. On depletion, terminate that Shelter component; on release/recipient death/maintenance loss/duration expiry terminate and return only still-Committed VP. A caster may release at an **own-turn zero-action decision point**, never as a new interrupt. To replace a living shelter with a new one, **end the prior conflicting instance first**; a fresh legal cast spends full new cost and creates a new budget.

## Calibration watches and deferred authoring

- **VS-01 Maintained recast versus fixed:** track **Vigor damage actually prevented / Spent VP**, actions spent recasting, average Committed VP opportunity cost and percentage of combat/elapsed time shelter runs, at low and high Max VP. Repeated 1-Spent new reservoirs are **permitted**, not silently patched with a cooldown; 8-budget and 2/1/2 prices remain provisional.

- **VS-03 Aspect damage benchmark:** compare Shelter first with representative **moderate non-Conflagration direct Vigor damage** and Basic Heal replenishment. Treat **Conflagration as a deliberately high-Vigor-burn specialist stress test**, not the universal benchmark for all damaging Aspects. Shelter need not stop most HP-first spells.
- **VS-04 Low Priest affordability:** L1 VA0 Chosen shelter ties up **all 2 VP** (1 Spent /1 Committed) and can leave no VP for another spell until release/VP recovery; compare Guided Regeneration Chosen (1 Committed; 1 Available remains) and ordinary Active Prayer decision pressure.
- **VS-05 Intercession:** price **2 Spent + Reaction** relative to remaining budget, often less than two points; range/awareness/resistance checks and exhaustion while still guarding original recipient.
