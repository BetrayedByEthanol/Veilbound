# Volition Points

> Canonical current rules. The metaphysical meaning of Volition and VP is defined in [Metaphysical Foundation](metaphysical-foundation.md).

## What VP represents

**Volition Points represent freely available Volition**: the portion of a character's Veil integration that is not already structurally expressed and can therefore be deliberately spent or committed to techniques and sustained effects.

VP is not a measure of the character's total Veil integration, total willpower, moral conviction, or personal worth.

Low- and Medium-Focus characters may have substantial Veil integration already expressed through embodiment, routing capability, body, reflexes, equipment interaction, or persistent magical structures. That integration remains real even though it is not represented as freely spendable VP.

Veilbound does not track a separate numeric "Total Volition" pool.

Universal **Resonance** measures the coherence of Volition, not its quantity. Resonance therefore does **not** modify Maximum VP or normal VP Recovery unless a future explicit rule states otherwise.

## Maximum VP

Maximum Volition Points scale by Focus, Veil Affinity and character level.

- **High Focus:** `max(1, 6 + Veil Affinity) × Level`
- **Medium Focus:** `max(1, 4 + Veil Affinity) × Level`
- **Low Focus:** `max(1, 2 + Veil Affinity) × Level`

Focus determines how much growing Veil integration remains freely available for deliberate allocation. Veil Affinity determines the character's capacity to contain and channel that freely available power; level deepens overall Veil integration.

## VP states

VP has three states:

- **Available VP** — may be spent or committed normally.
- **Committed VP** — tied to an ongoing sustained effect and unavailable elsewhere while that effect remains active.
- **Spent VP** — exhausted until recovered.

Releasing a sustained or committed effect has no action cost unless the effect explicitly says otherwise. Its Committed VP immediately returns to Available VP because it was never Spent.

## Zero-action timing

A rule allowing an effect to be released, rerouted, reassigned, retuned, rechanneled or otherwise changed with **no action cost** removes only the action cost.

It does not grant interrupt timing.

Unless a feature explicitly provides a Reaction, trigger or other out-of-turn timing permission, such a change occurs during the character's own turn at an otherwise legal decision point.

## No-refresh rule

Changing the allocation, target, expression, anchor, form or Vessel of an existing effect does not refresh or recreate limited output attached to it.

This includes:

- activation effects;
- Pulse uses;
- once-per-turn or once-per-round riders;
- once-per-window resources;
- limited healing, damage, control or Vigor restoration;
- other authored cooldown, charge or finite-use benefits.

A feature must explicitly state that moving or changing the effect refreshes such output if that is intended.

## Recovery

A character has:

```
VP Recovery = 25% of Max VP, rounded to the nearest whole VP
```

Minimum recovery is 1 VP when Max VP is above 0.

For every **completed 6-hour interval** of elapsed in-world time, restore one VP Recovery amount from Spent VP, up to Max VP. Partial intervals grant no recovery.

Examples:

- Max VP 30 → VP Recovery 8.
- 2 hours elapsed → 0 VP.
- 6 hours elapsed → 8 VP.
- 12 hours elapsed → 16 VP.
- 24 hours elapsed → recover up to the full pool.
