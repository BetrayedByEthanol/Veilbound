# Woodland Rapport v0 — Life / Nature

> **Executable Tier-1–3 proposal, numerical balance PROVISIONAL.** Woodland Rapport manifests **species-appropriate external peaceful cues** associated with the caster, giving **one ordinary, nonsapient animal** an opportunity to assess the caster as less immediately threatening. It can support **one +2 bonus to the caster's first qualifying noncombat approach check**, but **does not change the animal's protected inner state, force disposition, compel cooperation, communicate semantic information, tame the animal or create an Animal Companion**. **Used Tier scales eligible animal mass; Veil Control scales duration only.** Publication never grants automatic access to the technique.

## Used-Tier selection and mass threshold

**Select `T` at declaration**, not after assessing the target's reaction. Universal normal Tier ceiling and this technique's authored cap **both** apply. Level 3 may normally select T1 or T2; Level 5 may select T1/T2/T3. Level 17's general Tier-9 ceiling does not authorize a T9 Woodland Rapport. **T is never raised by high VC, a higher Pillar Accord or increased caster Level alone.** Even for larger animals, neither mass nor Used Tier grants permission to control a creature.

| Used T | Normal access level | Target mass ≤ | Full ordinary Spent VP | Examples, subject to actual body mass and disposition |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | L1+ | **25 kg** | **2** | Ordinary mouse, fox, owl, cat, small dog |
| 2 | L3+ | **125 kg** | **4** | Larger dog, ordinary wolf, some deer |
| 3 | L5+ | **625 kg** | **6** | Horse, many cattle, other suitably calm large animals |
| 4–9 | **Not authored** | — | — | A higher tier ceiling alone grants no access |

The examples are **not species-wide automatic classifications**: use the actual animal/stat-block body mass if authored, otherwise a **reasonable declared GM estimate before AP is spent**. No hidden mass roll or retroactive post-payment classification. A horse **over** 625 kg is ineligible even at T3. An actively dangerous 15 kg animal is not eligible merely because it is small.

**Qualifying disposition is checked separately from mass.** The selected animal must be ordinary, nonsapient, and at the moment of declaration/application **neither currently attacking/hostile nor panicking nor actively defending offspring, territory, a handler or itself from a present threat**. It may be cautious, wary, indifferent, merely unfamiliar or already receptive. Passive natural predation, fear or distrust does not automatically become a magical hostility condition, but an animal actually committed to hostile action is ineligible; the GM uses established observable circumstances. A guarded, aggressive trained dog following active attack orders is not neutralized. The GM must not treat unknown internal motives as a surprise retroactive cost; when necessary, communicate observable eligibility before legal declaration.

**No creature-targeting loophole:** An intelligent, sapient or extraordinary magical beast with an independently protected mental state is **not** rendered an ordinary animal by being below the mass threshold. A magical *ward on an otherwise eligible ordinary animal* is different: resolve that explicit protection normally. This spell authors **no special resisted Intrusion mode** for excluded creatures and no biological transformation/repair.

## External peaceful signals, animal agency and checks

### A. Peaceful Rapport — a Continuous external opportunity

A successful cast establishes a modest, **caster-localized external signal** that a member of the selected species can perceive through ordinary senses. For a social species this might be a harmless familiar tone or unobtrusive reassuring stance; for other eligible species a low-intensity ordinary sound, plausible visible body-language cue or ordinary scent-like signal may suffice. **Choose at least one signal type the animal can actually detect** through a real route at application; sensory limitations are not bypassed. The signal is not a translated sentence, counterfeit prey alarm, false visual identity, automatic species password, incapacitating odor, loud distraction, or sensory suppression.

This creates a **favorable opportunity**, not a set animal reaction. The animal may approach, watch, ignore, back away or flee. It retains cognition, instincts, real memories, social bonds, fear, trained responses and awareness of dangers. A fox can refuse food; an owl can simply leave; a horse can decline handling/mounting; a protective parent does not become compliant. **No fixed "Hostile→Neutral", "Neutral→Friendly" or "Friendly→Obedient" state adjustment is applied.** No generic +2 to Presence, Hunting, Tracking, Riding, Stealth, defenses or subsequent social checks is granted.

The effect has a **Continuous expression cadence**: while it is perceptible and valid, an animal may keep sensing the reassuring cues. This does **not** yield a repeat +2, automatic advantage every minute or a free new relationship check. The Persistent external phenomenon is **not Committed-VP Reserve**, doesn't grant concentration-like free control, and is not an Intrusion into the animal merely because a living creature later interprets a cue. Nothing forces the animal to stop being hostile **after** it has become hostile.

### B. Initial Trust — one +2 on a genuine first contact check

The first **actual eligible, meaningfully uncertain noncombat check made by the caster** for **peaceful initial contact with the selected animal** while Rapport remains active gets **one +2 explicit magical modifier** to its normal `d20 + relevant Ability + applicable narrow Training + circumstances` total, or to one properly invoked **single-task passive `10 + ...` comparison**. Determine the task and whether it qualifies **before** rolling/comparing; resolve the check and permanently consume this **one pending +2** on **either success or failure**. A task check declared before expiration but resolved afterward gets **no bonus**. **The Continuous peaceful cues remain** after bonus consumption until an ordinary effect ending event occurs.

Valid task examples: approach a wary fox with nonthreatening body language; persuade a calm owl to tolerate close observation; offer food to an unfamiliar cat; induce a calm horse to permit a first gentle touch. The player specifies the approach; GM determines whether the objective is plausible and requires a roll. Depending on approach, Presence, Agility, Awareness or another genuinely relevant Ability may be used. A **genuine narrow** Hunting, Riding, specialized Falconry, animal-care or similar learned discipline can apply **only if** that actual trained practice is directly relevant. **No universal "Animal Handling" skill is created.**

**Invalid bonus uses:** weapon attack, Guard/Dodge, initiating a grapple, calming an actually hostile animal under this ineligible profile, broad animal taming, riding/handling proficiency, species identification, tracking another animal, extracting information, requesting a command, ordinary passive perception, a second check in the same encounter, or social control. An already friendly pet greeting its owner is **routine** and should not be assigned a manufactured roll just to consume or benefit from the +2.

**No-roll peaceful contact:** If a genuinely successful or complete first peaceful contact occurs **without a check**, the pending +2 **is consumed unused**, while Rapport continues. If the caster only watches from afar and no contact task has concluded, the pending +2 remains until an eligible check, expiration or termination. **A failed qualifying check cannot be skipped to save the +2 for another attempt after its outcome is known**; the first one consumes it.

**Rapport is not animal communication.** The caster cannot transmit semantics ("scout the road", "find the child", "tell me what happened", "attack", "come home"), understand memories, issue commands, gain a new language, read thoughts, gain an animal companion, guarantee mounted consent or accelerate long-term training. Animals might respond to ordinary spoken commands **already genuinely learned** or natural instincts, but the spell neither teaches them a new command nor increases the separate ordinary command check. That remains a job for distinct mundane training or future Tier-2 **Instinctive Communion**.

### C. No rapid magical taming / reroll farming

Positive, genuine, repeated experiences may lead to an **ordinary** relationship under the GM's established fiction; Woodland Rapport itself records **no lasting loyalty counter, permanent friendliness, training progress or compulsory emotional aftereffect**. Once the first qualifying check or completed no-roll contact consumes Initial Trust, ending or recasting the spell **cannot retroactively undo failure** or entitle the caster to another check against **unchanged circumstances**. New food, genuine safety, time, a different nonthreatening approach, the animal voluntarily returning or a meaningful changed context may justify a later *ordinary* opportunity, at normal rules and the expense of a wholly fresh cast if magic is used. No arbitrary cooldown is added, and ordinary checks are not prohibited merely because a previous check occurred; the GM must simply follow fiction-first check eligibility. One cannot force an infinite sequence of rolled "approach" checks by paying VP.

## Target acquisition and persistence

**Initial 3D range metric — technique local:** Measure 1.5 m grid cells using fixed caster/animal reference occupied cells; for larger creatures use each's predesignated reference cell, or lowest-x then lowest-y then lowest-z occupied cell if none is recorded **before declaration**. With absolute axis offsets `dx,dy,dz` sorted as `a ≥ b ≥ c`, compute `d = a + 0.5b + 0.5c`. Preserve fractional halves and require **`d ≤ 12`**; e.g. `(10,4,0)=12` in range, `(11,4,0)=13` out of range, `(12,1,0)=12.5` out of range. No circular curved route, lowest-distance edge switching, Euclidean shortcut or range-rounding down.

**Acquisition at declaration and valid application:** The caster must **directly see** an exposed part of the actual animal and have **one straight unobstructed magical delivery segment through open air** to it. **Closed transparent glass blocks magical targeting despite visibility**. An open doorway or window may qualify if the real straight segment passes. A reported position, scent trace, creature beyond a wall, indirect mirror or distant animal sound is not a targeting substitute. Check the actual one animal, its qualifying mass, disposition, sensory capacity, normal tier/caster access, AP, VP, and all restrictions again after normal responses.

**Post-application sensory reach:** The source is attached to the caster's living location rather than to a permanent mark inside the animal. Each time the animal would rely on peaceful cues or a qualifying check would resolve, verify **`d ≤ 12`** and at least one channel through which **that animal actually perceives those external cues**. The caster does **not** need to see the animal or maintain the original straight magical delivery segment after successful application; an open or different route for natural sound/scent/visual perception may be appropriate. **A full loss of every suitable sensory route ends the spell**, as does exceeding 12 square-equivalents, rather than suspending it or preserving the unused +2 off-screen. A wall may block vision but admit sound ordinarily; a sealed barrier may prevent all actually useful signals; no telepathy or magical corner-bending targeting is granted. No hidden animal location is revealed to the caster by continued effect.

**Movement:** Rapport does not tether, restrain, attract, locate, call, or compel the animal to approach; any movement by the caster or animal uses normal rules. No new movement segment, MP discount, no OA prevention, Guard/Dodge penalty, surprise cancellation or line-of-fire alteration results from these cues.

## Duration, termination and transition between time modes

At successful application, **snapshot raw caster VC** solely to determine `D` in whole minutes:
```text
D_minutes = min(30, 10 + 5 × max(0, caster VC))
D_seconds = 60 × D_minutes
Round-window N = 10 × D_minutes
```
No post-activation VC, Level, Accord or selected Used Tier change extends the current timer. Negative VC yields minimum 10 minutes; VC +4 or higher reaches capped 30 minutes.

**Outside initiative:** Start a real elapsed `D_seconds` timer. **If cast during initiative round R**, expire **at Round Begin R+N**, before any new round's refresh or check there. At VC0 `N=100`, at VC+2 `N=200`, at VC+4 `N=300`. These are **technique-specific anchors**, not a universal "all rounds last six seconds" rule. Checking the first qualifying interaction before that boundary grants +2 once; after the boundary grants none.

**Changing timekeeping modes** uses the established conservative technique-local model, without restarting the clock:
- **Exploration → initiative:** If `s` whole seconds remain, `q=floor(s/6)`; `q=0` means expire at transition, otherwise expiry is **Round Begin R+q** in entering round R. Fractional seconds are discarded, never rounded upward.
- **Initiative → exploration:** If leaving in round C with expiry Round Begin E, `n=max(0,E−C)`; remaining **`s=6×max(0,n−1)`** whole seconds. If zero, expire immediately. Current partial round grants no extra time. Repeated transitions retain only remaining time.

The **entire Rapport** ends at the earliest of: duration expiration; caster death; actual animal death; animal exiting 12-square sensory reach; total loss of animal-perceptible signal channel; animal becoming actively hostile/panicked or immediately threatened; caster attacking, aggressively threatening or otherwise behaving incompatibly with the purported peaceful signals; explicit magical dispel/suppression that ends the manifestation; or voluntary **own-turn zero-action** dismissal. Unconsciousness of the caster **alone** does not terminate this already-established nonreserved signal while the source remains a living caster and the animal can perceive it; a separate rule that actually ends Manifestations still applies. No time is paused while the animal is uninterested or the caster is busy.

The **Initial Trust +2** has an **additional earlier one-use end**: first genuinely qualifying caster check or completed first peaceful contact without a check. That earlier consumption **does not end ongoing Peaceful Rapport**. An animal becoming suddenly aggressive after the first check ends the *whole* signal even though the check benefit was spent. No refund of any previously Spent VP on ending, running out of duration, failing to persuade the animal, or voluntary dismissal.

## Action, resource and application procedure

1. **Declare a legal cast:** Verify caster actually has Woodland Rapport technique access; select **`T=1,2,3` within both current universal tier ceiling and authored maximum 3**, one visible eligible ordinary animal with estimated known mass **≤ `M(T)`** (inclusive of the exact tier cap), and one available appropriate sensory route. Check nonhostile/nonpanicked state, **≤12 3D square-equivalents**, straight magical open-air sight/path, component freedom, current action capacity, one-active-per-caster/animal caps, and **full locked ordinary `2T` Spent VP** after only eligible discounts. **TAL-M03 Frugal Technique**, at L3+ when genuinely available and unused on own turn, may lower the **single ordinary `2T` Spent component by 1 (minimum 1)** **once per legally declared cast**; mark its once-per-turn use at legal declaration, even if later interrupted. It never lowers a separate Open Invocation surcharge or grants an extra cast. Illegal declaration: no AP/VP/use/responses.
2. **High Priest Open Invocation if applicable:** When this chosen-Life technique is **unprepared** and genuinely eligible for Open Invocation, add the **separate +1 Spent VP Invocation Surcharge**, requiring the **entire total Available at declaration/application**; at L1–4 require a **Full Action consuming all Turn AP**, at L5+ use the ordinary Focus-priced Projection Action. Prepared Prayer / L3 Open Prayer (legally filled inside existing prepared capacity) uses **normal** cost and action, no surcharge. Do not apply TAL-M03's ordinary component reduction to the Invocation surcharge.
3. **Commit action and allow responses:** Spend one normal Projection Action (High **20 AP**, Medium **24**, Low **30**) or the properly authorized Open Invocation **Full Action**. Follow native response and interruption order. No Reaction cast, free attack, free approach movement, or auto-rolled social check arises.
4. **Recheck and pay at valid magical application:** Recheck caster capability and actual selected animal access, tier/mass/state, sight/path/range/sensory eligibility, one-active caps and locked effective Available VP **after responses**. If illegal/interrupted/underfunded before magical application, **AP are lost but 0 VP Spent**, and any legally consumed TAL-M03 use remains consumed. On a **valid magical application**, move **the locked ordinary `2T` (or eligible discounted amount)** and any independent Invocation surcharge **Available→Spent**, even if an independently authored ward/suppression then prevents the Manifestation. If no protection blocks it, establish **one caster-linked continuous external cue signal**, one finite +2 pending Initial Trust, and snapshot expiry from caster VC. No Direct Veil contest, no automatic animal acceptance, and no Committed VP.
5. **Animal behavior and first check:** The animal chooses/responds according to circumstances, not caster command. For one genuine initial peaceful-contact check actually undertaken by the caster while signal still perceivable, apply **+2 once before resolving**, whether via d20 or one specific passive comparison, then mark benefit used regardless of success. Completing the contact with no check consumes the pending bonus unused. The signal remains until a whole-Rapport ending condition; other checks/mundane interactions do not reapply +2.
6. **End/recaste:** Ending the signal never refunds its Spent VP or creates a second opportunity. Recasting must be a **new legal cast** with fresh AP and full ordinary VP (plus any surcharge) after the old instance ends, and must not manufacture another valid check under unchanged circumstances. No tier-4 upcast, new target reassignment, per-turn +2 Pulse, repeated attempt grant, direct creature Intrusion, animal instructions or Animal Companion effect.

## Explicit Focus, composition and boundary restrictions

- **High Priest:** Prepared Prayer at Tier 1–3 when normally eligible; unprepared **Open Invocation** as separately authorized. For example, a Level-1 VA0 High Priest can pay **2 Spent VP** prepared, **3 Spent VP** unprepared with the L1 Full Action; a Level-3 Priest can normally select **Tier 2, ordinary 4 Spent** plus 1 Invocation surcharge if unprepared. Normal Miracle Petition rules are **separate**; at L3 a Tier-3 petition is **not** an ordinary Tier-3 cast automatically authorized by this profile.
- **Medium Priest:** Ordinary prepared Life/Nature prayer; **not** an Active Consecration, and no Reassign/Intercession, because this is an external, nonreserved environmental signal rather than maintained beneficiary blessing.
- **Low Priest:** Ordinary prepared Active Prayer; **not** an Inward maintained `Self` Devotional Rite, Chosen Devotion, extra Rite slot or L3 Exaltation. It can be cast when VP are Available even while an unrelated Rite occupies the single L1–3 Active Rite slot, but pays its full actual cost and must not override Rite rules.
- **Scholar:** Individual known technique can select legally authored T1–3; **no Secondary/Rider**, no exact `Single Target`, `Self`, `Rooted Plant` match, and no currently legal High Scholar composite pairing. **No automatic Low Scholar Active Embodiment or Medium Scholar Inward Boost**.
- **Gifted / Anointed:** Require independently real Aspect-link/bond, actual access and any valid special package; **this technique does not grant** a link, Signature, Anchor, Conduit, Aspect Amplification, persistent emotion benefit, animal bond or new power automatically.
- **Nature boundary:** External signal and first check, not Mind manipulation, coercive Intrusion or Secrets illusion; no direct brain/memory/emotion alteration. **Instinctive Communion (T2 candidate)** would provide bounded semantic communication, but **is not granted** by a T2 or T3 cast of Woodland Rapport. **Animal Companion** remains deferred; no summoning, mass-created creature, magical taming, remote scouting or autonomous servant.
- **Contribution/cadence:** Primary only. Continuous Peaceful Rapport + exactly one pending check modifier; no Secondary, Rider contribution, repeating Rider, per-turn Pulse, free action trigger, duplicate bonus, generic +2 to every social skill, or higher-tier VC/mass cross-scaling.
