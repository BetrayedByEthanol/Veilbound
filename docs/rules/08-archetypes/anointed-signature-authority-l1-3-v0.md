# Anointed — L1–3 Signature, Destiny and Lesser Authority v0

> **Canonical limited L1–3 rules (two pilot Aspects only)**. This supplements [Anointed Foundation](anointed.md) without rewriting future L4+ features. The associated [isolated rules referee](../../../scripts/b05_anointed_authority_l1_l3_v0.py) checks resource, packet, receipt and Focus gates; it is **not the native G0/G4/G5 event engine**, a complete character build, a full encounter certification, or automatically authored support for every Aspect. Where these rules have no native interaction yet, the caller must first resolve the existing authoritative action/defense/Intrusion/Regeneration procedure. **No new B06 milestone.**

## Bond, level and Destiny

These L1–3 rules require a **non-Dormant Anointed bond** to exactly one of **Life / Restoration** or **Fire / Conflagration**. A Conflicted Anointed may act normally; an enduring Forsaken choice makes the bond Dormant under [Anointed Foundation](anointed.md#mission-states). Dormancy suspends all Signatures, Authority, Destiny expenditure/recovery and active Investments/Embodiments; it does not remove ordinary HP, Vigor or separately acquired abilities. Reawakening is adjudicated under the existing foundation, not by spending Destiny.

For each level **1, 2 and 3**, **Maximum Destiny = 2**. The Anointed begins with 2 Destiny. Recover **1 Destiny per completed six-hour interval** of in-world elapsed time, capped at 2, with partial interval time carried forward toward the next interval. Neither combat rounds nor restarts of an encounter grant extra interval recovery. This is the L1–3 concrete instance of the existing provisional `2 + floor(Level / 5)` and 25%/six-hour formula. **Higher-level numeric progression, High L4 +1 reserve and higher grades remain provisional.**

The GM may award **+1 Destiny, capped at maximum**, for a **Meaningful Mission Event**: a consequential story beat legitimately expressing, challenging or deepening the Anointed's lifelong Mission, such as the party successfully curing a village. A Mission does **not** advance toward completion; this is not a numbered milestone, mandatory quest objective, morality/obedience test or objective XP schedule. An ordinary healing application, attack or other repeated small action earns no automatic award. One recognized story event is awarded at most once, even when described repeatedly. The GM judges significance; this rule does not infer state changes between Aligned, Conflicted and Forsaken.

## Lesser Authority — L1 through L3

An Anointed with the active bond always knows their **0-VP baseline Signature** and may apply the **one authored Lesser Authority** for that Aspect to **one otherwise eligible Signature application** by spending **1 Destiny**. The Signature does not need Destiny to work. Each authorized attempt chooses either baseline or Lesser, not both; higher grades **Greater (proposed L5 / 2 Destiny)** and **Supreme (proposed L10 / 3 Destiny)** are **not available under this L1–3 entry**. No same-Authority grade stacking, double Lesser, additional target or action, universal metamagic, free tier increase, extra technique slot or extra effect by repeatedly resolving the same action/Step receipt. Future High L5 Sovereign Projection cannot combine two grades of this same Authority; its existing text is not overwritten here.

**Timing and payments:** Check bond, Focus, real action/step permission, target, range/path, required VP/active reservation, and sufficient Destiny when declaring. Declare whether Lesser is used **before** attack/response resolution. Commit the actual AP of a paid action under the existing action sequence. Spend 1 Destiny **on the legal application attempt**, including a missed, dodged, guarded or resisted eligible attempt. If the effect is interrupted or becomes illegal **before** the eligible application, no Destiny is spent (committed AP is subject to ordinary no-refund rules). Do not spend Destiny for an ineligible no-op, such as healing a full-HP patient. A recurring/persistent expression never carries a prepaid blanket Authority: each enhanced eligible pulse or hit needs its own legal attempt and Destiny receipt. The same receipt cannot be applied or paid twice. VP payments for independent techniques follow their exact source procedures and **are separate from Destiny**.

## Common Focus expression boundaries

| Focus | L1–3 Signature delivery | Action/cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **High — Project** | Deliberately outward, one target/recipient. | **One Projection Action, 20 AP, 0 VP** per attempt; no bonus physical attack or extra projectile. High L2 **Expanded Projection** increases the pilot Signature's legal maximum range from **3 to 4 squares**, with ordinary visibility/path, defenses and +0 authored Range Difficulty where applicable. This range increase applies **only** to the Signature, never ordinary Conjure Flame or Basic Heal. |
| **Medium — Invest** | One actually eligible **single Vessel**; not a party, army, map area or other aggregation. | Establish/change one Vessel for **one General Tempo Action, 24 AP, 0 VP**; release free. Restoration invests **one consenting living patient** (self permitted). Conflagration invests **one currently wielded weapon**. Investment remaining in existence is not permission to project remotely or trigger an unbounded aura. |
| **Low — Embody** | Self-centred; for Conflagration, operates with **one actually wielded weapon**; for Restoration, self only. | 0 VP; no bonus Physical Action, free weapon attack or unbounded per-hit Rider. Suppress/resume at own turn's start as permitted by the foundation. |

Medium L2 **Channel Through Vessel** grants no extra action or unlimited remote use; an ordinary technique must independently authorize the profile at the moment it is used. Low L2 **Living Instrument** permits physical integration as specified below, **without creating another Signature occurrence**. This v0 gives no new reactive use, Dodge/Guard output, unrelated weapon profile or automatic per-strike damage.

### Returning Step — Life / Restoration

**Signature:** Convert the living recipient's own existing Vigor into immediate HP at **1 Vigor per 1 HP**, using no Spent or Committed VP. This is a distinct restorative application, not an extra natural Regeneration Step. Only a **living recipient at positive HP and below Max HP** qualifies. A Dying or dead recipient never qualifies. No free wound Repair Progress, stabilization, cancellation of a Dying loss, resuscitation, removal of a condition, Vigor replenishment, maximum-HP increase or extra regeneration throughput. Normal **beneficial bodily Intrusion** applies: genuinely unopposed benefit needs no contest; a current/prior explicit refusal triggers the **+8 Intrusion Barrier** and ordinary Direct Veil resolution. The target must be visible and reached by an unobstructed magical path.

- **High:** one target, including self, within **0–3 squares** (0–4 at L2+). On the successful application, restore up to **2 HP**, spending equal **recipient Vigor**. Pay **20 AP** per attempted Projection.
- **Medium:** one consenting living creature invested as the Vessel; when that exact recipient begins their **existing own-turn Regeneration Step** and is visible with legal path within **0–3 squares**, before their normal regeneration allocation, restore up to **1 HP** for 1 recipient Vigor. **One pulse per genuine recipient Regeneration Step**; no extra Step or activation AP. An inactive or out-of-range Vessel cannot produce a legal pulse. If the recipient has a still-effective refusal of the restorative effect, the legal pulse requires the ordinary **+8 Intrusion Barrier** contest; a resisted application that fails that contest restores **0 HP** but still spends any declared Lesser Destiny on that legal attempt.
- **Low:** while embodied, at the beginning of the Anointed's **existing own-turn Regeneration Step**, restore up to **1 HP** to self for 1 self Vigor. **One pulse per own Step**, not once per weapon swing, movement or physical action; 0 activation AP.

**Actual HP restored:** `H = min(Focus cap, missing HP, recipient Available Vigor)`. Spend exactly `H` recipient Vigor and restore exactly `H` HP, with no overheal or negative Vigor. If `H=0`, the baseline does not restore anything. The recipient's later ordinary regeneration may still consume additional Vigor at their normal throughput. Low L2's Living Instrument supplies **no second restoration pulse** or retroactive moved Step in this v0.

**Lesser Authority — Spare the Reserve (1 Destiny):** For the same legal Signature pulse, replace the normal Vigor-limited conversion with **up to the existing Focus HP cap**, no recipient Vigor expenditure: `H = min(Focus cap, missing HP)`. A recipient with **0 Vigor can still regain this positive HP** if otherwise legal; no extra HP, additional Step, wound repair or Vigor generation follows. High may project it only during its normal 20-AP Signature action; Medium and Low may pay it only at their legal once-per-Step pulse. The enhanced effect does **not** automatically carry onto Basic Heal or Guided Regeneration without the distinct L3 interaction below.

**Outside initiative:** High may perform legally timed 20-AP projections where actions are available and range/Intrusion remains legal; each normal heal still consumes the patient's Vigor, and each Vigor-free heal spends a finite Destiny. **Medium/Low require the actual existing Regeneration Step. There is no universal noncombat Step timer**; merely announcing a series of harmless actions, walking, or waiting a few seconds does not create pulses. Vigor and Destiny still use their separate **completed six-hour** recovery intervals, never accelerated by Signature use.

### Kindled Flame — Fire / Conflagration

**Signature:** One bounded nonpersistent flame application: **1 Fire HP damage** and **1 Vigor damage** as independent packets **on one actual qualifying hit**, 0 VP. Fire Resistance applies only to Fire HP; ordinary physical Armor does not automatically reduce the Fire packet or Vigor damage. There is **no implicit Veil Control output coefficient at L1–3**, no spontaneous material stockpile, persistent burning ground, environmental spread, Ignite, additional critical damage dice, light-radius or free attack. A miss deals neither packet; any positive-HP to nonpositive-HP crossing must be handed to the existing Wound/Dying resolver. No native creature outcome is implied by this document.

- **High:** one independent manifested Signature projectile within **1–3 squares** (1–4 at L2+), **one 20-AP Projection Action**. Use the same specifically authored **Throwing-based attack components and manifested-projectile ranged defense/cover/path procedure** as Conjure Flame's Thrown Projectile, including **+0 Range Difficulty** at each legal Signature square. This is **not** a free Conjure Flame cast and does not inherit its paid tier, damage, projectile attachment or VP. The attack is resolved through existing attack/response authorities, not an automatic hit.
- **Medium:** invest **one actually wielded weapon** (24 AP General setup). At most **one** actual, otherwise paid, legal Physical weapon attack **on the Anointed's own turn** may carry one Signature packet while the same weapon is still wielded and is the Invested Vessel. Investment does not create a weapon attack, grant free range or attach damage to all Reserved Weapon hits.
- **Low:** while embodied, at most **one** actual, otherwise paid, legal Physical weapon attack **on the Anointed's own turn** may carry one Signature packet through one actively wielded weapon. Not every attack in the Attack Sequence; no extra attacks, Guard, Opportunity or reactive Signature. The L2 Living Instrument doesn't multiply this cap.

**Lesser Authority — Consuming Flame (1 Destiny):** On that same **one declared legal Signature application**, add **+3 Vigor damage** to its single Vigor packet if it hits: total **1 Fire HP + 4 Vigor** before target reserve cap, at L1–3. The 1 Destiny is spent at a legal attack attempt **even if the eventual shot/weapon hit misses**. Never apply the enhancement on all subsequent hits via one payment. Any carrier's independently authored normal physical HP attack is unaffected. The prior design's `1 + floor(Level / 5)` baseline scaling is explicitly **not active at L1–3** (the value is 1 throughout); late-level adoption and the Greater/Supreme numbers remain to be evaluated separately.

## Level 3 — Lesser Authority through ordinary techniques

L3 unlocks an **expressly compatible Primary technique/profile** only. It does not grant the technique for free, create a generic Authority selection menu, waive components, target/attack legality, action costs, appropriate Intrusion, reservation duration, or normal VP. This is not a general permission to apply Authority to any spell with the same Aspect word in its name.

| Focus/Aspect | Exact eligible source technique | One Lesser Authority application |
|---|---|---|
| High / Conflagration | [Conjure Flame](../07-veil-magic/conjure-flame-v0.md) **`Thrown Projectile` Primary**, existing **[Projection]** profile | After one legal **20-AP** Projection declaration and **2×T Spent VP** (T1 or T2 at L3), add **+3 Vigor** to its **one** successful direct-hit Vigor packet; pay Destiny at legal attempt even if missed. |
| Medium / Conflagration | **`Weapon Enchantment` Primary** on **the actually wielded active Reserved Weapon that is also the Invested Vessel** | The paid 4×T **Committed VP** reservation must already exist, be valid and be tied to that weapon. Declare **one separate legal paid Physical attack** for the Lesser enhancement (+3 Vigor on its one successful hit); **no extra VP at that hit** and no new spell/attack. |
| Low / Conflagration | **`Weapon Enchantment` Primary** on an actually wielded active Reserved Weapon | Same single selected legal paid Physical attack and extra +3 Vigor on hit; existing **4×T Committed VP** from an authored normal activation, no per-hit Spent VP, no per-hit automatic enhancement. |
| High / Restoration | [Guided Regeneration](../07-veil-magic/guided-regeneration-v0.md) **`Single Target` Primary**, Fixed or Maintained mode | When the target's **existing** Regeneration Step genuinely has an eligible **+1 incremental HP** to restore, spend 1 Destiny to make **that 1 HP only** consume **0** recipient Vigor. No additional Step or +1 throughput beyond Guided's normal +1. |
| Medium / Restoration | **Guided Regeneration `Single Target` Primary**, Fixed or Maintained | Same single eligible +1 HP benefit, but it must be applied to the current Invested living recipient with existing delivery restrictions. |
| Low / Restoration | **Guided Regeneration `Self` Primary [Embodiment]**, Fixed or Maintained | Same single eligible +1 HP benefit to self only. |

For **Guided Regeneration**, the normal technique is first legally activated (2 Spent VP for fixed 60-second mode, or 2 Committed VP for maintained mode, existing expiration/channel rules, full normal Focus Projection Action on activation). This Authority does **not** pay, reserve, activate or extend it. At a later valid non-Dying Step the beneficiary retains their **ordinary player-controlled HP vs wound Repair Progress allocation** at the normal Vigor cost. The authored extra Guided throughput may restore **exactly one** additional missing HP with this one-step Authority waiver, even at 0 recipient Vigor, **only if one eligible HP actually remains to be restored and the Guided +1 throughput has not been fully suppressed**. It never converts normal wound Repair Progress into free progress or waives a Dying cost. If no additional HP is missing, **Authority cannot be paid to create a null benefit**. Each Step uses its own receipt and one new Destiny expenditure. Ordinary Guided Regeneration remains usable without Authority at full Vigor cost.

**Explicitly ineligible:** Basic Heal (its HP is already Vigor-free), Conjure Flame `Ammunition Shot`, any `Secondary` or `Rider` contribution, mismatched Vessel or dropped/sheathed weapon, expired Guided instance, other Aspects, other technique profiles, higher Authority grade, automatic Gifted/Scholar/Priest interactions, any unapproved remote manifestation, and any non-primary multi-hit recursion. These may be designed separately; they are not implied here.

## Source/replay contract and outstanding native bridge

The included Python referee [implementation](../../../scripts/b05_anointed_authority_l1_l3_v0.py) provides deterministic Destiny/mission accounting and one-application numeric packets. It **requires** its caller to supply **authentic** G0 action/AP/VP or active-reservation receipts, own-turn eligibility for Medium/Low physical carriers, legal projectile/weapon responses, beneficial Intrusion/refusal result, current active bond/Vessel, and existing G4 Regeneration Step/HP-wound allocation (including unsuppressed eligibility for the Guided +1). It does not mint or authenticate those upstream events. Recorded receipt IDs prevent duplicate use **within the same actor ledger**; they do not constitute cryptographically bound encounter evidence. Event ledger persistence across source/native actions must be separately integrated and certified. This avoids claiming a complete Anointed character, Wound/Dying bridge, out-of-combat timer, or full 48-Aspect support from isolated passing tests.

**Not part of this L1–3 v0:** Greater/Supreme Authority or L5 Sovereign Projection replacement, higher-level Signature damage scaling, medium hostile Investment, noncombat regeneration Step scheduling, High range/area options beyond the one explicit +1 Signature range, native actor action sequence integration, full Anointed L1–3 legal sheets, representative encounter balance, independent B04/B05 certification or a change to the original B06 G0–G8 milestone list.
