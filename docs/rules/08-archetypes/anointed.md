# Anointed Foundation

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34 plus the finalized Mission-state definitions from the preceding Anointed design pass. The structural foundation is resolved; Destiny numbers and Aspect-specific content remain provisional.

## Archetype identity

An Anointed is born during an exceptional **Pillar surge** and permanently invested by **one Aspect**.

The defining relationship is depth:

- one permanent bonded Aspect;
- full normal access to that Aspect's technique catalogue up to the universal tier limit;
- normal VP/action rules for ordinary bonded-Aspect techniques;
- one broad lifelong **Mission**;
- one **Birth Imprint** shaped by the circumstances that produced the surge;
- a baseline 0-VP **Signature Expression**;
- exceptional Aspect-specific depth through **Aspect Authority**;
- **Destiny** as the secondary resource for Authority.

Anointed are born, not later granted the archetype by ordinary training.

Universal **Resonance** and the Anointed's **Mission state** are separate. Resonance measures the character's internal coherence as a person; Mission state measures the relationship to the lifelong Mission imprinted by the surge. An Anointed may coherently reject the Mission and become Forsaken while retaining high Resonance, or remain Aligned while personally fragmented. Resonance does not replace Mission, Destiny, Aspect Authority, or bond Dormancy.

See [Metaphysical Foundation](../07-veil-magic/metaphysical-foundation.md).

## Surge-born origin

A Pillar surge does not require prior Pillar weakness and does not require a crisis.

The locked general pattern is:

> **successful practice → adoption → cultural reinforcement → synchronized collective Volition/commitment → Pillar surge**

Important contributors may include:

- successful repeatable practice;
- adoption by a community;
- cultural reinforcement;
- conceptual focus;
- visible successful results;
- synchronized celebration, rite, victory, harvest, unveiling, or similar event;
- explicit prayer or thanks, although formal religion is not mandatory.

The concentration that produces a surge is collective Volition coherently expressing the Pillar pattern; prayer can help coordinate that Volition but is not a separate fuel.

Priests may help cultivate these conditions but cannot simply perform a ritual and manufacture an Anointed.

## Mission

The surge imprints a broad enduring Mission aligned with the Aspect and the successful pattern behind the surge.

The originating event shapes the Mission but is not itself the Mission.

A Mission is intended to guide a lifetime and remain open to interpretation rather than act as a finite quest objective.

### Mission states

**Aligned**

The Anointed accepts and pursues the Mission. Bonded powers function normally.

**Conflicted**

The Anointed may doubt, struggle, fail, neglect, reinterpret, or temporarily act against the Mission.

Conflict does **not** automatically remove the bond.

**Forsaken**

Forsaken requires sincere, deliberate rejection of the Mission as an enduring choice.

The Aspect bond becomes **Dormant**.

Dormancy removes access to:

- Signature Expression;
- bonded-Aspect techniques provided through the Anointed bond;
- Aspect Authority;
- Destiny generation/expenditure;
- sustained Anointed effects.

The character retains:

- ordinary abilities;
- HP/Vigor;
- Physical Tempo;
- training;
- mundane equipment/combat;
- separately acquired powers from legitimate other sources.

### Reawakening

Reawakening requires sincere recommitment demonstrated through decision and action.

It is not a momentary tactical declaration and does not require a mandatory GM-authored redemption quest.

Genuine renewed pursuit of the Mission can restore the bond.

## Aspect Intuition

All Anointed possess instinctive, Aspect-specific sensitivity to patterns genuinely belonging to the bonded Aspect.

Examples:

- Restoration → injury, disease, failed healing;
- Forge → workmanship, stress, material workability;
- Fortification → weakness and structural integrity;
- Conflagration → meaningful heat/fire behavior;
- Spirit → lingering dead or disrupted passage.

Aspect Intuition is not generic detect-magic, omniscience, or a Mission compass. Normal checks still apply where uncertainty, concealment, expertise, or pressure matter.

## Birth Imprint

Birth Imprint records the particular successful cultural/practical pattern associated with the surge.

It shapes:

- Signature Expression;
- compatible/favored Authority interactions.

It does **not**:

- remove access to the rest of the bonded Aspect catalogue;
- grant outside-Aspect techniques or Authority;
- function as a hidden subclass.

Birth Imprint must remain broad enough to support an adventuring career rather than one hyper-specific historic act.

## Signature Expression

Every Anointed has a baseline Signature Expression.

Shared principles:

- manifestation of the bonded Aspect;
- shaped by Birth Imprint and Mission;
- **0 VP** baseline;
- always known/available while the bond is active;
- deliberately modest enough to coexist with normal techniques and ordinary actions;
- uses Aspect/Birth-Imprint-specific scaling rather than one universal legacy damage/healing table.

Focus changes how the Signature is expressed.

## Aspect Authority

Aspect Authority is the Anointed's defining depth mechanic.

Authority:

- is Aspect-specific;
- is not generic metamagic;
- is not a technique-tier increase;
- is not a universal menu of damage/range/area/save modifiers;
- is authored for compatible Signatures and bonded-Aspect techniques;
- normally costs **Destiny**, separately from ordinary VP.

## Destiny

Current numerical foundation remains provisional.

Baseline under calibration:

```
Maximum Destiny = 2 + floor(Level / 5)
```

- levels 1–4: 2;
- 5–9: 3;
- 10–14: 4;
- 15–19: 5;
- level 20: 6.

Provisional recovery:

- 25% Maximum Destiny per completed 6-hour elapsed interval;
- nearest whole;
- minimum 1.

Meaningful Mission Fulfillment may additionally restore Destiny or grant a temporary Destiny benefit.

Mission rewards are not the sole recovery source.

## High Anointed — Projected Authority

**Core question:** *How can I project the full power of my Aspect to further the Mission that created me?*

### Level 1 — Living Signature + Birth Imprint + Aspect Authority

Establish:

- bonded Aspect;
- Mission;
- Birth Imprint;
- High Signature Expression;
- level-appropriate Authority access.

High Signature is tagged **[Signature] [Projection]**.

Normally one compatible Authority may be added by paying its Destiny cost.

### Level 2 — Expanded Projection

The Signature gains an authored outward projection axis, such as:

- reach;
- area/extent;
- multiple compatible subjects;
- remote valid manifestation point;
- broader projected field/presence.

Expanded Projection is authored by the Aspect/Signature package.

It is not a generic menu, does not raise tier, does not automatically add raw potency, and does not grant free Authority.

### Level 3 — Manifest Authority

Authority may now be invoked through compatible bonded-Aspect techniques explicitly tagged **[Projection]**.

- normal technique pays normal VP;
- Authority pays Destiny separately;
- compatibility must be authored.

### Level 4 — Deepened Destiny + Ability Increase

Gain +1 Ability.

Current High-specific proposal is **+1 Maximum Destiny** beyond baseline.

That extra reserve remains provisional.

### Level 5 — Sovereign Projection

Once per turn, one compatible `[Projection]` Signature or bonded-Aspect technique may carry **two compatible Authority Expressions**.

Pay full Destiny cost for both.

No tier increase, discount, extra action, or second technique is created.

## Medium Anointed — Aspect Investment

**Core question:** *Where should I channel my Aspect right now to further my Mission?*

Medium Anointed concentrates the one permanent Aspect into one coherent **Vessel**.

### Valid Vessel

Examples may include:

- self;
- one willing creature;
- one hostile creature when delivery permits;
- one weapon, armor piece, tool, medicine, or object;
- one material body;
- one bounded location/structure;
- one coherent craft, treatment, construction, ritual, or undertaking.

A Vessel is **not** an arbitrary aggregation or abstract objective.

A settlement, army, population, forest, battlefield, kingdom, war, campaign, or “win the war” is not one Vessel unless an explicit effect says otherwise.

### Persistence and remote boundary

Investment may persist when the Vessel leaves immediate reach if the Aspect package permits.

Persistence does **not** create unlimited remote casting.

Normal range, line-of-effect, perception, contact, targeting, and delivery rules remain unless the individual effect explicitly overrides them.

### Establishing Investment

Current baseline:

- establish/change Vessel: **1 Attack Unit**;
- release: free;
- establishing Investment: **0 VP**;
- normally one primary Invested Vessel.

Hostile investment requires an appropriate delivery condition rather than declaration alone.

Exact hostile-resolution procedure remains calibration work.

### Level 1 — Aspect Investment + Invested Signature + Authority

Birth Imprint shapes an **Invested Signature Expression** expressed through, within, or upon the Vessel.

### Level 2 — Channel Through Vessel

Compatible Signature Expressions and bonded techniques may resolve on, through, or using the Vessel as the point of interaction when the Aspect/technique supports it.

This grants no extra action by itself.

### Level 3 — Deep Channeling

Compatible Authority may be invoked through ordinary bonded-Aspect techniques applied to/through/by means of the Vessel.

Normal technique = VP.

Authority = Destiny.

### Level 4 — Swift Rechanneling + Ability Increase

Gain +1 Ability.

Current retained structure:

> once per turn, change Invested Vessel without paying the normal 1 Attack Unit.

Eligibility/range/delivery still apply.

The exact cadence remains a balance point.

### Level 5 — Total Investment

When the Birth-Imprint Signature and a compatible ordinary bonded-Aspect technique are concentrated into the **same Vessel**, they may unlock one authored **Total Investment Synergy**.

Rules:

- still one primary Vessel;
- both effects must validly interact with it;
- normal technique pays all normal VP/action/duration/targeting costs;
- one synergy at a time unless explicitly expanded;
- synergy is authored by Aspect/Birth-Imprint package;
- no automatic extra action, full extra damage/healing instance, or extra targets.

## Low Anointed — Embodied Aspect

**Core question:** *How do I embody my Aspect through myself and my actions to further my Mission?*

Low Anointed expresses the permanent Aspect as a persistent part of body, carried/worn equipment, and ordinary physical action.

### Level 1 — Embodied Signature + Birth Imprint + Authority

The Embodied Signature is:

- 0 VP;
- self-centered;
- persistent while manifested;
- shaped by Birth Imprint;
- may include worn/directly carried equipment where appropriate;
- bounded by the universal cadence rules.

It may be suppressed or resumed at the start of the Anointed's turn without spending an action.

Full technique-scale damage/healing/Vigor restoration cannot trigger freely on every Attack Unit.

### Level 2 — Living Instrument

The Aspect may integrate with eligible ordinary physical activity, such as:

- Attack;
- Guard;
- Dodge;
- movement;
- grapple/shove;
- interception;
- tool use.

This does not create extra actions.

Worn/wielded equipment may be part of the embodiment where appropriate.

When an object is no longer worn, wielded, or directly carried, the Low-specific embodiment normally ends.

### Level 3 — Incarnate Authority

Compatible Authority may be invoked through:

- Embodied Signature;
- body;
- worn equipment;
- actively wielded equipment;
- compatible self-targeting or `[Embodiment]` bonded techniques.

Normal technique VP and Authority Destiny remain separate.

### Level 4 — Seamless Invocation + Ability Increase

Gain +1 Ability.

Current retained structure:

> once per turn, when spending an Attack Unit on an eligible ordinary physical action, activate one compatible 1-Attack-Unit `[Embodiment]` technique as part of that same Attack Unit, paying normal VP.

Guardrails:

- technique must support `[Embodiment]`;
- no extra attack/action;
- no recursive action generation;
- no unrelated outward technique as a free rider;
- no additional full technique-scale immediate damage/healing/control/Vigor-restoration instance unless explicitly authorized.

The exact once-per-turn cadence remains subject to numerical testing.

### Level 5 — Living Aspect

The Embodied Signature gains an authored advanced persistent manifestation representing deeper incarnation of the same Aspect.

This is not a second Aspect, new prepared effect, or changed Birth Imprint.

## Three-Focus Anointed distinction

| Focus | Core expression |
|---|---|
| High | **Project** the one Aspect with exceptional Authority |
| Medium | **Invest** the one Aspect into the Vessel that matters now |
| Low | **Embody** the one Aspect through self, equipment, and ordinary action |

Mission tells the Anointed **why**.

Bonded Aspect tells the Anointed **what** they command.

Focus tells the Anointed **how** they express it.

Destiny pays for exceptional Authority.
