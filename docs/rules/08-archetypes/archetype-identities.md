# Archetype Identities

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34. This document describes the shared identity of each Archetype across Focus. Exact repertoire counts, some secondary-resource values, and several level-specific numerical parameters remain calibration work.

Veilbound has four base Archetypes:

- **Priest**
- **Scholar**
- **Gifted**
- **Anointed**

Focus is a separate axis describing **how** the character expresses Veil power. Archetype describes the character's **relationship to and access route into** that power.

Approximate setting rarity:

| Archetype | Approximate prevalence |
|---|---:|
| Priest | ~1 in 100 |
| Scholar | ~1 in 1,000 |
| Gifted | ~1 in 100,000 |
| Anointed | ~1 in 1,000,000 |

These are setting-level approximations rather than encounter-generation rules.

## Priest

A Priest channels power through a deliberate relationship with **one chosen Pillar**.

Core access identity:

- one chosen Pillar;
- access to techniques across all three Aspects of that Pillar through the **Pillar Catalogue**;
- a limited set of **Prepared Prayers** selected through prayer/preparation;
- **Pillar Accord** representing sustained coherence between the Priest's relationship, intention, action, and the chosen Pillar;
- access to the Priest-wide **Miracle Petition** when positive Pillar Accord permits it.

Priests do not individually learn every technique the way Scholars do.

Their strength is **aligned access**: broad access inside one Pillar, preparation, Pillar Accord, and exceptional petition rather than cross-Pillar technical breadth.

At equal level and before Pillar Accord modification, the structural Prepared Prayer ordering is:

> **High Priest > Medium Priest > Low Priest**

The exact numerical capacities remain provisional.

### Priest motive boundary

A Priest's defining question is:

> **How do I serve my Pillar, strengthen its presence, and help it grow?**

This distinguishes Priest motivation from the Anointed Mission model.

## Scholar

A Scholar accesses the Veil through **learned technical methods** rather than prayer, emotion-linked instinct, or a permanent Anointed bond. **Scholar Pillar techniques** use real Pillar/Aspect patterns without requiring the Pillar's approval, recognition, favor, or personal relationship. **Universal Veil procedures** are a separate, Pillar/Aspect-independent category available in principle to Scholars and the other Archetypes; they use a Universal family identity instead of a Pillar/Aspect identity.

Core access identity:

- may potentially learn **Pillar techniques** from **any Pillar or Aspect**;
- learns each **Pillar technique** individually rather than receiving a whole Aspect catalogue automatically;
- **Pillar techniques retain** their actual Pillar/Aspect identity; **Universal Veil techniques/procedures instead use their Universal family identity** (e.g. Veil Inspection or Veil Translocation);
- normal techniques use their authored Veil Control scaling and costs; **Universal eligibility alone does not grant a technique, preparation slot, discount or special Archetype compatibility**;
- the Scholar does not receive a generic innate amplification simply for being a Scholar.

Scholar strength comes from:

- selection;
- combination;
- preparation;
- technical shaping;
- cross-Pillar repertoire;
- deliberately configurable breadth.

A Scholar is the Archetype most able to reshape their learned problem coverage over time. Advanced **Universal** procedure acquisition and known/procedure counts remain **unauthored**, so the Pillar technique learning model above must **not** be silently extended to them. See [Universal Veil Fundamentals](../07-veil-magic/universal-veil-fundamentals.md#universal-eligibility-is-not-free-mastery).

## Gifted

A Gifted is born with permanent links between emotion and Aspect. Gifted are natural phenomena of human Veil integration rather than people selected or deliberately created by Pillars.

Core access identity:

- exactly **eight permanent emotion-to-Aspect links**;
- one link for each core emotion:
  - Joy
  - Sadness
  - Anger
  - Fear
  - Surprise
  - Disgust
  - Trust
  - Anticipation
- each link points to one specific Aspect;
- the eight linked Aspects are fixed at birth;
- each linked Aspect comes from a different Pillar;
- no Pillar appears more than once among the eight links;
- no required distribution exists among External, Internal, and Transform;
- the Gifted has access to the full technique catalogue of each linked Aspect up to the universal character-level tier limit;
- Gifted do not use Priest-style daily preparation and do not individually learn **linked-Aspect techniques** like Scholars learn Pillar techniques individually.

**Emotion is the actual access mechanism for techniques belonging to the Gifted's eight linked Aspects.** The Gifted must genuinely evoke the relevant linked emotion, whether naturally or deliberately, to use those techniques. Outward theatrical performance is not required; memory, focus, concentration, or another genuine internal route is sufficient if it produces the emotion.

**Universal Veil Inspection and Translocation** are outside those eight Aspect catalogues. They require **no matching emotion or Active Emotion merely to use them**; eligibility does not automatically confer learning, free use, or Gifted emotion-based enhancements. See [Universal Veil Fundamentals](../07-veil-magic/universal-veil-fundamentals.md#universal-eligibility-is-not-free-mastery).

### Gifted breadth boundary

Gifted breadth is **large but fixed**.

Scholar breadth is **configurable**.

A Gifted may possess many techniques across eight Aspect catalogues, but those links cannot be redesigned later to answer new campaign needs. The Scholar must retain the stronger long-term ability to deliberately assemble cross-Pillar solutions.

## Anointed

An Anointed is **born during a Pillar surge** and permanently invested by one Aspect.

Core identity:

- one permanent Pillar/Aspect bond;
- one broad lifelong **Mission** imprinted by the circumstances and conceptual pattern of the surge;
- full normal access to the bonded Aspect's technique catalogue up to the universal character-level tier limit;
- a personal **Birth Imprint** that shapes the character's Signature Expression;
- a baseline **Signature Expression** that costs 0 VP;
- exceptional depth through authored **Aspect Authority**;
- Destiny as the secondary resource used for exceptional Authority interactions, with final numerical calibration still provisional.

The Birth Imprint individualizes the Anointed but does not remove access to the rest of the bonded Aspect catalogue and does not grant access outside that Aspect.

Aspect Authority is not generic metamagic. It represents unusually privileged or absolute expressions of the bonded Aspect and must be authored for compatible Signatures and techniques.

### Mission boundary

The Anointed's defining question is:

> **How do I use what I am to further my Mission?**

A Priest deliberately cultivates the chosen Pillar's presence.

An Anointed pursues the Mission imprinted by the surge. Doing so may strengthen the Pillar as a consequence, but strengthening the Pillar is not automatically the Anointed's primary motive.

Universal **Resonance** remains a separate character state for every Archetype. It measures internal coherence of Volition rather than Pillar loyalty, learned skill, emotional intensity, or Mission compliance.

## Universal Veil fundamentals — shared outside Pillar access

All four Archetypes are eligible for [Universal Veil Inspection and Translocation](../07-veil-magic/universal-veil-fundamentals.md). These operations are **not Pillar/Aspect techniques**, are **not a fourth Aspect or seventeenth Pillar**, and do not modify a Priest's selected Pillar, a Scholar's individual Pillar repertoire, the Gifted's fixed eight links, or the Anointed's one bonded Aspect. Eligibility alone does not grant mastery, remove technique or ritual prerequisites, or grant special interaction with Archetype-specific features. Detailed acquisition and cost rules remain deferred.

## Archetype comparison

The current archetype balance model is:

| Archetype | Defining access advantage |
|---|---|
| Scholar | **Configurable breadth** — learns selected techniques across the full Pillar/Aspect space |
| Priest | **Aligned access** — prepares from all three Aspects of one chosen Pillar and interacts with Pillar Accord/Miracle Petition |
| Gifted | **Fixed breadth + conditional intensity** — eight permanent emotion-linked Aspect catalogues |
| Anointed | **Depth** — one permanent Aspect with Signature and Aspect Authority |

None of these Archetypes is itself a fixed combat role.

Offense, defense, control, support, utility, healing, mobility, and martial emphasis emerge from the combination of:

- Archetype;
- Focus;
- Pillar/Aspect access;
- learned/prepared/fixed technique choices;
- equipment;
- character build.
