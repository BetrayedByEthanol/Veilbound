# Low Priest Exaltation Framework v0 — Universal Rules

> **LOCKED architecture; property numbers PROVISIONAL.** Applies to all 16 Pillars and 48 canonical Aspects. The two initial executable properties appear in [Life Exaltation Properties](../07-veil-magic/life-exaltation-properties-v0.md). Earth/Fire cross-Pillar tests are explicitly NON-EXECUTABLE.

## Eligibility

Exaltation is the existing **Level-3 Low Priest** feature. An Exaltation property temporarily changes what a particular **active Chosen Devotion Rite instance** can do; it is not a separate technique or the existing capitalized **Secondary contribution**.

1. The Priest must maintain the one identified active Chosen Rite, not just have its technique Prepared. A non-Chosen Rite, even a second copy of the same technique, is not an eligible host.
2. At Level 3–4, the property must belong to the **same Pillar and Aspect** as its host. Level-5 **Living Liturgy** may access a separately authored same-Pillar cross-Aspect property only after the unchanged-instance stability condition, and only where explicitly compatible.
3. The actual active profile must possess all **required host capabilities** stated in the property: for example a real Regeneration Step modifier, a finite remaining prevention budget, typed bodily protection, or a wielded-weapon Reserve. Having the same Aspect, using Committed VP or being inward is insufficient on its own.
4. A named property must have been authored, the host must remain legal, its activation window must occur, and the Priest must have enough Available VP for its actual listed Spent cost.

## Universal execution

- **One pending property at a time:** The identified Chosen Rite can have at most one armed/unresolved Exaltation effect. Choose an authored compatible property at activation, **not during prayer preparation**. Another pending property cannot overwrite, stack with, or refresh it. Resolution or expiry frees this pending-property slot, without overriding a property-specific frequency limit.
- **Action timing:** There is no generic free Reaction, Projection Action, extra Regeneration Step, or out-of-turn casting. Each property defines its window and actual AP/Reaction. A property may expressly modify an already-authorized event for **0 additional AP/Reaction**; that permission does not create another event.
- **Cost:** Pay the specifically authored **Spent VP at a legal activation** unless a property expressly states a different timing. An ineffective, expired or interrupted subsequent benefit does not refund that Spent VP. Chosen Devotion's -1 discount applies **only to the host's Committed VP**, never automatically to Exaltation Spent VP. Do not pay or refund a second host reservation.
- **No automatic spell mixing:** A property does not cast another technique, import its Primary/Secondary/Rider, add a target/action/recipient, increase used Tier, create healing/Vigor, or refresh finite output without a tightly scoped expressly authored rule.
- **Expiry:** A pending Exaltation immediately ends when its exact Chosen host ends or loses the mechanical capability required by that property. Already paid Spent VP remain spent. A different Rite never inherits it. Resolve any pending effect only under its own written trigger/expiry procedure.
- **Level 4/5:** Twofold Devotion never increases the number of Chosen instances. Living Liturgy does not bypass host requirements and grants no generic cross-Aspect property.

## Required property record

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ID / status | Stable identifier; EXECUTABLE v0 or NON-EXECUTABLE candidate |
| Pillar / Aspect | Canonical identity and optional separately permitted Level-5 cross-Aspect compatibility |
| Host capability | Actual recipient, active maintenance mode, channel and budget prerequisites |
| Secondary behavior | Distinct output, not a bare numerical boost or another spell |
| Timing | Action or integral event, declaration/VP payment, trigger and frequency |
| Ledger | Spent VP, Committed VP unaffected, Vigor or finite-budget usage |
| Expiry | Exact pending duration and host termination |
| Compatibility | Explicit restrictions, numerical balance watches and existing-talent interactions |

Mechanical patterns (deferred use, finite-budget conversion, conditional protection, alternative output) may be reused across the catalogue, but **are not themselves magical permissions**. External/Internal/Transform Aspect categories are lore boundaries, not delivery constraints.

**Content-readiness rule:** A Pillar's intended playable Level-3 Chosen Rites should each have at least one genuinely compatible practical authored property before their Level-3 build can be certified. This does **not** grant a free fallback power. Do not author one bespoke Exaltation for every technique or 48 placeholder properties.
