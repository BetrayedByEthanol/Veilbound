# Priest Foundation

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34. This file uses the revised Miracle Petition and Living Liturgy rules that supersede earlier Priest drafts.

## Archetype identity

A Priest forms a deliberate relationship with **one chosen Pillar** and may draw from **all three Aspects** of that Pillar.

Priests do not individually learn every technique as permanent technical knowledge.

During suitable prayer/preparation, the Priest selects a limited number of techniques from the chosen Pillar as **Prepared Prayers**.

At equal level before Pillar Accord modifiers:

> **High Priest Prepared Prayer capacity > Medium Priest capacity > Low Priest capacity**

**Level-1–3 v0 preparation counts** are now provisionally authored; see [B04/B05 starting repertoires](../../design/b04-b05-priest-scholar-starting-repertoires-v0.md) for complete numeric tests and open balance watches.

| Focus | Base Prepared Prayers L1 | Base L2 | Base L3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **High** | **3** | **4** | **4** |
| **Medium** | **2** | **3** | **3** |
| **Low** | **1** | **2** | **2** |

**All values are capacity, not granted spells.** Only distinct actually authored eligible chosen-Pillar techniques can fill these slots; High > Medium > Low before Accord, and each level's normal tier ceiling and technique prerequisites apply. The two automatic Universal procedures do not consume slots.

## Pillar Accord

**Pillar Accord** is a slow-moving Priest-specific measure of coherence between the Priest's genuine relationship, intentions, actions, and the principles/pattern of the chosen Pillar.

It is **not morality** and is separate from the character's universal [Resonance](../07-veil-magic/metaphysical-foundation.md#universal-resonance).

| Pillar Accord | Priest state |
|---:|---|
| +3 | Deeply aligned |
| +2 | Strongly aligned |
| +1 | Stable |
| 0 | Conflicted / uncertain |
| -1 | Dissonant |
| -2 | Severe conflict |
| -3 | Fundamentally opposed / inverted relationship |

Doubt alone is not automatically dissonance. Sustained action or a sincerely changed relationship that conflicts with the chosen Pillar can reduce Accord even when the Priest is personally honest and internally coherent.

Universal Resonance and Pillar Accord may therefore move independently.

**Starting Accord for an ordinary L1 Priest is +1 (Stable)**, while starting Universal Resonance remains **0**. A different start requires a deliberate campaign/story creation exception. **Accord change/Advance cadence remains provisional** and is not reset by preparing prayers, resting, or the passage of six hours.

### Prepared access

**Effective Prepared Prayer capacity = max(1, base Prepared Prayers for Focus and level + current Pillar Accord + any explicitly applicable base-capacity modifier).**

Thus +1/+2/+3 Accord adds +1/+2/+3 slots respectively, while −1/−2/−3 reduces capacity, **floor 1**. E.g. with ordinary starting Accord +1: High **4/5/5**, Medium **3/4/4**, Low **2/3/3** at L1/L2/L3. An actual [Expanded Liturgy Talent](../10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md#tal-p01--expanded-liturgy) adds **+1 to base capacity** (not Accord). The floor never conjures a technique where the selected Pillar lacks an authored one.

**Preparation window:** create the first list freely at character creation. Thereafter, replace/fill it through **10 uninterrupted minutes of deliberate prayer/preparation**, **at most once per six elapsed in-world hours**. Repreparation does not restore VP, reset Accord, repay Miracle Debt or re-activate a sustained technique. Empty slots may remain empty when the catalogue is sparse; do not duplicate one technique as two independent prayers or use cross-Pillar effects. An increase in capacity creates empty slots until the next permitted preparation; a decrease **immediately** requires the player to suspend excess prepared entries at the time of change (no retroactive effect-target choices), with sustained already-activated spells following their own end rules. Suspended entries may be reselected only through ordinary preparation.

**Preparation is a readiness list, not technique knowledge, casting, or recharging.** A Priest still uses only their **chosen Pillar's authored eligible techniques**, except independent Universal procedures; Open Invocation and Miracle Petition are separate permissions. See the [starting repertoire authoring tests](../../design/b04-b05-priest-scholar-starting-repertoires-v0.md#priest-starting-state-daily-prayer-capacity-and-recalculation).

### Why Accord affects capacity

Pillar Accord does **not** represent a conscious Pillar granting permission that Scholars somehow bypass.

A Scholar reproduces stable Aspect patterns through learned technical methods. A Priest instead structures practice around a sustained relationship of alignment with one Pillar. Higher Accord means the Priest's own Volition, practice, and interpretation fit that Pillar pattern more coherently, allowing more prepared expressions of that pattern to remain stably available at once.

The difference is therefore **technical reproduction versus relational coherence**, not approval versus disapproval.

### Pillar Accord Bonus

Positive Pillar Accord provides a small final-effect bonus equal to the positive Accord, maximum +3, where the individual technique supports it.

Examples include final damage, healing, barrier value, or movement distance.

This bonus is **not Veil Control** and is not routinely added to attack rolls or resistance difficulty.

## Miracle Petition

The revised Miracle Petition is an uncertain request for exceptional intervention rather than a guaranteed daily resource.

Its exact metaphysical mechanism remains open. Until that mechanism is deliberately settled, describe a successful Petition provisionally as **transient localized Pillar reinforcement** or **localized pattern amplification**. Do **not** call an ordinary successful Petition a "Pillar surge": that term is reserved for the exceptional surge phenomenon associated with Anointed origin.

### Eligibility

- Priest must have **positive Pillar Accord**.
- Requested effect must belong to the chosen Pillar.
- Normal benchmark is one technique tier above the Priest's normal maximum.
- A Petition does not permanently add the technique to Prepared Prayers.

### Petition check

Current calibration benchmark:

```
d20 + (2 × Pillar Accord) - (3 × Miracle Debt) vs 12
```

A final total of **12 or higher succeeds**.

The formula numbers are provisional; the structure is locked.

### Success

- requested Miracle resolves;
- use normal listed action and normal VP cost unless the Miracle explicitly says otherwise;
- gain **+1 Miracle Debt**.

### Failure

- attempted action is consumed;
- normally no VP is spent;
- no Miracle Debt is created;
- the same Petition cannot simply be spammed in the same unchanged situation.

### Miracle Debt

Miracle Debt is not VP debt.

Each successful Petition adds 1 Debt. Debt reduces later Petition chance and has no automatic daily reset.

When the Priest earns a **Pillar Accord Advance**, the player may normally:

1. increase Pillar Accord by +1, to maximum +3; or
2. repay 1 Miracle Debt while leaving Pillar Accord unchanged.

A Pillar Accord loss cannot be redirected into Debt repayment.

The choice between **deepening Pillar Accord** and **repaying Miracle Debt** is intentional. Pillar Accord Advances should be meaningful and infrequent enough that repeated Miracle Debt cannot be trivially cleared; exact cadence remains provisional and should preserve consequences for repeated Petition use.

### Tier 9

Once normal Tier-9 access is reached, there is no Tier 10.

A successful Petition instead allows one eligible chosen-Pillar technique from Tier 0–9 to be invoked at **0 VP**, while retaining its normal action, targeting, duration, attack/resistance, sustaining, and other rules. The successful Petition still adds +1 Miracle Debt.

## High Priest — Projection and invocation

**Core question:** *Which prayers did I prepare efficiently, and is this situation important enough to reach beyond them?*

High Priest uses three access layers:

1. **Pillar Catalogue**;
2. **Prepared Prayers**;
3. **Miracle Petition**.

### Level 1 — High Liturgy + Open Invocation

**Open Invocation** allows an unprepared technique from the chosen Pillar when:

- it is within normal accessible tier;
- it is not a Miracle-tier technique;
- normal prerequisites are met.

Open Invocation:

- requires an **authored, eligible, chosen-Pillar, normal-tier technique** not currently Prepared; all technique/profile/target requirements remain;
- pays the **full normal technique VP** plus **1 additional Spent VP Invocation Surcharge**, separate from ordinary Spent or Committed VP and **not reducible** by Frugal Technique or similar ordinary-component discounts;
- requires **Available VP for the technique and surcharge together** at legal declaration and application;
- **levels 1–4: one Full Action**, including when the underlying profile would otherwise be a shorter Projection Action; **level 5+:** the technique's normal action while still paying +1 Spent VP;
- spends the surcharge **with** ordinary technique VP at valid magical application (or at the legally authorized actual projectile launch before its roll). Illegal declaration costs nothing; an otherwise legally committed invocation invalidated/interrupted before application **loses the Full Action** but spends **0 technique VP and 0 surcharge VP**. Once applied, resistance, miss or later lack of usefulness returns neither ordinary Spent VP nor surcharge;
- cannot create a free weapon attack, prepared-ammunition batch, new action/reaction, second enchantment, out-of-Pillar access, missing spell, Miracle Petition, or Tier-2 access at L1–2. A legal ammunition integration still needs its paid physical attack and must satisfy the Full Action and its own timing.

This **+1 surcharge is a provisional L1–3 playtest value**. Example: an otherwise unprepared **2-Spent-VP** spell costs **3 Available VP** plus Full Action; a legal **4-Committed-VP** Reserve requires **5 Available VP** total, keeping **4 Committed** and **1 Spent** after application.

Long-lived inward/Embodiment configurations should generally require preparation rather than becoming casual spontaneous High-Focus setup.

### Level 2 — Expanded Liturgy

Increase **base Prepared Prayer capacity by +1** at level 2: High **3→4**, Medium **2→3**, Low **1→2**. Effective capacity continues to include Accord and applicable modifiers; new slots can be filled at the next legal prayer preparation.

### Level 3 — Open Prayer

During ordinary legal preparation, **one slot inside current effective Prepared Prayer capacity** may be left unspecified (not an extra slot). On its first later **legal use**, choose any eligible authored **chosen-Pillar** technique within normal accessible tier; it permanently fills that reserved slot until the next preparation. **No Open Invocation surcharge** is charged for this special filling, but the technique pays its normal legal VP/action/profile costs. The slot cannot repeatedly change techniques; an Accord decrease that removes the reserved slot suspends it before any later use.

### Level 4 — Expanded Liturgy + Ability Increase

Gain:

- +1 Ability;
- another Prepared Prayer capacity increase.

### Level 5 — Fluent Invocation

Open Invocation no longer automatically becomes a Full Action.

The unprepared technique uses its normal listed action while still paying the Invocation Surcharge.

### High Priest boundary

High Priest is **one-Pillar projected breadth and invocation**, not Scholar synthesis.

## Medium Priest — Consecrated distribution

**Core question:** *Who needs my Pillar's power right now?*

Medium Priest uses Prepared Prayers and Miracle Petition but does not gain Open Invocation.

Its flexibility is redistribution of prepared persistent power.

### Consecrations

A **Consecration** is a prepared sustained blessing placed on an eligible recipient or anchor defined by the technique.

Possible authored targets include self, ally, weapon, shield, armor, carried object, or a small location.

Consecrations remain actual Pillar/Aspect techniques.

### Level 1 — Consecrated Ministry

At levels 1–3, maintain maximum **1 Active Consecration**.

The Consecration:

- must be Prepared;
- uses normal activation action;
- commits normal VP;
- may be freely released.

**Reassign Consecration**

- costs one **General Tempo Action (24 AP)**;
- moves the same effect to another valid recipient;
- does not change the technique.

### Level 2 — Expanded Ministry

Increase **base Prepared Prayer capacity by +1** at level 2 (Medium **2→3**), applying current Accord and explicit modifiers. Fill any new entry on the next ordinary prayer preparation.

### Level 3 — Intercession

When another valid recipient is about to face an event to which the active Consecration applies, use a **Reaction** and spend additional VP to extend the same Consecration to that recipient for the triggering resolution only.

- original recipient keeps the Consecration;
- temporary extension ends immediately after the trigger.

Current benchmark:

```
Intercession = 2 Spent VP
```

This cost remains provisional.

### Level 4 — Dual Ministry + Ability Increase

Gain:

- +1 Ability;
- maximum **2 Active Consecrations**.

Each commits and resolves independently.

### Level 5 — Liturgical Relay

Once per turn, Reassign one Active Consecration with **no action cost**.

Further reassignments that turn still cost one **General Tempo Action (24 AP)**.

The universal zero-action/no-refresh rule applies.

### Medium Priest boundary

Medium Priest changes **who receives** a prepared persistent blessing.

It does not change that blessing into another function.

## Low Priest — Devotion and embodiment

**Core question:** *How am I embodying my Pillar today, and when is spending scarce VP worth more than simply fighting?*

Low Priest emphasizes persistent self/equipment expressions and selective Active Prayers.

### Devotional Rites

A **Devotional Rite** is a prepared sustained inward or immediately worn/wielded expression of the chosen Pillar anchored to the Priest or their own equipment.

### Level 1 — Chosen Devotion

During preparation, designate one prepared Devotional Rite as the **Chosen Devotion**.

It:

- belongs to the chosen Pillar;
- targets the Priest or immediately worn/wielded equipment;
- remains a Prepared Prayer;
- commits VP normally except commitment is reduced by **1 VP, minimum 1**;
- anchors later Exaltation features.

### Level 2 — Devotional Repertoire

Increase **base Prepared Prayer capacity by +1** at level 2 (Low **1→2**), applying current Accord and explicit modifiers. Fill any new entry on the next ordinary prayer preparation.

### Level 3 — Exaltation

Spend additional VP so the Chosen Devotion temporarily expresses a related authored secondary property of the same Pillar/Aspect.

Exaltation is not merely a generic numerical bonus.

A rough 1–3 Spent VP range is an early calibration benchmark only; each Rite defines compatible expressions and costs.

### Level 4 — Twofold Devotion + Ability Increase

Gain:

- +1 Ability;
- maximum **2 Active Devotional Rites**.

Only one remains Chosen unless a later rule says otherwise.

### Level 5 — Living Liturgy

If the Chosen Devotion has remained active and unchanged continuously since the start of the Priest's previous turn, its Exaltation may express **one authored compatible property from another Aspect of the same chosen Pillar**.

Rules:

- Chosen Devotion remains the anchor;
- no second Prepared Prayer is activated;
- pay the normal listed Exaltation cost;
- no generic VP discount;
- cross-Aspect property must be explicitly authored as compatible;
- no separate target, action, full technique payload, or independent duration unless explicitly authored;
- only one Living Liturgy cross-Aspect expression at a time unless expanded later.

Changing or ending the Chosen Devotion breaks the stable state.

### Active Prayers

Low Priest may still prepare and cast immediate projected prayers.

They use normal action and VP costs.

Low Focus therefore does not mean passive-only magic.

## Three-Focus Priest distinction

| Focus | Priest expression |
|---|---|
| High | **Invoke** — broad prepared projected access plus costly spontaneous normal-tier invocation |
| Medium | **Distribute** — move and temporarily extend prepared Consecrations among valid recipients |
| Low | **Devote** — maintain self-anchored Rites and deepen a stable Chosen Devotion |

## Retired Priest rules

The current foundation supersedes:

- Faith Rest resetting Pillar Accord;
- Pillar Accord-based VP recovery reduction/restoration;
- global Pillar Accord VP-cost modifiers;
- percentage miracle inversion;
- automatic universal inversion at -3;
- legacy Wisdom-save Resonance Collapse (the retired Priest mechanic);
- treating brief prayer as sufficient to erase genuine ideological conflict.

## Calibration still unresolved

- final balance of provisional L1–3 **base Prepared Prayer counts, Accord +1 start, negative-Accord floor, and Open Invocation +1 Spent VP surcharge** (now executable v0, not missing);
- final Miracle Petition numerical calibration/frequency behavior;
- exact Intercession cost/ranges;
- technique-specific Exaltation costs;
- Living Liturgy compatibility tables.
