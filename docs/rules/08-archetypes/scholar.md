# Scholar Foundation

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34. Structural level 1–5 rules are promoted here. **Level-1–3 repertoire and formula/preparation capacity now have explicit v0 playtest numbers**; broader technique-specific numeric values and post-L3 counts remain provisional where not authored. See [B04/B05 starting repertoires](../../design/b04-b05-priest-scholar-starting-repertoires-v0.md).

## Archetype identity

A Scholar accesses the Veil through **learned technical methods** rather than prayer, emotion-linked instinct, or a permanent Anointed bond.

A Scholar does **not** require a Pillar's approval, recognition, favor, or personal relationship. Pillar Aspects are real stable patterns within the Veil; when using a **Pillar technique**, Scholar methods reproduce, enter, route, or shape Volition according to those patterns. The Pillar remains metaphysically relevant to **that Pillar technique** because the pattern exists, not because it consciously grants permission. **Universal Veil operations** instead work with common Veil structures or spatial relationships and require **no Pillar/Aspect identity**.

- A Scholar may potentially learn **Pillar techniques** from **any Pillar or Aspect**.
- Every **Pillar technique** is learned individually; Scholars do not automatically gain an entire Pillar/Aspect catalogue.
- **Pillar techniques retain their real Pillar/Aspect identity** and do not become abstract setting-free buffs. **Universal Veil techniques/procedures instead use the appropriate Universal family identity**, such as Veil Inspection or Veil Translocation, and must remain within that family's authored scope.
- Ordinary learned techniques resolve at their normal listed effectiveness, VP cost, action requirement, and Veil Control scaling.
- Scholars do not suffer a generalist penalty.
- Scholars receive no automatic Priest Pillar Accord amplification, Gifted emotional amplification, or Anointed Aspect Authority.
- Scholar strength is **configurable breadth**: selection, combination, preparation, technical shaping, and deliberate cross-Pillar repertoire.

The final catalogue/repertoire calibration must preserve Scholar as the archetype with the strongest **player-configurable problem coverage over time**, even though Gifted may possess a large fixed catalogue.

**Universal Veil acquisition is automatic for all four Archetypes**, not a Scholar-exclusive learned spell list: Scholars **know [Veil Inspection v0](../07-veil-magic/veil-inspection-v0.md) at character level 1 and [Blink v0](../07-veil-magic/blink-v0.md) at character level 3** under [Universal Veil acquisition](../07-veil-magic/universal-veil-fundamentals.md#universal-eligibility-and-automatic-acquisition--locked). These procedures **do not consume Scholar individually learned Pillar-technique counts, Prepared Techniques or a Talent**, and cannot automatically become Composite Casting components without explicit compatibility. **Automatic knowledge is not free activation:** retain each procedure's VP, timing, spatial/subject and other eligibility restrictions. Advanced Universal procedures have **no usable automatic unlock until their own profiles and level gates are authored**; do not apply the Scholar's individually learned Pillar-technique rule to them.

## L1–3 individually learned repertoire — all Scholar Focuses

| Scholar Focus | Distinct **known** Pillar techniques L1 | L2 | L3 | Additional preparation / active limit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **High** | **5** | **6** | **6** | **1** prepared legal Composite Formula at L1, **2** at L2; **none required at L3** under Formula Fluency |
| **Medium** | **4** | **5** | **5** | No general prepared-spell limit; max **1 active eligible Inward Boost** at L1–3 |
| **Low** | **3** | **4** | **4** | **2 / 3 / 3 Prepared Techniques** out of known; max **1 active eligible Embodiment** at L1–3 |

**Starting known techniques are individually selected from actually authored Pillar/Aspect techniques** at the permitted character-level tier (L1–2 Tier 1, L3 Tier 2 ceiling). Scholar cross-Pillar knowledge is permitted; **no entire Aspect catalogue or unauthored technique is automatically learned**. Every distinct technique occupies **one known slot** regardless of its number of profiles, Used Tiers or permitted applications. [Expanded Repertoire Talent](../10-advancement/talents-level-1-5-v0.md#tal-s01--expanded-repertoire) grants **one extra known** technique when genuinely eligible, **not** an extra prepared spell or composite formula. If an eligible actual technique is unavailable, unused capacity stays empty until an independent later learning grant/retraining permits filling; it cannot create a new spell.

At **level 2**, **each Scholar Focus learns +1 eligible individual Pillar technique** and High's formula cap becomes **2** while Low's prepared subset cap becomes **3**. **Level 3 awards no additional known or prepared count**: High instead gains Formula Fluency, Medium Efficient Routing/Overrouting and Low Deep Embodiment. The Tier-2 level ceiling at L3 permits only a technique's **expressly authored Tier-2 mode**, not automatic knowledge of new Tier-2 spells. Universal Veil Inspection L1 and Blink L3 are **known separately and use no Scholar known/prepared/formula slot**.

**Technical preparation:** High L1–2 can replace their prepared legal formula list, and Low L1–3 can replace the prepared known-technique subset, after **10 uninterrupted minutes of technical study/practice at most once per six elapsed in-world hours**. The initial chosen set at L1 creation is free. Level-up adds capacity immediately but its newly eligible formula or Low prepared subset entry must be selected in the next legal preparation. Preparation **does not grant knowledge**, regenerate VP, waive equipment/activation requirements or create an Active Embodiment. Medium has no general preparation step for ordinary known-spell casting. At L3, High has **no prepared-formula list requirement** because Formula Fluency removes it.

## High Scholar — Projection through synthesis

**Core identity:** broad technical caster and situational synthesist.

**Core question:** *Which known technique solves this, and is combining two techniques worth the extra VP?*

### Levels 1–2 — Technical Repertoire and Prepared Composite Formulae

High Scholar starts at **5 individually known Pillar techniques at L1**, expands to **6 at L2**, remains **6 at L3**, and can cast any otherwise legal known Primary as normal. At levels 1–2, its prepared **Composite Formula capacity is 1 at L1 and 2 at L2**. A formula records the **ordered Primary and Secondary known-technique identities, exact shared eligible profile key and compatible host/delivery mode**; reversing roles or changing the key requires a different formula if independently legal. Formulas grant no extra technique knowledge, an extra casting action, permission for unsupported Secondary/Rider combinations or VP discounts.

A High Scholar may use **Composite Casting** with one known Primary Technique and one compatible known Secondary Technique. **The caster selects which known technique is Primary and which is Secondary**, and both must expose the **same exact authored profile identifier** for the chosen execution. Similar descriptions (e.g. `Thrown Projectile` versus `Ammunition Shot`) are not sufficient. Both must be otherwise compatible; a matching label does not waive an express ban such as a second Reserved Weapon enchantment.

At levels 1–2, the exact Primary + Secondary pairing must be prepared as a **composite formula** within the **1/2-slot limit**. If no two known techniques form an actually authorized combination, formula capacity may remain empty; it is not a guarantee of an invented composite.

#### Primary Technique

The Primary defines:

- target;
- range;
- area or shape;
- attack/resistance resolution;
- fundamental delivery chassis.

The Primary resolves at full normal strength.

#### Secondary Technique

The Secondary is incorporated into the Primary rather than resolving as a second independent technique.

**Interaction with [Technique Structure v0](../07-veil-magic/technique-structure-v0.md#8-existing-high-scholar-composite-casting-and-future-contribution-interfaces):** Composite Casting is **already the authorization** to select a compatible known technique's **Secondary contribution** as part of this Primary. An explicitly authored **Composite-compatible Secondary profile** provides its stated reduced output **instead of** (not in addition to, or halved again by) the approximate default below. If no such compatible Secondary values are authored, the default below still applies to an otherwise compatible known technique; the existence of a Secondary cell for item-only or environmental use does **not** itself grant composite eligibility.

By default it contributes:

- approximately half of normal scalable numerical output; and
- a diminished version of one compatible defining special property, where applicable.

It does not normally retain independent targeting, a separate attack roll, its full original area, its full original duration, or every original rider.

#### Cost and action

```
Composite VP Cost =
full Primary VP cost + full Secondary VP cost
```

There is no default discount. Composite Casting provides **action compression**, not VP efficiency. If both components expressly support the same integrated **`Ammunition Shot`** profile, the paid bow Physical Action/heavy-crossbow Full Action supplies the host action, the attachment adds **0 extra Projection AP**, and **both full technique VP costs** still apply; there is only **one** attack/projectile. Other mixed action categories require an express authored compatibility ruling; do not treat an integrated attachment as a free independent attack.

Use the slower action requirement of the two components:

- Projection Action + Projection Action → **1 Projection Action**;
- Projection Action + Full Action → **1 Full Action**;
- Full Action + Full Action → **1 Full Action**.

Ordinary single-technique casting remains fully competent and is often the more resource-efficient choice. **Do not pay two separate Focus-priced spell activation actions:** the composite costs the single slower action stated above. Its Secondary's reduced output does not reduce either component's **full VP cost**. Composite Casting does **not** by itself authorize a second separately reserved enchantment on one weapon; that is a later specialized use.

### Level 2 — Expanded Repertoire

Learn **one additional authored eligible individual Pillar technique**: High known **5→6**, and increase prepared composite-formula capacity **1→2**. The extra formula is chosen at the next legal technical preparation; no separate magic activation occurs.

This is breadth, not a generic damage/healing/potency bonus.

### Level 3 — Formula Fluency

Prepared-pairing restriction ends.

Any compatible pair of known techniques may be synthesized at casting time without preparing that exact pair in advance.

Formula Fluency does not reduce VP cost or remove the normal Secondary reduction.

### Level 4 — Expanded Repertoire + Ability Increase

Gain:

- the universal level-4 **+1 Ability** increase; and
- another meaningful repertoire expansion.

No generic Scholar-specific potency multiplier is added.

### Level 5 — Advanced Synthesis

When Composite Casting, choose one Secondary emphasis.

**Numerical Emphasis**

- Secondary contributes approximately half normal numerical output.
- Incidental special properties are strongly diminished.

**Property Emphasis**

- Secondary contributes little or no numerical output.
- One compatible defining non-numerical property is preserved substantially more strongly.

Examples include knockback, regeneration suppression, concealment, restraint, interruption, or terrain alteration.

The purpose is to construct the **right answer**, not simply stack raw output. Explicit authored Secondary output defines the baseline for synthesis; **Numerical/Property Emphasis still applies** and may be supplied by separately authored variants or by this feature's existing defaults. Do **not** apply a second automatic half-strength reduction simply because the contribution is already written as Secondary.

### High Scholar boundary

Composite Casting is a situational technical advantage, not a mandatory rotation.

The High Scholar must support combat caster, battlemage, controller, support/healer, generalist, and deliberately focused repertoires.

## Medium Scholar — Routing and repertoire

**Core identity:** rapid allocation of committed inward Veil power plus individually learned cross-Pillar techniques.

**Core question:** *Where should my power be routed right now?*

### Inward Boosts and channels

Certain learned techniques have the **Inward** property.

An Inward Boost:

- commits its listed VP;
- normally has no ongoing action cost once active;
- remains active until released or rerouted;
- occupies an applicable **Inward Channel**.

Representative channels include:

- Weapon;
- Motor Control;
- Reflex;
- Locomotion;
- Body;
- Senses;
- Regeneration.

An authored **self-targeted [Projection] technique or Continuous Reserve does not automatically become an Inward Boost** with rerouting permission. The learned technique must expressly support an **Inward** configuration, channel and compatible replacement conditions; otherwise it remains eligible only for its ordinary authorized casting profile.

Two limits apply simultaneously:

1. the Medium Scholar has a maximum number of active Inward Boosts;
2. only one active Boost may occupy the same channel.

### Rerouting

Rerouting replaces one active compatible Inward Boost with another known compatible Inward Boost while redistributing committed power.

- no action cost;
- no per-round frequency limit;
- levels 1–2: **3 Spent VP** per reroute;
- levels 3–5: **2 Spent VP** per reroute.

Rerouting does not itself create an outward technique or waive that technique's normal activation action.

The universal zero-action/no-refresh rule applies: rerouting does not grant interrupt timing and does not refresh limited output.

### Overrouting

Overrouting pushes an Inward Boost beyond ordinary output by drawing capability away from a defined competing function.

- benefit and sacrifice are authored by the technique;
- normal maximum: **2 steps**;
- **+1 Spent VP per Overroute step** in addition to the reroute cost;
- dropping back to the normal version of the same Boost costs no action and no VP;
- pushing into Overroute again requires paying the cost again.

### Medium Scholar progression

| Level | Foundation feature |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **Veil Routing** — max 1 active Inward Boost; reroute costs 3 Spent VP |
| 2 | **Technical Repertoire** — learn one eligible additional Pillar technique (**4→5 known**); no new general prepared spell limit |
| 3 | **Efficient Routing + Overrouting** — reroute becomes 2 Spent VP; Overrouting enters mature loop |
| 4 | **Dual Routing + Ability Increase** — max 2 active Inward Boosts; +1 Ability |
| 5 | **Matrix Reconfiguration** — one rerouting payment may replace both active Boosts at once; Overroute surcharges still apply |

### Medium Scholar boundary

Medium Scholar changes **what function** inward Veil power performs.

It must remain distinct from:

- Medium Priest, which changes **who receives** a Consecration;
- Medium Gifted, whose established conduits **retune expression** with emotion;
- Medium Anointed, which changes the **Vessel** receiving one permanent Aspect.

## Low Scholar — Prepared Embodiment

**Core identity:** prepared, martial-first, cross-Pillar technical toolbox.

**Core question:** *What did I prepare, what is worth activating, and when is spending scarce VP better than simply fighting?*

The Low Scholar remains fully capable of ordinary combat without magic.

### Known, Prepared, and Active

Low Scholar techniques exist in three layers:

1. **Known Techniques** — all learned techniques.
2. **Prepared Techniques** — at L1 choose **up to 2** from the **3 known** techniques; at L2/L3 choose **up to 3** from **4 known**; only these prepared techniques may be activated under Low Scholar's Prepared Arsenal, including their outward legal profiles. Preparation does not waive technique action or VP requirements.
3. **Active Embodiments** — the much smaller set of prepared inward/equipment techniques currently sustained.

### Embodiments and switching

An Embodiment is a prepared inward or equipment-focused technique affecting the Scholar or immediately used equipment, **only when the individual technique/feature expressly authors compatible Low Scholar Embodiment permission**. A self-targeted [Projection] spell, a general sustained Reserve, or an ordinary `Weapon Enchantment` profile is **not automatically** a Low Scholar Embodiment for switching, Intensification or special Focus bonuses.

The Low Scholar does **not** gain Medium-style rerouting.

To switch:

1. release the existing effect;
2. activate the replacement normally.

Released Committed VP returns to Available VP, but previously Spent VP remains Spent.

This makes changing configuration possible but intentionally inefficient.

### Active Embodiment limit

- levels 1–3: maximum **1 Active Embodiment**;
- levels 4–5: maximum **2 Active Embodiments**.

Channel conflicts still apply.

Low Focus does not prohibit outward techniques. Prepared outward effects use their normal action and VP costs, but the smaller Low-Focus VP pool makes them selective expenditures.

### Low Scholar progression

| Level | Foundation feature |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **Prepared Arsenal** — limited prepared loadout; max 1 Active Embodiment |
| 2 | **Field Preparation** — learn one eligible individual Pillar technique (**3→4 known**), increase prepared-technique cap **2→3**; choose newly prepared subset on next legal technical preparation |
| 3 | **Deep Embodiment** — compatible Active Embodiments may be **Intensified** by paying technique-defined additional VP |
| 4 | **Dual Embodiment + Ability Increase** — max 2 Active Embodiments; +1 Ability |
| 5 | **Entrenched Configuration** — if an Embodiment has remained unchanged since the start of the previous turn, its next Intensification costs 1 VP less, minimum 1 unless explicitly allowed to reach 0 |

Changing or replacing an Embodiment breaks its Entrenched state.

### Low Scholar boundary

Low Scholar rewards **preparation and commitment**.

Its preferred escalation is to Intensify a good prepared choice rather than repeatedly replace it.

### Explicit Tier-1 Medium/Low profile permissions (provisional)

[Scholar Inward Boost and Embodiment Profiles v0](../07-veil-magic/scholar-inward-embodiment-profiles-v0.md) now authors four **exact** Focus-specific alternatives: Medium Stone Skin Body and Guided Regeneration Regeneration **Inwards**, and Low Stone Skin / Guided Regeneration Self **Embodiments**. Each requires independently **learned** technique identity and, for Low, **prepared** selection. No general `Self` Projection conversion, extra acquisition, free timing, Priest discount, or unlimited Focus configuration follows.

The [B05 evidence record](../../design/b05-scholar-inward-embodiment-profiles-v0.md) verifies **isolated AP/VP and configuration contracts**. Independent source-authored Medium/Low Scholar character sheets, genuine G0/G1/G3/G4 effects and end-to-end role certification remain **open**. In particular, provisional L3 Overroute movement loss and Low Intensification still require native event binding.

## Three-Focus Scholar distinction

| Focus | Scholar expression |
|---|---|
| High | **Synthesize** known techniques into situational composite answers |
| Medium | **Reroute** committed power among learned inward functions |
| Low | **Prepare and commit** to a small active Embodiment configuration |

## Calibration still unresolved

The following remain outside the locked structural foundation:

- final balance of **authored provisional L1–3 known counts (5/4/3 → 6/5/4)**, Low **2/3/3 prepared** and High **1/2 prepared formulas**;
- wider technique-specific Intensification costs/effects beyond the provisional two Earth/Life Scholar modes and actual native Step/G4 proof;
- numerical Composite Casting burst calibration once High Projection Tempo supports multiple Projection Actions.
