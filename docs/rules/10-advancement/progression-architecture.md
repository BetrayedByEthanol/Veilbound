# Advancement Architecture

> Canonical extraction from Core Rules Audit v34. This file distinguishes locked Veilbound structure from inherited or still-missing advancement mechanics.

## Current level chassis

Veilbound currently uses **levels 1–20**.

**Level has a canonical in-world meaning:** it represents the character's increasing depth of integration with the Veil. Focus determines where that integration is predominantly expressed.

The choice of a 20-level numerical scale is still inherited from 5e rather than uniquely derived from Veilbound, but the meaning of progression within that scale is now Veilbound-specific.

Within that chassis, Veilbound's three-stage progression structure is locked:

| Levels | Stage |
|---:|---|
| 1–5 | **Foundation** |
| 6–15 | **Specialization** |
| 16–20 | **Mastery** |

## Foundation — levels 1–5

Levels 1–5 establish the base combination of:

- Archetype;
- Focus;
- core resource/access identity;
- baseline physical/magical play pattern.

The base identity should be functioning before specialization begins.

## Specialization — levels 6–15

The specialization architecture is locked as a **4 × 3 × 3 matrix**:

- 4 Archetypes;
- 3 Focuses;
- 3 specialization branches for each Archetype/Focus combination;
- **36 specialization branches** total.

The specialization feature schedule is:

- level 6;
- level 7;
- level 11;
- level 15.

The existence and matrix structure are canonical.

The complete mechanics for all 36 specializations are **not yet complete** and must not be inferred from names or older legacy material.

## Mastery — levels 16–20

The architecture contains **12 mastery identities**:

- 4 Archetypes × 3 Focuses.

The mastery architecture is locked.

The existing detailed mastery implementations remain **provisional/conflicting** in the audit because several still use legacy terminology or conflict with newer native systems.

## Ability advancement

Ability advancement is defined in [Abilities and Training](../02-character-creation/abilities-and-training.md):

- levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 20;
- increase one ability of the player's choice by +1;
- normal permanent natural cap +5.

## Training advancement — B04 v0

The **separation between Weapon Development and Learned Disciplines**, the **one point per directly invested +1** rule, the **melee-only half-transfer**, and the **single-family refinement restriction** are B04 design decisions. Numerical schedules and +5 maximums remain **provisional playtest baselines**:

- [Weapon Training](../05-combat/weapon-training-and-refinements-v0.md#provisional-v0-weapon-development-schedule): +2 maximum and 2 points at creation; +1 point at levels **4, 8, 12, 16, 20**, with +3/+4/+5 gates at levels 4/8/12 respectively.
- [Learned Disciplines](../02-character-creation/abilities-and-training.md#learned-discipline-progression--provisional-v0): 8 starting points and one additional point at **every even level 2–20**; +3/+4/+5 gates at levels 4/8/12 respectively.
- Character advancement does not require a player to broaden into an unwanted skill: points may be **saved** for eligible directly invested ranks or **future authored single-category refinements**. **No refinement effects are yet authored.**

These awards are separate from the existing universal Ability increases. A level-1 character can use the authored starter allocation even while later-level refinement catalog and balance remain open.

## Universal Veil acquisition — B05 locked

All four Archetypes and all Focus choices **automatically acquire authored Universal Veil procedures at their explicit minimum character levels**, without Talent selections, training purchases, known Pillar-technique counts, Prepared Prayer slots or other Archetype-specific technique capacity. [Veil Inspection v0](../07-veil-magic/veil-inspection-v0.md) is known at **level 1** and [Blink v0](../07-veil-magic/blink-v0.md) at **level 3**, under the [Universal Veil acquisition foundation](../07-veil-magic/universal-veil-fundamentals.md#universal-eligibility-and-automatic-acquisition--locked). Unlocking does **not** add Available VP, erase the full technique action/target/path requirements or guarantee a character can fund the technique immediately. Future Universal procedures require **authored executable profiles and level gates** before they join this automatic progression. Veil Analysis, Scrying and more advanced Translocation remain **unauthored** and confer no level-up benefits yet. Blink and Inspection's optional numeric technique-tier classifications remain unassigned; don't impose a fabricated tier or separate learning gate.

## Talents — B07 early-level playtest

The [B07 Talent Framework](talents-framework-v0.md) defines player-selected **Talents** as separate from Archetype Features, Pillar/Universal Veil Techniques, Weapon Style Refinements, and Learned Discipline Refinements. The [level-1–5 catalogue](talents-level-1-5-v0.md) supplies **32 provisional playtest entries**, favoring physical/combat options while retaining spellcasting, Archetype and noncombat selection. No magical item creation or potion manufacture is in this MVP.

The structural design direction is **Low Focus receives more selections than Medium, which receives more than High**. The current **provisional** level-1–5 award schedule is **High: 1 and 5; Medium: 1, 3 and 5; Low: 1, 2, 3 and 5**. These Talent selections are separate from both B04 Training budgets and Ability increases. The longer-level schedule (6–20), retraining and final numerical Talent balance are **not locked**. Do not import D&D feats.

## Restricted L1–3 MVP pilot — milestone awards (approved 30 October 2026)

For the **3–5-session MVP adventure only**, characters begin at **Level 1** and advance to **Level 2** and then **Level 3** by **two distinct, explicitly GM-declared story milestones** during the authored adventure. The GM identifies and records the completed event and level award in the adventure/session record; neither a fixed encounter win, character performance metric, clock interval, XP total nor an Anointed's personal Mission/Destiny story beat **automatically grants a character level**. If a significant personal Mission event also occurs, it remains separately adjudicated under its own rules. A milestone advances **one level at a time** and may not be reused for a second award or skip a level. Placement of the two events in the adventure is an **authored GM/pilot task**, not an invented universal session-number trigger.

On an actual L1→L2 or L2→L3 award, use only the already-authored legal Archetype/Focus/Training/Talent/VP/AP/universal Veil acquisition schedule for that new level and independently check the resulting sheet and available resources. No automatic spell affordability, new Aspect/Anchor/Authority, extra Talent, new VP refresh, or general XP conversion is implied. **Provisional watch:** evaluate whether two milestones within 3–5 sessions provide sufficient time to explore each Focus at L1, L2 and L3 and whether character role changes remain meaningful; revise placement after actual GM/player evidence. This is a pilot procedure, **not a general level-award rule for L4–20 or all campaigns**.

## Not yet canonical

The audit does not yet provide complete native rules for:

- general campaign XP or milestone level gain **outside the restricted L1–3 MVP pilot**;
- complete level 6–20 Talent progression and final Talent balance;
- general multiclassing;
- how VP, Focus, technique progression, and innate archetype traits combine under multiclassing;
- complete mechanics for every specialization;
- final mastery mechanics;
- hybrid Prestige Classes.

These remain explicit design tasks rather than gaps to fill from 5e defaults.
