# Canonical Pillar Aspect Identities and Boundaries

> **CANONICAL LORE — B05 identity and classification decisions, 13 October 2026.** This is the authoritative reference for all **48 named Aspects**, their External / Internal / Transform categories, and their positive/negative metaphysical boundaries. It supersedes provisional category and identity hypotheses in the [PR #59 design audit](../design/16-pillar-48-aspect-coverage-compatibility-audit.md). The [Veil and Pillars](veil-and-pillars.md) remains authoritative for overall cosmology.

## Reading the categories

- **External (E):** expresses a Pillar outward through phenomena, forces, interactions or influence upon the world. External does **not** automatically mean offense or technique-level [Projection].
- **Internal (I):** operates within **a subject's** bodily, mental or metaphysical condition. The subject can be the caster, an ally, an enemy or another valid subject. Internal is **not** limited to self-targeting or the technique-level Inward descriptor; a High-Focus projection can affect another subject internally.
- **Transform (T):** changes properties, state, composition, form, configuration or nature. The transformation need not be permanent and is not restricted to utility effects.

Each Pillar possesses **one of each category**. Categories describe a characteristic **metaphysical operation**, not a mandatory role or the only eligible target/expression. A technique's projection tag, focus compatibility, action/Turn AP, VP, delivery (Intrusion/Imposition/Manifestation), target, resistance, scaling and cadence must be **separately authored**. For example, a projected Internal effect on an unwilling creature's protected mind or body may be Intrusion. A manifested phenomenon remains a separate delivery decision.

The boundaries below say what an Aspect **does not automatically authorize**. They are not permanent bans on exceptional explicitly authored and balanced techniques. Pillars have no inherent moral alignments. Domain examples are **lore illustrations**, not executable spells.

## Individual Aspect identities

### Order
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Law | E | Magically enforces real or caster-declared rules within an authored scope. | Not universal lawmaking authority, moral truth or guaranteed unconditional obedience. |
| Constancy | I | Maintains an internal condition or range despite changing surroundings, like homeostasis. | Not universal immobility or freezing the external world. |
| Stability | T | Changes something to increase its resistance to subsequent alteration or disruption. | Not automatically physical fortification or unrestricted negation of magic. |

### Liberty
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defiance | T | Drives rejection and overturning of established conditions or constraints, beyond merely resisting them. | Not unconditional ward-breaking, escape or authority over others. |
| Autonomy | I | Maintains the value and exercise of a subject's own free will and self-direction. | Not blanket Intrusion immunity or power to decide for someone else. |
| Exploration | E | Reaches outward to seek, encounter and discover new things or possibilities. | Not automatically Blink, guaranteed knowledge or unlimited travel. |

### Life
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restoration | T | Restores damaged biological function and, in exceptional cases, genuine life after death. | Resurrection is exceptionally rare, may require a material-gathering quest, and lacks an authored general procedure. |
| Preservation | I | Protects living processes from deterioration rather than repairing what is already damaged. | Not indefinite death suppression or automatic restorative healing. |
| Nature | E | Expresses and interacts with living organisms and ecosystems: growth, entangling vines, treant-like forms and communication with animals. | Not general control of all elements, free creatures or bypass of relevant target defenses. |

### Death
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plague | T | Changes health into sickness, disease, decay or corruption; spreading disease is a possible expression. | Not guaranteed infection or bypass of living-body Intrusion resistance. |
| Mastery | I | Operates within an existing living or dead **physical body** to delay death, sustain dying or reanimate and command corporeal undead. | Requires a body; does not commune with or directly command disembodied spirits, restore life or repair injury by default. |
| Spirit | E | Communicates with and directs **real independent spirits** and other incorporeal posthumous presences. | Not corporeal necromancy, a fully explained afterlife, or automatic resurrection. Spirit persistence and ultimate fate remain mysterious. |

### Fire
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conflagration | E | Generates ignition, flame and outward spreading combustion. | Not every temperature effect or free area damage. |
| Warmth | I | Regulates useful heat and thermal condition within a subject. | Not generic wound healing or all external fire control. |
| Forge | T | Reshapes and joins existing materials through heat and deliberate workmanship. | Not unrestricted matter conversion or automatic permanent enchantment. |

### Water
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flow | E | Directs physical water currents, redistribution and pressure. | Not free creature movement, time manipulation or universal liquid control. |
| Calm | I | Establishes tranquility, stillness or reduced turbulence in water or a subject's inner state. | Not automatic sleep, compulsion or unlimited emotional control. |
| Medium | T | Changes water into a magical carrier able to store, preserve and release **other Pillars' Aspect expressions**. | Does not independently grant source-Aspect access or free power; Water plus source-expression investment is intended, with capacity, VP, duration and release mechanics unauthored. |

### Earth
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quake | E | Produces seismic disturbances and displacement of ground or stone. | Not unavoidable damage or unlimited collapse outside hazards and physical resolution. |
| Fortification | I | Reinforces physical integrity and protection in a body, object or defensive structure; stone skin is an illustrative effect. | Not free Guard, Intervene, Armor stacking or the same principle as Order Stability. |
| Bounty | T | Alters land or resource productivity, improving fertility, cultivation and yield. | Not instant wealth, unbounded matter creation or the totality of Life/Nature. |

### Skywinds
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gale | E | Projects gusts, wind pressure and directed air currents. | Not electrical discharge or unlimited forced movement. |
| Swiftness | I | Enhances bodily speed, agility and locomotion. | Not extra Turn AP, automatic Dodges or teleportation. |
| Sky | T | Transforms weather or atmospheric conditions, including clouds and large-scale sky states. | Not automatic flight or flying combat; flight is deferred to separately authored advanced techniques, if included at all. |

### Lightning
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strike | E | Projects direct, sudden electrical discharges. | Not automatic paralysis or bypass of legal attack/defense resolution. |
| Reflexes | I | Heightens a subject's responsiveness and reaction timing. | Not free Reactions, extra Turn AP or literal future sight. |
| Charge | T | Changes electrical charge through accumulation, storage, transfer or redistribution. | Not VP generation or unlimited conversion of arbitrary energies. |

### Kinship
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guardian | E | Extends wards and protective action toward others, including improved ability to guard another. | Not free Shield Guard, Protective Intervene or other defensive resources. |
| Sacrifice | I | Allows a willing bearer to assume another's injury, burden, cost or harmful effect. | Not costless transfer or unrestricted HP/Vigor/VP conversion. |
| Unity | T | Creates or strengthens supernatural bonds between people, enabling possible shared senses, silent communication and coordination. | Not forced loyalty, identity merger or extra actions by implication. |

### Strength
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | E | Expresses extraordinary real bodily strength and capability through direct physical exertion. | Not detached projected kinetic blasts; telekinesis belongs to Mind/Psionics. |
| Endurance | I | Lets a subject keep acting through pain, fatigue, exhaustion or injury, such as running on a broken knee. | Neither prevents nor heals the injury, and does not automatically alter the death threshold. |
| Training | T | **Temporarily elevates the practical mastery of an existing trained physical discipline**: a martial artist moves like a master; a swordsman reads combat patterns as if predicting movement; an athlete executes master-level obstacle technique. | Requires genuine training foundations, gives no literal foresight or unrelated skill, and does not permanently teach mastery or grant progression. |

### Fortune
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manipulation | E | Influences other people or existing external circumstances toward an intended outcome. | Not unlimited mental compulsion or rewriting established facts. |
| Charm | I | Enhances a person's charismatic presence and favorable social impression, potentially as an aura. | Not guaranteed persuasion or automatically enforced affection. |
| Luck | T | Changes the probabilities of **genuinely undetermined** outcomes. | Not guaranteed success, free rerolls or retroactive replacement of decided outcomes. |

### Mind
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Psionics | E | Projects psychic force or communication: telekinesis, force pushes and telepathic messages. | Not a general category for mind control, emotional domination or memory alteration. |
| Memory | T | Restores, suppresses, changes or fabricates memory; complete unwilling rewriting can amount to mind control. | Not free access to protected minds: resistance, especially Intrusion, must be respected. |
| Insight | I | Enhances intuitive understanding and sense of information instead of simply revealing answers; authored remote perception and scrying expressions may occur. | Not omniscience, guaranteed truth, automatic prophecy or unauthored Universal Veil Scrying. |

### Alchemy
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concoctions | E | Creates prepared reactions such as acids, poisons, smoke or explosives by coupling actual ingredient chemistry with Veil magic. | Not free consumables; ingredients and mundane reactions still matter. |
| Elixirs | I | Brews substances that internally alter living organisms through the combined properties of ingredients and Alchemy magic, including healing or enhancement. | Not merely bottled Life magic; ingredient variants can alter strength and produce secondary effects. |
| Transmutation | T | Changes material composition, form or properties through physical/alchemical processes coupled with Veil expression, sometimes more directly. | Not unrestricted matter creation, wealth or automatically the same as Fire/Forge. |

### Arts
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance | E | Projects artistic action outward, such as a distracting dance, compelling performance or very loud music. | Not automatic mind control or a generic bonus to all actions. |
| Inspiration | I | Invokes new ideas, motivation, courage, creativity or the right words for a speech inside a subject. | Not compulsory belief or permanent learned proficiency. |
| Creation | T | Transforms existing material into artistic works, possibly with magical properties such as a distracting painting or magical music box. | Not unrestricted material conjuration or automatic crafting/enchantment rules. |

### Secrets
| Aspect | Class | Defining operation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deception | I | Supernaturally enhances the deceiver's lying, disguise, appearance or false identity. | Not by default directly rewriting an observer's memories or thoughts. |
| Shadow | E | Creates genuine supernatural darkness and external shadows that can conceal or engulf the caster or others. | Covering the caster does not make the Aspect Internal; no automatic intangibility or teleportation. |
| Hallucination | T | Transforms apparent sensory properties, creating illusory noises, hologram-like images or painted people that seem to move. | Externally manifested illusions differ from directly manipulating protected perception; each technique specifies the correct defense. |

## Critical cross-Pillar boundaries

- **Life / Death:** Restoration restores life, while Mastery delays death or animates existing bodies without making them alive. Spirit addresses independent spirits; the afterlife and detailed spirit persistence remain mysterious. Exceptional resurrection needs authored requirements and costs.
- **Endurance / Preservation / Constancy:** Endurance allows function despite harm; Preservation prevents deterioration of life; Constancy maintains a subject's inner condition. No one operation automatically performs the others.
- **Stability / Fortification / Guardian:** Stability changes resistance to disruption; Fortification reinforces physical integrity; Guardian protects others through a relationship. No implicit free Guard/Intervene.
- **Training / Reflexes / Insight / Psionics:** Training grants temporary practical trained mastery; Reflexes improves reactions; Insight improves sensing/understanding; Psionics projects mental force and messages. The swordsman's seemingly prophetic prediction through Training is superior learned pattern execution, not precognition.
- **Forge / Transmutation / Creation:** Heat-workmanship, alchemical conversion and artistic transformation can produce similar objects through different metaphysical means.
- **Nature / Bounty:** Living biology and ecology are distinct from the productive qualities of land and resources.
- **Alchemy / Water Medium:** Alchemy's prepared ingredients have real chemical/biological contributions and possibly unwanted side effects. Medium stores an existing other-Aspect expression in water and is not a general replacement for ingredient-driven brewing.
- **Fortune / Mind / Secrets:** Manipulating situations, influencing undecided odds, enhanced charismatic presence, producing illusions, altering memories and sending telepathic messages are different operations. Hostile alteration of protected inner states remains subject to applicable magical resistance.
- **Insight / Universal Scrying:** Aspect-bound intuitive remote perception is permitted as future content; Universal Scrying is separately unauthored and not automatically acquired.

## Deferred technique design and implementation

The 48 names and this category/domain table are canonical; **no spells or numbers are created here**. Future entries must separately author tier, action/Turn AP, VP, targets, defense and delivery, persistence/cadence, scaling, Archetype and Focus eligibility, and concrete effect. Cheat Death is a historical locked framework but lacks an executable current technique family. Water/Medium's two-source investment direction does not yet define who supplies access/VP or how costs, capacity and release are calculated. Flying combat remains deferred. Mind control beyond fully altering memory is not automatically assigned to an Aspect.

Keep the **48 × 8 Gifted emotion compatibility matrix unchanged**: eight links across eight distinct Pillars, no category quota, and Strong/Plausible mechanically identical after character creation. The **eight-Pillar level 1–3 MVP set remains unselected**; this lore canonicalization does not close the playable-technique or starting-repertoire blocker.
